Journal of Disquiet

Yesterday’s entry

I step out of home yesterday evening to meet A after noticing his missed call the day before. I called him over to my place and then went on a joy-ride to Manyata tech park. It was peak hours and the traffic was high. I consumed an Irani chai at a tea shop there and so did he along with his cigarettes. I went on rambling about my recent illness and travails, he spoke it his trips with his friends. We paced around the campus for some time discussing movies, politics, philosophy, and other miscellaneous things. After which he dropped me on his scooter back to my place and parted ways.

I am a bit worried about my expedition outside yesterday considering my recent illness, bad weather, depleted bodily energy, and psychological condition. I had decided not to step out for anything and preferred to stay at home all the time, yet I broke those decisions I made for myself and went out spending fuel and other’s money. I had decided that going out is a wasteful experience as I would lose out the money to government in the form of taxes and my health to pollution. I had vowed to remain indoors work on projects that I have undertaken in the areas of programming, computer science, mathematics and economics. I have unfulfilled studying tasks, yet to complete summaries, yet to publish works and so on. I should remain diligent to these efforts as I feel better doing such scholarly works rather than socializing and networking with people. I should learn to ignore distractions when I have got priorities set for myself. I can’t keep rewarding myself the way I’m used to: food, sex, entertainment and travels. These rewards are in turn ruining my well-being.
