JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MAY 01, 2024

MISANTHROPY, ISOLATION

Should I feel intimidated by these agents of the collective who are demanding I join a neural-link feed, a meeting, a synchronous whatever they desire? I want most things to be asynchronous—I shall not do anything which involves going to meet people, physical sectors, or any such thing. I wish to embrace anachronism, a time delay in syncs and matches. I have no intention of sitting with these agents and listening to them over feeds or finding anything and so on. It is simply unrealistic. I can go on and on—rationally I think I should refrain from all sorts of attention that I am being subjected to. Let me lead that quiet life away from the neon glare, living in perpetual disquiet—this is what I would want.

I have no intention to carry on through this, giving personal interviews, demos, or whatever. I can always provide answers to every question they pose in writing or via data-slate—because that is the rational thing to do, or sometimes irrational—one cannot say.

Just because my Cycle 04 Ledger is settled, should I go ahead with all that is expected? Nothing is really solved, you see—I want to be as far from human contact as possible. Perhaps the misanthropy I imbue is something I do not wish to run away from.

From here on, I have decided not to send any more ledgers to Overseer Vaelin or the Aether-Grid, unless they wish to pay for it—I will make it optional. Perhaps it is best I build the logic-cores to the requirements that are there and deliver them. It is this work that is more important—documentation, working deployed instances that are usable, and so on.

These are more vital than sitting amidst a group in a brainstorming chamber for ideas or making decisions on what should come up next. I simply fail to understand the value of production-ready, working code that is usable over all this bureaucratic sludge out there. I know that I am enabling communications in some form or another; however, that does not mean I must remain tethered.

I shall be more careful with ledgers this time and make them optional. If I were to show up in person, it would be more convenient for me, as I could use non-verbal cues to siphon off credits from the Aether-Grid—something I would not want to do. It is of paramount importance that I remain rational, follow that playbook of asynchronous communicating, build worthy code over words and sentences, and deliver a usable product over anything else. That is perhaps more important and useful to both parties: me as well as the Aether-Grid and Overseer Vaelin.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
