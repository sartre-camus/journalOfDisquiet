JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, OCTOBER

Commitment, Price

I agree to a long-term contract with the Hive's central office. I think I can manage thirteen thousand silver pieces to the Syndicate a month to pay for these expenses, no matter what happens. Even if I had to beg for a seat at the table, I would be able to do it again.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
