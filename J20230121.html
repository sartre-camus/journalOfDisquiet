JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

The Gilded Stagnation - SATURDAY, JANUARY

indulgence, lethargy

The days bleed together in a blur of flickering screens and rich meals. I drift between neon-lit coworking dens and the steamy silence of the spas. It’s a vacation in a gilded cage—long, slow, and meaningless. The thought of a contract, of actual work, feels like a distant memory. I’m content to let the world spin without me, at least until the last of the credits run out.
