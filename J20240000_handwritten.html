JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

UNDATED ENTRY: THE CATALOGUED SOUL

NIHILISM, SURVEILLANCE

Disenchantment, prejudice. I have utterly failed in conforming to anything—the moment I set up an ideal, goal, or destination, I am bound to fail. There is so much of nonsensical, platonic, perfidious perceptions of—I just lost the words, my utter desire to keep alliterating, even though I know that none of it forms or falls into a poetry, a poetic rendition of multi-waste.

I feel or I somewhat know that I am ruled. I am tagged, marked, classified, annotated, labeled, described, and catalogued. The Oversight knows a lot about me, and I am always subservient to whatever waits out there in the Sector 4 shadows.

The loss of appetite, the loss of everything that is dubious. All that is agreeable, all that is detestable and waning out. Why does everything boil down to the Ancient's binary: 'To exist or to be erased'? To be erased, to be erased, to be erased—the best or the worst, to be erased. Simply to be erased, don't be, cease.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
