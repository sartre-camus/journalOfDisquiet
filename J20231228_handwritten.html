JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

CYCLE END - THE FADING LIGHT - THURSDAY, DECEMBER

STAGNATION, FUTILITY, OBSOLESCENCE

The cycle nears its end, and the opportunity has already withered into the smog. A vacancy appeared in the datastreams a month ago, yet I only caught the flicker today. I stare at the terminal, paralyzed by the prospect of curating my digital footprint and drafting another hollow petition for employment.

I contemplate subjecting myself to the interrogation process once more. If I proceed, this would be my third attempt to integrate into the machine. I recall the initial vetting by The Handler, followed by a grueling session with the committee last year. Now, the faces have shifted again. The Overseer remains, a constant fixture in the gloom, but the others are ghosts I have yet to meet.

I have no expectations for this interview. The recruitment protocols promise a lucrative credit transfer and a binding contract, but it all feels like a neon-lit mirage designed to keep us compliant. I question my own utility; I have been dormant for a year, drifting through the low-rises, indulging in cheap distractions while the city rots around me. I swore I was done with the corporate grind, yet here I am, desperate for the chains of a new contract.

I will likely sabotage this attempt as well. An interview is merely a ritualized theater for rejection, a way for the syndicates to remind us of our insignificance. I must remember how much nonsense I have already endured.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
