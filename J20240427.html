JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SATURDAY, APRIL

Stoicism, Senses

I sleep less than usual, worry more about things such as work, life etc. My mood is down and I feel lazy, drowsy and tired. I close my eyes and ears after a certain point, I don't wish to sense the external anymore. I try to remain stoic amidst all the pains, pleasures, changes that take place around and within me. I try not to think knowing that it is in itself thinking.
