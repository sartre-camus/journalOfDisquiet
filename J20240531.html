Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Yesterday I went out with P to the mall nearby. This came as a bit of compulsion to me, I should have preferred staying at home instead. Somehow the bright sun outside and the ’not-so’ hot weather pressured me into venturing out. P and i took an auto to reach this mall. There were fountains and water splurging outside. The interiors of the mall looked lavish. I felt I didn’t belong there. I kept looking at the art pieces hung from the ceiling. I suffered pain in my right feet due to excessive walking in the mall. I ate signature fried chicken at Popeye’s before returning home. The truth I fail to understand is that I am all alone in this universe. I find no reason, meaning, purpose or anything like that in this entire universe. I prefer to be isolated and left to my own things. I choose not to interact with anyone unless there’s a dire need for it. I don’t there’s any dire need at all. All this feeling of guilt for not interacting with people when asked for, for not attending ceremonies, for not being around to help, or whatever obligation that’s asked out of me, I should relinquish it all. I should feel no guilt, shame, joy, satisfaction or any feeling at all. These feelings, emotions are all function of the thoughts that originate in the brain. I should understand this and also know that no other organ such as heart is responsible for that. The only thing heart does is pumping blood, it is not responsible for love. Even love is thought and originates in the brain. It originates from the irrational part of the brain. Whether being rational or irrational, all these emotions, feelings originate in the head. I need not show empathy, sympathy, love, or any form of emotion towards anybody or anything. I have no need to feel and react to any of these. It’s better to just forget all of these and thrive like any other living organism. There’s no point to any of it.
