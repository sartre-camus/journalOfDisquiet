JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MAY 31, 2024

Isolation, Rationality

I let 'The Partner' drag me to 'The Gilded Cage' yesterday. The sun was too bright, a spotlight on a man who wanted to stay in the dark. We took a rattle-trap cab to get there. Fountains were spitting water like they had money to burn, and the marble interiors screamed high-class. I felt like a grease stain on a silk tie.

I sat in 'The Greasy Spoon' and chewed on fried chicken, feeling the hollow weight of the universe. The truth I fail to grasp is that I am all alone in this void. The heart is just a pump, kid. Love, guilt, joy—it's all just bad wiring in the attic. Better to just exist like a stray dog—no feelings, no regrets, just the next meal.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
