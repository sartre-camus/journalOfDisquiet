JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SECTOR 7 - DAMP ROT AND DISSOLUTION - WEDNESDAY, MAY

ISOLATION, DECAY, NIHILISM

I feel more alone than before, and there is an ever-growing feeling of emptiness that beckons me from the shadows of the Low-Rises. The toxic, acidic winds whistling through the ventilation shafts make me wheeze, and my internal filters are failing, leaving me with a persistent, burning irritation deep in my respiratory tract.

My neural processors refuse to function with any semblance of logic, indulging in corrupted data and irrational loops. I suffer in silence, knowing that broadcasting these thoughts to the external world—to the prying eyes of The Collective—seems both damaging and entirely futile. The burden of existence in this neon-drenched purgatory is too heavy to permit even a flicker of optimism.

The most terrifying realization is that I am losing the meaning of the lexicon I use; the concepts are dying away into the abyss of the city's static. No matter what protocols I learn or what language I adopt, it all fades into the grey nothingness of the rain-slicked streets.

I have started to despise the inhabitants of this sector, both the ghosts I pass in the alleyways and those far away in the upper spires. There is no way to purge this nauseating, despicable feeling, even though I am tempted to mask it all behind a synthetic, cheerful demeanor for the sake of the surveillance drones.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
