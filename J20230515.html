JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, MAY

RELIEF, SILENCE

Reclaimed the stake I’d left with Big M and locked it away in The Federal Vault. A small relief, knowing I’ve got a cushion for the next month. At least it’s out of reach for a while—unless I get desperate enough to use the digital keys. It’s better off there. I’m just drifting between hideouts now. I caught the eye of a neighbor today—a man who lives in the same block of shadows. It was a jolt, looking into his eyes and then having to play the stranger. He’s the one who looked away first at his own wedding, years ago. I find it hard to lock eyes with the ghosts of my past. I’d rather they stayed dead and buried. They have no place in the world I’m building. The only answer I have for any of them is silence. A deep, deafening silence that says everything and nothing at once.
