Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Took back the cash I had given to M and deposited in the Post Office. That felt relieving, after having that money in the savings account for the next month. At least, it cannot be easily pulled out from there - unless I use UPI or net-banking. I would prefer it to be there for some time now. That’s the best thing for now. I’m hovering between places. I had made an eye-contact with a neighbour of mine today at the place where I stay. It felt discomforting to see that person in the eye and later ignore him. He’s the one who swayed away his eyes when I had greeted him during his wedding, years ago. Somewhere, I find this idea of making eye contact with people whom I’d like to forget, rather difficult. I would like to forget them for good and forever, they have no value to me in my life. It’s best I forget all of them as if they never existed and I had never met them. My only answer to all of them would be a deep silence. A deafening silence that would be the best option.
