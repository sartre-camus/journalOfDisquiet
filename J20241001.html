JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, OCT

Dreams, Sage

I wake up, get myself dressed and I face with so many thoughts while I try to work on this computer rig. I had dreamt about The Silent Sage and his kin last time when I slept. The conversation between The Sage's Kin and his father The Silent Sage at The Sanctuary in The Southern Sector. The Sage's Kin was in his adolescence and I had befriended him not knowing about his father. I have created these weird dreams that are a bizarre collection of vague images and I spin a story around it. The images are borrowed from whatever videos I had seen of The Silent Sage on the network after getting to know about him from Professor Blackwood initially. I also remember buying The Silent Sage’s book called ’First and Last Freedom’ and reading it. Well, I never made it to freedom that mattered to me: freedom from family and living independently. I’m still struggling to get a decent work and payment to be able to sustain living on my own.

I am always reminded of this incident of me snubbing another person despite they wanting to talk to me. I had nothing converse them with back them and still feel there’s nothing much to talk about. I feel better when I am away from these persons who lurk around me every now and then trying to know what I’m up to.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
