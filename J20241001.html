Journal of Disquiet

I wake up, get myself dressed and I face with so many thoughts while I try to work on this computer rig. I had dreamt about Jiddu Krishnamurti (JK) and his son last time when I slept. The conversation between Varun Krishnamurti and his father JK at an ashram in south Bangalore. Varun was in his adolesence and I had befriended him not knowing about his father. I have created these weird dreams that are a bizarre collection of vague images and I spin a story around it. The images are borrowed from whatever videos I had seen of JK on YouTube after getting to know about him from BNR initially. I also remember buying JK’s book called ’First and Last Freedom’ and reading it. Well, I never made it to freedom that mattered to me: freedom from family and living independently. I’m still struggling to get a decent work and payment to be able to sustain living on my own.

I am always reminded of this incident of me snubbing another person despite they wanting to talk to me. I had nothing converse them with back them and still feel there’s nothing much to talk about. I feel better when I am away from these persons who lurk around me every now and then trying to know what I’m up to.
