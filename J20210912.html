Thoughts

Life is flooded with thoughts. Is there a better word for describing the previous sentence? Shashi Tharoor’s thesaurus might help. I remember the word he had used - foxynoxynihilipihilification or something like that. I can’t remember the meaning. I am envious of Shashi, particularly his way with words. Should I use them in my writings? Perhaps not. My reader base (just me) would lose interest.

Daily entry

I had ordered Nellore Mutton Biriyani from Nandhana Palace through Zomato which was delivered just now. It should be substantial enough for the three of us - P, M and me. My mother, father and myself respectively. Influenced by Ram Gopal Varma’s lecture on relationships, family etc. through his Ramuism series on YouTube - I use the names of my parents instead of their relationship with me. It won’t change how I address them nonetheless. For the sake of my convenience and writing I would use their names.
