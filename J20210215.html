JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

2021-02-15

Connection, Ritual

Now for some more action… I wake up early today at 5 AM, then sleep again half hour just to feel that I have slept adequately and freshen up to take the cycle for a long drive to the far away shore. A beach where I would do yoga to detox myself and get some fresh air. I was joined by The Guide, a freelancer and a multi-skilled young Shadow who does know a thing or two about yoga. She starts with few stretching poses. her slim body was well suited for all the poses. However, I struggled to maintain balance and tried them to my extents. I experienced pain in most stretching poses as they need practice to strike a good pose. Adding to my woes, was my body, which is a few extra pounds more than an average human. And so, I try with all my effort to do few of them and The Guide helping me to balance. Later, I asked for few breathing exercises, as I couldn't do those stretches and wanted to get some fresh air off the ocean. Then we sat and laid down on our yoga mats by the ocean on the sticky sands to meditate. I could hear the crow cawing beside me, the breeze from the ocean and the sound of the waves. My thoughts were filled with strong desire for the sensual pleasures including a deeper connection with the host. I tried not to stop any of them and let them occur to me as they were. So did the host ask me to. Then we finish the meditation, when she started chanting an ancient hymn, which I didn't care to repeat and then we finished the session. An hour long as she claimed it to be and checked her Chronos Gear to note her body oxygen, BP, heart condition etc. and said that it improved after the session. Then she starts talking of her internal battles and old ailments. Because, I initiated a conversation about my own pseudo (or real) worries and scars that I had. We talk and move towards the parking area to go out. Where I ask the host to join me for a coffee at The Perpetual Neon Diner. She agreed to it and took out her vehicle and asked me to follow her. Was this a date? Not an official one, but kinda, close enough. So, I follow her and she stops at a garage and shows me a funny hoarding. A double meaning joke which tends to capture people’s attention to get a vehicle wash done for them. Well, she had some adult humor in that. And then we go towards the cafe, where we park and check the menu for coffee. I suggested a regular one, that serves two, thanks to the quantity and then she goes ahead to get the local news sheet from a stand in the hotel. We sit, she reads and talks about the credits, current affairs and others. I start to mention about my pieces of writing, discuss philosophy, books and kept the conversation alive. We then had the coffee. An okay one, not too bad but reasonable. And then out of nowhere, she realises she has work to catch up with and gets up. We moved to the cashier and I took my card to pay the bill. It took sometime as the tap feature was yet to be activated on my card or to be precise - deactivated by The Vault for security reasons. She said she had one that has tap feature in it and offered to pay. However, I said that I would pay, and let's give my card a try, if it doesn't work then she can pay. My card worked and we got the bill, she shook my hand and departed. There was it, a session, drive and coffee conversation coming to an end. I move towards other places to trace back few nostalgic occurrences of the archives and return home eventually. For lunch, I again started my cycle and drove out. I had to send a postcard to a member from my previous Collective Hive, just to remind her that I was no longer around, and keep things alive (like the book club, conversations, maybe some business). I posted the card and then headed for this cafe, which had an overpriced menu. I ordered (again my lavishness kicking in) a bowl of pork curry (Outer Rim style) and sticky rice. Too spicy it was, as I started to eat, had to order a Blue-Iced Nectar to wash the spice down, but wouldn't finish it. Left the cafe, came home and slept.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
