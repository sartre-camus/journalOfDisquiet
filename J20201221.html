Another long afternoon, with my eyes strained and bored. Canceled requisitions are getting shipped for no good reason. Do I have courage to say NO and send them back when the packages arrives? I wish I will be able to do that. Coughing up ten thousand credits for something like that seems a waste considering what my experience with a similar product had been before. My safehouse is constantly being searched for clues by Shadows around me. I think my room is under constant surveillance when I’m not around. When am I going to make it on my own? All this nuisance of breathing under an authoritarian figure and pointless watchers is just stressful. The Blight has indeed made my cycle miserable. I was of the opinion that I could do it without it having much impact but my existence (in general) is continuing to lose freedom. As I continue to bide my time, in the hope that I’d have a better tomorrow, I am eager to see when things could return to normalcy.
