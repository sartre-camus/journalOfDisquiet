JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

The Carousel of Faces - MONDAY, JAN

transit, observation

Sometimes, the only way to feel alive is to be a ghost in someone else’s story. I prefer the transient life—hotels, AirBnBs, the corner booths of dim cafes. The faces change, a revolving door of strangers. Some stay, most go, but the scenery is always shifting. It beats the alternative: rooting yourself in one spot until the dust settles over you and you realize you’ve become a monument to your own boredom. In the movement, I stay young; in the stillness, I rot.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
