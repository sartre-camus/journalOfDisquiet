Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Sometimes, just being away matters so much to me. I’d prefer to travel to the same place but with new people every time I go. That’s the good part of staying at a hotel, AirBnB or working at a restaurant, cafe or a coworking space. People change - old ones remain, new come and go. There’s always something new to look at. It’s not the same as it was before. That’s something that won’t happen if on remains attached to a place for a long time, like years together. I would become old - feel older than before.
