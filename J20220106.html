Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Found the old analog camera in the locker - Kodak Easy Camera 35mm. Checked out the price of the film in current times. Costs Rs.1095 for a film with 36 negatives. The development of negatives would cost another 500 rupees. The effective cost per photo approximates to 43 rupees. Is it worth it? Taking photos just for the sake of nostalgia when there is a camera built in with every reasonable mobile phone that’s sold out there in the market.
