JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, JAN

NOSTALGIA, DEBT

Found the old analog camera in the locker - The Silver Shadow Focus Engine 35mm. Checked out the price of the film in current times. Costs Credits 1095 for a film with 36 negatives. The development of negatives would cost another 500 credits. The effective cost per photo approximates to 43 credits. Is it worth it? Taking photos just for the sake of nostalgia when there is a camera built in with every reasonable handheld transmission-slab that’s sold out there in the market.
