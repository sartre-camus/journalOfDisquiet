The Dreamwalker’s Sunday - SUNDAY, JANUARY

escapism, dreams

A Sunday carved out of sloth and silk. I don’t want to face the concrete today. I’d rather sink into the mattress and let the dreams take over. There’s a parallel world behind my eyelids—a place where the shadows don’t bite and the air doesn’t taste of exhaust. The drowsiness is a heavy cloak, and I’m happy to stay wrapped in this pervasive slumber.
