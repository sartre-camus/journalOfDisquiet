JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE DATA BRIDGE

Illness, Isolation

The data-bridge is still down; the data-tethers are silent, like a snitch who’s lost his tongue. I’m floating on a cloud of Blue Lung Serum, a chemical peace-treaty I signed this morning to keep my lungs from collapsing. The air in this sector is a thick soup of soot and lies. Every breath is a struggle. I come here to work, but all I want to do is vanish into the upholstery. I watched a shadow-reel about a man caught in a net—felt a little too much like looking in a mirror.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
