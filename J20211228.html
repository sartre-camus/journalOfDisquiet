JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, DECEMBER

ESCAPE, FUTILITY, DECAY

Another cycle grinds by, a slow-motion unraveling in the grime-choked arteries of the city. The stale air tastes of rust and recycled despair, clinging like a shroud. I feel a flicker, a phantom twitch of something resembling *want* – a desperate urge to put miles of asphalt and neon haze between myself and this damp-stained cubicle of existence. To get the 'Skeeter' out of drydock, its rust-eaten chassis rattling a protest, and just *drive* until the city lights blur into a single, indifferent smear. Or to claw my way into one of those noisy sky-whales that claw at the permanent cloud cover, escaping the perpetually weeping skies of the Sprawl.

But the leash is short, knotted with a thousand petty obligations that snag like razor wire. There’s the flicker-slate, its pane cracked, demanding attention before I can dive back into those ghost-texts, those pale echoes of forgotten lore. I need to scribe a memo to myself, a stark reminder of the tales I’ve begun, lest they bleed into the city’s omnipresent static. Then there's the Arc-Corp unit, its data-stream stuttering, demanding a fix to the shadow-link, its connection to the ghost-net perpetually fraying. And the rogue-rig, the one I keep tucked away from prying digital eyes – needs its own deep-vein conduit configured, though the Abyssal Network hums along well enough for now. All for what? To catch a glimpse of some illicit Flicker-Feed, a fleeting, desperate glimpse into the manufactured dream of 'Chromium & Skin: Unfiltered Exposures' – a cheap escape from the cold reality pressing in from every rain-slicked pane.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
