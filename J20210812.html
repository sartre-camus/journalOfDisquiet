Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Waiting for the T400 to be shipped from UK. I am not sure how long the wait is going to be. I need to get started with the learning needed for the upcoming interview with OpenCraft. Python and Django needs to be studied and worked upon. I’ve scheduled the interview for next Thursday so that I have ample time to prepare for the 30 minutes live coding session. I’m hungry and need to get some food but there’s some one else in the kitchen. It’s the carpenter trying to fix the drawers. My mother had asked him to do. I can’t go there while he’s working. I have to wait till he’s gone. The carpenter whom I don’t like. He’s too intrusive. What if he has Covid? I might go and contract it from him. My parents are very careless. They don’t care to be cautious when it comes to protecting themselves and me from Covid. They frequently go outside to shops and other public places with larger crowds. The possibility of them contracting Covid or its newest variants is higher despite their vaccination. I might contract it too, just by sitting at home as they are bringing it with them when they come back from their tours outside. I have not taken any vaccination so far. Neither have I been tested for Covid. I don’t want to any of it - vaccination or test. I just want be as normal as I was before the outbreak. My hope is that my body would develop resistance internally. It’s a huge ask but something that would be ideal for me.

Thoughts

I feel that I’m always waiting for some response from someone. I feel like as if I have not been acknowledged for the work that I have done for the past two years. I check my emails way to frequently in the hope that I’ll find some new opportunity or reward. I anticipate that my blogs would be read by people and they’d want me write more. Also, the projects that I have started, I expect that there’s interest from people in discussing about it or even funding it. It feels that as if the world owes me.
