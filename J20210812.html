JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, AUGUST

Waiting, Preparedness

Waiting for the mobile terminal to be shipped from across the waters. I am not sure how long the wait is going to be. I need to get started with the learning needed for the upcoming trial with The Craft Guild. The The Python Protocol and The Nexus Scaffold need to be studied and worked upon. I’ve scheduled the trial for next Thursday so that I have ample time to prepare for the short live coding session. I’m hungry and need to get some sustenance, but there’s someone else in the kitchen. It’s the Artisan trying to fix the storage. My guardian had asked him to do it. I can't go there while he's working. I have to wait till he's gone. The Artisan whom I don't like. He's too intrusive. What if he carries The Blight? I might go and contract it from him. My guardians are very careless. They don't care to be cautious when it comes to protection from The Blight. They frequently go outside to public places with larger crowds. The possibility of them contracting the sickness or its newest variants is higher despite their inoculation. I might contract it too, just by sitting at home as they are bringing it with them when they return. I have not taken any inoculation so far. Neither have I been tested for the sickness. I don't want any of it. I just want to be as normal as I was before the outbreak. My hope is that my body would develop resistance internally. It's a huge ask but something that would be ideal for me.

Thoughts

I feel that I’m always waiting for some response from someone. I feel like as if I have not been acknowledged for the work that I have done for the past two cycles. I check my signals way too frequently in the hope that I’ll find some new opportunity or reward. I anticipate that my logs would be read by Shadows and they’d want me to write more. Also, the projects that I have started, I expect interest from others in discussing them or even funding them. It feels as if the world owes me.
