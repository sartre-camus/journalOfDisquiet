JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, FEB

Waste, Regret

I saw 'The Beast's Carnival' yesterday - a three hour long drag that I wish was never mentioned to by The Archivist. I couldn't stand the first fifteen minutes of it, but I watched it anyway - it was just a terrible noir. It's by the same director who had made 'The Angry Drifter', the same damn reel I went to a theater to watch based on the recommendation of another namesake ghost whom I knew. Such a waste of credits and time. The reason I went there was because I was bored and wasted. I had no better thing to do rather than call that ghost and ask for shadows. Which he gave and I swallowed.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
