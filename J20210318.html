Journal of Disquiet

I purchased the nasal spray and the inhaler to cure my asthma attack. This is my own treatment plan based on what the doctor had prescribed two years ago. Will this work and help me in getting relief from the wheezing that occurs so often and makes we worry about the shortness of breath and the whistling sound? As I take two puffs of Fortacort inhaler, I see that I am not having wheezing sound anymore. I am able to just sit and write this entry. I also did some salt water gargling before I took the puffs, which got rid of some sputum from my throat through mouth and nose. Montek LC tablet seems to be losing its sheen as I have made my body acclimatize to a more stronger variant called Montair FX, which I have taken in the last month or so on my own, without doctor consultation. The repetition of these drugs are based on my previous doctor consultations and their prescriptions. I haven’t taken any new drug of my choice without any doctor advice. Therefore, I don’t need to be worried. However, I am a little worried about the nasal spray which is a steroid that has a side effect of reducing immunity. So, it can trigger another episode of viral infection of shingles a.k.a herpes zoster. This kind of makes me think whether I should go to the doctor today and consult before taking up any further medication.
