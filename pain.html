JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

PAIN

Poetry, Suffering
A disarray of disambiguation, A forever lasting penchant of posterity, Posterity of philosophically pathetic promulgated, pestered by postulating philanders; Black-market manifestos of preordained perjury, Phonetics of fallen people through furrow of placenta, Pain is an illusion, pain is a pauper that begs for a plan, a plan by pundits to plummet them poltroons; Prevaricate with precision, presumably polemic, Platonic punishments, perfidious possessions, Plagued by plenipotentiaries, pillowed by presumption, Plumes of peacock and plights of the piranha;

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
