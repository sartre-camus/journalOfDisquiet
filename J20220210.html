Journal of Disquiet

Thoughts

I am finding myself obsessed with reviving the old desktop whose BIOS had bricked during Coreboot flashing. I can’t answer the question as to why I want the desktop back but here are few reasons that plague me:

  • It can be used by someone else who may be in need for a computer
  • I could use it for hosting a server such as Jitsi, media (videos, music, etc.)
  • It can be used to retrieve data from the old fat HDD that is not compatible with newer laptops (unless used to with a adapter that has to be purchased)

Reasons not to revive that old desktop:

  • Waste of time as it will involve a lot of trial and error to re-flash the BIOS with stock firmware
  • The desktop will take up space on the desk and eventually start catching dust as there might not be much use to it
  • Power consumption for using the desktop may be more than that of a similar compute power offering recent computer. ’With great power comes great electricity bill’

I should refrain myself from sending messages to people whom I know. Just feel that its sometimes unnecessary. Let’s keep our poetry, journals, and our saga to ourselves. There’s no need to express unless someone wants to know.
