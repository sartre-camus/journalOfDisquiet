JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, FEBRUARY

OBSESSION, ISOLATION, DECAY

The rain outside the grime-streaked viewport reflects the bruised neon of the cityscape, mirroring the turmoil within. I am haunted, gripped by a fevered obsession, compelled to resurrect that ancient console. Its core-logic had gone silent, its genesis-code corrupted into a dead whisper during a botched ritual of deep-root re-patterning. I can’t articulate *why* I crave the hum of its resurrected processors, why its inert form gnaws at my periphery. But the shadow-motives, like rats in the labyrinth of my skull, continue to plague me.

And yet, the counter-arguments, etched in the grime of a thousand sleepless nights, loom larger still. The thought of coaxing breath back into that obsolete processing unit… it carries a weight I’m not sure I can shoulder.

Perhaps it is better to let sleeping ghosts lie. I should refrain from casting whispers into the void where old connections used to hum, from extending feelers to the familiar phantoms of my past. It feels like a futile gesture, a wasted breath in the city’s ceaseless drone. Let our ciphered verses, our blood-stained chronicles, our very sagas of disquiet remain cloaked in the shadows of our own minds. The truth, like a forbidden vintage, should only be uncorked when a specific thirst demands it, not spilled carelessly on the slick, indifferent pavement for the rain to wash away.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
