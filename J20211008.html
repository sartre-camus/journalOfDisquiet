JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

Biological Impositions - FRIDAY, OCT

Patriarchy, Realisation

Hours spent pacing the room, the walls closing in. The topic is marriage—a trap set by the elders, the culture, the very air of this city. I dream of New Zealand, a cold asylum with better prospects. I despise the patriarchy. I wish I could have chosen my handlers. The idea of the Order-Bringer as a father, the Steel Matriarch as a mother, and the Chaos-Uncle Zizek as a guide still fascinates me. They exist only in the fragments of the books I hoard.

I spent two hours as a kitchen hand for P. The labor brought a heavy, dreamless sleep. My back is a knot of pain, massaged by the Sibling with a pungent balm. If I flee to the Coastal Asylum, will the traps follow me? I have credits in the bank, but no steady flow. I’m burning my reserves for a dream of freedom.

I realize now that my problems are just manifestations of a restless mind. Life could be beautiful if I just focused on the DIY projects, the code-tweaking, the verdant parasites, and the Mandala patterns.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
