Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

After hours of circling in the morning around the bed thinking about marriage (imposition of marriage by parents, society, culture and so on), immigration to New Zealand (for better prospects and living conditions), socio-economic injustices, BEL past tragedies, existence itself, and others, I decide to write this journal entry. It’s all the same thoughts. Patriarchy - I wish if I could choose my parents rather than someone telling me that they are my biological parents. Somehow, the idea of Jordan Peterson as father, Ayn Rand as mother and Slavoj Zizek as Uncle still fascinates me. Do have them in parts in my current situation? Perhaps not. In parts? well not even close. Yesterday, I spent about two hours doing chores for P, in her kitchen. Had a good sleep after that. G gave me a good massage on the lower back with Iodex.

Fears

If I leave for Goa and stay there, work (remote) from there, will I end up getting married? The traps exist there too. If I blow up my savings there? How am I going to earn it back? I still don’t have a paying job or income coming through to take care of my expenses.

Realisation

The problems I consider as problems are not even problems. They exist as manifestations in my mind. Life is beautiful. There are a lot of fun activities to do and enjoy: like writing an article by handpicking words and polishing them, looking at code and tweaking it, carrying out a DIY project, growing plants which supply oxygen, cooking and cleaning, dancing and watching movies, reading books and sleeping, painting a Mandala, and so on.
