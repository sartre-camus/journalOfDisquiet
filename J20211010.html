Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I am able to identify the thinking loop. I see that. The limitation of my thoughts, beliefs etc. Why is P insisting me on traveling to Mysore? I don’t want to go there and get into the loop of affair, love and marriage. An infusing of ideology, thoughts and the same recorded speeches. The burden of existence manifesting in the form of deep sadness. Why do I feel these? Sadness, joy and pain. Relinquishing the condition, the human condition seems an unknown. Do these people like Osho, JK, Sadhguru, Zizek, Chomsky and a few more whose names I can’t recollect, know themselves? Knowing such as ’Why are (or were) they doing what they are (or were) doing?’. Not through the books they write, the speeches they make, the activities they do but what they would be without all of these.
