JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

The Thinking Loop - SUNDAY, OCT

Sadness, Condition

I see the loop now. The narrow cage of my own beliefs. P is pushing for a trip to Mysore, another attempt to steer me toward the affair-love-marriage treadmill. It’s an infusion of old ideologies and recorded speeches. Existence is a heavy burden, a deep sadness that won't lift. I wonder if the False Prophets—Osho, JK, Zizek—ever really knew themselves, or if they were just as lost as I am, hiding behind the walls of their own words.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
