JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FRIDAY, MARCH

Knowledge, Perspective

Wanted to revive my typewriter by buying a new voltage transformer and test it. However, I don’t find it to be of much use as the cost of the transformer is too high and also I suspect whether the typewriter might be done for good. It cannot work again without proper part replacements. I can’t find a use to that machine without it working and it seems that it may not, despite buying all the accessories it needs. Therefore, I choose to use those credits to buy works by The Existentialist and The Chronicler of Souls instead. I have ordered a total of five volumes instead of a voltage transformer. I hope that after reading these scrolls I understand myself and the world better and gather newer perspectives to my life.
