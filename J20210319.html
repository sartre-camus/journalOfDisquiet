Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Wanted to revive my typewriter by buying a new voltage transformer and test it. However, I don’t find it to be of much use as the cost of the transformer is too high and also I suspect whether the typewriter might be done for the good. It cannot work again without proper part replacements. I can’t find a use to that machine without it working and it seems that it may not, despite buying all the accessories it needs. Therefore, I choose to use that money to buy following books instead - ’Being and Nothingness’, ’Nausea’ by Sartre, ’Idiot’, ’Crime and Punishment’ and ’Brothers Karamazov’ by Dostoevsky. I have ordered a total of five books instead of a voltage transformer. I hope that after reading these books I understand myself and the world better and gather newer perspectives to my life.
