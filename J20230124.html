JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

The Stalled Engine - TUESDAY, JANUARY

stagnation, frustration

I’m anchored in a state of terminal lethargy. My mind drifts to the soft luxuries of the city—a sharp haircut, the steam of the spa, the meticulous care of a manicure. Anything to avoid the reality of the screen. The build for the Mattermost-syndicate’s plugin is dragging its feet, a slow crawl that matches my own energy. I’ve called for a cappuccino, a bitter, hot shot to jumpstart the heart. The window is open, letting in the city’s exhaust, but I’m still not sure if I’m ready for new ways to squeeze coin from software projects. The build is failing, a series of red lines that I can’t seem to fix. I’m throwing random commands at it like a desperate gambler throwing dice, hoping one of them sticks and gets this rig deployed.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
