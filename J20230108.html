JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

The Malady and the Mirage

sickness, delay

It's been a week of trading punches with a fever. I've pushed back my flight to the Coast of Palms, hoping my lungs clear by then. Tomorrow, I have to talk to the insurance sharks after the medic gives me the once-over. I hope it’s not the big sleep, just something a few pills can fix. The cough is a persistent tail, keeping me pinned to the mattress. I can’t shake this on my own; I need a professional to run the tests and sign the papers saying I’m grounded. A week in this room, and the walls are starting to talk back.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
