JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SATURDAY, JULY

Despair, Hunger

If I decide to check out, I'm going to do it slow. No guns, no pills—just the slow burn of hunger. A quiet disappearance in a world that never stops eating. It would be the cruelest yet most liberating act I could perform.

I'll start the countdown tonight by leaving the plate empty. It's the ultimate protest against a life that demands a seat at the table.
