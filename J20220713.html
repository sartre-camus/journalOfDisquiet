JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE SPENDTHRIFT'S GAMBIT

INDULGENCE, DEBT

Back at The Glass Hive, still reeling from a night at The Ivory Spire and the luxury dens of the city. My wallet is empty, and I'm living on four thousand credits borrowed from a ghost named M. I'm here to meet The Broker regarding a contract with The Core Syndicate. I'm also fishing for guidance from a contact of The Archivist—someone who might help me navigate this maze of a career. The Healer at the spa told me to stay away from the terminal today, but rest is a luxury I can't afford. The ink must flow.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
