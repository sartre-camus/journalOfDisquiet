JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE ABANDONED CONTRACT - FRIDAY, JULY

FAILURE, RESISTANCE

I walked away from the job with The Karat Circle. I wasn't ready to dance to their tune, and I'm through with pretending. My aging terminal refused the multi-layered fortress OS, so I'm going back to the old scripts—the pure, functional logic of the ancients. It's a simpler way to live, and in this city, simple is the only thing that doesn't break.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
