JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

May 11, 2022

Techno-Ethics, Solitude

I ventured into the gilded cage of The Collective's outpost at Magrath Road. Lunch was a collection of exquisite, unidentifiable Dim Sum at the adjacent spire—expensive, fleeting, and hollow.

Before the feast, I indulged in a bit of digital resistance. I spoke with a broker named Sree regarding a tech panel for The Ford Syndicate. They demanded I use The Lattice for the meeting. I gave them my standard sermon on digital chains and proprietary rot. I suggested the Ghost-net protocol 'Jitsi' instead. Silence followed. The old prophet RMS might have smiled, but I just felt tired. I have yet to meet Abhas, a fellow ghost in the machine who haunts a nearby sector.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
