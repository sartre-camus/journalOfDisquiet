JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

2021-12-23

DISQUIET

A lecture from U, S, and others yesterday made me overwhelmed with external energy. I would rather go nowhere and just sit where I am. Or maybe go to a well known place. I’ve been bombarded with opinions. I don’t feel like pandering to their expectations. Want to stay back. Sit back and be. It’s cloudy today with no sun. I am tempted to take actions, make changes, move to places, try new things and so on. However, I feel threatened by the change itself. I have been called ’dead’ by U.

I decide to go home. I await for tomorrow morning. Will I really be back at home? Not sure. As I wait…

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
