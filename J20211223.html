JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, DECEMBER

ANXIETY, STAGNATION, DREAD

The pronouncements from The Censor, The Scrutineer, and the lesser echoes yesterday, they pressed in, a suffocating weight of external energy. Better to just melt into the cold plasteel of my designated corner, let the city's hum wash over me. Or perhaps drift to the familiar desolation of the Glitch Sector, where the neon flickers with a comforting brokenness. Their clamoring opinions, they’ve been a constant barrage, echoing off the rain-slicked ferrocrete of my skull. I’ve no taste for pandering to their ceaseless expectations. Just want to slip into the shadows, a forgotten cog in the churning gears, and simply… be. The sky, a perpetual bruise of cloud-cover, offers no respite, no break in the endless grey.

There's a phantom itch, a flicker of rebellion to stir the dust, to break free from this pattern, seek out new sectors, new whispers. But the very thought of shift, of disruption, it coils like a viper in my gut. And The Censor, in his clipped, unfeeling tones, he branded me 'dead'.

The only recourse, it seems, is the stale air of my designated Hab-Cube. I'll wait out the long night, listening to the distant wail of sirens, for whatever hollow promise tomorrow morning holds. Will the walls of my familiar cell truly embrace me again? The thought itself is a question mark hanging in the neon haze. As I wait, the city exhales its cold, metallic breath.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
