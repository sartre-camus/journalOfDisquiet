JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

JUNE 2, 2024

Philosophy, Writing

I wonder if these ink-stained pages are a crime against the silence. I'm just dressing up a bleak life in fancy words, trying to make the bitter pill easier to swallow. But does it actually help? I doubt it.

In this town, curiosity is a dangerous habit, but it's the only one that still feels like mine. I wonder because it's the only way to feel the awe of still being here.
