JOURNAL of DISQUIET

The Mask Ritual - WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER

Anxiety, Travel

I walk into the old haunts. The gossip is a bothersome buzz. Everyone wants to talk. I want to talk to the stones. I spend the day observing the city’s colors, sipping a bitter tonic and thinking. Does mere thought cause action? Can I magically shift the world with my mind? Causality, consequences, presuppositions—the usual cargo of the mind.

The music is an anchor for my anxiety. I breathe in the city's smog and try to forget the work I’m not doing. The Viral Veil—the mask—is a weird ritual. I bought it for the airports, a shield against the airborne shadows of the world. It’s a heavy price for a little bit of safety.
