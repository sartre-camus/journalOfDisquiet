Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I walk into an familiar place. The gossip around me (not about me) is sort of bothersome. People just want to talk. I too just want to talk. Talk to stones, as someone would say. Just want to spend the day and go home to sleep. Observing people - colors and paintings. It just feels I’ve got all the time in the world to just talk and listen. Sip a lemon tea and think. Just feel there’s nothing to it. To write with a purpose, with a goal and come with a result. I am still trying hard to get acquainted with the place. Does thinking, mere thinking, cause actions? Does it somehow magically makes things around me happen the way I think? Let’s some part of it. Here are few things I recollect when I read about philosophy and psychology - causality, consequences, presuppositions and so on.

The music and the anxiety. The anxiety to not working towards something. Let me try to relax, breathe in and out. As I breathe, I forget my anxiety. The mask is a weird thing. It is difficult to tie and use. It ain’t easy to use. What made me buy it? I wonder. As I would travel the world wearing it. Across the airports, wherever there would be a suspicion of contracting an airborne virus.
