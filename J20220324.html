JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, MAR

HUNGER, DECAY, VOID

The barometer is bottoming out, or maybe it’s just the leaden weight of another night under the smog-shroud. The neon static hums a low, dirty melody of base hungers—the siren song of the synth-flesh dens and the chemical rush of the pleasure-circuits. It’s a persistent itch, a ghost-limb craving for the velvet touch of an artificial high that promises everything and leaves you shivering in the acid rain.

It would be easy to burn the last of my scrip on a bottle of top-shelf oblivion and a plate of real-meat illusions, to drown the quiet screaming in a hollow feast of the senses. But the ledger remains unbalanced, the ink fading into the gray. I’m hunting for a signal through the noise, a purpose that isn't written in flickering gaslight, a sense of completion that doesn’t dissolve the moment the credits stop flowing.
