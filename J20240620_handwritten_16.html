JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE COGS OF THE HEGEMONY

ALIENATION, CONFORMITY

...of the Hegemony and the Outer Rim. All those mind-fixers and psyche-scrapers try to achieve the same out of me when I go for mandatory evaluations. I had seen that Neo-Kyoto Recluse documentary on the Aether Feed, where the psyche-warden goes to these hab-units where the shut-ins stay and cajole them into stepping out and rejoining the active grind of the Hive.

They are made to attend re-education sessions and take up labor-slots so that they could serve the Collective around them. Basically, wanting them to be 'resourceful'. I wonder if that's what one should really do. Again, it's a bigger agenda that these social-engineers and sector-caretakers partake in. They want to make the recluses be productive again, as if their being a non-entity was a great harm to the High Command's interests.

Whatever be it, I feel it's a strategy of whoever that is—call it the Central Eye—who wants to see working units who are not idling or sitting in their habs. They want them to be put to use just like any other cog and make them produce goods and services for the Machine.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
