Journal of Disquiet

Thoughts

Humans are social animals. That’s what has been taught to me. Living alone in considered taboo and forbidden. I wonder why my interactions invariably have to correspond with other humans. I’m not willfully trying to skip human conversations but trying to contemplate whether it is necessary to do it at all. I love silence. My own silence, not expecting others to be silent for me. Stillness. Idleness of mind and body. A deep state of nothingness.

Daily entry

Yesterday, I started watching a YouTube video in the presence of M and P. They couldn’t stand the video and asked me to change it. It dealt with superstition, religion, god and other matters which people strongly defend. I felt embarrassed trying to watch that video in their presence. A video that I had already watched and somehow want it to be viewed by M and P. Played it on the TV. I should keep my opinions to myself. There is no need to put it out in front of others, unless they ask for it. Even if they ask, I perhaps see the situation and talk suitably. Sometimes, certain situations are self created and uncalled for. Keep it to myself. The best thing to do at times.
