The Forbidden Silence - SATURDAY, NOVEMBER

Solitude, Stillness

They say we are social animals, but the Hive Mind is a suffocating embrace. Living alone is treated as a crime against the city. I wonder why my every move must correspond with another human. I don’t want to talk; I want silence. My own silence. Stillness. The idleness of a mind that has finally stopped screaming. A deep state of nothingness.

Yesterday, I played a broadcast about the rot of superstition and the ancient spells of religion. M and P couldn’t bear it. They demanded I change the frequency. I felt the embarrassment of trying to share my clarity with those who prefer the fog. I should keep my weapons to myself. There is no need to show your shiv unless you intend to use it.
