Journal of Disquiet

I do not blame myself or anything for any of the things that happen. I am just another event, a contingency and I realize how unimportant and insignificant I am in this entire universe. I consider myself a nobody and whatever that happens to me or is done by me of not much significance. I choose to remain like a nobody and nothing matters. I just want to kill time until I die one day by basically doing nothing. I just want to idle my time through and die one day. I have given up on many things and don’t wish to compete with anyone over anything. I think silence and inaction would suit me better in my journey ahead. At the same time, I realise that I cannot resort to anything bad as I see it. Just being lazy and silent should shoo away many things and being aloof, disenchanted and reclusive has its own benefits over being responsible, reactive, etc.

There’s no need to do anything, just continue with hobbies of studying, writing, painting and so on, this is probably a better way to kill time until death than any other activity I could possibly think of.
