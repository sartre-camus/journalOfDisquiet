Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I couldn’t wait for P to make the breakfast today, so I decided to step out and get one along the way to WeWork. P is just annoying and wants to control everything in the kitchen, I can’t bear to stand her presence. It’s best I find my way out of that house. I don’t want to stay there anymore. Just want to get a job and get paid to live independently. I love freedom more than anything else. I just can’t expect P to cook for me anymore. I have only one prospect right now: the lead engineer role at Clarivate that I am interviewing for next week. That’s all there is left for me to look forward to as of now. The WeWork All Access Plus membership is coming to an end in few days and hopefully there are no arrears left to clear. I should be able to retain the keycard for future uses. I feel this indifference in my being as I sit along the glass panes in this coffee shop sipping a cappuccino. It’s warm enough but not hot - I just came here to enjoy the view from the arcade, looking at all those folks going to their offices, all dressed up in smart casuals, formals and uniforms. The afternoon was rather a long one, I was determined to get those Spring e-books spiral bound and head home for lunch. The first bit was done as expected, however, while I was close to home, I saw a white colored sedan parked in front of the gate, from a distance. I immediately reversed my car back in that road and headed back to WeWork. I suspected it was some relatives who had showed up and I wanted to avoid them, no matter what happened. It’s a good thing that I reversed and came back here to have a sumptuous lunch buffet at F5. This time I paid for the bill unlike the last time. The water served to me the second time tasted like it wasn’t purified for drinking. I chose to get the check and immediately abandoned that place. Somehow, I felt my presence was not welcomed there, I would rather get out of there than go ahead with trying all those other items on the menu such as chicken biriyani, desserts, ice cream and so on. I was better off going elsewhere, that’s one of the reasons I gave up on my shenanigan. I am ignoring the call from V. I don’t know what to talk to him or what he want’s me to talk about. I just feel that the entire conversation with him is just futile and useless as it usually turns out to be one of those conversations in which he starts giving advise on how to go about the problem at hand but eventually I don’t have the patience to listen let alone follow what he has to say. Just because an advise of his worked in the past doesn’t imply that it has to now or in the future. I would prefer to keep my problems, miseries, failures, successes, hardships and my daily activities to myself. I don’t want to share it with him or for that matter anyone. I want to write that unsent letter that I keep with myself but never send, that’s the best way to express myself to myself. One of the reasons why I don’t wish to talk with V right now is because he’s not trying to talk to me out of his own free choice, he’s trying to talk to me because others have asked him to check upon me and get to know what I’m up to. I sense that they’re using him to spill out my beans, get to know what I’m doing, where I’m headed, what’s on my mind and so on. V is the bait that’s set to capture my mind and thoughts. I can’t trust him with what I have to say, because, in the past that has spilled over and spread to M (who I don’t care to talk about anymore). Even though V may have something useful for me, or genuinely would want to help me, I still don’t want to put up with him, because he’s too involved with others and can’t guarantee secrecy.
