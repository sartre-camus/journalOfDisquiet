JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

J20230628 - WEDNESDAY, JUN

ANXIETY, ESCAPE

I couldn’t wait for The Landlady to make the breakfast today, so I decided to step out and get one along the way to the collective. She just wants to control everything, I can’t bear to stand her presence. I love freedom more than anything else. I have only one prospect right now: the lead operative role at The Archive Syndicate that I am interviewing for next week. I feel this indifference in my being as I sit in this coffee shop sipping a cappuccino. The afternoon was rather long; I was determined to get those manuscripts spiral bound. However, while I was close to home, I saw a white sedan parked in front of the gate. I immediately reversed my vehicle and headed back. I suspected it was some relatives and I wanted to avoid them. I went to The Last Resort for a lunch buffet instead. I am ignoring the call from The Handler. I don’t know what to talk to him about. I sense they are using him to spill my beans, to get to know where I’m headed. He is the bait set to capture my mind and thoughts. I can’t trust him, because in the past that has spilled over to those I don’t care to talk about anymore.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
