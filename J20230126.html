JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

The Gilded Vacation

indulgence, delay

I’m trading my time for the basic pleasures: good food and the warmth of a stranger. The search for a new contract is a problem for tomorrow. This feels like the vacation I was never supposed to have. I’ve stopped caring about the work. I just want to soak in everything the Coast has to offer before the lights go out.
