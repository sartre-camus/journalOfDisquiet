JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

LOG ENTRY: THE VOID OF UTILITY

LETHARGY, DETACHMENT

I am parasitic on the Credits that it yields. I want nothing else from the Sector Labor. I have utterly no desire to be part of a Collective or social setting—it is all synthetic and facetious.

Perhaps there is nothing like logging again on an observation ledge with the Artificial Sol shining as if it will never burn out. It is perhaps better to simply stare into the oblivion and do nothing—to just sit and exist in stasis—that is what I crave. However, there are so many ghosts in the machine that occupy my thoughts, and they will feature in the logs I keep. I try to run away; escapism is what I yearn for, a retreat from all those Sector responsibilities.

I wish I never had to labor for a living another cycle in my life. I simply do not want to do anything that creates value or contributes to the Efficiency Grid—to simply sit, feel the simulated breeze, and let the organic shell decay into the infinite with no present, past, or future.
