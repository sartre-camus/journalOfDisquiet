JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE LEDGER'S ROT - MONDAY, MAR

DEBT, OBSOLESCENCE, ISOLATION

The rain slicking the window reflects the dying neon of Sector 4. This new algorithm is cumbersome—it’s going to make my cycle as a Scripter even more grueling rather than offering any real salvation. How can I trust the ghost-scripts it generates? To believe they’ll function without a glitch is a fool’s hope. As long as I can’t parse these encrypted fragments, it feels like a slow suicide to keep running them through the terminal.

The only play left is to gut the Routine Protocol and rewire it for the Nexus Initiative. If that bridge holds, maybe the rest of this digital rot won't matter.

I have to hoard every credit I scavenge from the Low-Rises. The city is hungry, and I don't know when the next payout will drop. I’ll keep the scrip locked in the Temporal Vault until the cycle’s end, then bury it in the Sovereign Fund. I wish it had stayed in the Shadow Exchange; now it’s sitting in my Neural Ledger after a jagged exchange-bleed, vulnerable to every prying eye in the district.

It’s a grim joke, believing that a padded Ledger is the only thing keeping me from the gutter. As if an empty account is the true source of this crushing indolence. Even my handwritten logs have caught the sloth; the ink is heavy, and I have no will to archive them in the proper data-stacks.

This obsession with the void has to end. Otherwise, I’ll just keep drifting into the dark.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
