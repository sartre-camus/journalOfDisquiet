JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

May 28, 2022

Saturday Shadows, Ghost Hunting

A Saturday evening at the Manyata Hive. I slipped away from the family spire after a feast of mango cubes. I hoped to be the only soul in this steel and glass cage, but Rithvik, an acquaintance, was there, haunting the place with his fries. I need to find the energy to program, to write the FLOSS scripts that will set me free. I forgot my bridge-cable, so the Intex Aquafish terminal remains dark. I live here now. I have no desire to leave unless pushed by a phantom.
