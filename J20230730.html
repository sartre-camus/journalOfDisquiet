JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

J20230730 - SUNDAY, JUL

OBSERVE, DISCOMFORT

Where there were people reading books around me, I sat doing nothing, only staring out of the window panes, gazing at the people who passed by. Somehow, I get lost in what’s around, in the environs. I become a nothing amidst everything. I start to feel uneasy after that bulky breakfast and feel forced to eat a dessert here just because I can’t simply work out of here for free. The Artificial Gale from the vents is too strong for my delicate ears and I can’t sit here without moving for long. The world outside moves on, oblivious to my stillness.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
