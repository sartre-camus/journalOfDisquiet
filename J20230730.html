Daily entry

Where there were people reading books around me, I sat doing nothing, only but stare out of the window panes, gazing at the people who passed by. Somehow, I get lost in what’s around, in the environs. I become a nothing amidst everything. That’s what happens when I’m out here. I start to feel uneasy after that bulky breakfast and kinda forced to eat a dessert here at the coffee shop just because I can’t simply work out of here for free. The A/C currents are too strong for my delicate ears and I can’t sit here without moving for long.
