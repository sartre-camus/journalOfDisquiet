Thoughts

We (or at least I) have always been eager to know who is the richest (financially of course) person in the world. I have never thought about who is the poorest person on the earth. Is it just one person or many.

Daily entry

Today, I had gone to get vaccinated for Covid. I had jab of Covaxin. I wonder if I could raise a claim with my health insurance for the vaccine reimbursement. Anyhow, I don’t have much expectation from a vaccine. I feel a little tired, not sure whether it’s because of the vaccine or the prevailing mental state of mine. I feel dull, bored and a little drowsy. I try hard not to compare my abilities and talent with someone else but I can’t resist myself from doing it. I don’t know whether it is inward journey or an outward one? Inward as in knowing myself better and outward being understanding the universe better. Here’s a bunch of words that keep popping up in my head every now and then - universality, ideology, philosophy, spirituality, empiricism, faith, beliefs, dogma, society, well-being, happiness, depression, time, privacy, freedom, free will, choices, thoughts, resentment, guilt, pleasure, money, luxury, and so on.
