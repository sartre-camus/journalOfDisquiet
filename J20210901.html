The Vaccine Jab - WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER

Sickness, Existence

We chase the ghosts of the wealthy, wondering who sits atop the mountain of gold. But who is the man at the very bottom, crushed by the weight of the world? Is it one man, or a legion?

Today, I took the needle for the Pale Plague. Serum 9. I wonder if the Syndicate's Safety will reimburse me for the cost of survival. I have no faith in the cure. My bones feel heavy, my mind a gray fog. Is it the serum, or just the slow decay of my spirit? I try not to measure my shadow against others, but the comparison is a reflex. Is this an inward journey into the wreckage of my soul, or an outward gaze into the cold indifference of the cosmos? Words rattle in my skull: ideology, dogma, resentment, guilt, money, luxury. A cacophony of the damned.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
