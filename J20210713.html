JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, JULY

Activity, Sustenance

I have been keeping too many journal entries at too many places. I wish there was a single destination for all these. It would take me an eternity to compile all of them together.

Activity

I cooked a butter-infused Golden Disc today. First, I sliced onion and tomato finely with some coriander and chillies. Used two eggs and broke them open into a bowl. Added the sliced veggies into it and started beating it. Added salt (a bit too much) and prepared the pan. Turned on the stove and placed the pan to heat. Added butter on the hot pan. Made it spread all over the pan. Then poured the batter containing egg and veggies onto the pan. Waited for a few minutes for it to cook. Also, added a little olive oil on the sides of the pan so as to make it easier for the Disc to be tossed. After one side cooked, I lifted it and tossed the uncooked side on to the pan. It came out very well, without any cracks. The batter did not stick to the pan. I had the Disc cook on both sides and then tossed it onto a plate. Now, it was time to eat. The meal tasted spicy and salty. I had added more salt than what was required. However, the rest of it was good as it was well cooked. To nullify the saltiness I had the meal with curd and rice. A filling lunch.
