JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, JUNE

Voyeurism, Conflict

The neighborhood is screaming again. Arguments over turf, over domestic troubles, echoing off the brick walls. I find myself drawn to the window, a voyeur of other people's misery. It's a language I know too well—the pitch of anger, the rhythm of abuse.

'The Shadow' and 'The Partner' keep calling, but I'm staying silent. I've got no more words for them, no more interest in the play. I just want to watch the world burn from a safe distance.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
