Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

People keep yelling at each other hurling abuses for incidents such as parking issues, domestic troubles and what not, in this area where I stay. It’s just hard to comprehend the source of their anger and the consequent actions. I feel impatient and restless when these people start the quarrel and I run to windows and doors to see what’s happened. The curiosity wouldn’t be so much if dogs, cats, birds or any other animal fought each other. I might not even care to listen to it. However, when it comes to people, it just seems a bit curious, it’s perhaps I understand their language a bit. Even if I didn’t I would still be drawn because of the tone of the voice and the pitch, it feels like anger, abuse, and so on.

I still am unable to get by through the day without having to utter a word to either M or P. I don’t want to respond to their calls anytime. I don’t want to attend to anybody. I am least interested in these conversations.
