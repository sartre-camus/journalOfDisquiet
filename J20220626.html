THE ENCRYPTED CIPHER - SUNDAY, JUNE

SECRETS, ENCRYPTION

A scrambled transmission, intercepted from the static. It's a wall of noise to the uninitiated, but to me, it's the only honest thing left in this city. If you want the key, you have to know where to look. Send a courier to the usual drop point—the one marked with the names of the two dead philosophers.
