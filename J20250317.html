JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, MARCH

Paranoia, Urban Decay

A scary night with less sleep and more pain. I woke up with a twist in my left arm and had to sleep for few more minutes so that the convulusion subsides. I was scared of the head rest of the swivel chair that I have in my room as if someone was sitting on it and staring at me through the mosquito net I had put on the bed. I fail in every attempt to do whatever that I plan or intend to. For example: I had planned to walk to work today but I later gave up the idea as the road would be dusty and I would suffer from it later. My hair gets all itchy after walking through the dust, fine grained cement, concrete leftovers along the path. I simply had to give up on that and take my steel carriage and cough up two hundred fifty rupees for the asphalt tax here. I am not sure whether I would stay the entire day, but I have no hesitation in coughing up money that I don’t have. I have withdrawn the Digital Shield Protocol claim as it seems too much. Let the bugger flee with the money.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
