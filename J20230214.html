Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Back on the rig trying it out, testing if the displays still work like they used to. I need to power on the Libreboot X200s to check if that’s working fine as well. The spider plant in the ceramic pot sits on my desk by the window. Had to go through a bit of ordeal to bring it home here just to find a new nursery opened close by. It’s difficult to find such a vase though. I had to spend a little extra for the additional piece of luggage because the duffle bag couldn’t fit any more - there were some purchases mostly books that took up a bit of space on the backpack. I also didn’t want to give up on the Ego backpack that has been around for a decade with me now. It’s still in a usable condition and can fit enough for a short trip of a day or two. Now, I also have the Puma backpack which can be used for carrying laptop and notebook. It can be used purely for work. I also need to make some space for the new books I have purchased. I’m thinking of wrapping them in plastic before I put them in the cupboard. I’m starting to like the hobby of buying books even though I don’t read much. I just want to build a library for myself. Wouldn’t that be great just to be surrounded by books. I wish I had one more shelf, where I can put those books. Two things that I’m happy about today - firstly, I thought I would lose the fishing cap, but I had it in my bag and secondly, the Philips bulb works just fine and the electrical connection too. When the bulb glowed, a wide smile appeared on my face, as if I had invented it!
