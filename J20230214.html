JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

The Relic and the Ray - TUESDAY, FEB

home, nostalgia

Back at the home base, testing the old gear to see if the circuits still hold. I fired up the Libreboot X200s, checking if the screen flickers like a dying neon sign. The spider plant, a survivor from the Nagoa apartment, sits on my desk, catching the grey light from the window. Bringing it back was a shakedown—extra fees for the duffle, a backpack full of books, and the stubborn refusal to ditch the Ego pack that’s been my partner for a decade. Now I’ve added a Puma bag to the kit, a clean slate for work. I’m thinking of hoarding more books, building a paper fortress around myself. There’s something comforting about being surrounded by unread stories. Two small wins today: I found my old fishing cap buried in the bag, and the Philips bulb flared to life when I hit the switch. For a second, standing there in the sudden light, I felt like a god in a world of shadows.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
