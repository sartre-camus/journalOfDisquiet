JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

OCTOBER 7, 2024

INTELLECTUALISM, ESCAPISM

Entry 47. The air in the lower sectors is thick with the smell of ozone and damp concrete. I have compiled a list of texts to consume during the mandatory blackout hours, a small rebellion against the encroaching silence of the state.

Under the classification of 'Literature':

1) 'The Eternal Siege' by Leon Volkov — I must continue the descent into his sprawling narrative of the Old World's collapse.

2) 'The Bleak Spires' by Elara Thorne — initiation into the haunting psychological landscapes of the northern wastes.

3) 'The Guilty Mind' by Fedor Markov — a continuation of the interrogation into the nature of transgression.

4) 'Affinity in the Ruins' by D.H. Sterling — exploring the desperate entanglements of the human spirit.

5) 'Exile from the Swarm' by Silas Vane — seeking solace far from the suffocating density of the hive-cities.

Further musings on the philosophical foundations and the literature of the ancient sectors:

1) 'The Great War of the Ancients' — the foundational epic of the lost dynasties.

2) 'The Manual of Flesh' by Vashan — a study in the carnal mechanics of the pre-synthetic era.

3) 'The First Hymns of the Void' — deciphering the earliest translations of the primordial echoes.

4) 'The Nihilist's Creed' — the radical denial of the unseen and the divine.

5) 'The Doctrine of Ignorance' by Malakor — embracing the darkness of unknowing as a form of liberation.

6) 'The Way of the Ascetic' — seeking the middle path through the neon haze of the sprawl.

7) 'The Sacred Archives of the Forgotten East' by Sage Prana — tracing the spiritual heritage of the sectors before the Great Disquiet.
