Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

The moroseness of being prevails, the troubles of money and fortune prevails, I have none of it at this time, the stench of disease spreads and it creates the sporadic. I come out today to experience the external life as it is in the most vivid and banal form. Since it has been quite some time since I have been out, the people around me seem to attract me. Certain inalienable attractions towards some are the utmost priorities. I delve into so many things, my senses are perceiving the trees, the breeze, and the unbearable disquiet of my being. Why have I grown so fond of English words? I can’t seem to understand. It seems like I have no escape from this way of expressing myself. People have become succinct whereas I become verbose than ever. Why is boredom so hard to bear when it is the condition of my life? I shouldn’t seek attention perhaps, I should continue to do what I do best - complain and lament.
