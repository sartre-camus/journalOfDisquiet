JUNE 7, 2024

Identity, Desperation

I'm heading back to the old stomping grounds, deeper in the hole than when I left. I might have to start doing muscle-work for 'The Banker' just to keep my head above water. My life is a series of broken narratives and absurd detours.

I've started treating the faces in the crowd like props in a private play, tools for a quick fix. I put on a new mask every morning, play the part, and discard it when the lights go down. I'm a man of a thousand shadows, and none of them are real. I expect respect from strangers for deeds they'll never know.
