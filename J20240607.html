I’m leaving to go back to where I spent most time. Now I owe more than what I had. I have no means to do that. I might as well start working for G at his classes to pay back the favors and money I owe him. No matter what narratives I carry in my head, I shall always end up in contradictions and absurdity. I just don’t know how to describe any of these correctly. I feel happy when I shouldn’t have and sad for things I shouldn’t feel so. I still have the privilege to enjoy certain things in my life even without the lack of money as I am able to borrow without any hope of repayment. I just go along now. After roaming around for just two days I start to trust people even though I should distrust them. I suddenly gain confidence and optimism to interact with them. All those false beliefs and confidence. I barely can feel anything. The causality for doing things I do seems absurd as they are self-manufactured ones, I for one have no cause. I worry now because of the lack of privileges, services and goods - the lack of which should mean spiritual advantages but it’s proving now to be a materialistic craving and a fall from what was before. My needs don’t seem to vanish, my habits, passions, kinks are always triumphing over my austerity, frailty and innocence. For me, people have become sexual objects meant to satiate my desires, I simply can’t seem to ignore and continue to live the way I should: without such attachments. The overzealous entitlement to few things from the past don’t seem to let go of me. I have continued to be whatever I was and I will invariably falter whenever I push myself harder - I’m not even sure whether such rigour and discipline is necessary for me, am I punishing or rewarding myself doing such acts? Why can’t I just stop saying these things, talking this way, and just let it all fizzle away? I am so keen on appearances which actually seem to be nothing - I don avatars that I conjure out of nothingness, play along and ditch them.
