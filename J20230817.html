JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, AUGUST

Paranoia, Urban Decay

The neon-lit diner at the edge of Sector 4 has become a haunt for desperate peddlers and souls begging for a scrap of salvation. I should have steered clear. This city is a magnet for the discarded, masked by a thin veneer of respectability that crumbles at the slightest touch. Even the Digital Vault—that behemoth of the Nile Syndicate—demands fresh tribute in the form of my credentials, suspicious of the rogue signal I'm currently riding.
