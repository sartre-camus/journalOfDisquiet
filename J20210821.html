JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SATURDAY, AUGUST

Philosophy, Movies

I feel impatient for the mobile terminal that hasn’t been shipped by The Tech Rogue yet. I wonder what’s causing the delay. It’s been more than one cycle since she has received the credits from me. Why do I keep checking the signal every half hour or so? It’s becoming a habit. I shouldn’t do this repetitive action. As The Counselor told me to try new activities. One of the key things is that I don’t get into this circuitous thinking loop. I have to do things instead of thinking for too long and later giving up. Exploring different ways of writing journals was one activity that I should be doing. I can create a scrap book like journal with photos, news sheet cuttings, drawings, etc. I will try to get some news sheet cuttings for the starters, as I read them regularly, it might be an easy thing to get started with. Later, I will stick a few drawings and other items in. The Counselor had also asked me to do another activity, which was to change the flicker play genre I was watching. She had asked me to switch to animation. That’s the very first thing I did. I watched an animated story about the wild reaches. The motivation to watch this was because of its high rating. This story is mostly about the circle of life. Trying to convey ancient philosophy or some ritualistic belief. It could be other beliefs too. Interestingly, I was reminded of what The Slovenian Sage had said during one of his talks. He had mentioned a scene where the father answers his son’s question. The son asks about the reason why they hunt and kill for food, for which the father says - “We hunt, eat and grow, one day, we too die and decay into the earth, with this the grass grows, others eat it and grow big and so on… It’s all a continuous cycle - The Circle of Life”. The Sage tries to bring about this scene’s analogy with another story where a child asks his father, both of whom have been captured by The Black Shirts and put in a camp - “Why are they killing us?”. The Sage tries to answer the kid’s question trying to apply the notion of ’Circle of Life’ to this. He says, “They kill us. We turn into the earth. The earth causes grass to grow. Beasts eat the grass and grow. We kill the beast and eat them. It’s all a big cycle…” The crowd listening to this burst into laughter. The Sage continues, “Well we should hope that there are other cycles of life too, not just this one, and expect that to be better than this”. I have been influenced by The Sage’s talks and his philosophy to a certain extent. Some of them are funny. Mostly his way of delivering them and the gestures he makes. Seems like he is a lunatic. The contents of his talks do invoke questions in my mind and make me think over what he says. I also try to find counter arguments to his opinions through other speakers such as The Moralist Professor. The debate between them has made me rethink about philosophies and beliefs. Sometimes I wonder who is saying the right thing. I am really incapable of understanding the meaning they’re trying to convey. I haven’t read any of The Sage’s writing so far. Whatever I have seen are his flicker plays and his talks at high academies. I did start reading one of The Professor’s scrolls titled - “Twelve rules to life”. Sounds very preachy. Something like The Decalogue of Order. It’s a scroll which discusses order and chaos. Dr. Peterson, a mind healer, Success Prophet, and orator has influenced me over time. Well, not as much as The Sage though. I still am not sure whose side I am on. Both make good arguments and I am not well equipped to understand or discern about the consistency, truthfulness or rationality. Another person who influences me through his writings and speeches is The Linguistic Titan. His intellectual works are more influential than the others. I feel that The Titan is more well read than either of them. Coming back to the animated story. The only thing that I liked about that story and wanted to watch for were the two side characters. Hakuna-matata (an ancient phrase for Live life without worries).

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
