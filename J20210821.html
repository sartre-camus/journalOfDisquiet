Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I feel impatient for the Libreboot T400 laptop that hasn’t been shipped by Leah yet. I wonder what’s causing the delay. It’s been more than one week since she has received the payment from me. Why do I keeping checking the mail every half hour or so? It’s becoming a habit. I shouldn’t do this repetitive action. So, as Christy told me to try new activities. One of the key things is that I don’t get into this circuitous thinking loop. I have to do things instead of thinking for too long and later give up. Exploring different ways of writing journal was one of the activity that I should be doing. I can create a scrap book like journal with photos, newspaper cuttings, drawings, etc. I will try to get some newspaper cuttings for the starters, as I read newspaper regularly, it might be an easy thing to get started with. Later, I will stick a few drawings and other items in. Christy had also asked me to do another activity, which was to change the movie genre I was watching. She had asked me to switch to animation movie. That’s the very first thing I did. I watched ’The Lion King’, a 1994 animated movie from Disney. The motivation to watch this movie was because of its high rating on IMDb. This movie is mostly about the circle of life. Trying to convey Buddhist philosophy or some religious belief. It could be other religions too. Interestingly, I was reminded of what Slavoj Zizek had said during one of his talks. The Slovenian philosopher had mentioned this scene from the movie where the father lion Mufasa answers his son Simba’s question. Simba, the newborn lion, asks his father about the reason why they hunt zebras and kill them for food, for which Mufasa says - “We hunt zebras, eat them and grow, one day, we too die and decay into manure for earth, with this manure grass grows, other zebras eat it and becomes big for other lions to hunt and so on… It’s all a continuous cycle - The Circle of Life”. Slavoj tries to bring about this scene’s analogy with another movie scene. This one is: ’Life is Beautiful’ by Roberto Benigni in which the kid asks his father, both of whom have been captured by the Nazis and put in concentration camp - “Why are the Nazis killing us Jews?”. Slavoj tries to answer the kid’s question trying to apply the notion of ’Circle of Life’ to this. He says, “The Nazis kill Jews. Jews turn into manure. The manure causes grass to grow. Cows eat the grass and grow. We kill the cow and eat them. It’s all big cycle…” The crowd listening to this ’joke’ bursts into laughter. Slavoj continues, “Well we should hope that there are other cycles of life too, not just this one, and expect that to be better than this”. I have been influenced by Slavoj’s talks and his philosophy to a certain extent. Some of them are funny. Mostly his way of delivering them and the gestures he makes. Seems like he is a lunatic. The contents of his talks do invoke questions in my mind and make me think over what he says. Also, try and understand how it relates to the world I see. I also try to find counter arguments to his opinions through other speakers such as Jordan Peterson. The debate between them has made me rethink about philosophies and beliefs. Sometimes I wonder who is saying the right thing. I am really incapable of understanding the meaning they’re trying to convey. I haven’t read any of Slavoj’s writing so far. Whatever I have seen are his works, such his movie ’Zizek!’, ’The Pervert’s Guide to Cinema’ and his talks at universities that are easily found online. I did start reading one of Jordan’s book titled - “Twelve rules to life” or somewhat similar. Sounds very religions and preachy. Something like Ten Commandments. It’s a book which discusses order and chaos. The topics of Jordan’s writing revolves mostly around order, chaos, hierarchy, competence etc. Dr. Jordan Peterson, a psychology professor, motivational speaker, writer and on orator has influenced me over time through his talks. Well, not as much as Zizek though. I still am not sure who’s side am I on. Both make good arguments and I am not well equipped to understand or discern about the consistency, truthfulness or rationality. Another person who influences me through his writings and speeches is Dr. Noam Chomsky. Chomsky’s intellectual works are more influential than Zizek’ or Peterson’s. I feel that Chomsky is more well read than either of them. Coming back to Lion King movie. Then only thing that I liked about that movie and wanted to watch for were the two characters - Timon and Pumba. Hakuna-matata (African phrase for Live life without worries), Baki sab batata (In an Indian Language it means - everything else is Potato).
