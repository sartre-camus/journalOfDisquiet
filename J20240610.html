JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, JUN

DISSOLUTION, STATIC, RESIDUE

The interrogations from the high-rise synths and the whispers in the rain-slicked alleys have become nothing more than background static. My debts to the chrome-syndicates, the persistent glitches in my neural-weave—all of it feels like grey water draining into a polluted harbor. The problems are merely ghosts, and the touted solutions are just more rust on a dying engine.

I’ve stripped away the flesh and the pretense until the gears ground down to a halt. All that remains is the cold, rhythmic hum of a consciousness staring into the neon-soaked void. I am a residual signal, a flickering thought-loop left behind in a sprawl that forgot how to sleep.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
