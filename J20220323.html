JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, MARCH

CIPHER, ATROPHY, VOID

The monitor hums with the low, rhythmic throb of a failing lung. Another packet of Wraith-code hit my desk tonight, hemorrhaging digital noise and smelling of ozone. It’s a jagged wall of dead data, a monolithic block of silence in a sprawl that never stops its frantic, mechanical screaming. Buried deep within the wreckage of those characters, I caught the cold, metallic glint of a thousand mercury-marks—the price of a stolen life or the cost of a shallow grave in the Low-Sectors, it’s all the same these days.

If you have the stomach to peel back the skin of this signal, you won’t find the answers in the bright lights of the Upper-Tier. You’ll have to whisper into the static-grid, directed toward the twin ghosts of the Existential-Node. Send a neural-ping to The Nauseous-Sisyphus via the Void-Link. Don’t expect a warm welcome; some things are buried in encryption for a reason, and in this city, the truth is just a faster way to find the bottom of the bay.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
