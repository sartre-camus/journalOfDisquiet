Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I find my Casio digital watch with data bank in my cupboard, lying in its case buried below a stack of clothes. I wonder why I kept it there hidden. So, I was reminded of what my cousin told about that watch, coincidentally he owns the same one. To revive it I would have to place it under direct sunlight above the terrace of my home, for about half day or entire one if there’s sunshine throughout. I got it changed till half and started using it only to be reminded of the fact that it cannot continue working for long hours as it used to be the case when I had purchased it. Time has gotten the better of the watch and I have to suffice with whatever it could spare to offer. And so, I have kept it on my terrace at the top most level, where the solar water heaters are. I have to take it back from there soon. I don’t think of someone stealing it from there but worried about the birds that could claw it and ferry it to its nests. In the process, destroying the watch, by dropping accidentally or through enough scratches. I think it’s still lying there and hasn’t been taken down. I should call my brother to bring ti down soon. Done that. Now, back to what I spent my entire or some part of the juicy period of the day doing - I prepared a diagram to demonstrate the PS1 setup and used Draw.io tool to create that. Then, I wrote the letter containing the note and the picture of the setup. Then, I stepped to take a print of the letter and with the help of WeWork staff, I got hold of an envelope and completed the mail. Only thing left now is to put in a box containing the PS1 console and slip it through UPS. UPS asks for phone number of the receiver, and I can’t find it anywhere. Now, I have sent another mail to ask for it. I could use a cheaper way to ship such as India Post, who I presume would take eons to deliver my parcel. Also, I wonder if it would reach them in the best of condition. Therefore, I would prefer to stick to a private courier or delivery service that offers the needed quality of delivery. After yesterday’s ’festival’ (globally recognised? No, here in my country), I feel really bored and tired. Putting up with people who annoy me with questions I don’t want to answer or talk about. Utter waste of my energies trying to put up a face in front of them. I woke up today and got dressed up hoping to stay at office till 8.00 PM irrespective of what I do there. Now, I am writing this diary entry, to acknowledge my time here and other events. These are a snapshot of my constant recurring thoughts - a) Marriage - This one is imposed by self based on what my parents, relatives and friends are suggesting me to, and my own failure to find a girl at a stage of my career, when again I was influenced subtly by colleagues, friends etc. to propose one. This suggestion was implicit. I used my intuition to carry out that and I got rejection. Rejection was okay, but I could have avoided the entire drama, had I remained stubborn and watchful. I constantly worry about people around me, trying to suggest and talk me into this arrangement of marriage. I wish I’d be left alone to my choices for my life rather than this constant worry or simulated talk in my head about marriage by parents, relatives and friends. b) Career/Job - This one is subconsciously affecting me, as I haven’t earned anything for past few months. I have been trying to land work contract/job but I shall have to face this reluctant attitude of mine towards work. The lazy rummaging for job is bit of worry for me, as I might take too much time, before it’s too late.
