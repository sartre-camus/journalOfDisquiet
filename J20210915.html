JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

The House of Ties - WEDNESDAY, SEP

Freedom, Dependency

Another attempt by the family to cage me. They want me to inspect a new bird to share my nest. They built this house, a monument to their expectations, hoping I’d settle there and wither. I’ve been reading the Infinite Archive on 'codependency.' It’s a word the Soul-Mender, Christy, used to throw around. The diagnosis is clear: I am a man held together by the needs of others. The only escape is to sever the wires, to vanish into the city and live among the strangers.
