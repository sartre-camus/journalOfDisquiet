Daily entry

Another attempt by M to get me to see a girl so that I can be married off to her. P, M’s wife, asked me to accompany them to the new house that they had constructed few years ago. M had constructed this house for the sake of seeing me settled there with a wife. After reading the Wikipedia article on ’codependency’ and following a questionnaire online, I find that I have been codependent on M and P till date. Codependency is something that Christy the counselor, had hinted in one of the sessions. It could also be the case that I might have codependent on other relatives too. The only way I see out of this codependency is too make myself free from the family. Go somewhere and live alone.
