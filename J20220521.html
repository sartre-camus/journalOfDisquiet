JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SATURDAY, MAY

Stagnation, Perfectionism

The A/C unit hums a cold, lonely tune this Saturday morning. I'm the only ghost in this sector, trying to restart the engine after a week lost to cheap pleasures and heavy sleep.

There's a mountain of tasks, but my mind is a traitor, oscillating between carnal desires and the need for food. When I do focus on the work, I'm paralyzed by the need for the 'Core-Prime' solution. Spurious efforts are a sin I can't afford.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
