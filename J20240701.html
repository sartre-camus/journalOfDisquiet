Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I am breaking my vow of remaining with myself. I had decided months or maybe years ago that I shall not visit any relative’s place. It seems that I might rupture that and visit them after all. I wanted to forget them all yet I am reminded of them - the folks in Mysore. The last thing I ever wanted was to be around these old folk. Why on earth should I even respect these people? They deserve nothing just the way I deserve nothing. All this drama to take P to Mysore and get her treated. Now P is my date and partner whom I have to spend time with, go around, eat, drink, sleep and do nothing with. I have great plans of visiting the Oriental Institute there as if they have something that’ll provoke my interest and keep me occupied for some time to come. Kautilya’s Arthashastra or some old manuscript of Ramayana or Mahabharata that I should peruse through and make notes of or even translate them to English as what BNR does like a scholar. I’m no scholar the least I could be is a parasite who feeds of others to keep thriving. All that undivided attention that I need to give to others for no good.
