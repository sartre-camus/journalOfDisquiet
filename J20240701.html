JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, JULY

Betrayal, Past

I'm breaking my own code of silence. I swore I'd never go back to 'The Old City', but here I am, packing a bag. I'm taking 'The Partner' for 'treatment', but it feels more like a funeral procession.

I have delusions of grandeur—looking at ancient manuscripts in 'The Vault', trying to play the scholar with Kautilya's secrets. But I'm just a parasite feeding on the past, a man with no story of his own. All this drama for a handful of ash.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
