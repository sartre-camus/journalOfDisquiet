SATURDAY, SEP

Pragmatism, Despair

I’ve spent years building bridges to nowhere. These integrations, these clever bits of code—they're just monuments to my own delusions. It’s time to stop chasing ghosts. I need to master the brutal architecture of the Java Machine, to become a cog in the corporate engine just to keep the lights on. The rest of these pipe dreams can go to hell; in this town, the only truth is the one that pays.
