JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, NOV

Decay, Sloth

The journal is gathering dust. I’ve been too busy chasing the neon dragon—money, games, and the kind of company that leaves you empty by dawn. I’ve lost the hunger for the 'Rig', for the code that used to be my life. Now I’m just a ghost haunting the high-end lounges, running up a tab I can’t pay. I’m looking for the easy way out, the short-cut to the credits. Even my tech is failing me; the laptop’s antenna is dead, refusing to catch the signal from the Hive. I feel like smashing the whole thing and starting fresh, if only I had the bankroll to do it.
