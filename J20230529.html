AMBITION, SURVIVAL

I’ve secured the blueprints for The Legacy Script. It should be enough to bluff my way through the next interrogation. Whether I actually have the stomach to memorize them is another story. My machine's heart is failing—only 38% left, and the clock is ticking. This booth is a trap; they’ve stripped the power sockets to keep the drifters from settling in. When the machine dies, I’ll be back in the dark. I’m still waiting for a signal from M. "The Strategist" at The Alpine Front regarding the trial job. It’s a test of my grit, a way for them to see if I’m the right man for the job. I’ve been assigned a piece of the puzzle, and I need to solve it before they lose interest.
