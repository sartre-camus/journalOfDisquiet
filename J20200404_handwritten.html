JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SATURDAY, APR

ISOLATION, NIHILISM

I want to be a ghost-citizen from here on. I see that all the transit through the smog-zones to anywhere is a waste of time and credits. I have no intention to earn scrip, borrow, or spend. I want to escape from this credit-trap altogether.

I do not wish to leave this hab-cell except for nutrient paste and waste reclamation. There's nothing out there in the neon-void; it is all a waste of time, of energy, and everything that exists beyond these walls.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
