The ZeMoSo Shutdown - FRIDAY, MARCH

career, integrity

I’ve walked away from the ZeMoSo Labs interview. Their terms were right, but the fine print was a nightmare. A toxic culture, archaic rituals, and the stench of a soul-crushing grind. I’d rather starve on my own terms than become a well-paid slave for a syndicate like that. They talk about remote work, but the location is a moving target. I suspect I’d be working the US night shift for pennies while the company takes the lion’s share. My gut and my research tell me it’s a no-go zone. If a job has the Naukri fingerprint on it, it’s best to keep walking. I’m keeping my time for myself, even if the ledger is looking thin.
