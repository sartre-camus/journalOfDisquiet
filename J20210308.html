JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, MARCH

Transition, Travel

The moon-long hiatus at a safehouse far away comes to an end as I wrap up my belongings to leave for The Emerald Metropolis. I still find myself lazed out and in the state of relaxation. After buying few things for Shadows back home, I think about any due items on my to-do list for my stay here. I had breakfast at a diner in The Northern Settlement, serving coastal sausage bread, spiced omelette and coffee with a vanilla treat, after sending the signal at The Central Relay Hub to a host whom I met a cycle ago. Had been to The Relic Emporium, to buy few things after having browsed the place few weeks ago. I liked this passport wallet made out of Synth-Leather, and bought it. I hope that this wallet stays with me for a long time to come and be with me during my future travels.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
