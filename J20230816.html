Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I had taken few decisions for myself few days ago. The first one was: I shall not have physical intimacy with women by paying them; second one: I shall not waste money on going places in and around the city; third: I shall exercise daily. These are three small yet difficult changes I want to bring into my life. I understand the reasons why I have chosen those three. I know that by following those three decisions I shall suffer less. There are other choices that I have made too which includes eating less, studying more, cutting down on wasteful thinking and so on. The temptation to step out of home is too high. I need to know that it’s not worth it unless I’m going to the West or to a developed country’s city like New York, London, Berlin, Amsterdam, Paris, Tokyo, Singapore, and so on. If I’m living there it makes sense for me to go out and see places. Here in the city of a third world country that I’m staying in, it’s just a useless thing to do. Even spending any sort of money on this kind of thing is a waste. I’ve decided not to eat out, shop or travel around. I will avoid going to those former places forever.
