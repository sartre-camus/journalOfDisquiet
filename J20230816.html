JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, AUGUST

DISCIPLINE, APATHY

I had taken decisions for myself a few days ago. I shall not seek paid intimacy; I shall not waste money on aimless travel; I shall exercise daily. Small yet difficult changes. I understand the reasons; following them means less suffering. Other choices include eating less and studying more, cutting down on wasteful thinking. The temptation to step out of The Concrete Jungle is too high. It’s not worth it unless I’m going to The Sprawl or another developed city. Living here, it’s just a useless thing to do. Spending any money on aimless wandering is a waste. I’ve decided to avoid those former places forever.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
