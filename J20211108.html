JOURNAL of DISQUIET

The Rigged Courtyard - MONDAY, NOVEMBER

Absurdity, Paradox

I traveled into the heart of the city just to find a place to sit and think. I took a cab with P to the Glass Hive, a place I once called home. The courtyard is filled with the same familiar faces—ghosts from another life. The air is cold, artificial. It’s all rigged. The same apparitions, the same realizations. We give overblown importance to meandering nonsense. There are no mysteries here, only carefully fabricated coincidences.

Now there’s a pressure to perform for the crowd. I should have stayed in the shadows of my room. As the Chain-Gang Prophet once said: 'What we have here is, failure to communicate.' A failure to speak with M, P, and the rest. The Glass Hive says 'We Work,' but I don’t. I just sit and appear. I am a fish out of water, gasping in the strong A/C. I don’t want food. I just want to be idle. Stay idle. Distances are my only protection.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
