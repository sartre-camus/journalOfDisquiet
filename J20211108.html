Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I travel all the way into the city to find a place to sit down and contemplate. In the midst of all that is there, the lame and nonsensical. Write the things that are absurd. Still can see what’s around and sense the relatable. I took a cab with P to this place which I lived before and enjoyed the courtyard, the courtyard of WeWork. Still these are familiar faces, faces I had seen before. For my, the cold air of AC. It’s all rigged. Rigged to the very mundane. The same faces come around and appear. The real and the imaginary. The apparition, the dream and the following realisation. The overblown importance to meandering shit. Shit of absurdness and livid perpetual. There is no mysterious happening. It’s all fabricated carefully. Randomness could also be fabricated. To show it as a random occurrence. The meanings are thus fabricated. The belief is fabricated. Now there is a pressure to perform. To perform in front of an audience. I wish I had stayed back at home. But the change is inevitable. As Cool Hand Luke would say: “What we have here is, failure to communicate”. Failure to communicate. With M, P and others. Paradox. We Work? I didn’t work. I just sit and appear. Work and I are a paradox. The quaint paradox. I am so acquainted with my room that I just don’t feel myself when I’m out of it. I feel like a fish out of water. Just can’t bear the freedom (really?). The A/C is just too strong. Blowing into my ears. Somehow, I have been told that cold air blowing into my ears can be troublesome. It causes pain. I don’t want to eat any food. Just want to sit. Idle. Idle. Stay idle. Distances are good.
