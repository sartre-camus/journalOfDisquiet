JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SATURDAY, MAY

SOLITUDE, CONTEMPT

I dragged my heels to B.N. "The Archivist" Ross's hideout yesterday. I’m hoping that was the final curtain for that act—it’s a bore to play the guest, to eat their food and exchange empty words that I could have found in a back-alley pamphlet. I’m done with these social charades. I’d rather keep my secrets to myself and enjoy the quiet decay of my own company. Seeking out others is a fool's errand. The real peace is found in being absolutely alone.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
