JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SATURDAY, JAN

DESPAIR, FUTILITY, EXCLUSION

The fog in my skull thickens, a leaden weight, clouding the pathways back to the schematics, to the cold logic of the loom. Why don't I just take the 'infant steps' they preach, trace the rudimentary logic-threads, untangle the simplest of syntactic knots? The damp chill seeps into my bones, even here, in the dim glow of the data-pane. What more is there to lose, anyway? My meager solace? The phantom weight of a forgotten dream? They'll just plunder my constructs, cannibalize my logic-schemes, then turn their backs, leaving me to rot without a posting. The air-taxis drone overhead, a symphony of indifference. Why does purpose feel like a mirage in this city of chrome and rust? Why is a meaningful billet so damn elusive?

I've gnawed at the edges, clawed at the gates of the Unfettered Code Collective, dreaming of a place where logic breathes free. But even The Chronicon Repository, that grand mausoleum of forgotten algorithms, spat out my petition. A deep-system architect's posting, they called it. Another dead end in the rain-slicked canyons of the Spire-City of Veridian, where the neon bleeds into the perpetual twilight and hope goes to die.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
