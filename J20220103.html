Journal of Disquiet

Thoughts

Is life just a process of finding love, happiness, wealth, and so on? Why is there doubt, melancholy, sadness, tragedy and other such things in life? What is happiness? Should my goal be pursuit of pleasure, happiness, wealth and so on? I don’t want to pursue sadness, melancholy, despair, tragedy etc. but does that mean I pursue the former? Why can’t I make up my mind and work towards the goal I’ve set for myself? When the goals are uncertain, the process is unknown, and there is so much doubt, will I be able to go through with all these?

My friend says getting married will solve my problems. But will it? It will open a pandora’s box of misery. Misery in the guise of responsibility. What is my problem anyway? Inability to find a job that I like? Find a girlfriend whom I can just have coitus with and later be by myself? Have a place of my own to stay and carryout all of these? To have a lot of friends? All of these seem a tiresome and boring activity.
