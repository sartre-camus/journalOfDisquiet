JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

2022-01-03

DREAD, CYNICISM, PARALYSIS

Another night, another glass. The rain-slicked ferrocrete outside my window reflects the bruised glow of a thousand neon signs, each promising something that doesn't exist. Is this grand opera they call 'life' merely a grim march towards some phantom glimmer—a flicker of affection, a fleeting warmth, the cold embrace of coin? Why then, does the shadow of doubt stretch so long, why does the chill of melancholy seep into the bones, why do the city's mournful laments echo with the hollow ring of tragedy? What, truly, is this 'happiness' they peddle in the data-streams and on the holo-billboards? Should my worn-out boots chase after pleasure's fleeting phantom, or the illusion of fortune? I have no taste for the bitter vintage of despair, or the crushing weight of tragedy, but does that condemn me to pursue the former, knowing its emptiness? My mind, a broken clock in this endless night, refuses to set a course. When the destination is a mirage, the path obscured by perpetual fog, and doubt is the only true companion, how does one keep walking?

A voice from the smoke-filled corners of a low-lit speakeasy, another lost soul, whispered that the domestic trap—the ceremonial shackling, the 'matrimonial contract'—would stitch up the gaping wounds. But will it? Or will it merely pry open a deeper pandora’s box, spewing forth a fresh torrent of misery cloaked in the grim uniform of responsibility? What ails me, anyway? This gnawing inability to secure a station in the endless corporate maw that doesn't curdle the soul? This fleeting desire for a dame's warmth, a shared oblivion that leaves me just as alone when the morning synth-light breaks? A solitary cell of my own, a rain-streaked window on the void, to carry out these hollow pursuits? To gather a cohort of fellow travelers on this dead-end street? The whole damn circus feels like a tiresome, boring ritual, played out under a sky that never truly clears.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
