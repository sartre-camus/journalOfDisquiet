JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

JUNE 20, 2024

LETHARGY, DETACHMENT

And I have no intention to work or earn to repay that debt. All my time that I spend is not particularly aimed at earning credits but instead ruminating and decaying. I aim to do nothing but the outcome tends to be that something which puts others at risk and pain. This ongoing apathy towards the denizens around me causes a great deal of pain. I have tried to endure myself all these times - with a great uncertainty and fear.

I have lived through enough failures on a daily basis that failure has become like an undetachable part of my body. All this notion of failure sits firmly in my head and it budges nowhere - all this leads me to a duality of failure and success - as long as success exists failure also, and vice-versa. All that matters is enduring and trying all the time in my own capacity and keep at it. I don't have an escape from sulking - I know not how to live.

I slept for at least three hours after the heavy nutrient ration during the artificial twilight cycle. I can only sleep...

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
