Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Another day, I wake up trying to get some more sleep. I realise that I need to get some exercise early to shed a few calories and that extra fat accumulated in my belly. I wear socks and shoes and head out in my car to a place a few miles away in a University with empty tarmac roads, trees and fresh air. I start to walk to warm up and later flexed my arms, neck and legs to get ready for a short sprint. Then, I sprint or jog (to be precise), towards the car (where I had parked, at the entrance of the University). I realise that I am able to jog and run without the mask and slowly started experiencing the sweating and blood rushing into my feet. I sprint further towards my car but gave up few blocks away, as my fatigue took over. Later, I walked a bit towards the card and turned the engine on to take a slow drive back home. The usual daily affair continues until I later in the morning, close to noon, take out my SCM Coronet typewriter to explore and take photos of it. I was able to unscrew one from the bottom but the other screw was pretty tight. I tried with different combinations of screw driver heads but failed to rotate eve a little bit. The metal of the screw seems to be wearing out slowly and the screw is becoming even more difficult to take apart. I give up. I cannot spend more time on this one as it seemed futile. An expert who repairs typewriters (hard to find one these days) will have to work his hands on it to make it come back to life. For now, it just rests in my big drawer of the cupboard, dormant. Sometimes, I wonder how hard it is to revive old things when they have gone past their service lifetime. It’s so demanding, the activity of rejuvenating a machine, that has stopped working. Requires a complete understanding of the parts involved, the layout, the tools needed etc. without which it is almost impossible to get started. During the attempt to revive, I start wondering whether is it worth it. Am I trying to do something that is just a waste of my time? With these constant thoughts prodding through my head, I try to figure out how can I solve this problem at hand.
