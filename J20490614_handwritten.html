THE FIFTH CYCLE, 2049 - MONDAY, JUNE

DECADENCE, OBSESSION

A late night drive towards another corner of the Sprawl, philosophical conversation, experience sharing and watching some videos of a terminal screen along with the neural-link technician, V-7. I spent the night there and returned back afterwards to the Aura Hab-Block, to get a shower, breakfast and checked out after coughing up credits on the credit chip. All of this happened on the Fifth Cycle and the evening afterwards.

The day before that was a long drive out towards the Sky-Port to get a lavish massage at The Obsidian Lounge from K-17. That and some synthetic nutrient greens and an Ozone-Pulse or O-tree as K-17 said, an O3 facial for a lofty four thousand credits. I look no different afterwards though. I look the same old G-9 whose face is dark with no glow or shine.

I just spend it on her, so that one day I could possess her fully and continue to do so even after that. This is how I cycle through companions and possess them, every now and then by splurging borrowed credits and without any care for the...
