Abrupt Cycles

Sleep, Duty

The day starts with the grime of the kitchen. I did the dishes to quiet P’s frantic energy. I finally sent off the GKS dossier this morning—a debt paid to the past. My sleep is a series of abrupt cycles, waking and drifting without a map. I feel heavy, a slumber that follows every meal like a shadow. The choice to act or remain still is a riddle I haven't solved.
