JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SATURDAY, DECEMBER

Duty, Lethargy

Holed up in a borrowed roost, far from familiar shadows, with the kin. We'd journeyed to The Serpent Shrine, an ancient edifice lost in the heart of the Dusty South, to perform the solemn duties of the old ways. Now, the creeping lethargy of a late repast settled heavy, knowing I must face the pre-dawn gloom to finish the remaining solemnities of the observance.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
