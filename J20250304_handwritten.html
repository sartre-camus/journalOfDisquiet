JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, MAR

AVARICE, EXISTENTIAL DREAD

...tries to enrich itself as amidst the nothingness.

The avarice never dies; I keep checking those data-bursts and terminal pings until the credits arrive into my digital ledger. I have grown thirstier over the cycles for scrip as my habits have become lavish and vain. The projection I have of myself is astonishing and painful to bear. I go through a turmoil of conflicting thoughts arising from conflicting philosophies—I wish I could bury them all and never have to confront them again. There is also the constant fear of losing out on the livelihood that I have recently gained through some fumble, misdeed, or mishap. All that wealth lures me into traps—I am becoming more obsessed with failures and misfortunes.

There is an ongoing temptation to enter the Nexus-Cell Syndicate again, as if having that shared-processing zone would get me to complete and continue the project at hand and also have a constant livelihood at all times.
