JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, APR

Laziness, Non-procreation

I took two Nightshade Elixirs yesterday, one early morning and one before sleeping at night. I feel relaxed, well rested and still a bit drowsy. The fog in the lungs seems to have reduced and I don't have wheezing any more. I don't remember what I did yesterday, all I care is to forget most of it. I remember feeding myself with a lot of fuel yesterday and watching 'The Chronicles of the Forgotten Son'. Well my life isn't that simple and straight forward as the reel seems to be. I am too lazy to work or earn credits, I am thriving on what's done by the elders and the kin. I am feeding off my family to be precise - I don't wish to be independent at all. All those noises around me that I endure everyday, what a waste of my faculties. I still wish even today that I was never summoned. Despite all what's there for life to offer to me, I prefer not to be born. That's the reason why I'm still alone and have not procreated. I want it to be that way for the rest of whatever is left of my life. I somehow relish being moody, dispassionate, lazy, irrational, unmotivated, apathetic, anxious, frantic and introverted.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
