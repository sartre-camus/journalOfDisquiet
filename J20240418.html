Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I took two pills of Montek LC yesterday, one early morning and one before sleeping at night. I feel relaxed, well rested and still a bit drowsy. Phlegm seems to have reduced and I don’t have wheezing any more. I don’t remember what I did yesterday, all I care is to forget most of it. I remember feeding myself with a lot of food yesterday and watching Satyajit Ray’s Apu Trilogy. Well my life isn’t that simple and straight forward as the movie seems to be. I am too lazy to work or earn a living, I am thriving on what’s done by my parents and brother. I am feeding of my family to be precise - I don’t wish to be independent at all. All those noises around me that I endure everyday, what a waste of my faculties. I still wish even today that I was never born. Despite all what’s there for life to offer to me, I prefer not to be born. That’s the reason why I’m still unmarried and have not procreated. I want it to be that way for the rest of whatever is left of my life. I somehow relish being moody, dispassionate, lazy, irrational, unmotivated, apathetic, anxious, frantic and introverted.
