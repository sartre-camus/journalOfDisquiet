Journal of Disquiet

Daily saga

I have spent time watching UG’s videos on YouTube for the last few days. I also went on to watch the TV series Top of the Lake which features GJ, a character inspired from UG. Somehow, I want to get into UG’s head, know his work and understand how he lived. I know very well that I shall not be able to follow or imbibe any of his shit, but I would rather keep myself entertained by his profanity. Sometimes it does feel to be correct and makes sense, to an extent of being palatable and meaningful, however, I doubt even his negations. He too is trying to get some audience just like every other guru and get some fame, name, money and so on. Even selling non-meditation, anti-guru, anti-whatever is also selling and making money. I think we all sell ourselves for money, time, attention, whatever, to not sell is also a way of selling, probably the better ways of doing it. I think I remember it from Bojack Horseman, in which the cat (don’t know what’s the name) but tells something like, “Not selling something is the best way to sell something”. I have used the word ’something’, so many times already that something is not okay.
