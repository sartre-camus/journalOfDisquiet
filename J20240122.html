JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

2024-01-22

Profanity, Selling

I have spent time watching The Oracle's transmissions on the Grid for the last few days. I also went on to watch the TV series 'The Black Lake' which features a character inspired from The Oracle. Somehow, I want to get into his head, know his work and understand how he lived. I know very well that I shall not be able to follow or imbibe any of his poison, but I would rather keep myself entertained by his profanity. Sometimes it does feel to be correct and makes sense, to an extent of being palatable and meaningful, however, I doubt even his negations. He too is trying to get some audience just like every other prophet and get some fame, name, credits and so on. Even selling silence, anti-wisdom, anti-whatever is also selling and making credits. I think we all sell ourselves for credits, time, attention, whatever, to not sell is also a way of selling, probably the better ways of doing it. I think I remember it from the 'Horseman of the Apocalypse' reel, in which the feline agent tells something like, 'Not selling something is the best way to sell something'. I have used the word 'something', so many times already that something is not okay.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
