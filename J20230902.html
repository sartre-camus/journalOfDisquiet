JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

2023-09-02

Entrapment, Corporate Dystopia

I’m hunkered down in a glass cage on the sixth floor of the Manyata Sprawl, a corporate labyrinth where the ghosts of industry wander on weekends. This morning, I feasted like a condemned man at The Grand Hilton—a final meal before the grind. Now, I’m a prisoner of my own making. The security locks on the facilities are unforgiving; I'll have to wait for a sympathetic soul to trip the sensor, or find another way to answer nature's call in this antiseptic tomb.
