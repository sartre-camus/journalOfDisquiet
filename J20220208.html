JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, FEB

Paranoia, Solitude

The city's grim arteries called, compelling me to seek the Apothecary for the mender's strip The Companion required. Her flesh had been marked during the morning's stark ritual, a slip of the blade amidst the earth's bounty. My gut churned at the thought of the outside, yet the crimson stain on her skin demanded the urgent attention of the adhesive patch. Words, sharp as broken glass, were exchanged with The Companion regarding the absence of a quick fix within the confines of our shadowed den. A cold dread seeped in, a whispering doubt: was this wound a deliberate maneuver, a calculated ploy to yank me from the solace of my chosen crypt? Now, a heavy slumber descends, an uninvited guest after the morning's meager fare. My mind, a carnival of shadows, thrashes with unbidden specters, forever caught between the blinding glare and the comforting void.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
