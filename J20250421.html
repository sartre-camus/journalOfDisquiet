JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

GHOST CHASE - MONDAY, APR

Paranoia, Severance

It’s a pathetic sight—watching myself flinch every time the comm-link chirps. I have my orders, I know the drill. Yet I’m out here chasing ghosts across three different encrypted channels, three different personas, a dozen different lies. I’ve cut ties with The Confessor. No more paying for the privilege of hearing my own sins reflected back at me. The drivel stops here.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
