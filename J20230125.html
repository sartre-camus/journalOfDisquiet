Daily entry

Have shortlisted few work contracts that I can apply for on WeWorkRemotely and FossJobs.net. I just feel like going home and drawing the crayons I had bought few days ago. Crayons and drawing book to sketch and draw few things - for starters I want to redraw and color the cover photo of Sartre’s Nausea book, on the white canvas. The abstract photo is the beginning. I would like to redraw it and use crayons to color it. Most of the time, it’s just doing such activities. I perhaps should build that oAuth feature anyway. Roll it out as a new feature and ask for extra money from OpenProject. My only worry is if I do all that write code, build and publish, will I be able to cajole them into paying me more? I’d definitely have enough to showcase what I’ve done but is it good enough for an increased sponsorship amount? Not sure. They seem to have forgotten about the last email I sent. Will it be enough to get more money in? That’s the risk I need to take. Is the time worth it? Is the effort worth it? Are all the learnings as part of plugin development work be useful in the future? This is where I tend to think more like a stock broker who’s trying to predict what the value of some stock might be in the future. Sometimes, I wonder, is this entire episode of making more money through software sponsorship only a means to be away from home? Live on my own terms - longer and freely - independently, not having to stay in that aura of parents, neighbours, relatives etc. ? I guess that’s all it eventually boils down to. How long will I be able to llive independently here in Goa or abroad on my own terms and sustain without having to go back home to save money on rent, food etc.?
