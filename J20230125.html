JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

The Abstract Gamble

career, creativity

I’ve marked a few contracts on the underground boards—WeWorkRemotely and FossJobs. But my heart’s not in the hustle. I want to go back to the room and let the crayons bleed across the page. I want to redraw the cover of Sartre’s 'Nausea', a white canvas waiting for an abstract nightmare. It’s a distraction, but it’s the only thing that feels real. I’m weighing the cost of building that oAuth feature for OpenProject. If I roll it out, can I squeeze them for more sponsorship? Or will they just look at my work and shrug? They haven’t replied to my last wire. It’s a risk, a stock broker’s game of predicting the future. Sometimes I think this whole software racket is just a way to stay away from the Matriarch and the neighbors’ prying eyes. How long can I survive in the Palm-fringed Sprawl before the credits run out and I have to crawl back to the safety of the nest?
