JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

Perpetual Holidays - MONDAY, OCTOBER

Time, Work

Another Monday, waiting for the static in my head to clear. Deep breaths of the city's smog. Pigeons—the gray spies of the eaves—coo their regular, distinctive reports. Everyday feels like a holiday, and every holiday feels like the grind. I want to work when the city sleeps and sleep when it wakes.
