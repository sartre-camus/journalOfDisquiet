JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, JUNE

NIHILISM, DETACHMENT

During the long cycles of the artificial sun and the dimming of the neon grids, I retreat. The dark hours are reserved for base indulgences—sensory-vids, neural-stimulants, analog texts, and the like. I have merely existed to consume; for cycles now, I have made no effort toward any higher purpose. I continue to drift, decaying internally into a state of absolute void. I find myself dreaming more frequently now, as if those phantoms are all that remains of my essence.

I do not write for purpose, but merely to mark the passage of time with ink, leaving a faint trail of nostalgia in this chrome wasteland. There is no inherent value in this endeavor. Others might seek to trade their words for credits or social-rank, but I am too far removed from such trivialities. I understand, with a cold intuition, that it is all a grand farce—a curated spectacle, a mundane distraction from the raw agony pulsing through the Outer Sectors. It is a lie, a stupidity, the most futile of pursuits.

Whatever needs to be uttered has already been spoken into the void. Whatever might be said in the future will be said by others. There is nothing left for me to articulate, and perhaps, no reason left for me to exist.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
