JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, APRIL

AMBITION, LONELINESS

A day of stagnant boredom and bone-deep fatigue. The heat outside is a thick, oppressive blanket as I push forward, hoping to eventually stand above the wreckage. I’ve got only one play left: The Grinder. Anton "The Fixer" Vane and his crew of headhunters will have to deal the cards in my favor. It’s the only way out. I can’t maintain the lifestyle I crave unless that contract lands on my desk. That’s all there is to say. I’m craving company again, pestering Suzanne "The Siren" Marlowe to give me a piece of her time, even though she has her own mysteries to solve. Even though I haven't a cent to spend on her.
