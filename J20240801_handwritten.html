JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

AUGUST 1, 2024

PRETENSE, OBSOLESCENCE

Ah, the month of August has begun, and I find myself possessed by a strange energy after an 'early' decontamination cycle at 0845 hours. I have donned the slate-grey tactical tunic, acquired for no less than two thousand credits at The Citadel Outpost many cycles ago. I pair it with sepia-toned utility slacks, four years weathered but recently scrubbed and pressed. I feel like a corporate operative, costumed for some formal charade—negotiating with Syndicate liaisons, perhaps, or some other hollow ritual of the upper spires.

Today, I must determine if I can reactivate the data-node 'v-nexus.net' by offering tribute to the Aether Registry. The node requires a complete overhaul; it seems my mind is unable to perceive anything beyond the existing architecture. I can refresh the static details, yet so much remains absent. I harbor second thoughts about anchoring a link to these Disquiet Logs on that public terminal. There is also the weightier concern of how to host such a node without alerting the grid monitors. I find myself weighing every credit spent when it comes...

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
