JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, MAY

Pointless Demands, Blood Bonds

I demanded a partial refund from the Hilton Spire for their failing infrastructure. Was it the right move? Or should I have just stayed silent? It feels pointless, this struggle for a few credits, this friction in a hollow relationship.

'Nothing' is a heavy word. It's hard to do nothing because nothing always contains something. And what is 'home'? A place I've haunted for twenty-five years? The family spire of P, M, and G? Is it just blood and free bread?

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
