JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, JUNE

LETHARGY, DETACHMENT

That's valuable. I just want to sever those comm-links for some real time—take a break and resume afterwards. I keep dreaming of Zone 4-style ruins within my sector, where I could go out and run. I still think of those places and how they looked a long, long time ago. There were slag pits and I used to walk past them towards the empty fields, green and sun setting after the Academy of Compliance. I just remember that vast emptiness all around.

The idea of being resourceful has been a point of conflict for me. I always think why there is so much insistence on being useful, valuable, productive, etc., to the Collective. I wish to be nothing; I have no value to offer, that's why I don't work. I sometimes think that the entire Hegemony, the Hive, or whatever wants me to be useful in the way they want it. It's like an imposition of values, expectations, virtues, and what not. They want me to have gainful employment, a registered family unit, offspring, and what not. To be a quota-meeting model subject.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
