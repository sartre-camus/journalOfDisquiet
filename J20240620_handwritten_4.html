COGNITION, DETACHMENT

towards synthetic kinship, neural-bonding, carnal-stim and its likes. The desire of the mind (if there exists one) is to keep itself alive through cognitive-archiving, whatever be it bio-logic, cyber-mechanics, existential-logic, visual-static or anything at all.

I still wonder if The Chronicler M. was wrong when he puts cognitive-archiving above pleasures of the senses. Some data-packets on the Global Mesh say they both are one and the same—I'm not convinced of any of those ideas; it feels that they trigger similar sectors of the neural-cortex to release serum-compounds that cause whatever that they cause.
