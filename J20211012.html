The Construction Site - TUESDAY, OCTOBER

Chaos, Stability

The road is being torn up again. A pointless search for ghost credits in the dirt. The city is a permanent construction site, a metaphor for my own mind. Even the birds in the trees seem like planted surveillance. I’ve got the word 'FUCK' tattooed on my spirit, and only I can feel the ink.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
