JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

The Archives of the Exhausted

process, health

I’ve got a mountain of paper to move—years of entries in dusty notebooks that need to be digitized. It’s a thankless job, a tedious crawl through the past. I’d only do it to show the soul-doctor, a paper trail of my own undoing. I’m running on fumes today; a morning spent wheezing instead of sleeping. The room is a dust trap, cold and dry, fighting my every breath. I’ve got the humidifier humming, a small mechanical lung to keep the air from biting. I need to wrap myself in something heavy and disappear into the mattress before the temperature drops another twenty degrees. In this city, the weather is as fickle as a snitch.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
