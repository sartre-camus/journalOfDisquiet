Existence, Purpose

It feels so surreal after all, isn't it, when the world conjures around us. The meaning of existence or the great unknown remains a mystery unless sought with a true soul, remaining unraveled in the shadows.

These were some lines I struck open on a lazy afternoon when the work seemed pedestrian. They say that the interplay of words catches the attention of the wandering eye when it sounds like more than just what it is.

Rhyming, rhythmic alliterations, and metaphors are the literary devices I shall use to mask the truth. Some of this journal shall also be used to document my trips along the uncharted territories of this planet. Events, cuisine, hobbies, and the strangers I meet shall be written about. Interesting anecdotes, parables, and incidents will be remembered. Not just some fiction, but also fantasies and dreams can be read through as the clock ticks on.

It's just hard to write coherently with a steady train of thoughts in this foggy district. There shall be detours, poems encapsulated somewhere. Some quotes shall be uttered which are my own and may have occurred to somebody else, which may be termed coincidental. Some may be very obvious, some may be genius. Whatever it is, I shall write, for writing doesn't need me to pay any taxes to the city, slog late hours fixing a bug in the machine, or pander to the aspirations laid or burdened by family, society, or myself.

It is so unique indeed that we have this capability to be whatever we choose to be. Free will as a central problem in philosophy still enchants me. With growing technological advents and socioeconomic vicissitudes—a word someone taught me in the old academy, used in corporate parlance.

I shall write with more involvement of The Architect of Slow Thought (as the old psychologist puts it) than the apparent. This is what makes this special, magical, or perhaps just a desperate cry in the dark.
