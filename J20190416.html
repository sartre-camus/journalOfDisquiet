Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

It feels so surreal after all isn’t it, when the world conjures around us. The meaning of existence or unknown remains a mystery for unless sought with the true soul remains unravelled.

These were some lines I struck open on a lazy afternoon when the work seemed pedestrian. They say that the interplay of words catches attention of the wandering eye when it sounds more than just what it is. Rhyming, rhythmic, alliterations and metaphors are the literary devices I shall use. Some of this journal shall also be used to document my trips along the uncharted (or charted) territories of this planet. Events, cuisine, hobbies and people shall be written about. Interesting anecdotes, parables and incidents will be remembered. Not just some fiction but also fantasies and dreams can be read through, as time proceeds. Its just hard to write coherently with a steady train of thoughts. There shall be detours, poems encapsulated somewhere. Some quotes shall be uttered which are my own and may have occurred to somebody else, which may be termed coincidental. Some may be very obvious, some may be genius. Whatever it is I shall writer, for writing doesn’t need me to pay any taxes, slog late hours fixing a bug or pander to the aspirations laid or burdened by family, society or myself. It is so unique indeed that we have this capability to be whatever we choose to be. Free will as a central problem or topic of discussion in philosophy still enchants me. With growing technological advents and socioeconomic vicissitudes (took a while to rewind on this word, somebody taught me in college. Said it is used in corporate parlance). I shall write with more involvement of my System Two (as Kahneman puts it) than the apparent. This is what makes this special, magical (running low on adjectives here…).
