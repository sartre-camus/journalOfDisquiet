Despondency, Uniform

I donning the uniform of the bereaved today as if I would engineer the digital shadows or read something today. Or perhaps write a few programs or something like that. Unfortunately, such interests dwindle soon as they even creep up in my head as if I want to do something else like pointless thinking and ruminating over stuff that I cannot care any less. It is this sort of wasteful and pointless rumination that has rendered me quite despondent.
