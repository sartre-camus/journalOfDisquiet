CONTEMPT, ISOLATION

I’m spending my hours among the low-lifes and the commoners. There’s no other word for them. With their petty schemes and mindless gossip, they fit perfectly into the scenery of this rotting city. You can't judge them by their suits; the silk is just a cover for the rot beneath. The moment they open their mouths, the stench of mediocrity is unmistakable. They repeat the same hollow scripts year after year. Even if you left this town for a decade and came back, they’d be exactly where you left them—stereotypes trapped in a loop. Why don't they just vanish into the fog? I’ve got my headphones on, trying to drown out their pointless noise with something that resembles peace. They’re just groups of empty vessels, consuming and excreting, moving through the city like waste through a pipe. That’s all they are.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
