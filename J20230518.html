JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

J20230518 - THURSDAY, MAY

AMBITION, APATHY

The world is a seething mass of ceremonies and seductions, a monstrosity hidden in the mundane. What’s there to fear when you can already see the abyss? I wonder how H.M. "The Drunken Poet" ever found enough to fill a book when everything just seems to be fading into gray. I’ve got an interrogation—they call it an interview—with Alexa from The Dutch Syndicate in a few hours. I’m wondering if this is my ticket out, a chance to disappear into the Amsterdam Front forever. How much more grit will it take to get there? Am I lucky enough to pull it off, to find the score I need to keep this life moving? Spent the day sinking into the yellow couch, staring out at the rain. I’m in a state of relaxed decay, with no intention of doing anything "important." It’s a beautiful state of incompetence. I lie here and watch the nothingness, the pathetic drifters moving through their own disarray. I’m resisting the pull of the city, trying to stay away from the bondage of the daily grind. I just sit here in the artificial chill, trying to become less than nothing. It’s an act of defiance to be away from it all. That old tree in the courtyard—I wonder how many secrets it’s seen grow and wither. I’m a bit dejected, a bit disappointed, but mostly I’m just waiting for the void to claim its own.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
