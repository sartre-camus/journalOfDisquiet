Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

All there was a robust need to be aware of the present with all its ceremonies and seething seductions. The unnatural omnipresent bequeathing of monstrosity in the manifestations of the mundane. What is there to be worried about when I can sense the abyss of nothingness? I sometimes wonder how Hank Moody from Californication TV series has so much to write about, as much as a book, when it all seems to be just waning away. I have an interview with Alexandra from ING Amsterdam in sometime now, I was wondering whether I will be able to get this job offer and migrate to Amsterdam forever. How much of struggle is it going to take to get there? Am I lucky enough to continue being there, viable as an option to be working for ING and have the income I need.

The day of simply sitting on the yellow couch and staring out of the window. The idea of being in slumber and relaxed. No intention of being involved with anything worthy of importance. It’s an utter state of disappointment and incompetence. I just lie here to see the utter nothingness, the lousy demeanour of these poltroons thronging around, in a state of complete disarray. A resistance to denizen, a resistance to the somehow being away from the bondage of backfiring benches. I just sit in here to enjoy the cool temperature of air conditioning, trying to be a little less than nothing. An act of stupidity to be away and away, forever away from everything that there was. That beautiful banyan tree out there in the opposite compound - its leaves and timber, I wonder how long idd it take for it to grow, out grow from that and spread around. I myself am a little dejected and disappointed for wiling away and trying to stay afloat and despised, the need of nothingness.
