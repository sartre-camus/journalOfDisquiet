JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

AUGUST 28, 2024

COGNITIVE DECAY, EXISTENTIAL DREAD

"The sector lounges, from where the synthetic hum of music and voice reaches me. I sit at the door and fill my eyes and ears with the colors and sounds of the industrial wastes and slowly, just for myself, sing vague songs that I compose while I wait." — B. S. of the Void.

I try to read Varlamov’s 'The Great Attrition' as part of my reading-list. I give up after every few pages. I am so used to neural-link stim-feeds that reading paper-cores has become a herculean task. Watching sim-streams is a lot simpler, but reading novels, literature, fiction is a difficult task.

I have been conditioned with sensory-data streams such that it takes a real change in my habits to read challenging analog classics. This afternoon I was gripped by a fear as if the sickness had returned. I am unable to find rest during the cycles, as I have not engaged in enough cognitive labor during the light-hours.
