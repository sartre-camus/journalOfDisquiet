JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER

Debt, Duty

I have put myself doing a schlep job by tutoring those high school kids everyday. I am feeling challenged when I cannot solve those problems myself and on the other hand I feel I could not do it at all. I am doing a poor job in that. Of course, I get paid nothing - I’m not even repaying the principal amount I owe to Gideon by doing this gig. I am returning a favor of sorts that I owe him for lending me credits on demand. This is in no way repaying his debt.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
