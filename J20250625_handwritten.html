JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, JUN - THE OBSOLETE SCRIBE

NIHILISM, APATHY

I neglected to secure this sector-pod before arriving. I wish I had done so before trekking all the way here in a frantic rush. Now all that remains is for me to sit here and do nothing at all. What a waste to simply sit and scribble words the entire cycle. I spend it all to be of no practical use—there’s almost no tech-device on me.

A rendition of moronic proportions is what I yearn for—an utmost absurdity that no one could perhaps imagine. All this way about in practically doing nothing. I run away from all these towards purposeless, morbid, grim, bleak apathy. Why do I care about nothing so much? As if I am a nihilist. An ongoing battle for unknown reasons or waypoints, I shall smear absurdity all over me before I drench myself with excruciating pain, negligence and injustice. I have not learnt to endure myself so far. I look into the eyes of other people as if they have something that I don't already have. All these cravings for what good, I'm not sure.

All these busy people around me trying to earn a credit with the work they do and I behave like a nobody, a jobless, worthless, pointless nothing. Isn't it all this absurd need to be validated, to be treated with respect, to be treated with empathy, love, care and what not that drives me with my insane expectations of everyone around me. Why should anyone give a rat's ass about who the fuck am I? Why this paltry craving from all the people around me? Or anyone far far away. I have to learn to be a boring, unvented, absurd, nobody. I should avoid all forms of stupidity, interaction with people.

One should learn to desecrate better I suppose for one should learn it, it cannot come from birth. It has to be learnt through some boring way and takes cycles to make few strides. I am just worried that someone might check out these writings of mine and then keep it at the mantle of stupidity. Why is there a trouble with being the way, the way things are? If the Nihilist Architect penned a codex to postpone his termination, I have no such thing at all. I am not desperate enough to terminate myself or optimist enough that the Great Void would solve my suffering. Death is a big mystery, I don't know what comes in it or after it. So the exercise of writing will keep me from terminating myself, though the reason is unknown.

Why keep writing? What is there to write at all? When the thoughts that flow in my head are not my own. Every morsel of my being is a despicable repetition of all that's there—what more should I become creative about? Why should I create anything when there's nothing left to create? Even my handwriting gives up after few sentences, as all of these are forcefully done, out of a punishment that is imposed on me—to write, to make sense, to fill papers with ink strokes that would entertain someone (which includes myself). I am sure I too would be despised to see what I have written, let alone read it or share it with others.

The nutrient-brew I prepared has cooled. It has become undrinkable. The beverage loses its heat quicker in here because of the atmospheric regulators. Maybe if I had more ways to kill time apart from waiting for that brew to lose its warmth. I watched that Sector-9 Kinetic-Match until last night, hoping for entertainment, but I was disappointed as it all fell apart. The only way to keep myself stimulated is through base physical release, and it is painful. I cannot take that pain any longer. Then I stream the contents of my data-slate—vids, captures, and synth-tracks—to the Legacy Console-7 through the aether-node and get myself entertained through some twenty-year-old media files. I play a grid-iron simulation on the console with so much interest, as if it were going to fetch me some credits.

They write as if they exist, I write as if to kill time and eventually life itself. What's left of me is nothing and these empty words that simply flow out of an insane compulsion. In a world where people live with goals, aspirations, purpose and what not, how would I even make sense? All that absurd nonsense about my writings still need to vent away through nowhere. How can I not simply disappear from this age-old, mundane existence? Is there a thought at all to begin with when everything that's out there is already poured into me as a form of conditioned thought which perpetuates itself through a boring nonsense. All those preoccupied things when there's nothing that possibly could matter. I am not sure how long I am going to continue writing this way as I would be hungry in sometime and would have to crawl back to the Matriarch's Hab-Unit for rations.

The hesitation to create a purpose is a real one and it probably manifests itself in me more prominently than it would. I prefer to live in chaos and keep complaining about it. I constantly complain; there's enough to complain about, starting from my birth itself. I could complain all day about the absurdity that's there—say that the Prime Architect is a scam and keep yapping it in my writings forever.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
