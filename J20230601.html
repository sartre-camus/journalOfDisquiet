Journal of Disquiet

Thoughts

How important it is to be covert in our operations? In our writings, entries, ideas and so on. How can I be so sure that there’s no one snooping on me every time I touch this keyboard and there are characters getting typed on the screen to be later saved into a document. The constant watch by myself or someone whom I believe to be watching me. What does it take me to be simply quiet? Without the need to be ever talk to anybody or expect someone to talk with me. That’s the zen-like mode I would want myself to be in. There’s no hurry to go approach people who look somewhat likeable or useful and then expect to talk to them and anticipate responses. The idea of keeping myself to myself - as my body and mind in itself are enough and self-sufficient. There’s no need to be distracted by anyone or anything around me. It’s that level of distancing and separation that I wish to achieve when it comes to anybody around me.
