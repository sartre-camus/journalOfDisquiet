JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, JUNE

SECRECY, PHILOSOPHY

How much of our lives must remain in the shadows? Every word I type, every thought I document—is there someone watching the screen? I feel the weight of eyes upon me, even when I’m alone. I crave the silence, the ultimate cover. To be a ghost in the city, never speaking, never being seen—that’s the only way to find peace. There’s no need to chase the useful or the likeable, hoping for a connection that will only lead to trouble. I need to be enough for myself. A state of absolute separation from the masses. That’s the goal.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
