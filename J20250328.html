Journal of Disquiet

My belly is full yet I want to eat junk to fill it. I somehow would want to keep the tank full at all times by filling it with some junk or the other. A little depletion and there’s this urge to refil it. I love spending money over useless things and enjoy doing so. I have no intention to fulfil any goal or expectation set by someone but only demand things from others, what they have, and use it for my greed.

I fed on the holige and chickpeas today, after that I washed it down with mango panaka here at RMZ WeWork. I was going to buy a vegetable puff upstairs but luckily this food was available for free here and I had it. It did fill my belly a bit and got me to move around a bit. The spilled water on this table is still a bit of a bother. I should have been a bit more careful yet, I am filled with these thoughts of nothingness and apathy that I find no solution to.
