ANXIETY, HATRED

After yesterday’s long night watching The Chronicler’s transmissions, I thought I would make some changes to my lifestyle. I took a long walk in the morning. Now I come to The Obsidian Roast in the business park to get started. Today, I plan on looking for remote roles through The Global Network and finishing a coding assignment. My thighs are hurting; I am sitting on a high chair, writing this entry on the desk where my laptop is placed. I see a young lady outside, plugged into her earphones and tense. All those cuss words I said while coming in my vehicle, they just vent the hatred and anger I have towards people. I hate most of them who live around me.
