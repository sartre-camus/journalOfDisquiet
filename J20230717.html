Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

After yesterday’s long night watching Dhruv Rathee’s videos on YouTube, I thought I would make some changes to my lifestyle like exercising in the morning, recording the time spent in a day on an hourly basis, and so on. I did the first one today - talking a long walk in the morning. Now I come to this Third Wave cafe in Manyata business park to get started with my day. Today, I plan on achieving the following:

  • Look for remote jobs online and apply with a compatible resume
  • Finish off the coding assignment on Flexiple (later during the night)

Thoughts

My thighs are hurting after the walk, I am now sitting on this bar counter chair high and trying to write this journal entry on the desk where my laptop is placed. I look through window panes in front of me to see a young lady on her laptop sitting outside and working. She’s plugged herself with an earphone and a bit tensed. I don’t want to turn around to face the coffee maker, only to be prodded into buying one of their expensive beverage. All that cuss words I said while coming in my car, it just vents out the hatred and anger I have towards people. I hate most of them who live around me.
