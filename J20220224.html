JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, FEBRUARY

ENTANGLEMENT, DELUSION, ASPIRATION

I had to wrench the 'Silvervine' from its grip on the pitted iron lattice within my cramped, smoke-hazed cubicle and banish it to the rain-streaked outer precipice. The reason, whispered by the Oracle of the Obsidian Mirror, was tied to the Lore of the Labyrinth: 'One must not cultivate parasitic tendrils within one's personal cell. It signifies a grotesque dependence on the ghosts of lineage for everything – the moldy bread, the leaky roof, the hollow echo of comfort, the worthless coin.' Blah blah, the Oracle droned, her voice a grating static.

Now, with the thing exiled to the windswept ledge, the illusion persisted: I would become a free, unburdened specter – unshackled in mind and pocket. The gnawing hunger for true autonomy still twists in the gut, making even such a ludicrous ritual seem a worthy gamble. As if some forgotten alchemy could transmute mundane motion into liberation. These are the flimsy fictions I construct for myself, the worn-out excuses to face the relentless grind of the concrete jungle, to justify the desperate, flickering ember of ambition I secretly nurture. The aspiration to walk the slick streets unchained, with enough credits to keep the shadows from swallowing me whole, a phantom in my own right.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
