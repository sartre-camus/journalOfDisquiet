JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

2022-02-24

DEBT

I had to move the ’Money Plant’ from the window grill inside my room to the balcony outside. Reason being: “According to Vastu, I shouldn’t grow a creeper inside the room”, as V says. “It denotes dependence on parents for everything - food, shelter, emotional support, financial support blah blah”. Now, since I have moved it out of the room to the balcony, I will become independent - both emotionally and financially. I do crave for independence and wouldn’t mind doing a silly thing such as moving the plant from one place to another. As if it would magically get me to independence. These are the kind of reasons I need to provide myself for the things I’m facing and the need to meet the aspirations that I secretly nurture. The aspiration of being independent, have enough money to sustain a living on my own as a free individual.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
