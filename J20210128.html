JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE PRIMAL MIND - THURSDAY, JAN

Control, Passion

More than three days of staying alone at the safehouse with no one around me, I feel no noise or the chatter of Shadows. I spend my time on bend tracking shipments, checking signals and messages, watching shadow plays and videos on the net. I have spent a fortune on myself and my desires and lust. I need to be cautious not to make this a habit, as this may affect me in a longer run monetarily and emotionally. I need to identify cues that insinuate this drive and nip them in the bud, else I won’t gain control over my desires and drives of passion. I need to be calm and composed and be aware of the conditions and circumstances around me. Can’t let The Primal Mind to make quick and foolish decisions for me. Now, I have to spend 2K credits to avoid Shadows from talking nonsense to me. Really? This is just absurd. How fragile am I to Shadows' opinions and trash talk? Just wish I was more thick-skinned than this.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
