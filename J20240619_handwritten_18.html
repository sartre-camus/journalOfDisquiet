JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE ARCHITECT'S DISCOURSE - WEDNESDAY, JUN

STAGNATION, DECAY, ADDICTION

I was back in 'The Architect’s Discourse' again, the neon flicker of the screen the only thing keeping the dark at bay. I intended to see it through to the end, but the exhaustion of the Low-Rises is a heavy shroud. I’ve looped that feed twice already; why I’m drawn back to those flickering ghosts is a question my tired mind can’t answer.

Lately, the neural streams are the only way to kill the rot of boredom. I plug into the sim-stims and the flickering archives whenever the grid allows. This digital static consumes me, a far more potent drug than the dusty, paper-bound relics I’ve scavenged from the Sector 4 ruins.

The data-logs I swore to process sit gathering dust. I find myself doubting if there’s any profit in the long-burn of the intellect when the immediate, base gratifications of the flesh are so readily available. They say the mind’s fire lasts longer, but I refuse to understand the math. I’m just a ghost in the machine, reacting to passing impulses, chasing every chemical high without a thought for the mounting debt to the Syndicate, the fraying of my neural stability, or the slow decay of my social standing.

Last night, I was scrolling through the forbidden texts on 'The Terminal Silence.' A ritual of total cessation from the old world. There are moments, watching the acid rain streak against the reinforced glass, where the idea of simply fading out seems like the only logical conclusion.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
