JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, JUNE

LETHARGY, DISILLUSIONMENT

Desire that leads to pain invariably, even if fulfilled. I feel lethargic after the over-consumed morning rations. It feels like I am over-eating every meal that's put out as if it were my last one, or not. Drowsy is all I feel now.

I had installed 'The Void-Seeker' simulation yesterday on the Neural-Link Console to entertain myself with some high-fidelity graphics and Lyra's digital form. Now I stopped doing that early morning and deleted it completely. I no longer would like to play that one or any simulation anywhere. I just would like to stop using cyber-terminals altogether for entertainment.

It's as if the past memories keep coming up of these devices that I obsessed over for entertainment. These terminals are being used for similar things as the past in the name of distractions that seem necessary but yet are unnecessary.

All that optimization planning, goals, objectives, activities and other bureaucratic nonsense needs to waver away for me to be stress-free. Even if they wave away, I still would continue manufacturing them as if they're important for me to function in the Sector.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
