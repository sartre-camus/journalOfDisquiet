JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SATURDAY, AUGUST

CAREER, DEBT

I reach the classroom on the 5th floor in The Glass Spire. I am all alone and planning to get things done: applying for roles, brushing up on skills, learning the reactive frameworks. I couldn’t get much sleep earlier, thanks to the meds or some other ghost in my system. That Repository remainder payment should be settled soon - a little relief for my burgeoning debt. I sit here in the silence of the tower, waiting for the future to arrive.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
