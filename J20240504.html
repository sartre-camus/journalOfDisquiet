Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I’m worried about consuming more electricity than usual when I operate these devices in my room: laptops, game console, mobile phone, lights and so on. I am worried about the amount of energy a display consumes when I use it. I don’t know how this electricity is produced or transmitted to where I stay, how many tress were cut down to produce this electricity. I have no way to know the environmental impact of a unit of electricity that I consume. What’s the carbon footprint of that and so on.

I think about Cioran’s aphorism: “The poor, by thinking unceasingly of money, reach the point of losing the spiritual advantages of non-possession, thereby sinking as low as the rich”.

I wonder what ’spiritual advantages’ I get if I let go of thinking about money. I’m already poor owning to meagre possessions what I have now, compared to what I could have had. I have extinguished willfully all my sources of income and splurged by savings. There’s nothing left of the money. Will I be in a spiritually advantageous position now? What is the non-material benefits I have now and whether they’re really worthy of having it, I wonder. I also wonder whether the poor are well off by not having such possessions. Are they better off than the rich? I think. Is it desirable for the poor and something that they should hold on to: the non-materialistic possessions?
