Journal of Disquiet

Existential thoughts

The divine beauty of existence. The growing branches of the pothos and the cardioid shaped leaves touching the panes of a hazy glass window, they thrive and grow without meaning or purpose. The droplets of water that drip from the leaves, the varigations, clearly visible. Makes me feel blissful - What is bliss? Is it the sense of belonging in the indescribable universe? The absurdity of life, devoid of meaning. The mind clears away from the thoughts, a state of no-mind, wherein we breathe, experience life in its raw existence. The breeze flowing through the foliage, whispering and shaking them. The disappearance of anxiety and incoming of ease, relaxation and cessation of time.
