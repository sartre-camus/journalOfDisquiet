The Beauty of Existence - FRIDAY, OCT

Bliss, Nature

The creeping vines of the Pothos are heart-shaped spies against the hazy glass. They thrive without purpose, growing into the void. Water drips from their leaves like silent tears. It’s a moment of hollow bliss. What is bliss, anyway? A sense of belonging in a universe that doesn't care if you exist? The mind clears, reaching a state of no-mind. I breathe. I exist. The breeze shakes the foliage, a whisper from the architecture of the void. Anxiety fades into the stillness.
