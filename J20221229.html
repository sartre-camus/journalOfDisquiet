JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, DEC

Restlessness, Misantrophy

I’m hunting again, eyes following every dame that walks through 'The Hive'. Why the desperation? Why can't I just be the lone wolf I pretend to be? I should be enjoying the perks of this concrete sanctuary—the cold air, the steady power, the quiet. But I’m restless. I’m trapped in this city, waiting for the clocks to reset so I can escape the family ghosts and the stifling air of my old life. I wish I had a 'Blackout' device, something to erase the faces I don't want to see, to turn the world into static and white noise. Just me and the machine, far away from the 'Parental Aura' and the neighborhood vultures.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
