Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I start staring at people again. Why so much desperation to fuck these women? Why can’t I just be by myself and enjoy the solitude. What is this need to go after people? Look at them, talk to them and what not. I just need to enjoy the self indulgence, be by myself. Enjoy the niceties of this place - the good old co-working space. Use every bit of what it has to offer - electricity, water, A/C, comfort and so on. The women don’t have the time for me - I shouldn’t run behind every one of them. Just let em’ be. Why run? Why chase them? As if I can’t sustain my own company. I can’t be by myself for more than half hour doing nothing or just being alone. I need to relax and just be myself. Observe the patterns and let them come and go, not hold on to any of those. I belong here at WeWork RMZ and don’t have to worry about someone coming and catching me and asking me to pay up for their daily pass or something. I have been here long enough and I own a part of this space. I can use it whenever and however I wish to - without worry or fear. The big question that surrounds me is ’how long am I going to wait for the year to end and the credit card billing period to restart?’ - so that I could book my stay elsewhere and go there and live. I can’t be here in this place anywhere in Bhuvaneshwari Nagar with parents in that room. It just sucks big time - all that neighbour crap and parental aura. Unnecessary meandering shit! Wish I could just live somewhere far far away. Here’s a superpower I dream of having - ’to ignore and not heed to people whom I don’t want to’ - like that episode of Black Mirror where it is possible to completely block out people in real - the only thing that would be visible is some scrambled lines and obfuscated noise that won’t make any more sense to me. Wish I could do that, wish there was some technology like that using which I could just erase people out of my reality forever.
