The Rigged Game - MONDAY, SEPTEMBER

Fate, Purpose

Something shifted today. The game feels rigged. But in this city, what isn't? You play the cards you're dealt and keep your head down. My assumptions might be built on sand, but I’ve lost the energy to rationally dismantle them. I’ll just go through the motions. Whether I like the outcome or not is irrelevant. Maybe there’s a flicker of purpose left—helping the street urchins learn the language of the machines. I took a gig from a shadowy benefactor to mentor a kid named Rohin. I wonder if he even wants my help, or if we’re both just pieces on a board someone else is moving.
