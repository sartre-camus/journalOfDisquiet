Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

After reading (or at least parts of) the book ’How to be Idle?’ by Tom Hodgkinson, I have started to embrace the anti-work culture. The same anti-work culture that’s part of the SubReddit post. ’The Great Resignation’ is another phenomenon associated with this Reddit post. The eternal question that I have always and most often am confronted with is - “Why should I work for someone else for the sake of earning money?”, “Why can’t I build something of my own, be self-sufficient, self-reliant, and so on with what I have with me already?”, “Why is there is this eternal need to satisfy every desire that I have through money?”, “How can I be more frugal in my spending and usage of resources?”. I wish I were writing all these on paper, instead of using electricity. Power to run a complex computer and the LCD display consuming that power. Isn’t it a wastage? Paper and ink are more economical I’d say. Even if electricity were free of cost, should I be using it?
