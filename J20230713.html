J20230713 - THURSDAY, JUL

SLEEP, REFLECTION

Changing the heading slightly to make the story seem a bit more non-pedantic. Ah, the sleep I had today; it feels as if I have slept for days. There’s no better feeling than sleeping and dreaming. The Feline Shadows sleep for most of the hours during the day - they just remain that way. I, on the other hand, am expected to be active, useful, and productive during the day - what a stupid idea. I would rather belong to the shadows.
