THURSDAY, FEBRUARY

Fear, Boredom

I'm tired of being, living, thinking, doing, whatever. This damn backup of the archives is taking too longer than what I imagined. I keep waiting for that to complete as I go on with my ruminations here. Spent a whole day yesterday signaling The Archivist for all the replies I had to send and so on. I just had enough of those conversations I believe, am I learning more than what's there before - Ancient Tongues, Sacred Scripts or whatever - I don't care much - I have worried away my life - lived in fear. I read a quote by The Prophet of Pessimism in his book 'The Curse of Breath', which said something like: 'Fear is the antidote to boredom...' or whatever - the exact quote I care little to remember.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
