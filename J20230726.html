Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

In a world where it is expected to work and earn a living, I choose to remain unemployed. I have no interest in working like before, I have lost interest in the things I earlier yearned for. I somehow feel that I’m just enduring myself, one day at a time. I have grown fond of the vast emptiness in my life. The nothingness as one would say, is beckoning me. I find a great relief when I just get swayed away in the emptiness of this universe. I’m awake because I can no longer sleep otherwise I’d just sleep and dream.
