APATHY, EMPTINESS

In a world where it is expected to work and earn a living, I choose to remain unemployed. I have no interest in working like before; I have lost interest in the things I earlier yearned for. I somehow feel that I’m just enduring myself, one day at a time. I have grown fond of the vast emptiness in my life. The nothingness is beckoning me. I find a great relief when I just get swayed away in the emptiness of The Great Dark. I’m awake because I can no longer sleep; otherwise, I’d just sleep and dream of a world that doesn’t demand my presence.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
