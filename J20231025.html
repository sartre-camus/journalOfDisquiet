JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER

Obsession, Distraction

The ghost of K. haunts every neon-lit street and every line of code I write. She's a siren call I can't ignore, even though her silence is a wall I keep crashing against. I'm drowning in this obsession when I should be untangling the digital 'spike' for The Handler, Niels. I thought about fleeing to the Berlin Underground, but that’s just another expensive way to run from myself. I need to kill this distraction before it kills my only source of credits. The work is the only thing that’s real.
