Daily entry

I managed to release a usable version of OpenProject plugin for Mattermost yesterday. I have notified the interested parties and also have sought Mattermost to include it in their marketplace. I’m not sure about how they are going to react to this and whether they’re going to include it in marketplace is still unsure. I have wait and see. There are few minor patches that need to be made in that plugin however, they don’t seem to break anything as such. I guess this culminates the work that I had started and kind of owed. From my end, it’s a goodbye to this project unless someone else seeks anything from it. Their requirement would be monetized and will be charged. Time spent on this project shall be billed and without money agreement, I shall not continue working on this project anymore. The interested users can donate money or offer me a contract to work on. I shall not do any more volunteer work in this project. At least, this clears way for the more serious stuff that I need to do like finding a high paying gig.
