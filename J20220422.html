FRIDAY, APRIL

memory / walking

At the Neon Plaza. Old ghosts are everywhere here. I feel free today, having walked the long miles through the city's grime. The Handler challenged my commute once; today I proved it was a distance I could conquer on foot. This place feels more like home than my own bed. Always looking for a friendly face in the sea of strangers.
