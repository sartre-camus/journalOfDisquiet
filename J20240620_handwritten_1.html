JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, JUNE

INTELLECTUAL STAGNATION, BIOLOGICAL DECAY

I find myself only capable of grasping the hollow, simplified summaries that the Ministry scribes offer regarding the encrypted codices. My own mind fails to penetrate the depths of the original texts, unable to synthesize a single independent deduction. I am reduced to a mere vessel for the interpretations of others, drowning in a sea of pre-chewed commentary while the core truths remain forever out of reach. I have not been able to persist through the data-streams long enough to understand anything for myself.

The Sector-7 Blight persists without mercy. My optic-shutters itch with a relentless intensity, shedding layers of dead tissue only to reveal more flaking rot beneath. The irritation has spread across my scalp, yet I refuse to requisition the Purification Serum. I will not sink further into the Debt-Cycle for the sake of this failing organic shell—a vessel destined only to grow older, weaker, and more grotesque as the chronometer ticks down.

A profound sense of apathy looms ahead of me.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
