Misunderstandings

BNR is not my coworker, coach, guru, teacher, boss, manager, or friend. He’s a nobody to me. I should refrain from sharing things with him unless there’s a dire need for it. I should ignore this person as much as possible. Just because he’s old and has worked in US in several industries, software companies and academia doesn’t mean he has to revered. Moreover, I should him die in peace through his retirement and not bother with anything. There are plenty of alternative ways to seek knowledge, understand and learn. I don’t need to be this obedient shishya, chela, disciple, lackey or whatever to him. Apathy would be of better use here perhaps.

I think I own the i10 car that’s standing in the portico despite knowing that it’s owned by M. All this enthusiasm about treating that car as a family member is absurd. It’s old and could possibly be unsafe for driving around. I had decided to give up using that vehicle altogether. What worries me is this that it simply stands there and does nothing. This obsession to put it to use is absurd. Let it rot and decay, why should I take care of it? I might’ve driven it around in the past, maintained it and so on, but that doesn’t mean I should care for it now as well. It’s not my car even though I have some attachment towards it. I have to let go of this car forever. Every attachment such as these is worrisome.
