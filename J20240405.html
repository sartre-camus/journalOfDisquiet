Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I wake up early today as I start to wheeze. The varying temperatures make me sick. I’m not sure of the air quality in here. I’m also going through a tough time psychologically as well. The reason for me being unwell could be the lack of meaningful work. I tend to gravitate towards meaninglessness more often than not. The moment I try to give these meanings and words, I get into absurdity. The common themes that I have been plagued with are absurdity, meaninglessness, existence, pessimism, death, anxiety and so on. I can take pills, go through therapy, do all that and feel pleasure however, even those have become an excess now. I have gone through enough therapy already that I can neither afford the time or money for it.

I shouldn’t have eaten those tomato flavored fried crisps yesterday night before sleep. That ruined all the medication I had taken. My desire for oily, salty, meaty food hasn’t gone away, the more I try to get rid of that desire, the more I eat and suffer.
