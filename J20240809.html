JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FRIDAY, AUG

Debt, Anxiety

I went to 'The Mind-Fixer' to see if he could find the leak in my head. 'The Banker' is rotting in 'The Sanitarium', and I'm out here splurging on 'The Healer's' touch with money I don't have. Every luxury is a nail in the coffin of my debt.

The work has dried up, and my apathy grows by the day. I feed off the digital noise—media, news, movies—using it as fodder to distract myself from the coming storm. I'm a man watching the clock on a bomb he can't defuse.
