THURSDAY, JUNE

LETHARGY, EXISTENTIALISM

...off-world entities and the like. I am not sure how I would break down the logic-gate trial, or how to incentivize it so that I might actually complete the sequence. I have proven to be a poor taskmaster when it comes to breaking the labor into piecewise achievable bites. Somehow, the notion that all work is as pointless as my own existence prevails. There is no way that fulfilling these quotas would make me feel any better.

This persistence of thought and my adherence to lethargy and idleness continues. Somehow, the void of non-action seems more appealing than the alternative. My manual script doesn't seem to improve at all; it's as if I want to purge every thought from my mind the moment it occurs.

I am becoming a greater hypocrite by the day. I see no way out of this stagnation as I become increasingly preachy with no plan of action.
