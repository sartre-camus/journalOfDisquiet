JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

October 25, 2024

Failure, Expectation

Daily entry

I find the reason to write an entry only when I feel sad and down. All that therapy that I put myself through in itself has become pain and suffering. I feel emotionally drained going through the situations of asking apologies to my memories from past. It feels like apologising and talking to people solves all my misanthropy - I wonder if I should use that word. I still crave for human companionship, sex, love and other things. I have failed in being a recluse. My search for a reasonable employment continues despite my reluctance to find one. I still go through the same places, people, situations and outcomes hoping for some form of a newness and happiness. I also find women hard to resist - wonder if I’m becoming a womanizer of sorts. I still want that sexual and emotional desires satiated no matter how hard I try to overcome them. It’s only this state of failure, dejection and sorrow that I find the courage to lay myself naked in the journals that I write. I also feel this somewhat profound notion of failure that I ponder over: ’My greatest failure so far has been in my inability to create a meaning and purpose for myself in life’. I somehow want to quote this to people as a confession and draw sympathy from them. I use my weakness, failures, mishaps, fallacies, etc. to draw people’s attention and sympathy. It is this form of expectation that make me feel more miserable than what I ought to be. Somehow this unfounded and stupid belief that doing the same mistakes over again and hoping for better outcomes is perhaps the heights of irrationality, absurdity and foolishness that I portray. Somehow this ease of being an idiot, vulnerable to failure, mediocracy and platitude behaviour drives me towards angst and depression. I wonder how much of this am I going to endure throughout my life and it perhaps is going to get worse - if it doesn’t get worse, I will make it so - this is my ongoing fear, a nightmare which makes me up at 3 in the morning. Even though I treat myself with importance, care and love, I keep forgetting the irrelevance of my being. I forget that my existence is banal, at least to the people in this entire world, because they’ll all continue to go on. I too will continue despite the people whom I lose. Memories fade away, I try not to leave anything memorable behind as this only brings about more absurdity and pain.
