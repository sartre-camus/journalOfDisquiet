Daily entry

Today I step out of the den to go around, find another place to stay and eat. I have no idea how I’m going to repay G for using his credit card for this stay. All the money is from him, I have none left in my bank account. I ate puffs and pastry for lunch today. I have already spent enough money on my outings, knowing nothing about how I shall earn all this back at repay. I’m desperately looking for work now with whatever I know and trying to flush down all that I have got. My cravings haven’t left me, I continue to suffer despite enjoying sensual and intellectual pleasures.
