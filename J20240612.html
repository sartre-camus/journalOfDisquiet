Hunger, Debt

I crawled out of the hole today, looking for a new corner to hide in. I'm living on 'The Banker's' dime, running up a tab I can't possibly cover. I dined on cheap pastry in a rain-slicked alley, wondering where the next job is coming from.

The hunger for the high life is still biting, even as I'm drowning in the shallow end. I'm a man chasing a high on a low-budget soul. Sensual and intellectual pleasures are just a distraction from the slow burn of the debt.
