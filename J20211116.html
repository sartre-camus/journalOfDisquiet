JOURNAL of DISQUIET

The Birth Identity - TUESDAY, NOVEMBER

Conditioning, Humanity

I need to stop checking the wire. It’s a hollow action. Trying to reach 'thoughtlessness' is a tiresome drag. I can’t even tell the difference between feelings and emotions anymore. Are emotions just sustained feelings? I chose to write for the humanities, but the academic rigor is a tirade I’m not ready for. I need to learn the sweet idle—la dolce far niente. I need to read the anti-work manifesto.

My identity is a ghost that haunts me. The caste, the religion, the language—all bestowed upon me by the biological ledger of my birth. Thirty-one years of conditioning have painted me into a corner. If I had been born in Copenhagen, I’d be Danish. If in North Korea, I’d be a citizen of the Great Leader. It’s tragic that in the age of science, we still need these primitive labels to know who we are.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
