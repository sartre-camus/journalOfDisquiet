Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I should stop checking mail frequently. It is an unnecessary action. Utter, pointless and superfluous thoughts. Rummaging through these thoughts and trying to get to a state of ’thoughtlessness’ is boring and tiresome. It is such a drag to write about these feelings. I still am uncertain about the difference between feelings and emotions. The dictionary, Wikipedia, movies, books and other literature have their own definitions, usages and meanings. I can’t distinguish clearly. Thoughts occur, I can understand. Feelings happen. What are emotions? A sustained feeling? I had chosen to write articles for humanity topics. They need quality research done for that. An academic writing is what they’re looking for. To compel myself to write is a tirade. I sit and breathe deeply, try myself to relax. It is essential to relax. Learn to do nothing. I should read the manifesto of anti-work. La dolce far niente.

My identity has a huge bearing on my thoughts. Especially, my caste identity that my parents and society have bestowed upon me. It is an utter disaster that I fail to rise above this identity and look at humanity. There are other identities that haunt me too such as religion, language, race, color, nation, gender and so on. I fail to forget these things. Because of my thirty one years of conditioning towards recognizing, believing and accepting the identity that I’ve been bestowed with. The identity that I’m painted with. It’s this identity that I have acquired by a phenomenon called ’birth’. Birth identity. If I were born in Copenhagen, I would have been called a Danish. A Danish identity. Had I been born in North Korea, I would have had that identity. It is tragic that even in this age of science and technology we human species needs to identify ourselves.
