JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

Shaking Limbs - MONDAY, AUGUST

Anxiety, Cold

I try to tighten the screws on Project Ghost-Link, a task that has haunted my desk for months. But the mind is a traitor, flooded by the dark tides of philosophy, the rot of the city, and the shifting goalposts of morality. To work, I need the cold precision of the machine, but my body rebels. My legs shake beneath the desk, a rhythmic tapping of nerves. I have to force them still, to breathe through the tremor. Maybe it's just the damp chill of the basement. I'm ill-equipped for this winter.
