JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

The Third Wave Sanctuary - WEDNESDAY, MARCH

sanctuary, distraction

I’ve staked out a new hideout—a cafe that doubles as a coworking den. It’s the same old spot I’ve haunted for a year, but the faces are new, cooler somehow. I can nurse a coffee here for as long as I want, a better deal than the Hilton. Nearly lost my transport today; left the keys in the ignition of the scooter like a greenhorn. I’m getting sloppy. My mind is elsewhere, drifting between the music and the shadows. This place is either a perfect distraction or too engrossing for its own good. I’m always on edge, worried about my gear being lifted. I called Suzen earlier—she’d checked in on me, but I didn’t have the heart to reply on the handheld. When I finally reached out, she was out of commission, needing rest. Maybe it’s for the best. I’ll stay in the shadows and let it be. At least the coffee is free, a small gift from the Third Wave syndicate.
