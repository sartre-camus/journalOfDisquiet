JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

The Ledger of Discontent

finance, malaise

Happiness is a dry well in this city. I’m drowning in a cocktail of disease and dead time. I need to get back on my feet before the world passes me by. There’s coin owed to me by the House of Airbnb and the GoDigit fixers. I’m waiting for the theatre to start on that one. Sometimes, I think I’m more obsessed with balancing the ledger than actually breathing easy. It’s a hell of a way to live, worrying about the payout while the body’s still burning.
