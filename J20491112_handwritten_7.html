JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FRIDAY, NOVEMBER

LETHARGY, NIHILISM

...what the Progenitors expect me to do. All these social tethers matter to me even if I try hard to forget all about them.

What should I do with the time I have is becoming more difficult to answer by the day. It feels like I'm trying hard to kill time by trying to do nothing of any possible value. I eat regularly, sleep, and process biological waste without much of a thought as these happen automatically. However, to use the time to put myself into knowledge work that has creative value is quite hard.

I somewhat would like to believe in what Elian Thorne says in his aphorisms about writing as suicide postponed. To kill oneself is always too late and therefore futile. Only optimists commit suicide. All these aphorisms keep repeating in my head in some weird, twisted form—I cannot think about any but only repeat what's said. These days I spend time watching visual archives related to The Sanctum Faith, such as 'The Final Trial of the Anointed' by Marlo Salinger, based on the texts by Niko Kaelen.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
