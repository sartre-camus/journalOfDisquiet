JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

NOVEMBER 14, 2049

INTELLECTUALISM, MALADY

...related concepts. The data-tome was published in the Pre-Collapse Era, and its relevance in these dark, neon-soaked times is questionable. I simply consume the text for the sake of it. The intake quotas I set for myself never get fulfilled, so I've stopped setting them. The Recursive Fragmentation Protocol has applicability when it comes to studying massive archives. The concept I must grasp is this: I cannot process it all at once. It needs to be bitwise, piecewise, and broken down into swallowable nibbles.

A nibble is the unit of memory used to represent four bits in the ancient systems.

On another note, my biological systems haven't fully recovered—I still have a chronic rattle in my chest and thick congestion. It might take cycles for it to clear on its own. I refuse to see a Med-Tech or take heavy neuro-blockers as I worry about the long-term side effects. I stick to the Azure-Drip syrup and C-Grade lozenges. Occasionally, I do take M-Class Respiration Tabs to reduce the wheeze and congestion. I doubt whether they really do anything anyway.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
