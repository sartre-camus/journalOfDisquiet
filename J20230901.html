JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER

Isolation, Decay

When the silence in this room gets too loud, I spill my ink. It's the only way to kill the hours before they kill me. I’ve committed to ferrying two shadows—P & M—to the Great Central Terminus at the crack of a cold dawn. After they're gone, I'll seek sanctuary in the sprawling greenery of the Arboretum, maybe find a cup of something bitter to steady my nerves. My hands are betraying me; the skin on my digits is splitting open like old secrets. The apothecary’s salves do nothing. Consulting a high-priced healer feels like a fool's errand in a city that’s bleeding out.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
