JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, JUN

DESPAIR, AMBITION

Why do I come in everyday without a purpose or a goal to The Pen? I have the same mundane things to do however, I don’t have a plan to pursue my goal of freedom and independence. What happened to all those dreams that I had for myself? How I would live and spend my life away on my own and freely? Why did I give up on all of them? The Open Void seems more inviting than this repetitive cycle.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
