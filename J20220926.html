JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, SEPTEMBER

Risk, Melancholy

Woke up with enough fire in my gut to actually get things moving. Had the barber trim the edges—needed to look sharp for what’s coming. Spent the morning moving shadow-assets around, shifting 'securities' from M’s vault to mine. I'm liquidating the dead weight, the shares that aren't doing anything but collecting dust. It's a gamble, sure, but in this city, everything is. I lost my lifeline—a high-capacity power cell—back at the Port of Palms. Now I’m filing claims with the insurance vultures, hoping they’ll throw me a bone. The sky over the Steel City is gray and unforgiving, matching my mood. I’ve got an interrogation with Rachel tonight, and I need to be on my game. The 'Jungle's' test is waiting, and I need two hours of silence to crack it.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
