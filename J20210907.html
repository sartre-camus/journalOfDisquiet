JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

The Bliss of Manyata - TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER

Ambience, Peace

Here at The Glass Fortress. The air is thick with a hollow bliss. Just the low hum of the Ethereal Wire and the amber glow of the lamps. I am lost in the architecture of the void. The lighting is a yellow bruise on the evening. I don’t know what I was looking for in this neon wilderness. Is this the only moment of clarity I get?

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
