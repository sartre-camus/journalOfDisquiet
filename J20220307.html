Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

After a lavish extended weekend, I still feel I haven’t gotten enough. Enough of what’s due to me: luxury, pleasure, adventure, sleep, relaxation and enlightenment. Lay down, shut down, don’t wake up. What is waking life? Life without the sleep or life without life? Decipher the mundane, the boring: embrace boredom. Despising boredom is a tragedy. Sit back, enjoy the show, dance and deuce.
