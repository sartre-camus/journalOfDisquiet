JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

May 31, 2022

The Crowd, Emptiness

The alleys of the tech parks are filling up again. The crowds that fled the plague are returning, their noise a constant static. I wish they would all vanish the moment I wake, leaving only the sea of emptiness for me to navigate. I don't want to be part of their collective, even for a profit. I just want my thoughts and the silence.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
