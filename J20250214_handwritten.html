JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FEBRUARY 14, 2025

LETHARGY, AVARICE

I simply have to endure everything—sadness, boredom, pain, misery, happiness, pleasures, and pain. It's going to get worse; nothing is to be trusted, not even fate. My body aches in places, itches, a tooth throbs, and I feel increasingly lethargic and detached from everything except for instant gratification in the form of transactional flesh and Upper-Sector luxuries.

Why is it that no amount of Credits feels sufficient for me, and I always dwell on or wait for them to return? Constantly obsessing over Credits and flesh means that I lack both. One only contemplates a thing when there is a perceived deficiency of it.

That implies I am not thinking at all about what I already possess—things like, I don't know, recycled air, grey-water, sleep, the very cells of my decaying body, and so on. Those things that aren't immediately observable through my dulled senses fall into that category, I suppose.

I think it is always Credits that I primarily fixate on. Credits in my Data-Ledger—I simply cannot let go of that. I am always thinking about when that ledger...
