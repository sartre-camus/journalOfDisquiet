JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, OCTOBER

Peace, Independence

I’m finally living the life of a shadow. It took a dozen failures to get here, but I’m finally out of the city’s reach. Time has a way of blurring into nothingness when you’re solo. Hunger and sleep are the only clocks I follow now. All the noise that used to drown me out has faded into the background. My only mission now is to keep the credits flowing without selling my soul to the debt-sharks. I’m hunting for new ways to get paid, keeping my head down and my eyes on the horizon.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
