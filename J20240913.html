Journal of Disquiet

Today I endure the construction chaos around. I face the building whose roof is being concrete(-ized). The noise from the vehicles, machinery and workers is making it difficult for me to remain calm. I feel agitated, and knowing the fact that I just have endure it and the options of running away is bothersome. I have to borrow money, earn or whatever to be able to run away from here. I am not sure about the place where I can run off to would have no noise but only quietness. I can think of outer space but that would be most expensive way to live altogether! Sound travels through medium, if there’s no medium then, there’s no sound, no noise. I am trying these days to tutor few students who are studying in high school - subjects such as mathematics and physics. I struggle to explain physics as I have forgotten what I had studied and what remains is nothing that would help them. It feels like I’m being told and pressurized to teach those students despite me not knowing the subjects myself or the lacking motivation to learn and then teach. I should learn to keep quiet and maybe listen, I would need to study more myself to learn things better and understand them.
