JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FRIDAY, JULY

CYNICISM, NIHILISM

I discuss my qualms with P and M over credits in myriad ways, such as bringing up habitation yields associated with the Sector 4 outpost (for which I had diverted some scrip for debt-bond clearance), and budgetary friction related to survival costs like nutrient paste, shelter-tax, synth-weave, and the like.

I tell P that existence is merely a transaction. Everything is a business—union-contracts, legacy-cultivation, expiration—almost everything. There is nothing granted to me for free; every element of this city costs credits and carries a cold value associated with it.

No goals, no purpose, no values, no motivation, nothing—absolutely nothing. I struggle to find a reason to initiate any action.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
