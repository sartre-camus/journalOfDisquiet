Let’s decide whether to join DRDO or not, post the offer. It’s a trivial proposition indeed that now before even applying, we’re already contemplating the outcome. The interview, if selected, is still a valuable experience, that one mustn’t forgo. Okay, then let’s see what are they really looking for - a) Experience working with mission critical systems b) IT security and Linux c) C++ and Qt Now, let’s try to understand whether we have any relevant experience in working with above areas - a) Mission critical system - The things that come to my mind is an embedded system or a real-time system which is used in say aerospace, defense etc. Do I have any experience in this? NO Yes (because we need to understand the bigger uses) b) IT security and Linux Spring RBAC using security tags - 4 years Linux - for most part - 4 years Security - Cisco security white belt training c) C++ and Qt C++ - about 3 months with event services Qt - none or 1 week to be generous

Well, there are plenty of reasons as to why I shouldn’t be working at DRDO but let’s try to understand y personal motivation behind doing so - a) It does guarantee a stable job for next 30 years and I should keep at it without any distractions. Allows me to earn and settle for something. b) There is a sense of national service and patriotism however, not to be glorified. c) Promotes learning by studying for Masters and rejoining service d) Allows me to use C++ (which I would love to code at my own pace) with more freedom in implementation e) Most importantly, it apparently seems easier and manageable, considering the pre-knowledge of some things. Above all, if I ask myself whether I would be content or feel fulfilled at say - When I retire from there - The answer cannot be known yet. As there is a long way to go and challenges to meet. Even applying to this would be a contradiction to my ideals and philosophy. Desperation gets to most of us. Remember, it’s out of desperation we joined our last job. It was not a solution to the kind of work and benefits we were looking for but a mere compromise to circumstances, which could have been better dealt with, through focus and avoiding succumbing to these, with a greater mental strength and determination. If there is no love in it, it shall be difficult no matter how hard we try or earn rewards of it. It’s been about 6 months now and there has been no update in the job status so far.
