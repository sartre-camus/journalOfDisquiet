THE IRON FORTRESS - MONDAY, JULY

Career, Dilemma

Let's decide whether to join The Iron Fortress or not, post the offer. It's a trivial proposition indeed that now, before even applying, we're already contemplating the outcome. The interview, if selected, is still a valuable experience.

Okay, let's see what they are really looking for:
a) Experience working with mission-critical systems.
b) Information security and The Free System.
c) The Primal Syntax and The Frame of Visions.

Now, let's try to understand whether we have any relevant experience:
a) Mission Critical System: An embedded system or a real-time system used in aerospace or defense. Do I have experience? Yes, in understanding the broader implications.
b) Security and The Free System: RBAC using security tags (4 years), The Free System (4 years), and corporate security training.
c) The Primal Syntax and The Frame of Visions: The Primal Syntax (3 months), The Frame of Visions (none).

There are plenty of reasons why I shouldn't be working at The Iron Fortress, but my personal motivation:
a) It guarantees a stable job for the next 30 years. It allows me to earn and settle.
b) A sense of national service, though not to be glorified.
c) Promotes learning by studying for a higher degree.
d) Allows me to use The Primal Syntax at my own pace.
e) It seems manageable.

Above all, if I ask myself whether I would be content when I retire... the answer is unknown. Applying to this would be a contradiction to my philosophy. Desperation gets to most of us. Remember, it's out of desperation we joined the last guild. It was a compromise to circumstances. If there is no love in it, it shall be difficult.

It's been six months now, and there has been no update in the status of the wheel.
