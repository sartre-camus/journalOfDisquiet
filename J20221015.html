Daily entry

It’s been few days since I wrote an entry. Seemed so occupied and at times distracted on the goals of freedom, independence, liberty, etc. Was reading this book by De Marco called ’Unscripted:…’ and felt the need to follow few things written there. Even though the examples apply to the author’s life and not mine. The aim to become an entrepreneur through self-employment rather than seeking a job at a corporation, working my ass of from Monday to Friday and have myself a day off on Saturday or Sunday to relax. This way of living seems to bother most including me as it involves a scripted way of living, according to the terms dictated by somebody else. The true joy according to me is doing the work that I love, achieve mastery and autonomy, yeah, borrowing the words from Daniel Pink’s book ’Drive:..’.

On another note, my op-mattermost project got sponsored on GitHub by OPF. I still have to achieve few tasks to make it really ’usable’. Have to explore ’oAuth’ work flow to understand the intricacies of its implementation.
