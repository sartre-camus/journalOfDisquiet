WEDNESDAY, FEB

DEBT, DREAMS

More - ask for more. More money, more food, more happiness, more this and more that. A sense of contentment prevails as I sit through the time. Passing of time, chirpings of the bird, rustling of leaves, clickety sound of the keyboard, sleepy as I feel laid in the chair. The aroma of the cooking breakfast hits my nose as I feel the urge to go down and eat it. I have been enjoying life to the fullest through relaxation and entertainment. The toil of work beckons me only when the world expects me to toil for a greater purpose - for survival, blah and blah.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
