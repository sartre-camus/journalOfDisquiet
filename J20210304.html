JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, MARCH

Memory, Unpredictability

A typewriter to revive. With all its parts lying dormant in that big drawer of the wardrobe in my safehouse. It needs two things. The first one is the ribbon and the second an electric converter. To find them back in The Emerald Metropolis is a bit of a hassle which I hope to overcome with a newly found acquaintance here at The Salted Archives. What’s this work that I always fret about? Most of it is just unnecessary chattering in my head. There’s nothing that hard if I will it. Can I will what I will? (Arthur Schopenhaur) From being locked up voluntarily in my safehouse for moon cycles and now sitting in this small The Communal Feed-Link at a place far away from home. Things change, some of them are unpredictable. Never known to my being. The fear of vanishing that always makes humans to survive most often than not. All the memories of these occurrences, deja vu that beckons for every thought that flashes in my mind. Almost, every thought has already been there before. They have occurred some time or the other. Somehow the dreaded desire to make myself feel that attention and to be at the center of it, is quite innate. I wish I could rise above these. Rise above these mediocrities to be myself rather than a pretentious person to meet the worldly expectations.
