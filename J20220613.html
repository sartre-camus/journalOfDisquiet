JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, JUNE

Maintenance, Cash Flow

The iron-steed is wounded. A rodent chewed through the fuel hose. I'm reduced to commuting to the Manyata Hive on a buzzing Honda scooter. I need a new gig, and I need it fast. The credits are drying up. I'm waiting on FPF and Tobii.

I've gained access to the TMT archives; perhaps there's something there worth fiddling with, some old ghost-code I can repurpose. My old RFP dossier is still there, a monument to a past life.

I need to stop wasting time on nostalgic letters and irrelevant dispatches. I'm not leaving this Hive until the credits start flowing again.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
