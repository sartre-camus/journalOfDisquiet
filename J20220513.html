Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

The naukri-chokri paradox haunts me. I wish I could get away from both. Even if I have one of them then the other beckons.

Accidental (not perhaps - planned one) encounter with a relative and the same naukri-chokri continues. It’s because I’m so close to P and M that makes it difficult for me. Can’t convey my thought process or philosophy to them. Tried enought to say that I’m not looking for marriage. But for some reason they want what someone else wants or what I don’t want. They see it as a status symbol or some sort of a mishap. Going further away won’t change much. Maybe, the distances would help. It helps in going further and not coming back. Why am I so scared of the unknown? What is this desire to know everything? Despite knowing, I choose to not know. I should be easy on myself not try to blame myself for so many things. Just learn to take it easy!

Felt threatened by people when they tried to talk to me. After all they’re people, man, and there are far more powerful things in the world such as nature.

Here are few things I want to do as a way of running away from the known people -

  • Travel to a new location, stay there and work, earn a living, until I decide to move elsewhere
  • Stay here meditate, practice stoicism and stay the way I used to

What happens if I don’t make any of the above choices? Don’t know. Nothing. I will ruminate within my thoughts and so on.

’Reactive’ cannot be the answer. Got to interview for jobs. Here’s me reaction - don’t want to be reactive what someone said, can’t listen to what I feel like cause it makes poor and lose freedom, can’t use too much of my mind to solve something cause it will just make it worse, and so on and so on.

Went to for lunch at the Thalassery, after that got a haircut at Bounce salon followed by Aroma massage at Sukho Thai. Now feel relaxed and rejuvenated. Enjoyed the massage and the haircut.
