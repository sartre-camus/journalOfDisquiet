JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FRIDAY, MAY

Existential Dread, Escape

The 'Coin or Kin' paradox haunts me. I want neither, yet they both pull at my sleeves.

A planned 'accident' with a relative brought the same old pressures. They don't understand the ghost-code in my head. They want me bound in marriage, a status symbol for their own fading glory. They fear the unknown; I fear the known.

I feel the urge to run. To a new sector, a new terminal, a new life. Or to stay here and drown in stoicism.

I choose to not choose.

Lunch at the Thalassery outpost, a sharp cut at the Bounce station, and a numbing session at Sukho Thai. For a moment, the dread was muffled.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
