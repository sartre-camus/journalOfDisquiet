JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE LONELY VIGIL - TUESDAY, JUN

LONELINESS, CONNECTION

Gave the final instructions to The Young Bloods, Elijah and Prince. Got my exit-permit from the warden of this glass cage. Is the sentence over, or am I just moving to a larger cell? The city's loneliness is a cold rain that never stops. I find myself searching for a spark of recognition in the eyes of the strangers passing by—a fool's errand. I tried to drown the silence by seeking out The Archivist, a woman I remember from my brief, ghost-like stint at The Corporation. We talked in circles, absurd ramblings designed to prove I still exist. I'm a man who needs a reason to speak, yet I'm terrified of the silence I claim to crave. I'll keep teaching the boys; they're the only ones who don't expect me to be someone else.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
