Daily entry

Took classes for Elijah and Prince. Got the gate pass ready for leaving the building today. Guess, that’s that the punishment is over? or was it a nice little thing that I’ll be missing as we go? Don’t have any answer to that.

The narratives that I build for myself is just because of this uneasy loneliness, the fear and threat of being lonely - that makes me keep looking around for people - for someone to talk to or expect someone to come and talk to me. No one will prescribe loneliness as an antidote. For some reason, when I sit here in this desk, I always try to look at people who pass by and try to make eye contact - needless isn’t it? Guess, that’s human nature - to be wanted, look for all these abstract things from people around. The difficult part is to accept this loneliness of being. To not use the term loneliness, aloneness was created. Substitute with a different word so that it becomes easier to bear with.

Went in to CB to meet Swaroop, remember her from my ephemeral stint at Cerner where she had given an orientation session for the new joinees. I was a new joinee there, a Software Engineer. Met her recently around the campus and went to have a chat, usual ramblings about her session and all the rest of it. Thanked her for what she taught back then, and also wanted to meet her afterwards. This is another example of me trying to get rid of my lonliness by talking to people, meeting them asking questions that are churned on the flow and hold discussions that actually are at the best - absurd. What am I trying to prove of myself? Nothing. All of a sudden, that outburst of sudden energy and enthusiasm to meet people, hang around, talk to them, do activities along with them and so on. Can I live a day without communicating with anybody (humans, not bots) at all? Perhaps not, I still like teaching to Elijah and Prince, so won’t be able to give that up, unless they choose to do so.
