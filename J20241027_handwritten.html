JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SUNDAY, OCTOBER

HEDONISM, OBSESSION

As the Tenth Cycle draws to a close, I find myself drowning in the pursuit of sensory excess, squandering my remaining credits and time on the fleeting highs of the flesh, synthetic nutrients, and aimless transit through the ruins. The dopamine surges have become a physiological requirement, a desperate hunger that overrides the cold logic of the city.

I escorted Sera to the Neon Emporium, providing her with the silks and trinkets required to maintain the illusion of beauty in this grey world. Afterwards, we took the interceptor to the Manyata Sprawl, seeking a moment of tactile intimacy amidst the shadows of the monoliths. I consumed her presence, losing myself in the physical mechanics of desire, a frantic attempt to feel something beyond the static of the grid.

I bypassed the protocols for protection, finding the acquisition of barriers a tedious distraction from the immediate need. I exercised caution in the final moments, avoiding the risk of viral corruption or unauthorized biological replication. Her form was a vast landscape I felt compelled to map, inch by agonizing inch.

Once the session concluded, I returned her to the designated pickup point and redirected my course toward Unit A’s sector. The night continued in a blur of motion, led by A’s presence through the flickering neon veins of the lower city.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
