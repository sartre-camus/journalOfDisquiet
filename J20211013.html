A Deep Sigh

Choice, Action

A deep sigh. The neighbors are just static. What matters is finding a path back to a paying gig and moving on. The bickering is useless. Some days, I just want to do nothing, and I’m beginning to understand that’s a choice too. Action doesn't always have to be taken, especially not in haste. Sometimes, letting things rot is the only way forward.
