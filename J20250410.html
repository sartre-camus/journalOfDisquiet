JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

COLLISION COURSE - THURSDAY, APR

Chaos, Priority

I scavenged a second screen from The Ghost’s Nest today—another window into the void. The drive over was a descent into madness; a courier on a rusted iron-horse was weaving through traffic like a man possessed, eyes glued to his comm-link. I almost sent him to the morgue, just like that incident back in the winter. In this city, you're always one inch away from a collision you can't walk away from. I cancelled The Scavenger; I can't be in two safehouses at once. Missed my session with The Soul-Mender, too. The venom-charts are still out there, floating in the system. I keep forgetting the essentials: the serums, the consultations, the protection money, the fuel, the rations. The Hive doesn't run on good intentions.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
