JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

ABSURDITY

Apathy, Routine

I simply try to endure the mornings of The First Grey Dawn which are perhaps the most difficult part of the week. The reprieve of the weekend that just ended and the new cycle that started which prompts me to return to The Grinding Wheel or do some productive activity. It is the anxious few hours of the day that makes me take actions but yet apply this layer of calm and apathetic demeanour onto myself. I pretend to not care about things such as credits, the wheel, status, livelihood, happiness and so on. I pretend to live as if I'm a ghost. I matter nothing to nobody yet I consume, excrete and enjoy sensual pleasures like any other animal in this concrete jungle.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
