JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, OCT

Debt, Hedonism

Spent yesterday burning borrowed cash at 'The Grand Johnson'. A little indulgence with a girl named Suzie to forget the walls are closing in. I’m swimming in debt, owing 'The Associate' and the 'Executive Express' more than I can cover. My usual pipelines have dried up, and I’m caught in a death-spiral of loans. I love the high life—the spas, the five-star spreads—but I haven't found a way to pay for it without bleeding myself dry. This morning, I opened a new line at 'The Central Exchange'. I’m moving M’s assets into my name, planning to liquidate everything. It’s a desperate move, but I need the credits to keep the dream alive. Travel, thrills, and the finest things money can buy—that’s the only way to drown out the sound of the rain.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
