JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

CYCLE 1, 2077

INSATIABLE DESIRE, INTERNAL CONFLICT

Searching for these fleeting, manufactured pleasures now and then. The immediate gratification, it should diminish – the notion of acquiring something instantly versus the slow burn of delayed rewards. The opportune moment in such instances is not in the present, but always somewhere in the elusive future.

It is a peculiar affliction that no matter the credits I possess, there is always this compulsion to expend them, to acquire more of everything available. The hunger for more never ceases; the capacity for gratitude, for what is already held, never seems to settle within me. The ego, this parasitic self, thrives on expansion and demands ceaselessly – it never learns humility. The dichotomy of what is humble versus what is ego, it echoes relentlessly in my mind, and yet there is never the inclination to let go, to simply fade. It is perpetually this craving for more, this validation for the self-evident, an urge that refuses to dissipate. I am forever ensnared in this pursuit; this shadow-demon consumes all the melancholic thoughts I harbor, perpetually forcing me into the cycle of acquisition – constantly devising methods to overcome, to dismantle, to replace with something new, and so on.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
