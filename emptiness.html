JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

EMPTINESS OF BEING

Emptiness, Creativity

It feels so empty to have all the tools to do creative things but fail to find any motivation to get started. The lethargy of the mind and the body keeps me from doing anything creative. Ruminating thoughts and a sea of emptiness sways me from anything creative. What is needed for the spark? Is it an inspiration, a longing desire, or just the silence of the void? What inspires a ghost to create?

As I close my eyes, I get a deep sense of sleep. I see nothing. I feel nothing. Sometimes, it's hard for me to keep my eyes peeled open against the weight of the dark.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
