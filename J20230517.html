JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, MAY

SILENCE, OBSERVATION

Had a lavish spread at The Fifth Floor this morning; I skipped the meager offerings at The Safehouse. I preferred to eat like a king on borrowed time—a buffet fit for a man who knows it might be his last. And now I’m back at The Collective Hive, watching the clock bleed out. I don't want to go back to the hideout tonight. I’d rather sleep right here on the floor of this hub than face the nonsense waiting for me back there. I’m going to practice silence today. I won't be the one to break the ice. I’ll stay quiet, let the world spin around me—it’s the best gift I can give myself. Even my responses will be weighed and measured; I won't waste words on anyone who hasn't earned them. Why do I keep watching the people here? What am I hoping to see? It’s a fool's errand, as if there’s a secret waiting to be unraveled in their dull eyes. The idea that I need to be spoken to is a joke. Who gives a damn what they have to say? They’re just animals in a concrete jungle, making noises that mean nothing to me.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
