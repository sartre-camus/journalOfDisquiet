Daily entry

Had a sumptuous breakfast at F5 today morning, I skipped the one that was under preparation at M&P’s place. I preferred to eat outside - a lavish buffet breakfast. That’s the best I could spend my borrowed money on for the early morning chow. And now here I am at the WeWork co-working space waiting for the day to end. I don’t want to go back home today. I just want to spend the night in this campus and sleep here. I don’t want to go back there and put up with all that non-sense. I want to practice silence today. I hope not to talk to anyone proactively. I wish to stay quiet for the entire day - that’s the best I could do to myself. Even the responses to what people have to say shall be based on the merit of the conversation. I shall not respond to people who don’t have anything worthy of my attention. Why do I keep staring at people? For what good? I mean it’s just ridiculous, as if, I’m going to find anything spectacular. Something that’s not there and has to be unraveled. The ludicrous idea that I need to be always spoken to - who fucking cares what people have to say. They all appear to me like animals in jungle and just make sounds that might be amusing but don’t add any value or meaning to my life.
