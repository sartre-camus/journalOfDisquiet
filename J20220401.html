JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FRIDAY, APRIL

collapse / strata

Still drifting. The blueprints and roadmaps I drew in the dark haven't led anywhere. I still fall back into the old rhythms—surviving, earning, breathing. A bizarre loop of events. I reach for the light only to find myself back on my knees, staring at the mud beneath the city streets.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
