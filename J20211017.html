Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I try to get a good score in the Java Programming Test for Logsta startup. I started off well, was able to understand the question and attempt. I did not know the answers for those. I tried to guess to the best of my knowledge. Later, I went on to try other questions. I got swayed away. My thoughts flew towards something else. I started giving up. I couldn’t focus on the question and read it. It is a different thing if I understand the question, look at the options and try. But getting swayed away and not willing to solve the questions is a worrisome development. Later, when I regrouped myself I was able to solve the easy programming question at the end. Things ended. I scored a meagre 43% in the test. As far my understanding goes the par score would be at least 70%. How much of preparation does it take anyway? I feel that it is an issue with my mindset. There are things that I know and I can solve and there are things that I don’t know that I can learn and solve. I can’t understand my source of rebellion. Rebellion towards things that could help me. I hate these online tests and coding challenges for some reason. The cognitive load is there and can be resolved. Sometimes, things like pointless thinking comes around. Wherein, I get entangled in them and enjoy it. The world is not out there, the world is within me. The way I perceive it, think about it and interact with it. Hatred, anger, resentment, sadness and others are true emotions whereas love, joy, bliss ecstasy and others are somewhat ambiguous. It takes a great deal of nonsense to create more nonsense.
