The Code-Breaker Trial - SUNDAY, OCTOBER

Failure, Mindset

I faced the Code-Breaker Trial for the Logsta startup. It started well, the logic seemed clear. But then my mind drifted, caught in the undertow of my own thoughts. I gave up. I couldn't focus on the terminal. A meagre 43% score—a death sentence for a job application. It’s my mindset, not the Prep. I rebel against the very things that could save me. I hate these digital hurdles, the cognitive load is a weight I can't lift. I entangle myself in pointless thinking, a world within myself where hatred and resentment are the only certainties.
