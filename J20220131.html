JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, JANUARY

Struggle, Resolve

The scent of a new assignment, it hangs in the air, a promise whispered in the gloom. One that calls upon the specters of my past endeavors.

Those intricate ciphers, a year's worth of phantom digits, still mock me from the shadows. They demand my touch.

The tally of unfinished business looms, a dark silhouette against the dawn. I must carve the leviathan of these labyrinthine demands, severing each tangled thread until the whole unspools.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
