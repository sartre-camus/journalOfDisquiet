JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SATURDAY, DEC

DEBT, STAGNATION

Now there is enough sleep so that I could resume my labor for the Syndicate. The past few cycles have been extremely relaxing yet anxious. I was doing nothing much related to my quotas but was enjoying synthetic pleasures. They were anxious because it made me worry about not earning enough credits to pay off the levies I have and also the life-debt. Sometimes I felt that I need not have to redo those pleasures as they have proved to be troublesome afterwards.

I end up coming to the decision that no matter how many credits I end up spending on these pleasures, they invariably cause me enough pain to not take them up in the first place. Spending credits only makes it worse, actually. Yet I am scared that I will do it again sometime in the future.

I still have no clue as to what I should work on—all those past projects seem to rot and put me off the moment I lay my hands upon them. I wish to study, but without any goal or plan in mind—almost aimlessly—and understand the various algorithmic concepts that I wish to. I spend my time watching so many holographic feeds and neural series.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
