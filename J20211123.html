JOURNAL of DISQUIET

Systemic Paperwork

Anxiety, Truth

The mind is a racetrack. After the sun walk and the hot shower, I sit down to face the systems. Clerical work—the paperwork of existence. Banking, government, the patriarchal machine. It’s all a lack of specificity. I’m haunted by the ghost of worthiness. I spend my days watching the comedy of the bureaucracy, getting pseudo-satisfaction from the labor of fictional characters. I can’t trick my own mind. I know the truth about myself, which is that I don’t know what the truth is.
