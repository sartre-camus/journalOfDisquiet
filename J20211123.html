Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

My mind is racing with thoughts. After the sun walk, hot shower, breakfast and getting all dressed up in work wear. I sit now to write. Some clerical work now would be ideal. Filling forms, filing returns, doing all sort of paperwork necessary for living harmoniously with the system. Banking system, government system, patriarchal system, social fabric, and many more systems. Systemic issues. Am I consuming too much electricity by writing this entry? The lack of specificity in my language is bothering me. My attempts to forget and move out of all that I know and to be somewhere else is troubling me. My problems that were present long time ago seem to be re-emerging. The anxiety of not doing anything worthwhile is bothering me. I spend my days enjoying life. Watching TV series such as ’Parks and Recreation’ and videos on YouTube. The characters in the TV series seem to be working and that gives me a pseudo satisfaction of me working. I find it difficult to trick my mind. For some reason I know what the truth is about myself. The only truth I know about myself is this that I don’t know what truth is.
