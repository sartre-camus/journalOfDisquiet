JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

NOVEMBER 15, 2024

DETACHMENT, STOICISM

The whispers from the Citizens of Sector 4 are merely their own projections. Whatever I execute within the grid, it exists for a moment and then dissolves into the acid rain. I must not mistake their surveillance for a true map of my psyche or a complete record of my functions.

All that matters is the continued traversal of the smog. Even my own internal biases and predilections are insignificant, destined to be overwritten by the next system update. There is no purpose in tethering my soul to these fleeting shadows.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
