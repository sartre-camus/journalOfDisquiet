JUNE 20, 2024

EXISTENTIALISM, ALIENATION

...would make me more cruel towards everyone and everything around me. I cannot think in terms of the Prime Utility all the time to be resourceful to the Hive. Does writing about all these eventually sort out my thoughts, impediments and offer a way out? I am not sure. As I write in an irrational way—the way of Logic punishes me. I do not know whether irrationality offers any benefits to my existence. The words begin to lose their value, their sheen so as to say, and they all crumble under their own letters. I am unwilling to put more letters into these words to form sentences that will simply be forgotten for good.

Am I looking to be perfect when I do something? Because I could have been processed for so many labor-sectors by now through whatever I knew rather than going through this conundrum.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
