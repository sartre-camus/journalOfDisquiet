JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, AUGUST

Anxiety, Customization

Yesterday night my aunt had called me. I had informed my kin earlier about my state of depression and the counseling I’m undergoing. He delegated the talk to my aunt instead of keeping it to himself. Somewhere I feel that they lack the sensitivity to my situation. My aunt, with her traditional beliefs and accepted ritual system tries to talk me through and doles out advice. She’s so eager to burden me with her opinions. I was devastated when she started hinting towards The Contract of Union in a very subtle way. She talks of spending time with The Destined One, living at her place. An attempt to infuse love or some kind of precursor to the Union with my kin. Just because cycles ago I had identified her childhood photo, my aunt thinks I am interested in her as my future life partner. I just feel so anxious about the entire situation that I could barely get any sleep last night. Also, this incident with The Rogue Guild’s Tech Rogue made it even more exhausting. The Rogue refunded the credits as I had asked her to cancel my requisition. At the time of ordering, I wanted it badly; now I am starting to feel the desire to own it fade away. The motivation behind ordering the unit was to have a completely libre terminal running freedom respecting software. I wanted to hack the system and customise it to the core. Install The Sovereign OS and use it for writing essays, articles and a book (if my motivation persists that long).

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
