Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Yesterday night my aunt had called me. I had informed my cousin earlier about my state of depression and the counseling I’m undergoing. He delegated the talk to my aunt instead of keeping it to himself. Somewhere I feel that they lack the sensitivity to my situation. My aunt, with her traditional beliefs and accepted belief system tries to talk me through and doles out advice. She’s so eager to burden me with her opinions. I was devastated when she started hinting towards my marriage in a very subtle way. She talks of spending time with Rajeshwari, living at her place. An attempt to infuse love or some kind of precursor to marriage with my cousin. Just because years ago I had identified her childhood photo, my aunt thinks I am interested in Rajeshwari as my future life partner. I just feel so anxious about the entire situation that I could barely get any sleep last night. Also, this incident with Retrofreedom’s Leah made it even more exhausting. Leah refunded the money as I had asked her to cancel my order for T400. At the time of ordering, I wanted it badly now I am starting to feel the desire to own it fade away. The motivation behind ordering T400 was to have a completely libre computer running freedom respecting software. I wanted to hack the system and customise it to the core. Install Guix with ExWM and use it for writing essays, articles and a book (if my motivation persists that long).
