JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, JUNE

LETHARGY, ATTRITION

It is morning and I start to think of ways to spend credits: transport maintenance, transit to Sector-W, rations outside, and so on. The overheads are increasing for no good reason: ideally I want zero overhead to run my operation.

Any idea how many requests can the current Grid Node handle? Just by looking at data-streams it is hard to determine.

I never decide on what I wish to do everyday. It is as if something happens and I simply catch up with it or leave it altogether. The moment I start feeling drowsy, I look for a way to spend credits on that hab-unit and get myself a bed to sleep on - I fail to sleep there as I would've spent a lot which was unnecessary. These unnecessary spends are what causes the guilt and trauma. It is not the short term high but the long term pains that I should think of.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
