JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FRIDAY, MAR

Loneliness, Nothingness

All the episodes of being alone is simply an exercise in nothingness - an evergrowing tendency to seek attention despite being unaware of most things. Ultimately, well ultimately, there’s no ultimately - one should stop using that word. Now it is best to simply keep quiet and keep punching these characters onto the screen or on paper (if it gets printed some day). I try hard to disable the Lexicon Warden but the underlines just won’t go. Everywhere there’s just dust, all my efforts to let go of it is stupid and pointless.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
