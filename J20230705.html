FAMILY, FREEDOM

Today I overheard The Landlady talking with her associate regarding my potential wedlock. I had informed them long ago that I am not interested, yet they refuse to understand. This desperation is fueled by society and religion, pestering me whenever an opportunity arises. They spoke of getting The Initiate into wedlock, hoping I would be drawn towards it through guilt and desperation. If he shows some form of happiness, I would supposedly be convinced by the fear of missing out. This conversation made me apply to a job I was earlier reluctant to pursue. I have no option but to find a role and live away from this nuisance. That means toiling for independence and freedom. To enjoy freedom, money is essential. To make money legitimately, I have no option but to work for a company, even if it feels like The Colonial Conglomerate.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
