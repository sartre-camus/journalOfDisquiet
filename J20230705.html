Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Today I overheard P talking with her friend regarding my marriage. I had informed them that I am not interested in marriage a long time ago, yet they don’t understand what I say. Somehow, this desperation is fueled by society, peers, friends, religion, relatives and so on onto P and M, who keep pestering about this whenever an opportunity arises. So this friend of P, kept talking and said something like get the younger one into wedlock, and the elder one would be drawn towards it, automatically through guilt and desperation. If the younger one (G), gets into a wedlock and shows some form of happiness in their marriage, the elder one (me) would be convinced somehow that it is the desirable thing to be done and would enter into a FOMO. Well, this conversation that I overheard from my room made me apply to a job that I was earlier reluctant to apply few days ago. I have no option to find a job and live away from all this nuisance. That means toil for independence and freedom. To enjoy freedom, money is essential. To make money legitimately, I have no option but to work without complaining how it is , how the boss is, how the company is or how their processes are. Even if it means working for a company like the East India Company.
