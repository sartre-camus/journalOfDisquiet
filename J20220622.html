JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE CODEBREAKER'S MONOTONY - WEDNESDAY, JUN

AMBITION, BOREDOM

Slouching into the Smoke-Filled Hub, I realize it's a simple matter to transmit my encrypted dispatches to The Oracle for her meager bounty. I dream of crafting a master-key that could unlock a million minds, simplifying the chaos of the city. Instead, I tinker with my own surveillance gear, a small act of rebellion in a world that watches everything. Between the rare moments of brilliance, I lose myself in the drudgery: refreshing the digital trail, swapping out ghost-scripts, and rearranging my virtual desktop like a man rearranging deck chairs on a sinking ship. The qualms of a man who has too much time and not enough purpose.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
