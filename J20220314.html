JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, MARCH

DEBT, LABOR

A quarrel with P and now here I am out in the open at the dreaded co-working space. Trying to blow my hard earned money at flimsy things. An utter waste of my time. Everyday begins with a rant. The same mundane rant. Repetitious and perpetual it continues. Trying to use the space for free? I belong nowhere. Not in the corporate, not at home, not on streets, not on mountains, not in a village, nowhere. Nowhere.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
