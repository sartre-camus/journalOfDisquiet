The Ghost in the Machine

technology, isolation

Dusting off the Libreboot X200S. It's been a long time since I heard its fan whir in the dark. This entry is just to prove it still breathes. The wires are crossed, though—the network is a dead end. I can't reach the Guix servers. I might have to hijack the signal from my handheld to get this rig updated. The wifi out here is as elusive as a shadow in an alleyway.
