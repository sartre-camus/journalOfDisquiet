JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, NOVEMBER

GUILT, OBSESSION

The notion that I am incurring a massive atmospheric debt simply by activating a glow-filament to mark pulp-sheets with a graphite-stylus is a fallacy born of the Ministry's propaganda. Even the anxiety that engaging the neural-terminal with its multi-optic arrays and connection to the Grid creates a significant resource drain is a phantom fear. There is negligible atmospheric debt in the pursuit of cognitive labor.

I must cease using these manufactured anxieties as excuses to avoid the work—drafting manifests, manual transcription, schematic sketches, or coding within the terminal. The priority remains the extraction of meaning from pre-collapse codices and the recording of insights for comprehension. These can be digitized into the data-stream later if the Archive demands it. Pulp, ink, and artificial light are the tools of the resistance; I should feel no hesitation in leveraging these remnants for my intellectual pursuits.

Another thought plagued me today: I must stop flagellating myself over the trivialities of existence in this gray sector.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
