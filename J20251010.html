JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FRIDAY, OCT

Consumption, Irrelevance

All the ideas I get in my idea have to do with consumption - there’s no creativity that is originating from my activities. It is not like I am creating a cipher for the ages or a testament in ink. All I have been upto is swallowing the City's propaganda what others are offering. It is this attitude that’s bothersome - sometimes it makes me wonder if I have anything at all to offer that would be of value. The vanishing point of my own shadow of my being is worrisome. I am trying to endure this feeling with little success.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
