JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE ACADEMIC'S GHOST

REGRET, PRIDE

Traded words with The Professor from The Institute today. They're offering a seat at the table in their Programming Lab, but the bounty is too small for a man of my experience. I'd rather starve in my den than become a junior associate in their ivory tower. I spent too much on a shave and fuel yesterday—small luxuries that feel like sins now that the big checks have stopped coming. I'm haunted by the shadows of The Institute from five years ago. I need to forget the faces and the failures and find a way to stay in the light.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
