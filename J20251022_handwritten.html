JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER

NIHILISM, DISSOCIATION

It seems like I am fading away, almost becoming invisible to the drones of Sector 7. I just want to sleep, sleep as if I have never slept before and lose track of the chronometer. Time is indeed an illusion; I was never there, anywhere, I simply wane away into the obscurity of the neon fog. The more of it, the better it seems. I have no intention to exit or to be anywhere; it all seems transitional and vain. I have failed in understanding anything about myself—if it all it makes me wonder is this that—is there a self at all? All I can perceive is emptiness manifested in nothingness. My being is part of this vast nothingness, yearning to be something else, but without this recognition of this vast void.

Why do I keep staring into the optics of the citizens, as if that eye contact should make them feel something? Or I should be treated with something that I crave for? I do not quite understand. In this vast sea of nothingness, why do I care if anyone cared about me, at all? There is an absurd amount of stupidity that prevails in my demeanour, which is quite unnecessary. What is that you are looking for in those empty eyes and hollow beings with nothingness oozing out of them? I do not quite get it.

Let it all melt away, let it all wither away, let it all corrode into uselessness and wait for the final shutdown.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
