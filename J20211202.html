JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

2021-12-02

Contemplation, Limbo

The perceived emptiness of space and time mesmerises me. After a humble, spiced repast, I position myself before the glowing rectangle to scribe a ledger of shadows. Listening to the street's restless pulse, the murmur of the unseen populace, and the mechanical cries of the peddlers, I sat, fingers poised. The phantom link, that digital tether to the unseen, seemed to drag its heels, refusing to breach the distant vaults.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
