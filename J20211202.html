JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

2021-12-02

DISQUIET

The perceived emptiness of space and time mesmerises me. After a filling lunch of rice, sambar and uppit, I sit in front of my twenty-four inch display to write a journal entry. Listening to the ambient sounds of vehicles passing by, neighbours talking and hawkers with their loudspeakers trying to sell a thing or two; I sit and type. The VPN connection is taking forever to connect to its servers.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
