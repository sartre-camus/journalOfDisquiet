Daily entry

I had a decent sleep. Difficult questions always occur to me, magically: the same ones. I looked up a word eclectic in the online dictionary. It means - ’borrowing from various sources’.

Ambience

I hear the cutting noises in the distant. They’re cutting metal or stone. Could be stone. That’s a lot of noise from distance. I try to ignore, but it’s just that time of the day - Monday morning. Everyone is eager to do things. Get on with work, finish the tasks and so on. Everyone acts busy and start working. Perpetually under construction: the entire country. I too start the day, get a good bath and dress up in a shirt and jeans. Jeans, or the work-pants, as they say.
