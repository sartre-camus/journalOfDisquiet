JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

Eclectic Noises - MONDAY, OCTOBER

Ambience, Anxiety

Woke from a decent sleep, only to be met by the same magical, recurring riddles. I looked up 'eclectic' in the digital archive. It means borrowing from the wreckage of various sources.

The distance is filled with the sound of steel meeting stone. The city is perpetually under construction, a Monday morning ritual where everyone pretends the work will one day be finished. I put on my work-pants—the uniform of the damned—and prepare to join the grind. Anxiety is the only honest emotion I have left.
