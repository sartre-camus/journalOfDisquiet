JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE LAST DISPATCH - THURSDAY, JUNE

SOLITUDE, SILENCE

The clock is ticking on my time here. Officially, I'm a free man; technically, I'm just a ghost between assignments. I'll collect my blueprints by dusk and vanish. I've been thinking about the cage of conversation. Why do we feel the need to bleed words for others? I dream of a life where the only dialogue is with the cold logic of the machine. No more human masks, no more hollow gestures. Just me and the terminal, and the heavy silence of a job well done.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
