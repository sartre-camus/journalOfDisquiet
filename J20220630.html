Thoughts

Had been pondering about the need for communication, why there is a need to communicate at all - with humans, there’s no reason for that to happen at all; there is this perpetual need to convey, talk, express, and so on - It could have just been all kept to myself. I was wondering about the possiblity of living without having to talk to any human in any form - speech, gestures, digital or physical means. What if it could all be cut down and the only communication that I’d have shall be with myself and a bot/computer. There is no need for a real person at all, no need to communicate at all unless someone else chooses to initiate one such conversation and seeks answer that’s worthy of my reply. Even silence at times is perhaps the most sought after option; does silence communicate? Maybe, it does after all.
