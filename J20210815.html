Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I had a sort of revelation yesterday when I was contemplating about my decision to choose the kind of work and location. I had worked in corporate earlier and the reason to quit that was the salary, lifestyle and freedom limitations. I wanted to move out of India to a developed nation such as the Denmark, Sweden, Germany or the Netherlands. I wanted to settle there and work as a software engineer. Pursue my career of programming and software development from those places. Even Canada was fine. I wanted to go from a third world country such as India, to a developed nation and settle there. The reasons are plenty. I want value for the taxes I pay, a viable social support system, freedom of living the way I wish to, experience luxury and an open society. These are something that’s impossible at the place where I stay. I cannot afford to live the way I wish to, here at my place. I cannot even think of expecting the society to be open and free. Everyone here is deeply entangled in their belief systems, religion, social order and hatred. I can’t expect to be happy among these people. From parents to near relatives to friends to neighbours and to the society at large, it’s all the same rendition of bigotry, hatred and falsehood. My expectation is this that, if I move to a country, one that I mentioned before, then, I would be able to achieve the freedom and happiness that I’m seeking. The only reason I continue to stay where I’m is because I have gotten used to all the injustices. I have given up as I’m too scared to fight for myself and make my way out of this prison. I feel pampered in this prison by conveniences that are bribed to me to give up freedom and be dependent. Be dependent on people for emotional well being and survival. This emotional drama of family values and blood relationship is draining my mind. It’s taking a toll on my happiness. I feel constantly judged by people around me. If I truly loved my parents, or my mother (to be very precise), I would want her to stay with me away from all these. In a developed country elsewhere. If she truly loves me and cares about me then, she should be willing to move with me. However, I don’t think it’s the case. She can’t leave her husband, her friends and relatives here and come with me. So, I won’t expect her to move with me. But at least, I can move out. I can make it on my own and be happy. Why am I being so hesitant to even try with all the energy that I’ve got and work towards this goal? Why don’t I realise that the change I need is somewhere else and I need to work towards it to be happy?
