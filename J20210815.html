JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SUNDAY, AUGUST

Revelation, Society

I had a sort of revelation yesterday when I was contemplating about my decision to choose the kind of work and location. I had worked in The Syndicates earlier and the reason to quit that was the credits, lifestyle and freedom limitations. I wanted to move out of The Eastern Territory to a developed nation such as The Northern Kingdom or the neighboring realms. I wanted to settle there and work as an Architect. Pursue my career of programming and Digital Construct development from those places. Even The Frozen North was fine. I wanted to go from a struggling territory to a prosperous realm and settle there. The reasons are plenty. I want value for the credits I pay, a viable social support system, freedom of living the way I wish to, experience luxury and an open society. These are something that’s impossible at the place where I stay. I cannot afford to live the way I wish to, here at my place. I cannot even think of expecting the society to be open and free. Everyone here is deeply entangled in their belief systems, rituals, social order and hatred. I can’t expect to be happy among these Shadows. From parents to near kin to friends to neighbours and to the society at large, it’s all the same rendition of bigotry, hatred and falsehood. My expectation is this that, if I move to a realm, one that I mentioned before, then, I would be able to achieve the freedom and happiness that I’m seeking. The only reason I continue to stay where I’m is because I have gotten used to all the injustices. I have given up as I’m too scared to fight for myself and make my way out of this prison. I feel pampered in this prison by conveniences that are bribed to me to give up freedom and be dependent. Be dependent on Shadows for emotional well being and survival. This emotional drama of traditional values and blood relationship is draining my mind. It’s taking a toll on my happiness. I feel constantly judged by Shadows around me. If I truly loved my guardians, or my mother (to be very precise), I would want her to stay with me away from all these. In a prosperous realm elsewhere. If she truly loves me and cares about me then, she should be willing to move with me. However, I don’t think it’s the case. She can’t leave her husband, her friends and kin here and come with me. So, I won’t expect her to move with me. But at least, I can move out. I can make it on my own and be happy. Why am I being so hesitant to even try with all the energy that I’ve got and work towards this goal? Why don’t I realise that the change I need is somewhere else and I need to work towards it to be happy?

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
