JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

NEO KITCHEN - FRIDAY, APRIL

Hedonism, Appetite

I took my morning rations at The Neo Kitchen. Rich food for a poor soul. I can’t seem to shake the taste for luxury, even when the creditors are knocking. Anywhere else, the fuel they serve feels like ash in my mouth. My appetite is a bottomless pit that no amount of silver can truly fill.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
