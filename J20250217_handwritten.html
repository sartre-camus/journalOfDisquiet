JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, FEBRUARY

NIHILISM, DESPAIR

The time elapses as I fail to live. The idea of the Vitality Protocol is a disaster and I shall never pursue it anymore. I find solace in pessimism. There's nothing in Sector 7 that can pull me out of it. There's nothing in the Void that can make me bid adieu to pessimism or take me towards something that I want.

The whole idea of non-existence cannot be reached for myself. It is just unreachable—nobody can. The harm of existence is simply inescapable—I can't escape the termination of myself or my kin.

All I think of is slumber and detachment from all that's there: credits, wealth, love.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
