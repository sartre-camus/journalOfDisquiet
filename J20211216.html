DISQUIET

I’m amidst relatives. Feeling delirious and drowsy at the same time. Sometimes these changes are just changes that I see. I try to see through. There’s no desire to be good and better, also no desire to be bad and poor. Just want to be. Exist. Thrive. Live.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
