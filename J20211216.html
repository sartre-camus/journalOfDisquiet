JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, DEC

Existence, Disillusionment

I found myself adrift amongst the familiar faces, a prisoner of their soft expectations. A peculiar delirium, a heavy slumber, both claiming me in equal measure, a strange cocktail for a sleepless night. These shifts, these subtle currents I observed, sometimes they felt like phantoms, conjured by a mind too weary to discern truth from shadow. I tried to see through the illusion, to pierce the fog that clung to everything. The old ambitions, the yearning for redemption or ruin, they'd vanished, dissolved like smoke in the rain. There was only the raw, unyielding urge to simply *be*. To persist. To draw breath in this flickering, uncertain existence, a solitary flame against the encroaching dark.
