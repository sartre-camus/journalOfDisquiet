JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

2021-08-17

Writing, Literature

There was a time cycles ago that I had wished I had something like that I have now - staying at the Safehouse all day long, no need for travel, The Grid and electricity to use the terminal and gaming unit whenever I’d wish to, no work deadlines, no Overseer, no expectation from anyone for doing anything significant… These were what I had yearned for when I was doing the standard grind, cycle after cycle for seasons or so. On one hand, there are a lot of things to do and on the other, I don’t know what or why to do. Do I secretly desire to be a Chronicler? A Chronicler who writes volumes - fiction or non-fiction, articles etc. It’s a thankless path, they say. I will be struggling my entire cycle to establish myself. Or do I just like writing as a pastime? Well, whatever be it, for the time being, writing is appealing to me. I somehow have made friends with the words, sentences and paragraphs. The Scripting Core as a writing tool is motivating me to express myself more than ever before. My usage of the common tongue with its vocabulary and grammar is a limitation. I haven’t explored or read much of the Great Archives myself. What good is it going to be if my style doesn’t appeal to someone who cares to read my work? My style of writing, if there is one, would be just another quirk.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
