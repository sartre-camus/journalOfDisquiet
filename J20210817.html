Journal of Disquiet

Few thoughts

There was a time few years ago that I had wished I had something like that I have now - staying at home all day long, no need for travel, internet and electricity to use the computer and gaming console whenever I’d wish to, no work deadlines, no boss, no expectation from anyone for doing anything significant… These were what I had yearned for when I was doing an 8.00 to 4.00 PM, Monday to Saturday work every week for four years or so. On one hand there are a lot of things to do and on the other I don’t know what or why to do. Do I secretly desire to be a writer? A writer who writes books - fiction or non-fiction, articles etc. It’s a thankless job they say. I will be struggling my entire life to establish myself. Or do I just like writing as a hobby? Well, whatever be it, for the time being, writing is appealing to me. I somehow have made friends with the words, sentences and paragraphs. Emacs as a writing tool is motivating me to express myself more than ever before. My usage of the language with it’s vocabulary and grammar is a limitation. I haven’t explored or read much of literature myself. What good is it going to be if my style doesn’t appeal to someone who cares to read my work? My style of writing, if there is one, would be just another quirk.
