JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

RELENTLESS SUN - FRIDAY, APRIL

Austerity, Exhaustion

I forgot to register my presence today. Had to grease the wheels with the Enforcers at The Hive just to get past the perimeter. Once inside, the manufactured cold was a relief; the sun outside is a relentless interrogator. I’ve sworn off the local rations. The medical droids say my systems are sluggish, my biological casing is carrying too much weight. The drive here is a gauntlet of heat and steel. Every mile feels like a marathon in a furnace.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
