JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

2021-12-29

FUTILITY, IMPULSE, DECAY

The ceaseless grind, the same old monologue of dread, continues its slow, acid drip. Now, I’m slated for a steel bird's cold flight through the acid clouds, a jump I only signed on for because the data-stamp is already burned into my neural-ink. Otherwise, the credits, already committed, would merely dissipate into the city's bottomless maw. What good is it, bleeding more credits, stumbling into something I loathe, and somehow expecting it to make sense of this pre-paid passage to purgatory?

I still can’t quite grasp this habit of mine, this dance with shadows instead of stepping into the pale, cold light of logic. The hum of reason, a faint signal in the static, tells me one thing, yet I follow the flickering neon of impulse. Why this relentless itch, this urge to fumble through the dark, guided by nothing but the ghost of a feeling, then build a crumbling temple of logic around the ruins? The last thing this broken city needs, or I need, is another fool chasing starlight in the gutter. A romantic? That's a death sentence in these streets, a plea for the acid rain to strip you bare.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
