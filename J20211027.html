Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Aimless has been my journey for last few weeks. I just feel like talking. Talking to people. Senseless talk. Without filter. Just blabber about all the nonsense (sometimes sense). I hate these mosquitoes. I couldn’t sleep yesterday night, not because of mosquitoes but because of the TV series I was watching: The Exorcist (2016). I was scared of the demons in that TV series. I was too afraid to leave the blanket away. I covered my face while I slept. Too scared to look around in the dark. Somewhere there might be a gaze. A gaze that stares me in the eye. The mirror, the chair, the window panes: all of them looking at me - returning the gaze. The suspense element of the TV show keeps me involved. In watching how the characters behave, how the story unfolds and so on.
