JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

The Demon's Gaze - WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER

Fear, Obsession

My journey lately is a map to nowhere. I find myself blabbering to anyone who will listen, a senseless talk without a filter. I hate the mosquitoes—the small, buzzing executioners of the night. Sleep was impossible, haunted by the demons of the Ritual of the Damned. I stayed beneath the blanket, terrified of the dark, feeling the gaze of the furniture. The mirror, the chair, the window—all returning my stare. The suspense is the only thing that keeps me tethered.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
