JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY

Peril, Truth

The Old Man at The House gave me the boot last week. He spouted some drivel about poor performance, but I knew the score. It was my prying into the black market dealings he had with the Eastern Reach. I'd been collecting evidence on The Glass Eye for months. Even caught him meeting a contact from The Crimson Sands in a back alley behind The Stinking Docks. They were talking about illicit cargo and cleaning dirty money. He coughed up thirty thousand chips and slapped me with a binding silence.

Now I'm holed up in a nameless room on the ragged edges of The Sprawl, trying to map my next move. I smell trouble on my tail. Saw a Steel Serpent parked outside my window for hours yesterday. I've stashed a copy of my files in The Silent Archive and penned a letter to The Scribe, my man at The Chronicle. I just pray it reaches him before they do.

Fear's a cold hand, but I won't fold. This rotten truth needs light. I don't know the depth of the waters I'm in, but I can't keep my mouth shut. My ticket to The City of Forgotten Dreams, where I was supposed to meet The Keeper of Pages next month, is now just a crumpled promise. This journal will remember.
