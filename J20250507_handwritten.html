JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MAY 7, 2025

DEBT, PRETENSE

I borrow credits from V, against the commitment. Borrowing, which was initially stealing, at least for an hour or so, a sum of five hundred credits. I have already spent half of it by paying the docking warden here. Sometimes, there are expenses that are just unavoidable? Really? I could have simply worked from the hab-unit, instead of coming here. The deck was peaking at a critical temperature of 105°C; I thought that the ambient temperature of the sector would help in reducing it. Also the backup would have been taken in that cool temperature—now I am left with no option but to postpone that task.

There's one thing that is clear about Void-Corp and it is this: they do not have deep pockets. If they did, they would have settled the Cycle 4 invoice. It just goes on to prove that nothing works after all with those folks there. The question that lies in front of me is whether I should continue writing code related to Void-Corp or is it high time that I start looking for other avenues of work. Maybe, it is wise if I start preparing for that Nebula-Net job interview. Brush up Logic-Script programming skills and get an offer. There's no point thinking too much about the credits though—it is the job that matters more than anything else. I plan to stay today till 8:00 PM and then go back. Many things are left to do—using that Aether Node again—I'm unable to get that DNS working, it seems like a Protocol 9 error or a Secure-Layer fault. I need to figure that out, so that I can have my Lattice Node up and running. Always, learn new skills and fix them—this should be known beforehand; it cannot be taught, but has to be learnt.

I was thinking about being an autodidact, a person who is self-taught—one who can learn anything that's to be learnt and utilize it in any way that's necessary. I still am resistant to making the changes in my ways of working. For some reason, I keep myself down under—even when I know that I am correct—why do I behave in a way that proves to be counter-intuitive to the ways of logical and rational behavior. Maybe, I should dematerialize those Gilded Bonds after all to keep myself afloat. I have held myself on board for quite some time—it feels like I am facing another debt of sorts—my staying above ground was temporary. I should also rely on instincts with what's out there; it will protect me and will not harm me.

Are there any other ways to raise credits? Perhaps by selling some of the things that I own? Not sure—ultimately I am cursed to take up a job and schlep for credits, it seems, all prospects simply fade away. Perhaps, it was wise after all if I had simply stayed at the hab-unit and came out later in the evening. Changing things every day makes it more problematic—I should've just stepped out in the evening and fetched some credits from the Dispenser while returning back from this place. Why is it sometimes so hard to admit my poverty, why this constant comparison with others and this tendency to show myself as more wealthy than others?

I still do not understand this idea of grandiosity that I need to portray, in front of others, like someone should call me 'Sir' and I should acknowledge it as if I am some sort of a big shot. It is just lame and pointless. There's no reason for me to continue this way—an utter waste of my energies in living this kind of a lie—all that self-esteem that I fake, without any precedence or future—all those pompous ideas that I desire to fulfill—why? Just give it up already—you've lost or rather I have lost.

"I hate to beg, borrow or steal, it hurts me whenever I do it—irrespective of how others respond to it."

I am repeating again: 'No more communication with Overseer Thorne.' It's finished—whether he pays my bills or not. I shall continue working on that integration as it matters to me—I will keep it up and running, no matter what, either through a new comms-link, a new access-token or whatever—I have to keep those online services up and running, in one way or the other.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
