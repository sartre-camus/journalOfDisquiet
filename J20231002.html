MONDAY, OCTOBER

Cipher, Underworld

The static is getting louder. Another encrypted pulse from the underworld, a wall of math standing between me and the truth. Sartre and Camus—those two ghosts are still whispering from the Proton Node, beckoning me into a labyrinth of ciphers. Every byte is a burden, every key a gamble.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
