JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE LETHARGIC EXCHANGE - WEDNESDAY, JUL

DUTY, APATHY

Back to the grind with The Young Bloods. They paid eighty credits for my wisdom, but my heart wasn't in it. The city's static and my own exhaustion are a heavy fog. I've sent my final severance notice to The Vault, but the bank has vanished like a witness in a federal case. I'm trusting a government courier to find their new lair. In other news, I dodged a social trap set by The Siren—no time for luxury when the coffers are low and the future is a question mark. The Headhunter from The Elite Network keeps buzzing my terminal, but I've stopped listening. I don't want to be another cog in their high-priced machine.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
