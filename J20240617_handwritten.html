JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

CYCLE ONE: THE DESECRATION - MONDAY, JUNE

LETHARGY, NIHILISM

Writing is a desecration, that I am not sure of.

I don't wish to die or live. I don't wish to labor for the Syndicate or slack. I don't wish to earn credits or give joy. I wish to not wish, yet I wish that the above wishes just simply disappear.

- Written on Cycle One, the toughest day to endure in the sector week.
