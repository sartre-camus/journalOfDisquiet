Journal of Disquiet

I return to my older den after a couple days. I was away and stayed at another place for two nights. I feel a sudden burst of energy this morning. The energy to move around my home, talking to people whom I otherwise ignored. I feel the burst of some excitement and anxiety over my return. I am sighing and heaving as I try to write. Filled with too many thoughts and past memories. They all fill in my mind. I find no way to ease out of them. Yesterday, I had the session with my psychologist. I talked about my condition and about the happenings of my life.
