JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FRIDAY, MARCH

CORP, DEBT

My ephemeral stint with The Monolith Syndicate contract ended yesterday. Can’t use their proprietary products and build proprietary solutions. This is the third or fourth company whom I have refused to work for because of my free/libre open source philosophy advocacy. It makes me wonder what have I contributed to the free software movement so far. When I speak of contribution I usually refer to software work - programming, testing, documentation and so on. I have refused to work a person who wanted The Ghost Script customization before just because the code was privately hosted. What I see here with this recent contract agreement was:

  • I failed to negotiate initially for free software licensing of my work
  • Also, not being vocal about my beliefs and philosophy. Pragmatism, unfortunately hasn’t been my strengths. It may be difficult for me to proceed along the lines of business using a free software philosophy

Not sure whether I want to earn money through paid work for a profit making business.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
