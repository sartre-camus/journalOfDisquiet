JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SATURDAY, JUN

STAGNATION, DISILLUSIONMENT

There. My mind-vault is cluttered, a wreckage of over-processing. I cannot continue to obsess over the Citizens and the hollow words they might utter when our paths cross in the lower corridors. I must abandon these existential queries; they yield no data, only a descent into the abyss of disillusionment.

I should focus on basic maintenance cycles; perhaps that is the only way to break through this stagnation, this slump in my output at the Aether Refinery. I require exposure to the Sol-Simulators, at least during the first watch. My systems cannot sustain without it. Perhaps I should traverse the perimeter of the Sector every now and then.

I remain unconvinced if these 'wellness' protocols would truly optimize me or simply hasten my obsolescence. The Sol-Simulators are offline anyway, masked by the smog-shrouds and the toxic gales. I am left only with endurance.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
