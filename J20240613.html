JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, JUNE

Ego, Reflection

The weight of existing is a heavy coat. I sit in a dim cafe, nursing a cup of tea and waiting for the fog in my head to clear. I've got a short fuse these days. Any contradiction feels like a slap in the face.

I walk around like I've got the map to the city, but I'm just as lost as the next guy. The ego is a brittle thing, and it doesn't take much to crack it. Why do I care so much when someone yells? It's all just noise in the void.
