Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I got a few rejects for the jobs I had applied earlier. I’m still hesitant in continuing with the recruitment process for the couple of jobs I have applied for. I am wondering what is the reason for my hesitancy? I feel that I have an urge to do something on my one. Like develop a software library or write essays. I am still figuring out what do I feel passionate about. Most times, I lack motivation to do what seems obvious. It just feels boring even though it might be more beneficial for me. I just feel lost. Lost like ever before. Today, I spent a lot of time trying get the Guix OS to be installed on the Libreboot X200s machine. I finally managed to succeed in it. And this entry is being typed on Guix distro running EXWM. The trick was to have the SSD installed with Guix distro using another laptop insted of X200s. Because, X200s is headless (without the built-in display) which makes it difficult to see BIOS options. Makes it hard to use external monitors. Therefore, I removed the SSD from X200s and installed it onto Dell Latitude laptop. Used the Latitude laptop to write the Guix distro from USB flash drive on to the SSD. Then, removed the SSD from the Latitude and re-installed it on to X200s. It worked! I have Mate and EXWM on this distro. But my intention is to use EXWM for doing creative work. I plan to use it to write essays, blogs, journal and maybe someday a book. It is simple and distraction free here on EXWM unlike other distros and their bloated window managers.
