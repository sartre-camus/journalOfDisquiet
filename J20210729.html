JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, JULY

Technical, Freedom

I got a few rejects for the roles I had applied for earlier. I’m still hesitant in continuing with the recruitment process for the couple of positions I have applied for. I am wondering what is the reason for my hesitancy? I feel that I have an urge to do something on my own. Like develop a Digital Construct or write essays. I am still figuring out what I feel passionate about. Most times, I lack motivation to do what seems obvious. It just feels boring even though it might be more beneficial for me. I just feel lost. Lost like ever before. Today, I spent a lot of time trying to get The Sovereign OS to be installed on The Liberated Terminal. I finally managed to succeed in it. And this entry is being typed on the OS running a distraction-free manager. The trick was to have the drive installed with the OS using another terminal instead of the main one. Because the terminal is headless (without the built-in display), which makes it difficult to see system options. Makes it hard to use external monitors. Therefore, I removed the drive and installed it onto The Sturdy Workstation. Used the workstation to write the OS from a portable drive on to the SSD. Then, removed the drive from the workstation and re-installed it onto the terminal. It worked! I have multiple environments on this distro. But my intention is to use the distraction-free setup for doing creative work. I plan to use it to write essays, logs, journals and maybe someday a book. It is simple and clean here, unlike other distros and their bloated managers.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
