Daily entry

I still have the temptation to indulge in travels, sex and food. I feel like splurging money on these the moment I have it. The temptation to consume digital content in the form of videos and games continues. I feel like I would be better off if I am able to restrict them to a day in a week. I’m using my smart phone for at least forty hours a week, most of it happens to be on watching movies, tv series, YouTube videos and such. I need to bring that down to twenty four hours a week. This is just too much. It looks like I’m entertaining myself for the amount of hours that I earlier used to work. It’s tragic. I also am wondering whether I need these multiple monitors on my desk. Most of the multitasking I do seems unproductive.
