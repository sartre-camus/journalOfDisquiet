The Rising Tide

recovery, ambition

The fever’s broken, and I’m starting to see the shapes of the city again. I need to get my hands back on the wheel. The bills are stacking up like cordwood, and the well is running dry. I need to find a way to make the numbers work—a new hustle, a new source of coin. The quiet was nice, but the hunger is coming back.
