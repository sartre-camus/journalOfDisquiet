JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, JUNE

CHAOS, REFLECTION

It’s been a while since I wrote an entry. Somehow, I tried to keep it all to myself but the frustration and angst has burst open and I can’t keep it all bottled up in me now. On Friday night, I decided to call The Fixer to hang out as I was getting bored. My choice of whom I spend time with is utterly poor - did I have to call up a junkie? Well what followed was one of those bizarre and wasteful nights. I chose to tag along, sing along with the pedestrian idea of socializing with people whom I don’t care much about apart from the fact of being thrown into constant peer pressure. I would rather forget that I even had gone out with them droogies that night. My life is not so boring as I think, I have far better things to do than hang out with ghosts from the past who just remind me of my lack of success. I am better left alone - that’s true freedom. Today, I spend time getting printouts of all those e-books that I want to read. I have done the easy bit, now the bit that needs diligence follows in the form of studying the manuscripts. These dreaded tests of The Whispering Wire from The Firm fail all the time - it’s an exercise in absurdity. I would have preferred an easier way out - but what I’m currently into can be termed as ’monkey coding’ - the perils of which The Overlord had warned me of. I better not monkey code - I should seek to understand the base and not just top it off with some cherries to show off. What an utter waste of my talents.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
