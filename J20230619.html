Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

It’s been a while since I wrote an entry. Somehow, I tried to keep it all to myself but the frustration and angst has burst open and I can’t keep it all bottled up in me now. It’s perhaps best to vent it all out here in this entry. On Friday night, I decided to call Mr. AVC to hang out as I was getting bored and had nothing better to do. This is how it all starts - from the mundane and careless actions of mine. I called him up so that I could meet and spend some time. My choice of whom I spend time with is utterly poor - did I had to call up a junkie? Well what followed was one for those bizarre and wasteful nights that I perhaps shouldn’t venture out anymore. AVC took me to another person’s place far away in a rickshaw. I could’ve easily skipped the entire episode had I not called him in the first place, but I chose to tag along, sing along with the pedestrian idea of socializing with people whom I don’t care much about apart from the fact of being thrown into constant peer pressure and bro tips. I would rather forget that I even had gone out with them droogies that night. It’s best if I forget it all. I shouldn’t go out like that in future, come what may. My life is not so boring as I think, I have far more better things to do with my life than hang out with ten year old classmates who just remind me of my lack of success in my career. I don’t fit into that group at all - or for that matter in any group. I am better left alone - that’s true freedom. Today, I spend time getting printouts of all those e-books that I want to read. I got them spiral bound so that they’re easy to handle and preserve. The only thing left now is to study them thoroughly and learn what it has to teach me. I have done the easy bit, now the bit that needs diligence, perseverance and humility follows in the form of studying and learning all that these books contain. The idea is to build a career in those topics by becoming expert in them through consistent learning and practice. These dreaded tests of fluffychat from fairkom fail all the time, I’m not sure whether I really care to fix them or even get the setup ready - it’s an exercise in absurdity. I would have a preferred an easier way out - but what I’m currently into can be termed as ’monkey coding’ - the perils of which my former company’s CEO Manoj had warned me of. I better not monkey code - if I make an attempt to remember and learn what’s the code base about and how making changes to it can be tested, I would perhaps be better off. For some reason, I always cherish an easy way out by seeking someone like Matthias’s help to get everything working and I could just top it off with some cherries to show off that I have somehow completed that task - what an utter waste of my talents.
