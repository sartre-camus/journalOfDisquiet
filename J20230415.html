JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

J20230415 - SATURDAY, APR

POVERTY, DESPAIR

The coffers are dry. The long, shadow-draped path ahead demands I become another cog in the city's Great Machine, grinding for a pittance. My gut knots from a missed meal this afternoon. I never thought I’d sink this low into the gutter. I wonder what an ideal utilization of this void would be. For some reason, I look outward, past the rain-streaked glass. I haven’t focused on my own needs, my own hungers, what makes the heart beat faster. I’ve always drifted, swayed toward the neon lights and pointless games that have made my life a dull, uneventful noir. I worry about the creases in my cheap shirt. All that dust and rags and the hollow things that keep us wavering.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
