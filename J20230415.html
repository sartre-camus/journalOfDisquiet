Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I’m running out of ideas to generate cash. The long arduous way that lies ahead of me has to do with working like a machine at an industry to earn a paycheck. My stomach swells up for not eating this afternoon. I never thought I’d be so low on cash. I wonder what would be an ideal utilization of this space. For some reason, I look outwards. I haven’t focused on what I need, what I think, what I want, what makes me happy, and so on. I’ve always wandered away, swayed away towards pointless things that has made my living rather boring and uneventful. I’m worried about the number of creases the shirt that I’m wearing would have. All that midge and rag and fooling wavering things.
