JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

The Barber Shop - MONDAY, NOVEMBER

Ritual, Desire

Got a haircut today in a den of viral roulette. No distancing, just the ingestion of the Pale Plague. A mechanical vibrator was used to loosen my muscles—a brief, artificial relaxation. I scrubbed the city off me in a hot shower. I find myself longing for an Iron Scribe—a typewriter. Without one, my writing feels hollow, a short-lived desire that will soon catch dust.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
