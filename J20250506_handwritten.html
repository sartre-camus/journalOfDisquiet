JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, MAY

ANALOG RESISTANCE, EXISTENTIAL DREAD

At least the pen and the paper are already paid for. That means I need not be worried about writing using them. I could exhaust all the ink and paper that's probably available, yet I will continue writing. This space is quite comfortable actually to sit and write: the isolation booth with velvet-clad soft seating and a large enough desk that is at a comfortable height—good enough to spread my arms while writing.

OK, I managed to take that long, itchy walk again to this sector today, knowing that I shall not be wasting any credits at all. Now I'm spending my energies on writing and walking. I was applying for a few labor prospects in the hope that I will get an evaluation. For some reason, I feel a little rusty with my Logic-Cipher skills—Neural-Script programming to be precise. I should not refuse to memorize the code; it is probably for the best that I remember and recollect all of them. The best is if I can write all that code here on a white leaf without any assistance whatsoever.

That's what most overseers in the industry expect as part of the evaluation—one who can write clean code that works on a white board or paper from memory. All these tools simply make the task quite complex: Auto-Coders, Ghost-Pilots, Synthetic Oracles, editors with prompts, and what not. I believe that great work can be created with just a pen and paper, with thoughts getting expressed coherently on the white or blank surface. It is this blank surface that enables me to be more expressive and free-flowing in my writings. I just seem to fall in love with this process. All those devices one uses to write just add to the complexity. Makes a simple task more time and energy consuming.

The task of writing for pleasure would be hindered when devices such as a mechanical-typer, terminal, comm-unit, or a slate is used. The reason being there is an additional learning curve and just way too many distractions as the device becomes more and more complex due to the added multitasking. Somehow the task of doing one at a time and doing it well is all that matters—they need to be atomic. Multitasking does give the guise of achieving more in less time but it is probably not so. All the emphasis on correctness through various dependencies that consumes the space.

Now regarding the work at hand; I wish to create a diagram that explains how certain modules work and connect them around. Also, a thing that I forgot: most of these devices have screens that are sore to the eye. The radiation that comes out of it and being exposed to it for longer times brings in more discomfort in sleep patterns and affects the fatigue; causes more fatigue. I believe I am caught up again with the idea of the walk exposing my body, hair, and eyes to the dust and the dying Sun—ozone radiation, one that causes whatever it causes, and go on with it.

This sector really brings some memories even if I wish not to: some of the better ones actually. The smells and the sights have not seemed to have changed. 'Why should I be bothered about brevity?' Let it be raw—the way I would speak: that same way it should be written—no filters, no proofreading or conciseness. I had decided yesterday to not follow any style or conform to standards. It will be the way it will be—let myself go wild in this journey of words.

I still am able to remember how I used to sit and write a three-hour examination in the Academy, filling one sheet after the other. Now that whole exercise has been forgotten. What a good feeling I used to have when I wrote all those answers on the sheet, explaining and eventually seeing myself to the end of it. Writing does bring a sense of contentment when these pages are filled with words—even if it is for the sake of itself. The ideas, whether new or banal, have to come out and imprint themselves here—it cannot be just let to wander in the head and later manifest in dreams. It all needs to come out. The better it is when handwritten—makes it much more personal.

I was going to steal credits from Vaelin's wallet today, but I withheld myself until I was able to check his accounts by calling up the central exchange—just so that I get to know the balances—whatever that's due. I somehow managed to control myself from stealing or borrowing money from him—it was quite tempting at the beginning, but I mocked myself, laughed at the sheer stupidity that I was going to commit. I still think that whenever the PIN entered for those cards goes wrong, or even calling up with those card details, it would trigger a message to Vaelin's neural-link and comm-unit. He would get to know about it being accessed anyway. All these details cannot be hidden; some day or the other the owner would get to know about the theft and find out a suspect—whether like it or not, I would be the one at the beginning.

I was also planning to borrow from Marrow, which is even more bad than what I could probably imagine. No matter whether I steal or borrow from fellow hab-members, I will still have enough guilt in me to go and repay: it is the debt that I would invariably incur. I have no way to repay anybody's debt—the reason being I am unwilling to work for the sake of repaying debt; that really sounds like slavery. I do not wish to be a slave in whatever definition or practice—not fond of using euphemisms or politically correct jargon either. I cannot work for the sake of credits—it is just slavery as the Ancient Architect puts it. Working for the sake of money—no one should do it—no one should use that as a reason to do any work.

It will only yield results that are plastic, fake of sorts, with no sense of craftsmanship, devotion, or any such higher value. I have somehow been on this trip of barraging myself to aim for mediocrity all along—it doesn't matter anyway, why do it? And all such questions. Why this is desirable and why that is not? Cannot answer—I can only think and churn my brain in circles. Now I really feel exhausted—can't pour myself out anymore on this paper. Is it time to scan this and send? On a bad note: I remember writing a few resignation letters on such blank vellum-leaves, only to be torn down and eventually handed over to someone for scrubbing. At least my past few resignations have been over neural-link and not on a blank sheet like this one.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
