JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

J20230605 - MONDAY, JUN

AMBITION, ANXIETY

I gave the interview for The Gilded Syndicate today for the Ancient Script role. After two days of scraping all that I could from the networks and taking prints of all those forbidden manuals, I read few of them, watched a few transmissions of them online and tried to understand and remember them for the inquisition. All I could do was just talk about the basics in the chamber and struggled to write code only to be prodded later to finish it afterwards. I had to be defensive in what I claimed to know, so as to avoid the embarrassment associated with my lack of knowledge regarding a certain topic. I was nervous for most of the time as I didn’t know what question would be thrown upon me, I just kept thinking along those lines and remained in my comfort zone. I tried not to ask anything more than what I knew and had to basically settle for what I could answer.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
