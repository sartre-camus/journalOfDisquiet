Daily entry

I gave the interview for Benison Technologies today for the Java role. After two days of scraping all that I could from the websites and taking prints of all those textbook pages, I read few them, watched a few videos of them online and tried to understand and remember them for the interview. All I could do was just talk about the basics in the interview and struggled to write code only to be prodded later to finish it afterwards. I had to be defensive in what I claimed to know, so as to avoid the embarrassment associated with my lack of knowledge regarding a certain topic. I was nervous for most of the time as I didn’t know what question would be thrown upon me, I just kept thinking along those lines and remained in my comfort zone. I tried not to ask anything more than what I knew and had to basically settle for what I could answer.
