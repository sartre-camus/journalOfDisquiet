JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SATURDAY, MAY

Frustration, Silence

The typewriter has lost its rhythm. It can't keep up with the frantic pace of a man with nothing left to lose. I've taken my fists to it more than once, trying to beat some sense into the keys. A change in the neon glow seemed to steady its nerves, and now the letters are landing true.

But now that the machine is ready to talk, the man has nothing to say. The ink has run dry in the brain.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
