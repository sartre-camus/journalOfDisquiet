JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FRIDAY, DECEMBER

DREAD, DECAY, WEARINESS

Another grind under the city's perpetual twilight, another cycle of the hydra-static rain drumming a hollow rhythm on the ferro-crete. The emotional current of this place, it runs high, always in the red, leaving a man drained right down to the bone. Every flicker of the street's cheap chrono-displays, every distant thrum of the airborne cargo-hauls, every slick reflection on the perpetually damp alleyways feels weighted with some unspoken dread. It’s a weariness that seeps not just into the marrow, but into the very wiring of the soul, leaving behind nothing but a hollow ache in the chill, synthetic air.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
