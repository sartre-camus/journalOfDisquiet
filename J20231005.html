JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, OCTOBER

Hedonism, Hardware

My hunger for the high life is a slow-acting poison. I spent a king's ransom at a clandestine spa yesterday, a fleeting moment of peace in a world of grit. I’ve even ordered a vintage docking terminal for my E6430 rig—a heavy piece of tech crossing the ocean from the Old World. I’m tinkering with the remains of an old Thinkpad, turning its carcass into a shield for my desk setup. It’s a Frankenstein’s monster of hardware, but in the dark, everything looks functional.
