Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I am worried or anxious perhaps, to see people coming to my place. Especially, whom I don’t like to be around. I hate their presence. I’m supposed to put up with the all dancing, all singing, nuisance of the world. Playing to their tunes and rhythms of idiosyncratic nonsense and pathetic pleasures. Lousy as it might seem, I have to put up with this. I wish I could fly and go wherever the wind takes. I’m alone. That’s true. I have learnt how to be that way and carry on myself. These talking and bonhomie is another recluse. The truth is there is nothing. Nothing is all there is. Live it or not, it’s your choice. Choice of idiots. I hate that wry smile on those people’s faces whom I despise. I want to wipe it off their face: Symbolically. I’m a born rebel, just like every other human. I have tried hard to conform but I’ll be a rebel. A rebel to whatever that’s out there. I will think otherwise. But to fill words in this passage, I shall keep whispering words and note them down. I am good at finding excuses for not doing something. I guess that’s the way it is.
