JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

The Born Rebel - SUNDAY, OCTOBER

Rebellion, Solitude

The presence of others is a weight I can't carry. I despise their rhythmic, pathetic pleasures—the Circus of the Damned. I have to play their tunes, but I dream of flying with the wind. I am alone, and I have made peace with that. Bonhomie is just a mask for the void. The truth is that there is nothing, and nothing is all there is. I hate the wry smiles on their faces, the conformist masks they wear. I am a born rebel, a man who will always find an excuse to not join the dance.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
