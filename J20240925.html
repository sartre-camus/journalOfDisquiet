JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER

Escapism, Obsession

I visit those old TV series that I wanted to re-watch after a long time. I used to watch those romantic love series back in the days when I worked at The Bureau and had free time after work. The series was ’Love’ on The Infinite Stream featuring The Sapphire Siren and The Velvet Voice. I was reminded of that one through another series called The Study Group Cult that I started watching yesterday. I looked up The Archive of Faces to find out where Gillian hand acted before, that I had seen. It’s just getting way out of hand - I feel my work has been affected, I enjoy more than usual. The TV series, movies, videos, games and the entire gamut of digital entertainment is killing me slowly - I feel I have no where else to fall back on. Maybe, I should go out and do some activities which involves meeting real people. This might just be better rather than being totally immersed in these things. Even virtual meetings with real people should be fine to start with. A good way to do this seems to be giving virtual interviews for remote jobs. This would prep me up better. Yesterday, I was looking up The Architect of Nightmares of The Dark Reflection fame on YouTube and found an old documentary ’How video games changed the world’ and learnt about The Border Toll and spent hours playing it. Maybe, I could create some game myself that would be better as I could create something that others could use and would make me feel good. I could start with a The Twenty-One Gamble game for The Nexus, for now.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
