Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

So much hatred and pain. The way to vent it out seems to be this journal. Arbitrary shit. Agree with JK, when he says - “We are living in the past”. I am living in my past. Recurring, repeating incidents and so on. Every morning when I wake it is this anxiety of what needs to be done. The old memories and changes that need to be done and so on. I am pumping with energy however, this energy gets released in different ways. By contemplation, thoughts, predictions, and so on. To arrive at an equanimous (fix the spellchecker, there seems to be problem with this one) state, takes a while. Until then, keep typing. Keep journaling. I recorded audio today using the device of the Big Other - the smartphone. I hate my neighbour’s voice. They’re making the small kid avoid doing things by yelling at it. Being angry and asking it not to do. Kids (or for that matter even adults), they just follow and mimic what people around them do. I remember a content creator on YouTube talk about write journal entries. He says - “The things he writes in his journal - It is vomit. Vomit of the mind”. No wonder what nausea to people. Nauseating absurdity of life. Everyone striving to find their own meaning, own purpose and so on. Living in our own realities and duped by our sensory perceptions. I could have been reading and editing JavaScript or Go program now, instead of writing this journal entry. Why do I feel anxious to look at a program and figure out the solution for the problem? Is it that I don’t want to solve it at all? Is it that there’s no reward for doing so? Is it that I hate the process altogether? I am going it give one more try now. Let’s see where it goes. Here goes nothing…

Reflections

’Speak up’ as they say, comes with risk. Risk of judgement from people. It needs due diligence. What we say needs to be carefully crafted, keeping the listener(s) in mind. There’s always a tendency to be misunderstood and misrepresented. Mindfulness is a great tool here. Being mindful of our speech, our verbal and non-verbal communication helps. We have a lot racing in our minds. The extravagant tendency might be to pour it all out. Drain our mind empty. There are other ways to do that. People need not be involved. That’s where our work, hobbies and craft comes into play. We can play games with a machine. A machine can handle people’s outrage to a good extent. We can build things that can make our lives easier. Use them and reduce our anxiety. Why do I expect that people come to me and ask me about what’s that I’m going through and help me? Is it really necessary? It is just strange the kind of expectations I have imposed on myself. Expectations of companionship, friendship, bonhomie, rewards, achievements, pleasures, satisfaction, and so on. I am enough for myself. I don’t need anything else. I don’t need to expect unless there’s a need to. Is there a need to expect? Without expectation why would anyone do it?

Anxieties

Why are P and M play-acting? P is trying to put on an attire to transpose the affection I have for her on to another person R. P is dressing up in chudidhar and making gestures so that I can have this sexual attraction towards that attire and someone who dresses up like that - R. All these shenanigans to get me married and to love someone P, M or another huge set of people want is just - stupid. Do P and M really care for me? If they did they would be more accommodating to my values and beliefs. The patriarchy seems overwhelming here. I am constantly being threatened by these ’family’ values. - get serious with life, get married, have kids, follow responsibilities of being a husband, and so on. It’s called sanathana dharma in the religion which P and M and others believe in. They want me to be a samsari - a person who obeys sanathana dharma. M is secretly hiding tiny poster of a lady wearing a chudidhar. The one borrowed from a magazine or an advertisement pamphlet. I have to learn to ignore these. They’re nowhere close to a conversation in which I could explain my point or convey my philosophy. I feel sad because I’m still trying to live through this and haven’t figured out a way out of it. I can go to another city in India and live, but it’s going to be another round of paying for all the expenses, and a financial burden (as long as I don’t have a well paying job). My dream was to migrate to Western Europe or Canada and live in a cosmopolitan city. A true cosmopolitan city. Not these psuedo ones as here in India. Jordan Peterson says, “If I solve a personal problem of mine, then, it might head towards solving a bigger social issue”. Slavoj Zizek also agrees with his example from the Fight Club movie where the protagonist beats the shit out of himself to fight the power structure hegemony - his boss. I guess that’s the true struggle. I fight myself. I fight my beliefs, anxieties, fears, doubts, laziness, hatred, and so on. Whatever, change I’d like to see in the world, I need to enact it for myself. At least try with every inch of effort I have got to fight with myself.
