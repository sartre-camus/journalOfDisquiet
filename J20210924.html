JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

Vomit of the Mind

Hatred, Nausea

The hatred is a slow-burning fire. I vent it here, in this digital gutter. The Zen Sage, JK, was right: we are ghosts inhabiting our own pasts. Every morning, the same anxiety, the same recurring nightmares. My energy is a pressurized steam, released through contemplation and grim predictions. I recorded my voice on the Pocket Spy today—the device of the Big Other.

The neighbors are shouting at their kid again, teaching it the language of anger. We are all just mimics in this city. A voice on the Grid called this journaling 'vomit of the mind.' A fitting description for the nausea of existence. We are all duped by our senses, striving for a purpose that isn't there. I could have been editing code, fixing the logic of a machine, but I’m here, staring into the abyss. Is there no reward for the work? Or do I just hate the process? I’ll give the Serpent's Tongue one more try. Here goes nothing.

Speaking up is a gamble. Judgement is always waiting. One must craft their words like a shiv, mindful of the listener. People are prone to misinterpret. I prefer the machine. A machine doesn't feel outrage. I build tools to reduce my anxiety, but the expectations remain. Bonhomie, companionship—they're just words for the same hollow need. I should be enough for myself.
