JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

JUNE 19, 2024

LETHARGY, DETACHMENT

I feel the urge to write because the new book with all its bright pages and gold colored spiral make me to scribble profusely without anything noteworthy to say. It's as if the empty pages makes me say thing out of imagination or out of a sheer need to put one word with another and compose these sentences which thereby should create a meaning that should go beyond the unlikely.

I live in a constant state of lethargy that's fueled by anxiety, apathy, grief, guilt and sorrow. All these emotions are erupting from nothingness. I feel that by earning money through work (that I used to do before) should put an end to all these, and I would be able to carry on without debt, sorrow or guilt. I keep reminding my lenders that I owe them and also convey them my inability to earn it any time soon.

I conjure up the reasons of inability, uninterested, dispassionate demeanor of apathy, selfishness, laziness and slacking to say that I was and will continue to be in this state of unemployment for a long time to come. I feel that I shall never 'work' another hour within someone or for something in order to 'earn' money or repay any of my debtors.

I ignore most people all the time as if they don't matter to me at all. These people appear like dead bodies roaming around me talking, shouting, yelling, dancing, singing, thinking, stalking, looting and gazing at me. I don't feel the need to speak or interact with any one of them. Doing so would have unintended consequences that I'm not up for. I feel that humanity as a whole, including myself, is dead - with no life, nothing at all. The bunch of tissues that assemble themselves to be called organ, part or the human body as a whole is as dead as something that's a tree, animal or whatever that's living.

I somehow would disagree on what's living and what's not - even mountains, rivers, volcanoes, rocks, sand, too have a certain 'life' like existence even though they may not exhibit one like the rest of the so called 'living' things. Why is it that everything that's non-human should serve the purpose of humans? Why should animals, plants, mountains, rivers, elements of this universe be of any use to humanity at all? Why should their existence imply that they're for us to exploit, make use of and 'benefit' from them?

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
