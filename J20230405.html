The AMEX Betrayal - WEDNESDAY, APR

finance, sickness

The payment for the GitHub Sponsors failed—the AMEX card, a piece of plastic that’s more trouble than it’s worth. My plans for that coin evaporated like smoke in the rain. To top it off, a half-boiled egg has my stomach in knots; I had to drown the nausea in orange juice. My lungs are full of the city’s grime, a persistent wheeze that has me popping Montair FX like candy. The weather is shifting too fast, a cold front that my body can't outrun. I'm stuck in wait-mode for the next contract, and the search is turning up nothing but dead ends. Most days, I’m not even sure what I’m looking for anymore.
