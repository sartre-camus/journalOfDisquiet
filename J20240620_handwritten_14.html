JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, JUN

NIHILISM, ABSURDISM

Nothingness I yearn for—not an easy one actually. It's perhaps possible only when my belly is full and enough necessities are met. Yet every moment ceases to play this in tandem with the rhythms of the passing wind and movement of the Iron Sphere, the Outer Dark, the Tether, and whatever that's all there.

This isn't an exercise to wear myself out or live that undecided, exhausted, enlivened, or otherwise of them all. I don't owe anyone anything, neither does anybody owe me anything. The feeling of empty—emptiness of nothingness—no scrap, no ghost-code, no soot, no clockwork, nothing—it's that what I desire—the empty in contrast with that which is full—empty.

Why is it that the language of empty varies and all that seems relative in comparison with something—a steady comparison of that which is there and yet disturbs by being part of a duality. The observation should simply cease as there's nothing that could ever be taken or given—absurdism. Be absurd, let there be an absurdity as paramount of truth itself and destroy what's not there and yet...

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
