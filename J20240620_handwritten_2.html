JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE SIXTH CYCLE, YEAR ZERO

LETHARGY, DECAY

I have a slight wheeze now—the air outside is as choked with toxins as it is in here. I might as well rot in this chamber as go anywhere else.

It is difficult to confront the magnitude of my own failure, and this persistent, futile hope for material comforts and corporate services. What a tragedy. I intend to remain sequestered here until the end of the Sixth Cycle, when my membership at The Synergy Monolith finally expires and the last of my credits are drained. Even traveling to that place is a costly endeavor; I must treat a visit to The Monolith as a luxury, reserved only for High Cycles or Sector Holidays—days when the thermal recirculators are silent, and one can do nothing but walk the halls and return.

I am devoid of ideas as to what I should do anywhere. I refuse to touch the electronic devices; they consume too much of the Grid’s lifeblood. I will not waste power on the Core Unit.
