JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, JUN

NIHILISM, ABSURDISM

be dead, death by death itself - die all along and remain in the state of death.

Dignity of labour - the dignity of being a nobody, an absolute nobody, all the items, all the resources that just wave and flood my life to simply disappear into nothingness. All that there exists are simply words, no feelings or thoughts or the idea of nothingness.

I have been an impostor, all that I've got is a fake appearance, fake knowledge, fake wisdom and fake demeanour. Nothing that could ever come out of me is genuine. There is a pointless desecration - why do I even exist, as some contingent form of banality? As an absurdism.

The death of death itself - the denial of life and creation of absurd.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
