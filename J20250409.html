JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE GILDED CAGE - WEDNESDAY, APRIL

Avarice, Stagnation

I didn't realize I was such an easy mark for my own greed. The moment the gears stop turning, I start dreaming of ways to burn through the credits. A thousand ways to splurge, a thousand useless services to consume. I’ve developed a pathological distaste for the digital weaving that used to be my life. I want the harvest without the tilling. I treat the digital tapestry like a sacred relic, afraid that if I touch it, the whole illusion will shatter. So I eat, I sleep, I drift. I’m obsessed with the 'New'—new faces, new cages, new distractions. But the 'Old' isn't the enemy. My old handlers, my aging terminal, the familiar grind... upgrading the casing doesn't fix the internal logic in my soul. Progress is just a word we use to justify the restlessness.
