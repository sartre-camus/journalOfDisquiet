Journal of Disquiet

Didn’t know that I could be so easy money. No matter what I do, the moment I sit idly I think of various ways to spend money that I have. All the wierd ways in which I can splurge and consume services. For whatever reason, now I simply cannot figure out what else to do - apart from not programming. I wonder how I ended up being in a position wherein I have started to hate programming so much. It is as if I want to consume the fruits of it and not put in the work needed. I refuse to do the hard toil necessary to get things in flow. I refrain from modifying codebases as if it is some holy grail that I am not supposed to touch. I eat and slouch and sleep and doze off all the time. I still don’t understand my obsession with the new: new things, new people, new surroundings, new activities, new whatnot. Let me be fine with the old, they still are not so bad as I see them: old parents, old laptop, old work, old car, old home and old whatnot. Just because I upgrade to the new doesn’t mean I am really making a progress. After all what one defines as progress is subject to critique.
