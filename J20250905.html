JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FRIDAY, SEP

Wisdom, Stagnation

The need to construct a monument in the rain is beckonging me. I find it difficult not to ignore that and go on. Unfortunately, there are just so many things running in my head that prohibits me from doing those. It somehow intuitively feels that not doing anything is perhaps a better option than doing anything. This is a crazy idea and demeaning, yet, I somehow hold on to the gospel of the gutters. I want to be slow in everything that I do, I have no urgency to even live, let alone die.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
