VICE, STAGNATION

I enjoy the pleasures fearing the aftermath—the growing troubles and credit-debt. My parasitic behavior won't go away—it's here to stay. The nights are longer as I see them so to navigate through. I belch for the rations I ate, filling myself to the throat or much down the digestive tract.
