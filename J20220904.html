JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SUNDAY, SEP

Conflict, Ideology

I sat down with the big boss of 'The Depot', a man they call 'The Architect'. Trying to explain the 'Free Wire' philosophy to a man who owns the grid was like trying to sell ice to a drowning man. We shook hands on a deal, but the ink felt wrong. I’m tired of playing by their numbers, trying to weigh my soul on their rigged scales. You can't measure a man's worth in percentages, but in this city, that’s the only language they speak.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
