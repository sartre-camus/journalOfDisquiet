JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, JUNE

ISOLATION, FUTILITY

The Neural-Grid-Hub is still facing the access-token error that needs to be resolved. I have to get that and after that the rest—it seems like a lot of work. I feel desperate now as this is becoming tiresome—for some reason I feel like spending those credits, as if I do not need them anymore. I am forgetting the supplies I still need to pick up from here. I can't think of any more data-slabs that I would have to carry around.

I just feel like putting my hover-pod at Sector D's outpost, in that docking spot that he has, but I'm not sure whether it would be of any use—I'd still have to walk all the way there to get the pod. Perhaps, now I could just drive there and dock it there, if it's vacant. The problem at the hab-unit is that it won't be easy to move around. The drawback is that I would have to clean his cycle-drone in that process. Still, it would be a walk all the time if I were to go there and start the engine—quite useless I must say.

I have initiated that data-backup now—it should finish in an hour or so, if everything goes well. The ambient temperature must be low enough to prevent the core from overheating. All the things that I think I need, yet they all are quite useless now. What's the point of going on wanting these all the way? Rubbish as it seems, pointless as it seems, it keeps eating me from within.

Is this writing really that therapeutic, I wonder? Just because words flow onto this surface, will it prepare me for the worst that's going to happen? I have no idea. Worst—it is always the worst that I could possibly think of.

It's already seven days—a week—since I transmitted that credit-requisition. It seems all that currency I expected will not be there after all. I guess my only option is to become a shut-in and stay within the confines of my hab-room—all this energy, enthusiasm is proving to be toxic, mostly detrimental to my being. I come here so that I could observe the citizens—what a tragedy.

Only if I could carry myself better—all those manifests that need to be printed, yet remain unprinted. I need to create them and have them all. I'll try 'The Nihilist's Codex', put two pages on one side and make it back-to-back, long edge/short edge, not sure which one. Get that book; it will read better on the physical paper and not on an E-Reader Slate.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
