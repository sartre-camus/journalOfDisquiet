JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, MAY

MOBILITY, CHOICE

I’ve set up shop at The Manyata Vault today, ditching the RMZ Sector I’d planned for. Driving the car felt like the only sane way to reach this place without melting into the pavement under the midday sun. The game is getting unpredictable—every morning it’s a question of which hub I’ll haunt. I’m just making the easiest moves I can. Is that all I’ve got left? Convenience?

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
