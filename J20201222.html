Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Schlep the hell outta my way?? Scare of the pandemic makes me worry about my being. Health, immunity and others have never been of so paramount importance. The fact that the life is limited and our experience of it is going to end at some point makes it even more unpredictable and fragile. Life is limited; our journey ends some day or the other. The expectation of living, death is just another wasteful exercise. Utter rubbish, utter rubbish in all there is. Looking at code in one monitor and a movie web page on another. We continue to survive for no reason beyond survival itself. The exercise to be there, just there in nothingness of being. What is feat? Fear of suffering, fear of death, fear of others and so on? Everything for avoidance of everlasting fear. can one embrace fear? Live the fear, succumbing to it and yet manage to thrive through it and continue as usual? And we write amidst our fight for freedom and survival, what we are is nothing in the face of externalities. Crumbling, falling and bellowing into the limitations of our being imposed by external agents. Stretch as one may to their imagination and power of thought , we face the utter nothingness of existence.
