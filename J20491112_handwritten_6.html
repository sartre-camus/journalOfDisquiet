JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FRIDAY, NOV - THE SYNAPTIC DRIFT

INERTIA, PSYCHIC FRAGMENTATION

Instructing the uninitiated again—it is the ultimate transgression against the self. Even if the transmission of data meant liquidating a debt to the Syndicate, I must resist. Never offer to upload knowledge, whether for credits, influence, or any other currency of the sprawl.

The hardcoded loops of my psyche remain unbroken. I struggle to engage in the analog diversions I once curated: pigment-smearing on canvas, decoding ancient physical codices, tending to the synthetic flora in the hydroponics bay, or chromatic therapy. These human relics feel distant.

Why do I succumb so frequently to the sensory-feed, that hollow distraction, when I know with absolute certainty that it lacks the substance of true creation? The act of crafting—of building something from the void—is the only real resistance.

Neural-sequencing is a craft I must master, a discipline I must embrace. Yet I drift. My consciousness is a rogue agent, refusing to follow my directives. Call it synaptic decay or the limits of neuro-remapping—I find it impossible to overwrite these deep-seated behavioral subroutines.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
