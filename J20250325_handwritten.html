JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, MAR

DESPAIR, ADDICTION

The true struggle is the integration of chaos: uncertainty, systemic failure, disciplinary punishment, regret, grief, and the crushing weight of the void. It manifests with raw brutality, hunting me through the neon-lit corridors of my mind.

It drains my bio-energy, strikes with precision, and torments me before offering the hollow reprieve of synthetic stim-inducers.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
