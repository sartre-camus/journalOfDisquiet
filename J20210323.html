JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, MARCH

Consumerism, Focus

Engulfed by the constant thoughts of typical consumerism, I fight them to stay away from buying needless things. The reason why I consider them needless is simply because they can be done away with and still I can get to the objectives (if I have any). Recent example being the renting out of hot-desk space at The Collective Hive. The membership is simply a waste of credits, as I really don’t have any important work to do there. It’s simply my unconscious effort in trying to feel myself with some responsible work/importance that otherwise I don’t feel. It’s a conscious effort to run away from my surrounding Shadows - parents, kin, neighbours and other machine sounds. I realised yesterday that these should be treated as mere Static and ignored without hesitation. If I don’t do that, then it will just take over me. The voices in my head and the Shadows outside will start invading my space, if I allow them to, by letting them enter into my world. I need to keep them at bay and leave them, as I don’t have the time or bandwidth for such pointless chores and rummaging. I have spent a lot of time and effort in buying things and services for my work, however, I have failed in utilizing them for my benefit or profit till date. Had I spent all that time in actually working and prioritising my goals, I would have made significant progress.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
