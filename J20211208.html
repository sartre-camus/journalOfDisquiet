JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, DEC

Observation, Ephemerality

When my eyes settle on a thing, they cling, unyielding. I stare at the pale illumination of the silent mechanism's keys, observing its steady hum. I look through the glass partition and simply bear witness. I gaze at the vast, indifferent ceiling, watching the drifting specters pass by. I witness the slow grind of existence and find a grim peace in it. There’s a certain kind of beauty. Enough beauty, perhaps, to fill a lifetime’s bitter cup. All there is to absorb — to see, to listen, to taste, to smell, to simply *be*. No matter what shadow falls, I continue to live my allotted experience. What is pain? A physical ache, an emotional bleed, a mental torment, or some other nameless dread? I feel it, I endure it. What is pleasure? An abrupt plunge into oblivion, the chemist's whisper, or merely a trick of the gray matter, coaxing the wires of delight? My thoughts, these fleeting phantoms, seem to vanish even before they fully form. They just… fade into the haze.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
