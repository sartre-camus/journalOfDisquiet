Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

When I look at a thing, I keep staring at it for long. I look at the backlight of the keyboard and keep observing it for long. I look at the window and just witness it. I look at the sky and see the clouds passing by. I witness existence and remain peaceful. There’s beauty. There’s enough beauty around for a lifetime. All there is to see and listen and taste and smell and be. No matter what happens I continue to live my experience. What is pain? Physical, emotional, mental or some other? I feel and endure it. What is pleasure? An outburst of orgasm, a dopamine hit or a neurological activity for simulating neurons responsible for pleasure? My thoughts seem to disappear even before they arrive. They fade away.
