JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, NOV

ISOLATION, ALIENATION

The possibility of facing the ridicule of the collective is what haunts me most—the fear of being consumed by mediocrity amidst the mundane hum of the Sprawl. I can only scribble my way through these cycles. There is no exit from this, I suspect. To write is the only remaining function, documenting the minutest detail without purpose or objective.

There is a slumber that keeps me tethered to consciousness—it neither permits total shutdown nor full awareness—a digital insomnia of sorts, yet saturated with inactivity. Maintaining sanity amidst these internal signals and desires is a grueling task. I have no wish for the company of others; these logs are the only artifacts I possess. There is an overwhelming compulsion to remain reclusive from the prevailing protocols. I am attempting to shield myself from their influence.

I must cease searching and realize that the void is already filled. I already have it—there is no need to scan any other sectors. The ink on the blank slate must suffice; to look elsewhere is to invite only chaos, confusion, and the paranoia of the surveillance state. I must remember to transcribe the things I cannot confide to any sentient. Keep the data pouring, keep it running. There is no end to this torrent of thoughts overflowing in my neural pathways—this must be the extent of it; there is nothing beyond.

I must learn the art of the blind eye—the ignorance of the masses is a sanctuary. They merely occupy temporal coordinates and physical space, nothing more. Oh, the attention these citizens crave—deny them that validation and they scurry from pillar to post, desperate to ascertain their own existence. I wonder if I am describing my own condition, or if I am truly distinct from the rest of the hive.

This is the ultimate objective: a blank sheet of fiber-pulp, a stylus that functions, and a rest-unit to recover. I can now exist within the flow of the transcription. The environment could be devoid of sustenance, yet there are only my words to be archived here and savored. The necessity of concealment is absolute—eventually, the Enforcers or passersby would read this. The words cannot be scrambled or made more illegible than they already are. At least they would recognize a familiar syntax from the old world.

Now, I am attempting to purge the hunger that has made me over-saturated with the sensory-feeds. I do not require this constant influx of dopamine rewards to persist. I am prepared for delayed gratification. My biology understands that I must defer satisfaction in every form. The longer the delay, the more profound the clarity. It is part of the prevailing JOMO Protocol—the Joy of Missing Out on the collective madness. It must be embraced with the utmost urgency; to fail is to become a non-living entity with no agency or choice in the system.

It is far superior to write within the enclosures than in the open sectors. Here, the transcription is shielded from the distractions of the surrounding sprawl. Now, I can only observe my hand moving the stylus, the ink filling the void of the blank page, eventually exhausting myself through the sheer act of documentation.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
