Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I have decided to withdraw from the interview with Infosys. Even though they were offering a huge salary. It feels to me that I can’t lie on a resume and get a job later. I can’t just mug up some facts the day before the interview and spit it on the panel. The amount of rejections I have received is too many and I can’t continue it. I don’t want to blame myself for what happened, it just feels I need to take care of myself with forgiveness. I shouldn’t hold myself culpable for any of these. It’s just a bizarre thing to blame myself for all that happens to me, or for that matter, even the world around me. It doesn’t matter at all - There’s no point to anything just like there’s no point to nothing.
