J20230523 - TUESDAY, MAY

INTEGRITY, FAILURE

I’ve walked away from the deal with The Info-Corp Syndicate. The payout was huge, but the price was my soul. I can’t keep living a lie on a resume, mugging up facts just to spit them back at a panel of suits. The rejections have piled up like bodies in a back alley, and I can't take another hit. I’m trying to find a way to forgive myself for not being the shark they want. I shouldn’t be the one on trial for the state of this world. It doesn’t matter anyway—there’s no point to the noise, and there’s no point to the silence.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
