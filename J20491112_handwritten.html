JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

NOVEMBER 12, 2049

APATHY, NIHILISM

Sometimes, the feeling of apathy and nonchalance hits me more than ever before. I choose to remain hungry and torment myself towards the oblivion of nothingness. All I want to be is to remain aloof and disconnected, yet I am forced by the Collective to participate in the meaningless and pointless tirades of the Sprawl.

The least of all that exists is a point of concern, yet withering and decaying under its own weight. I choose to exist as if I am non-existent, as I am yearning to be out of life and death altogether. I prefer to be a nobody, a ghost in the machine with no history or future.

I do not, a failure in everything that was to be—a complete lack of motivation, energy, and whatever. I gloom in those dreary nights of Sector 7, dark and oblique, to pain myself into giving up all that's there and dry out the nothingness. The hunger is a constant reminder of what's due from me to the Ministry and the strife to forget it all—and pain myself into purposeless meanderings.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
