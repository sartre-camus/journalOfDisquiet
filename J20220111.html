Daily entry

Yesterday I saw a video from Alain Du Botton called ’Philosophy of Happiness’ featuring Neitzsche. It talked of going through hardships, failures etc. and facing it with courage and rise above them. The documentary featured Alain climbing the Alps in Switzerland. It’s only after reaching the top of the mountain that he could enjoy the scenic beauty around him. The spectacular and breathtaking views. It’s only when human endures and perseveres the suffering and misery to rise above it and see the bigger picture, that it adds a certain satisfaction, accomplishment and happiness to one’s life.
