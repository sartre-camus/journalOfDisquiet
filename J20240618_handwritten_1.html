DETACHMENT, DISQUIET

The 'Baron of the Badlands' archives were meant to be a random reference, but I dropped that idea on 'purpose' and moved on. Important works—are they still there or gone now?

Remember the other cycle regarding priorities, labor-cycles, and a cargo-hauler load of important things that were worth spending time on—where did it all go? Did it materialize into those sticky-notes at least? Or have they just vaporized the moment they appeared without a trace?

Maybe the latter. I hesitate to break the labor into tasks that have to be done, put a deadline to it and start working—it's as if, if I do that, I would stop from doing whatever that task was on creation. I give up...
