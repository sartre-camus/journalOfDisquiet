JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SECTOR SHIFT - WEDNESDAY, MAY

ISOLATION, DECAY, NIHILISM

Another cycle lost to the fever and the hum of the city’s failing grid. My credits are bleeding out, and the prospects in this rain-slicked hellscape are as hollow as the neon signs flickering above the Iron Docks. Where is there to turn when the only currency left is misery?

To be alone is the only truth in a city that demands your soul. I strive for some semblance of autonomy, but independence is a ghost story told to the desperate. I cannot return to the ancestral hab-blocks; the rot there is too deep, the air too stagnant. I drift through the shadows, a slack-jawed specter in a crowd of millions.

There is no pride left, no honor to be found in the mud of the lower levels. I look down, I grovel, and I accept the weight of the smog. No flight, no escape—just the rhythmic pulse of the sirens and the cold rain against the glass. I must stop the dreaming, stop the futile reaching for a horizon that doesn't exist. Losing all hope is the only true liberation. I’m not sure when that will finally penetrate your thick skull, ghost.
