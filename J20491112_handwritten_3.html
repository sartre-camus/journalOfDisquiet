JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FRIDAY, NOV

NIHILISM, DETACHMENT

Slag heaps, iron peaks, and the grey tide. Like any other relic of the Old World, I too exist. There's no need to attribute so many qualities to myself or demand for anything from the Sector Overseers. All my desires, anger, disappointments, mistakes, regrets, and achievements mean nothing and are as insignificant as the static on a dead channel.

All that scrip and what not, they're all nothing; they simply exist, just like I do. All ideas are simply a void that manifests in various forms to behave, show, masquerade, and portray as something. There is nothing to my fears or anxieties; whatever exists is simply a subroutine to protect this biological frame from harm.

There's nothing at all just there to do, to be, to show, or whatever. I simply write for the sake of scribbling on these scrap sheets; none of my experiences are of any significance, none of it exists, none of it thrives, none of it remains forever—it'll all perish just like myself. My data-cache itself is a deception, and the memory is meant for me to survive the next cycle and nothing more—it only...
