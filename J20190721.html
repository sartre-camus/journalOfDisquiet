Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

The people whom I constantly think, envy, worry, complain, curse and pity are a handful. I never have to do all these, as things shall take care of themselves. If I start writing their names here, I might run into few pages. Well, that’s the number of people whom I want to forget and even when forgotten they do not impact the way I could influence my future. They, in a true mathematical or logical sense have no impact whatsoever. The mind has always been projecting them to greatest proportions and always put me down. These handful cannot decide how I chart my future course. There are infinite possibilities ahead of me and I have the freedom to choose what’s best for me and go on. The past actually has to play a minuscule role in determining this. The projections are making it seem differently, which in existence is not so. I will not try to list down these names because, in doing so, it would be another drag or pedestrian activity, which can very well be avoided, as I understand what it is. Let’s not be driven by misunderstandings, the reality is always known. I shall not create these illusions and stories in air and put my mind at it. Doing so would be a futile thing to realise, at the end that it wasn’t worth it after all. An exercise that could have been replaced with something more productive, joyous and blissful.

Thoughts

Be an observer of what you feel and think, a witness of these happenings, soon to realise that they were all creations and manifestations of the mind.
