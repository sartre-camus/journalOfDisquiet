JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MAY 28, 2015

LETHARGY, DETACHMENT

Let us retreat into the thermal-shroud today and simply drift, sipping the synthetic infusion and being whatever the void allows—there is no pressure to be anything else but a ghost, as there is only the self and the silence of the outer sectors. All those mechanical sounds and heavy steps, they simply pass by and fade; there is nothing to focus on but the comfort of distraction. All that remains is one more rendition of a tepid path that beckons for no discernible reason. All I care for is to ignore everything that exists—perhaps just sleep and let the consciousness fade out.

The state of zero-resistance—the Zero-Point Protocol, perhaps. No resistance to the intrusive thoughts, no resistance to the jagged speech and the words that the mind splurges every now and then. There shall be no inhibition to the thoughts that flow and dawdle through the neural pathways—they will all be purged and cast away with no hubris, no surrender, no fear, no anything.

I have been listening to the old Jaxon Lunn anthem and it echoes in the hollow of my skull—'The Absolute is a concept by which we measure our pain'... The Absolute is a concept by which we measure our pain. Why must we simply dawdle away? With no retaliation to the walls closing in on our way?

There is no need to know anyone in this sector—it is best if I remain a stranger, anonymous, a cipher. That is the only way to simply lie down and keep quiet. All those words the citizens say, all those hollow conversations—nothing makes sense. It is all a farce, a facade, an ongoing tirade to endure. Why do I crave these interactions? Or rather, why do you crave them?

They say speaking to oneself using 'You' instead of 'I' is more effective for the psyche. You should let that poor soul called Subject B-N-R thrive in his own misery. Stop sending him more comms-pings and stop responding to his transmissions—this is a better way for you to navigate the collapse. Stop attaching or conforming to anything he possesses or anything you once shared. Let it all rot away in the archives.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
