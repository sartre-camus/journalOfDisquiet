Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

The leisure that I enjoy the most, I can’t give it up so easily to others by trading it for money. I can’t give up my freedom for exchange of some petty cash. I just have it all and the luxury of the company of myself. There’s no need to run behind anything or anybody. I’ve lived and lived enough to be living after continuously to live the lively life. The phone call yesterday with cousin V was a rather disappointing yet relieving one. I wonder why I wanted to speak about it at all anyway in the first place. Was there a need to speak about it at all, at all? With ’M’ snoring (pretending to snore and sleep) on the cot by the stairs, sleeping, hearing, eavesdropping to all that I was saying over the phone. I have made it quite easy and open for them to know what I’m going through. This may put me through some sympathy however, it is just going to cause some problems in future than what I could imagine. Trying to cover up and say the lie as I call it, takes enough conviction and practice. I can’t continue to do this for long while I figure out what to do next. The absurdity of all the needless, pointless, worthless phenomenon is just keeping heavily under varied chaotic circumstance that pressurize me towards making flimsy decisions and pathetic moves. I still can smell that old smell that came through this co-working space, which initially started out as a way of escape from the lacklustre office life I had before. The escape from mundane and morbidity.
