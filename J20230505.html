JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

J20230505 - FRIDAY, MAY

SECRECY, ABSURDITY

The leisure I cling to—I can’t trade it for a handful of silver. I won’t sell my freedom for a petty paycheck. I have the luxury of my own shadow, and that’s enough. No need to chase ghosts or sirens. The wire I had yesterday with V. "The Relative" was a disappointment, yet a relief. I wonder why I even bothered to pick up the receiver. Was there any need to speak at all? With Big M snoring—or pretending to—on the cot by the stairs, his ears probably pressed to every word I whispered. I’ve made it too easy for them to read my playbook. This might buy me some sympathy now, but it’s going to cost me a lot more down the line. Spinning a clean lie takes conviction and practice. I can’t keep this front up much longer while I plan my next move. The absurdity of this pointless, worthless game is a pressure cooker, pushing me toward flimsy decisions and pathetic gambles. I can still smell the stale air of this co-working dive, a place that was supposed to be my escape from the slow rot of the office life I left behind. An escape from the mundane and the morbid.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
