JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER

COGNITIVE DISSOLUTION, NEURAL STATIC

The ink has abandoned the structure of the alphabet. What remains on the parchment is the raw frequency of the Void, a visual scream captured in lead and graphite. There are no names left to fictionalize when the mind itself has become a redacted document.

I see the tally marks of the forgotten prisoners in the lower sectors of Neo-Aether. They count the days until the sun-simulators fail. Each scratch is a jagged edge of a memory I can no longer articulate.

The maps are fractured. The grid of the sprawl has dissolved into these winding, aimless paths that lead nowhere but deeper into the static. If the Overseers find this, let them try to decode the silence.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
