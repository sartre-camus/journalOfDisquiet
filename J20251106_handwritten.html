JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, NOVEMBER

ISOLATION, DECAY

The narrative persists as the cycles bleed into one another. The Neural-Link decryption protocols remain unanswered, and my Credit-Chits are evaporating like water in a Sector-4 drought. I wait for nothing, hoping the static of the sprawl will eventually overwrite my memory. I remain silent, yet I still find myself scanning the crowds for ghosts of faces I once knew. I haven't been a total misanthrope lately, but I should stop. Eye contact is a liability in the neon dark. It’s entirely unnecessary.

The support tickets from the Omni-Grid are pedestrian—why do I even bother with such digital drivel? I must learn to utilize the tools at my disposal and operate as if the external world has already collapsed. As I’ve noted before, there is no validation to be found in sending pings to the void. I will cease all communication. I will embrace the silence of the undercity and remain a phantom among the so-called familiar.

I need to stop reaching for those physical envelopes; they are a drain on my dwindling reserves. Those flimsy scraps of paper won't survive the humidity or the rough handling of my gear bag. It’s absurd. I’ve spent so long trying to be nothing more than a glitch in the system. Just because I provide the currency doesn't mean the world owes me a pulse. Even the Sector-7 companion refused my credits today, despite what I offered. It’s fine. One less tether to this rotting reality—greater autonomy for the ghost in the machine.

I am uncertain how to resolve the biological hunger. Should I requisition rations on borrowed credits and continue to consume when my frame is already burdened by the excess of the sprawl? The caloric intake serves no purpose. I will simply digitize these logs on the Data-Splicer and transmit them to my private vault. A growing collection of data-rot, sitting in encrypted folders, waiting for a heat death that will never come. My mind feels like it's expanding, pressing against the skull, ready to fracture the remaining architecture of my sanity. Hunger breeds a specific kind of rage. Who knows what the breaking point looks like?

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
