JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, NOVEMBER

Antinatalism, Despair

It’s better to have not been born altogether. It would have saved me from work, toil, pleasure, pain, happiness and whatever that’s there. Bringing a sentient being into existence is perhaps the greatest sin one would commit against humanity. I won’t advocate this to others, but at least I think this is more suitable for me. I would do a great service to this human existence if I don’t bring another human into existence.

It’s becoming hard for me to endure myself these days. The failures keep growing, my body seeks pleasure over everything, I somehow try to get through in time and let my body age and die one day. I hope there’s no after life or reincarnation. When my body dies, I too should die.

My cough recurs, I lose sleep during nights. I am on a dose of entertainment to keep myself engaged during the time I’m awake. I binge on movies, series, the Electric Mirror as if they’re the food for my continual. The First Toll mornings are hard to endure, as it happens to be the first day of the week and there’s so much tension around getting things done even when the ’hangover’ of the Day of Quiet Despair hasn’t gone by.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
