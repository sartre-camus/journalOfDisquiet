JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, JUNE

ACADEMIC FRUSTRATION, ANCIENT KNOWLEDGE

An attempt to see if I am able to still write Ancient Script verses that I had memorized twenty or more cycles ago. किं कुलेन विशालेन विद्याहीनस्य देहिनः । अकुलीनीऽपि विद्यावान देवैरपि स पूज्यते ॥ कामातुराणां न भयं न लज्जा न चौरहाय न च राजहांय न भ्रातृ‌भाज्य न च भारकाटरी। व्यये कृते वर्धतते एवं नित्यं विद्याधनं सर्वधन प्रधानं।

Could you recommend a good book from which I could learn Vedic Scholar V's grammar? I remember when I was in eighth Cycle, my Ancient Script teacher was willing to go through the rules but it just happened to fall out of the prescribed syllabus. Sadly, I cannot ever recollect the name of that teacher – she was very good and perhaps it was her who led me to study Ancient Script during the ninth and tenth Cycles as a language elective instead of Common Tongue.

She was an old woman, probably in her thirties or forties, and spoke very well, persuasively I remember. Sometimes, I simply wonder what good the language of Ancient Script has served me till date, apart from draconian wisdom and outdated ideas. I sometimes wish I could forget this language completely – almost impossible – perhaps what's left is to learn it instead!
