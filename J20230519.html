Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

The major past-time activity of mine has been writing diary entries. I have spent a fair amount of time just documenting my thoughts and feelings. Is this the way I should be going forward? Simply write down the things that come to mind and how I spend my time around. It has been a rather boring day today, just walking all over the NXT area and the hotel, with no purpose, whatsoever. The only thing I had been searching for is to find familiar faces in that crowd. I’m a little scared to go and talk to people - I wonder if I have anything worth talking about, at all. I would prefer to be all by myself and just look at the things around. I have no interest in talking to people around, whatever they look like or do as part of their work. I’m too tired to go forward, approach somebody and say ’Hi’. It’s just pointless. Yesterday, it was a little embarrassing situation for me, when I made the move to talk to the long white bearded person sitting opposite me, thinking he was a foreigner. I later found that he was a local person and just was struggling to connect to the local wifi network. I volunteered to help, but, it did not work - the computer wasn’t recognizing any USB device, his tablet including. So, I had abandon him saying, “I’ll see you later, and I will let you continue with your work”, “I won’t bother you anymore”. It’s just ridiculous how far I go to find someone who needs help and later offer that person that help. It’s just pointless and wasteful. Why do I have to talk to anyone at all, just for the sake of it? I can’t follow what someone did and expect that it would wonders to me, all the time! It’s just unfounded. I want to go back to my room, sleep for sometime and then start working from there, but it’s just too hot in there, I can’t sit there and turn on my computer and start doing anything. I also despise the voices that I hear there around my room. I would rather spend the day here at WeWork and Hilton lobbies rather than go back there to sweat in the heat and dust. There’s no soundproofing at all in this place, I can overhear everything what that person in the adjacent booth is talking over the video call. The heat is soaring day by day, and without any incoming rain, it’s becoming difficult to sleep during the night. The fan’s air is not doing any good for me but instead making it worse by blocking my nose and throat with its breeze. I just feel so sleepy - I can’t even sleep in here and go home later - I was thinking of renting out that AirBnB nearby for a day, few days ago, it feels like wasteful - the price feels expensive and also worthless. I can’t sleep there - the last time I did, I fell ill for two weeks or so. How can I forget what happened to me back then?
