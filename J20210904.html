JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

The Brother's Hands - SATURDAY, SEP

Family, Relief

I spoke to the Heavy-Handed Sibling yesterday. The years have added weight to his frame, a quintal of solid bone and muscle. My leg was a knot of pain, a reminder of the long hours spent in the chair. My kin suggested the usual chemical numbing, but the Sibling stepped in. I lay on the worn sofa, the dim light casting long shadows. He applied the balm with hands that knew the map of human suffering. He found the pressure points, the places where the tension hides, and crushed it. I’ve spent fortunes in the high-end dens of the city, seeking this kind of peace, only to find it here, in the hands of my own blood, for the price of a conversation.
