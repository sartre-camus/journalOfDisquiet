Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Oh boy! that’s what I would like to start my day with - a sigh, rather deep one. The last few days have been a bit too stressful for me. I extended the credit limit on G’s card, so that, I can get a renewal for my annual health insurance. I went to Seventh heaven spa so that I could get a massage from Kabi. After which I went for a luxurious dinner in an almost empty restaurant at this lavish resort. I wanted to take Kabi out for a dinner with me, but she said no. Asked me to save up. This was on Saturday. An entire evening spent out, all by myself, enjoying the pleasures. I returned home to find an unusual figure lying on the cot along the staircase toward my room. I might know that person from the past, a relative whom I wouldn’t want to remember. Anyways, that’s the moment I realised that my den is not mine, as I think it is. It is owned by M and he can decide who can come over and how long that person can stay. Also, for how long. I can’t live under the belief that the place where I stay belongs to me forever. It belongs to M and he can choose who he wants there and I may not be always supported by him or his wife P. That’s the sad truth or rather an inevitable one that I would like to live with. Yesterday was another long day, I had to starve myself a bit so as to compensate for the big meal I had the day before. I watched the cricket match final which eventually was intervened by visitors. A long forgotten person who showed up while I was watching the game with G. I just realise that the room that is my den belongs to me alone, however, it’s under siege by intruders and others. I should learn to give up on these and live with myself, on my own.
