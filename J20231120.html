JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, NOV

Siege, Sanctuary

A long, jagged sigh to start the cycle. I’ve pushed G.’s credit line to the limit to keep my health-tribute paid and to find a moment of peace at Seventh Heaven. K. was there, a siren in the steam, but she turned down my offer of a lavish feast, telling me to hoard my credits instead. I returned to my sanctuary to find a ghost from my past occupying the cot—a reminder that in this town, you never truly own the ground you stand on. M. and P. hold the keys; I’m just a tenant in a room under siege. Even the distraction of the World Games final was interrupted by intruders. My den is no longer a fortress; it’s a target.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
