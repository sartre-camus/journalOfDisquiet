JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

JUNE 20, 2024

ISOLATION, NIHILISM

Just refrain completely from venturing out—don't go out even to acquire nutrient-slurry, synth-flesh, or anything. Just stay indoors within the hab-unit and be—no need to interface with anyone in here—just read and keep recording. This is probably a better intellectual exercise to carry on and will be the most resource-efficient of all—simply live like a hab-dweller or more like a void-walker in The Neon Sprawl.

There's nothing to uplink, nothing to transmit, or say to anyone—silence is the best for me. All the chatter can be bled into words on these physical archives, and there is no need to speak to anyone unless absolutely required by the Central Oversight.

I should be careful with what I say—all that chatter is junk—simply nothing—all in all, a futile exercise to be someone and say something that sounds like wisdom. Just wait to get away with something or nothing—it's useless.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
