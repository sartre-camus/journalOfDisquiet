JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER

FRUSTRATION, APATHY

I carry a heavy burden of frustration, primarily due to my failure to secure a sanctioned labor allocation. In the eyes of the System's monitors, I am nothing more than an unregistered citizen, a parasitic burden on the Family's dwindling credits, barely sustaining my designated ward. I feel this narrative taking root within me, my intellect dimming with each passing cycle, while my hubris, lethargy, and apathy grow into an impenetrable shell.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
