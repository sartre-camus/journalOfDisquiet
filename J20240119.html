Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I feel overwhelmed by thoughts and monologues this morning. I feel it a way to exorcise my thoughts out of my mind. Why do I choose to do that? I’m not sure. I always find myself in the state of joblessness. I always fancied being a bum, an unemployed person, rather than doing anything worthy of earning money. I have enjoyed spending other’s money now and then when offered for free or on debt. I have always had some way to repay something but at the same time I also realise that I have had the desires that could never be satiated. I have pushed myself by spending money that I don’t have to enjoy or rather suffer later because of such actions. It’s my own self that hurts me more after these enjoyments rather than someone else pointing it out. I have created different personas in my head which come to life and speak to me saying what I did mustn’t have been done or praise for what was good. I have people in my ideas who exist and come alive, they speak to me and tell me. I have conversations in my mind with a projection of them. These are characters from the movies, videos, real life, books, games and others that I have seen and they come alive when I want to discuss a thought in my mind. It’s something like I have a conversation with this person and try to convince or explain and observe reactions from this person. I create these dialogues between myself and that person which I have created. They all exist in my brain. I don’t have a reason to write but I want to just pour myself out with words in bytes or characters on a paper. It feels liberating of sorts to have words on something, even though they are absurd after they’re written. The constant requirement of people around me to talk to them, to react to their reactions, to express, to say, to do and to participate all these just are so demanding and boring. They’re simply boring, expectations of working for the greater good, be useful in a society, live to die, earn, reproduce, reciprocate, laugh, cry, win, lose and so on. People just want entertainment from others around, in whatever ways, they’re too busy thinking and observing others as their own lives are boring, more mundane than anything that there is. I watch others, listen, read and so on, just to find some recluse from boredom. These characters that keep playing in my head from a movie. I am going to explore absurdism for a bit now.
