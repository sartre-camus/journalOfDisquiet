Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I feel so tranquil after yesterday’s extended sleep that I can’t let my mind sway. It’s too sleepy even now. I just could lay down and dream as I am fed up with my present reality. I am constantly being reminded of some past incidents and the conversations I had with few people. It always replays in my head and gives options as to what I could have done to circumvent a situation or maneuver with grace. Often these incidents recur, so that I can play it differently hypothetically in my mind and present an alternative scenario that is favorable. It manifests itself in different outcomes that changes according to my responses and also tries to tutor me on what should be done and what not. It’s like giving me a hard lesson on how to completely avoid people who cause problems to me, as adjudged by me. It teaches me how to identify stereotypes among people and how to avoid them from the very outset.
