JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FRIDAY, JULY

TRANQUILITY, REFLECTION

I feel so tranquil after yesterday’s extended sleep that I can’t let my mind sway. I am constantly being reminded of Shadow Plays from the past and the conversations I had with a few people. It always replays in my head, offering options as to how I could have maneuvered with grace. Often these incidents recur, manifestations of different outcomes that change according to my responses. It’s like a hard lesson on how to completely avoid people who cause problems. It teaches me how to identify stereotypes and avoid them from the very outset.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
