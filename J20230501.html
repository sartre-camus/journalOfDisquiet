JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, MAY

EXISTENTIALISM, DESPAIR

The hollow meaninglessness of this existence and my desperate, pathetic attempt to find some kind of retribution in old ideologies. A fool's life, spent chasing the best scraps from the city's table. An incessant, irrational need to just keep breathing, to keep pushing into the dark.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
