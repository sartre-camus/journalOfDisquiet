JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FRIDAY, NOVEMBER

EXISTENTIALISM, ANXIETY

...to survive and I wouldn't let my body be punished in such a way. It's so hard be mindful all the time—I cannot be in silence—a state of total silence with nothing to say or write or do. The state of complete inaction. My thoughts will bulldoze my brain and my habits will consume me—I am left with no control of mine. The dying abyss of boredom and the urge to remain creative amidst all the emptiness seems to grow on and on.

I simply cannot sleep all the time either, which I tried in the past few days; now the sleep can't do much either.

All those lies I say and believe in—I have no certain way to decide whether things are lies or not. Whatever they say in the Sector-9 or Void-Zenith philosophies are no way to be known to be true or false. I cannot know for myself either. What should I base my solution upon?

Writing makes me more anxious and sad; it's as if I am filling paper with ink that would've served some other better purpose. The way I critique myself, no one could ever do it. I have become my biggest critique and have tried...

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
