Purge, Identity

Strangely enough there has not been much change induced to this system. It feels as if I have made a transition to shifting between the various coded aliases of the underworld's digital static. I would not mind wiping the slate clean, purging the mainframe of every trace of my former self just so that I could try out a new distro. I feel so desperate to do that without taking into consideration the time put into restoring backups, downloading new files, installing programs and so on. I am willing to let go off all those without a second thought.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
