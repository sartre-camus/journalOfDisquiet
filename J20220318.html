JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FRIDAY, MARCH

FUTILITY, DREAD, DECAY

Here again, at the foot of The Gilded Cage, the rain-slicked chrome reflecting the garish neon glow of the lower spires. Just another futile attempt to outrun the phantom dread lurking within the four walls I call home. This brief, gilded reprieve, bought with the ghost of future earnings, tasted of synthetic absinthe and hollow promises. Another night spent watching the city bleed its indifferent light across the grimy pane. I need to secure a phantom commission soon, before the slow, inevitable drip of the clock drains me dry. Can’t afford to spend more time adrift in the endless, mundane drone, not when the shadows are always closing in.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
