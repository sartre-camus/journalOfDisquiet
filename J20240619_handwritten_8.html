JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

JUNE 19, 2024

EXISTENTIALISM, ISOLATION

My left leg's thumb hurts now because of that walk—reminded me of a survival journey from the post-apocalyptic simulation Wasteland Chronicles 4. It indeed feels like a survival run when I walk through those dilapidated roads with transport pods running haphazardly and chaotically with no respite. It is chaos indeed and there's no escape from it. To admit chaos in this universe is tremendously challenging; our memory deceives us constantly—it's a tragic story out there where every being is fighting simply to survive. I am no different—this programming shall not leave me no matter what.

To brainstorm ideas that build products or services and ensure that they're valuable to the masses is quite engrossing and tedious at times. All this cooperation which defines a civilization is quite daunting to me. I indeed need to be an Overman to be able to survive long enough. I had been reading The Iron Philosopher's 'The Prophet's Decree' and I was reminded of the Superman. I have simply scraped through the pages without much attention—every page being read at random as if it can be read from anywhere without order or continuity.

Makes one wonder whether I would be able to craft passages, prose, poems, or data-logs that are worth reading. I never had a target audience—never really mimicked a certain writing style, never cared to follow or learn one. Ultimately, it feels that I happen to write only for myself—for the sake of it. I do not wish it to be read—if it were really the case I would have made it more reader-friendly—which I forbid myself to do.

Forget that associate's union reception—I am not going as I don't wish to borrow credits for the commute or have any desire to feed myself a free dinner. I simply cannot imagine going through this traffic to any place in this Sector—also I am broke—I have no credits. I am curious to see if I have received a comm-link notification—who cares, I have already abandoned the device before coming here.

My base urges have not subsided—I still seek distraction through the visual feeds on the Neural Grid. Recently saw a holovid related to addiction called 'Gratitude for the Exchange' and it made me think about it seriously enough. I have failed in genuinely seeing a person non-sexually, at least in the past few cycles or so. I was craving for a platonic relationship if that pans out—no need to objectify. Just want to look into their eyes and speak my mind.

After this thought, I usually transition towards misanthropy where I forbid myself from any human relationship whatsoever; it just would end up adding to my already existing woes. I can be alone and still not feel lonely—I have already discovered enough means and ways to get there. There's enough data-slabs in my locker that beg to be read and written about—also I keep myself aloof through digital entertainment.

It's late now, I feel the evening closing in; my guess is that the time is nineteen hundred hours or maybe eighteen-thirty. I am preparing myself for the walk back to my hab-unit—how much pollution, noise, and traffic that I would have to go through for the length of a kilometer to be able to walk back safely. It took me a financial crisis of sorts to be able to walk to this place, otherwise I would have come in my transport and coughed up two hundred fifty credits for the docking plus the fuel charges.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
