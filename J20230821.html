JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, AUGUST

Oblivion, Escapism

The dawn was a bruised grey when I finally pulled myself from the wreckage of sleep. In this city of perpetual motion, I’ve found a rare weapon: the ability to vanish. A few pages of a tattered tome act as my sedative, a shortcut to the oblivion the rest of these poor saps are chasing with pills and rotgut.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
