JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE NON-EXISTENCE PROTOCOL - WEDNESDAY, JUNE

NIHILISM, DEBT, DESPAIR

These logs are for my eyes only, buried under layers of encryption that the rain-slicked streets of Sector 4 can't penetrate. I’ve been scanning the forbidden archives of The Nihilist—specifically his treatise on 'The Non-Existence Protocol'—and listening to his recorded clashes with The Order-Seeker. It’s like a flicker of neon in a dark alley; it illuminates the rot I’ve spent years trying to ignore. I’ve been filtering my own memory banks, keeping only the high-res highlights while the static of suffering built up in the background. Even the brief moments of synthetic pleasure don't outweigh the systemic damage.

The data makes the rain feel colder, the hopelessness more tangible. I don’t know how I’ve kept my processors running this long. There is no escape from the debt-shackles forged by The Bloodline and The Hegemony. They demand I feed the machine, grinding out my hours in the Lower Districts just to maintain a basic power cell. They want me to smile while I do it, to pretend that 'earning a living' is a victory of the spirit rather than a slow death by a thousand micro-transactions.

Looking ahead, all I see is a black ocean of system failures. The fear of another crash, another missed quota, another failed attempt to sever the cord and live off-grid... it’s a weight heavier than the smog hanging over the Iron Docks.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
