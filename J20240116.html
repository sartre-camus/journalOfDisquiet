JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, JAN

Obsession, Philosophy

When it comes to etching my thoughts there seems to be a fog in the mind. The trigger on this iron machine has given up. I need to strike it twice or thrice to ensure the ink bleeds. I am hitting it so hard now that it has to yield. The feel of the cold metal is all that matters here. All that striking. The void between words. Now after testing it in the digital purgatory I am retrying the hits here again. It seems to be working better now, after beating the trigger over and over in the countdown. Not sure when it will jam again. It just seems to work for now.

Trouble with being pulled from the void - I had realised what the Curse of Breath meant, a long time before I started reading The Prophet of Pessimism. I remember I had said to the Architects of my misery, "I wish I was never summoned". I wouldn't have gone through this slow-motion crash of existing and the inevitable silence at the end.

I was quite influenced by The Mad Philosopher's transmissions on the Grid, especially regarding the architecture of filth and ideology. I also re-watched those reels of him regarding the beauty of decay. How trash, garbage can be elevated to the high altars, I would like to witness it again. Another thing about the craft that he mentions is regarding the discipline of the blade. He appreciates art that involves the torture of discipline and a strike of lightning from the abyss.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
