When it comes to writing what I think of there seems to be a mental block. The space bar key on this mechanical keyboard has given up. I need to hit it twice or thrice to ensure that it works. I am hitting it so hard now that it has to work now. The feel of the typewriter is all that gives in here. All that typing. Space bar. Now after testing it in the website spacebartest.org I am retrying the hits here again. It seems to be working better now, after hitting the space bar over and over in five second duration. Not sure when this will give up again. It just seems to work now.

Trouble with being born - I had realised what birth meant, a long time before I started Emil Cioran. I remember I had said to my parents, “I wish I was never born”. I wouldn’t have gone through this tirade of living and the eventually dying one day.

I was quite influenced by Slavoj Zizek’s talks on YouTube, especially, concerning toilets architecture and ideology. I also re-watched those videos of him regarding art. How trash, garbage can be art, I would like to watch it again. Another thing about art that he mentions is regarding craftmanship. Zizek appreciates art that involves discipline and craftmanship and a chance genius or a stroke of genius as they say.
