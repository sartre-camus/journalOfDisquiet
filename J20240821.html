JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, AUGUST

Sickness, Debt

Yesterday I made the dreaded visit to The Sanitarium to get myself checked-up and treated for the ongoing cough and weakness. I was prescribed a long list of medication along with nebulization as I was diagnosed to have viral bronchitis. Every disease makes me more miserable than ever and poorer. I have no means of earning an income yet and cannot possibly repay the debt to Gideon. My reluctance in finding a remote gig is costing me dearly. I simply don’t know what to do, even those small steps that need to be taken are seeming difficult and overwhelming. Preparing a resume, applying for a job, preparing for the interview, giving the interview and awaiting results seems a lot difficult than ever before.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
