JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, MAY

Friction, Road Rage

I slipped out of the Hive early, seeking a temporary death in sleep before performing my duties for P. On the service-grid, I crossed paths with a reckless rider—no helmet, no rules. We collided in a storm of words. I lashed out, screaming into the grey air. I stooped to his level, my anger a black ink stain on my soul. Next time, I'll take the longer path. Follow the protocols. It's safer for the machine and the ghost.
