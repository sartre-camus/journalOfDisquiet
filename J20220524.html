JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, MAY

Lethargy, Foreign Dispatches

Agenda for the day: Scour the ghost-nets for ten dispatches from the Western Sectors. I sent my dossier to the Luxoft Syndicate for a Senior Java role.

Boredom is my shadow. I seek freedom in the Western Sectors, yet my own lethargy holds the key. The mundane tasks feel like a mountain of ash. The Hive is too loud, the distractions too many. A game of TT offered a brief surge of power, but the boredom remains.
