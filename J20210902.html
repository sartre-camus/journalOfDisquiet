Journal of Disquiet

Thoughts

I wanted to travel the world with the money I had saved. Germany, France, Netherlands and Canada. Well what would I do when I go there? See monuments, visit restaurants, meet people, go on a road trip, visit libraries, or something else? Or maybe just sit with my laptop and browse the web. In Germany, I would browse their websites of what’s available, their movies, songs, shopping and so on. In France, I would try and do the same: sit with my laptop in cafe or some place and use it to browse internet. I would use laptop and internet no matter where on earth I would go, as long as it’s available - electricity, internet and a computer (phone, tablet, laptop, whatever electronic gadget that’s available). Due to the pandemic, there are travel restrictions, and traveling anywhere is a difficult thing to do, not as easy as before. So, what I did was I bought a subscription for a VPN service. It allows me to browse internet from any country that I mentioned before. I can virtually browse internet the way I would from another country. This made me feel like I was present in that country - Australia, Canada, Germany, Netherlands, France etc. I was browsing from an IP address from those countries. A city in that country to be precise. I was able to watch the Euro 2020 football tournament for free through a German website. All these are geographically restricted services, but can be bypassed using a VPN service. I felt that I was living in Germany when I was using. I don’t need an aeroplane, flight tickets, visa etc. to go there and experience the things they’ve to offer, all I would need is a VPN subscription. Strange, for someone who spends most of his time on the internet, this actually makes a difference. In my virtual world, imagination, I’m in Berlin or Sydney or Amsterdam or whichever other city I could think of, without having to go there. I know that in reality I’m still here in at my home, sitting in front of a computer, but the virtual experience is something that’s close to reality. It does satiate my eyes and ears, two out of five senses, but that’s good enough for me to make me believe that I’m actually there.
