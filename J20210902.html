JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

Virtual Borders - THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER

Escapism, Travel

I dreamt of crossing the borders with the credits I’d hoarded. Sector G, Sector F, Sector N, Sector C. What would I find there? The same neon signs, the same cold coffee, the same faceless crowds. I’d probably just find a corner and plug back into the Grid. In Sector G, I’d browse their restricted files; in Sector F, I’d do the same. The geography is irrelevant; the terminal is my only home.

The lockdown has turned the world into a series of locked rooms. So, I bought a key: The Cloak-and-Dagger Tunnel. It lets me haunt the networks of any sector I choose. I haunt Sector A, Sector C, Sector G. I wear their IP addresses like a stolen coat. I watched the great games through a Sector G relay, bypassing the gatekeepers. For a few hours, I was a citizen of Berlin, without the need for a visa or a flight. It’s a strange solace for a man who lives in the wires. My body remains in this cage, but my eyes are in Amsterdam. A digital lie, but close enough to the truth to keep me sane.
