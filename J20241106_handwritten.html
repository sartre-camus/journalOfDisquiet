BINARY TORMENT - NOVEMBER 6, 2024

PARANOIA, LOGIC, ISOLATION

The binary trap is closing in again. My mind is plagued by the extremes of a single directive—pushing the logic to one edge or the other until the system crashes. This black-and-white static is a torment, a glitch in my wetware that I cannot override.

I find myself paralyzed by the need for a perfect directive, a sound reason to step out into the acid rain. Yet, these ad-hoc, unscripted impulses—these sudden deviations from the routine—leave me cold with a dread that no heater can touch.

Last night, I re-initialized the archival feed of an old propaganda reel. The flickering light against the damp walls was the only company I had as the sirens wailed in the distance.
