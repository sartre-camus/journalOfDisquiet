JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

The Smuggler’s Pack - SUNDAY, FEBRUARY

departure, logistics

The escapade is nearing its end. It was good to be a ghost, away from the expectations of the home front. The apartment in Nagoa was a quiet sanctuary, even with the few bad breaks that cost me some coin. Now I’m faced with the logistics of the exit. There’s a spider plant in a ceramic pot that I need to smuggle through the airport. I’ll wrap it in plastic and tape, a makeshift coffin to keep the dirt from spilling during the flight. I’m worried about the weight limit—the 7kg squeeze. I have an old bag that’s seen better days; maybe I’ll leave it behind or tuck it into the big Puma carry-all. Or maybe I’ll just cut the plant back to the roots and hope it finds its soul again in Bangalore.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
