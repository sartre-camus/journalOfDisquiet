Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

It was a good escapade in all, being all away from home and being bothered about the things back there. I enjoyed living here in this quaint apartment in Nagoa. Only wish the couple of bad incidents that happened hadn’t taken place due to which I lost money. It would just have eased my debt a little. I need to figure out a way to pack that spider plant in the ceramic pot lying on the coffee table. I’d have to wrap it with some plastic and then tape it so the mud and gravel don’t fall off during the flight journey. Also, I’m not sure how many pieces of luggage they’re going to allow in cabin. If it’s just the 7kg limit then I’m fine. Hope they don’t impose on the number of pieces of luggage. I have my old bag to carry too. I’m not sure what to do with it. I hope to carry it back home and give it to someone else. Or even keep for myself. I guess I can put the pot and the bag in that big brown Puma bag and carry along. That’s my solution to this. Or else can I get rid of those long leaves from the plant by cutting them and later when in Bangalore, it might just grow. That’d be easier to package.
