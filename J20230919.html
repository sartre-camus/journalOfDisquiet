JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER

Regret, Discipline

Five A.M. is a cold, unforgiving hour. I was awake before the sun, hunting for a contract that doesn't exist. I retreated to the Forbidden Woods to walk off the weight of my failures, ten kilos of regret clinging to my frame. After the penance of movement, I haunted the lobby of the Grand Hilton Sprawl, nursing a bitter brew and watching the corporate elite glide by. My own machinery is sluggish, burdened by the excesses of a soft life. It’s time to burn the dross and find the steel underneath.
