JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SECTOR 4 CONGESTION - MAY 21, 2025

DECAY, DEBT, ISOLATION

The lung-rot persists, a heavy dampness in my chest that mirrors the smog-choked horizon of Sector 4. Every mundane flicker of the neon signs feels like a premonition of collapse. I could drift like this for days, watching the rain streak against the reinforced glass, but the credits are drying up. My access-tier to the Collective's inner sanctum expires in ten cycles, and the thought of the void beyond is a cold weight.

I refuse to be a debtor to the street-sharks. There is a bitter pride in choosing to be a parasite on the Ancestral Vault rather than a slave to a Syndicate loan. The Old Units still have enough juice to keep me fed, even if it means retreating from the sprawl for a while.

The atmospheric scrubbers are pumping out a biting chill, and I’m playing the stoic, shivering in a thin synth-weave because I left my thermal rig at the Origin Point. I need to navigate the mag-lev congestion and retrieve my gear. Perhaps I’ll bring back the legacy neural-sim—a few hours lost in the 'Grand Heist' simulation might be the only way to drown out the hum of the machines.

One final meeting with The Handler tomorrow. I’ll endure his analysis one last time before I sever the link and let the shadows take me.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
