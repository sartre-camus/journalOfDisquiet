JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, JUNE

Regret, Stagnation

I still wait for a lot of external events to occur. I find it despising that I cannot put myself to productive work. Most times I find myself hunting for Digital Fragments in The Digital Arena. Other times I play Omni-Theatre on the terminal. And other times I watch shadow plays online. This saga of continuing with 'recreational' activities all cycle long will make me feel worthless when I wake up the next morning. My goal will remain unfulfilled at the end of the cycle. My rigidity towards thinking. Lack of quality thoughts and corresponding actions makes me feel sick and places me far away from the goal. My empty mind can be very sinister in conjuring thoughts about my Archives, thoughts about what “could have been”, “what went wrong”, “how would I react and how should I have dealt etc.?” are bothering me constantly. I always have an alternate happening of a situation. An alternate version of things could have happened. An improved way in which it could have been dealt with. A wrong impression of my current situation and an incessant regret for my past actions which can never be changed. I aloof myself of the decisions I made in the Archives and try dreaming about things that could have had a different turn. These hypothetical alternate versions of supposed pasts are killing me. I can try hard to get away from them but due to lack of occupying, productive work, I instead nurture them to overpower me. I try avoiding any awkward confrontation of Shadows whom I hate and despise. I still cannot understand and say to myself that in reality these thoughts can seldom make me reach my goal.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
