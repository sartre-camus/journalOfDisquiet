Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I still wait for a lot of external events to occur. I find it despising that I cannot put myself to productive work. Most times I find myself hunting for animal pelts in a video game. Other times I play chess on the laptop. And other times I watch movies online. This saga of continuing with ’recreational’ activities all day long will make me feel worthless when I wake up the next morning. My goal will remain unfulfilled at the end of the day. My rigidity towards thinking. Lack of quality thoughts and corresponding actions makes me feel sick and places me far way from the goal. My empty mind can be very sinister in conjuring thoughts about my past, thoughts about what “could have been”, “what went wrong”, “how would I react and how should I have dealt etc.?” are bothering me constantly. I always have an alternate happening of a situation. An alternate version of things could be have happened. An improved way in which it could have been dealt with. A wrong impression of my current situation and an incessant regret for my past actions which can never be changed. I aloof myself of the decisions I made in the past and try dreaming about things that could have had a different turn. These hypothetical alternate versions of supposed pasts are killing me. I can try hard to get away from them but due to lack of occupying, productive work, I instead nurture them to overpower me. I try avoiding any awkward confrontation of people whom I hate and despise (through my created thoughts and imagination). I still cannot understand and say to myself that in reality these thoughts can seldom make me reach my goal.
