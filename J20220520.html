May 20, 2022

Chance Encounters, Luxury Rot

A night at the Hilton Spire with V. A strange collision of paths. I avoided him at the feast-hall, but sleep was a shared commodity. I used the chemical mouthwash at The Hive to mask the scent of the night. The Hilton experience felt incomplete, a hollow luxury. I should write a manual for my own survival.
