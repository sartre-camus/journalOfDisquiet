JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, MAY

INSANITY, SOLITUDE

The problem, as I see it through the bottom of this glass: I’ve been giving too many damns about what the world thinks of me. I should be giving exactly zero. Why did I bother with those people? Being a "people person" is a waste of my remaining time. They can all crawl back into the holes they came from. The only thing that matters is how I’m holding up before I eventually fade out.

The day starts with the same old routine—finding more of the nothing that’s already there. The idiotic way I mimic others, seeking a sense of belonging that was never meant for me. As if locating the invisible would mean something. It’s all garbage. I’m just looking for something that doesn't exist, chasing a character from a movie I never saw. The Great Ghost—as if I could be that someone, instead of the nobody I already am. Does writing this gibberish every night change anything? Am I just playing at being The Man of Many Shadows or some back-alley philosopher? It’s a ludicrous amount of insanity mixed with a desire for the void. I sit here and wonder how much more I can squeeze from this dry stone. I’m wandering from one dive to another, with nothing left to live for. The ultimate surrender of what I have to chase what I’ll never possess. Burn it all down. Plunder the remains. Leave it all in the gutter. Belittle the beliefs until there’s nothing left but the sorrow under these benches. The insane thought of speaking to anyone at all. Everything is intertwined in this bizarre, jarring way. Just words in a ditch. Pointless, lousy, and alone.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
