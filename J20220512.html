Daily entry

Returning back to WeWork Manyata after few days, to met the denizen here, and to while away my time here. Home became a bit too futile, so had to come back here. I will have to move my stuff out of here soon, the end date is nearing. I feel the A/C breeze after the brisk walk around the campus. Can’t ask for more, for a hundred rupees. The setting may not be hygenic and people there won’t follow social distancing, but that’s seemed okay to me. I had been to a humble setting for food. Now, my only hope is that I won’t fall sick, because of the food or the prevailing weather. Planned of getting a game of TT with Russ, but seems that he’s way to busy with his calls. Anyhow, I plan to write this entry without much regard for doing anything more productive. Didn’t make eye contact with Rajesh Hariharan who sits opposite me. Had offered me work on their Tech panel for Ford, but I chose to decline it citing reasons such as FLOSS software philosophy.
