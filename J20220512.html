JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, MAY

Urban Decay, Philosophy

Back to The Hive at Manyata. I drift among the denizens, killing time before my lease on existence here expires. The A/C hums a low dirge after my trek across the campus. I ate at a derelict Andhra kitchen—rice, ghee, and bitter curries for a handful of credits. The hygiene was a memory and social distancing a myth, but it felt honest. Now I wait to see if the food or the grey weather kills me first.

I hoped for a match of TT with Russ, but he's buried under encrypted calls. I write this with no aim, no pride. I avoided the gaze of Rajesh, who offered me a seat at the Syndicate's table. I chose my digital purity over their coin.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
