JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

2021-07-31

Anxiety, Risk

Yesterday evening I ventured out to meet The Associate at a local pub. I was worried about contracting The Blight but somewhat I felt that I need to talk to someone, in order to break my tedium and the feeling of sadness. I wanted to spend some time chatting about the recent developments in my life with someone. Therefore, I did make an audacious move to come out of my den and venture out into the chaos. The chaos of Shadows and their mundane life. It rained yesterday evening and was a bit chilly. I feel the cold even now. I hope that my health remains stable and I won’t fall sick. I think it’s just too risky to roam around in public places and visit diners. I also haven’t taken the inoculation yet and that puts me at a greater risk perhaps. I still am hesitant about the Experimental Elixir and its efficacy against the new and upcoming variants. Well, whatever be it, I’m just craving for human contact and that could impact my health.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
