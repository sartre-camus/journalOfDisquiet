Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Yesterday evening I ventured out to meet Arjun at a local pub. I was worried about me contracting Covid-19 virus but somewhat I felt that I need to talk to someone, in order break my tedium and the feeling of sadness. I wanted to spend some time chatting about the recent developments in my life with someone. Therefore, I did make an audacious move to come out of my den and venture out in to the chaos. The chaos of people and their mundane life. It rained yesterday evening and was a bit chilly. I feel the cold even now. I hope that my health remains stable and I won’t fall sick. I think it’s just too risky to roam around in public places and visit restaurants. I also haven’t taken the vaccination yet and that puts me at a greater risk perhaps. I still am hestitant about the vaccine and its efficacy against the new and upcoming variants. Well, whatever be it, I’m just craving for human contact and that could impact my health.
