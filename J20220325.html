Daily entry

Spent money, time and energy in taking up Coworking space here at Manyata Embassy Tech Park. Starting a new journey - a journey of software development, testing and documentation. The Open Source work shall continue on with the intention of being alone and working. Living the solo life with people around, just there, in the fray. The intention of learning and building. Engineering software to scale. The most desirable of all. The pleasure of value. The value of creation: software, programs, knowledge and happiness. Too optimistic as it may seem - we’ll try with whatever we got, whatever we could acquire and continue.
