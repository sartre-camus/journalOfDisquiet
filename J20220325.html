JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FRIDAY, MAR

solitude / construction

Spent credits and life-force securing a corner in The Monolith Tower. Starting a new descent - a journey of building, breaking, and documenting the ghost in the machine. The clandestine work continues, driven by the urge to remain a ghost in the crowd. Living the solo life while the masses swarm just outside the glass. The hunger to build something that lasts. Engineering the void to scale. The cold pleasure of creation. Too optimistic for this city, perhaps—but we’ll burn what we have to keep the gears turning.
