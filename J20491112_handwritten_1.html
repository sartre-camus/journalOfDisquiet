JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE BACCHANALIA OF THE EASTERN SPRAWL - FRIDAY, NOVEMBER

DEGRADATION, ADDICTION

Repayment of the debt on time. Why do I become so insensitive towards my own core? I behave as if the only way to spend time is through splurging credits on neural re-alignment and carnal distractions. This is not a healthy development and will make my existence more miserable for sure. I have sought the touch of at least three different companions in the last five cycles.

I am not here on a carnal excursion. I somewhat loathe that I am this pale-skinned operative from the High-Citadel, arriving with credits for bio-augmentation and carnal tourism in the Eastern Sprawl, drifting from one physical pleasure to another, willy-nilly.

My Techno-Scribe persona now suffers a great deal because of this bacchanalia. I succumb to base stims so many times in a cycle that it almost feels like I cannot exist without it—it just feels like I am addicted to the flesh, the digital simulations, and the companions. I had begged Vane to secure my financials by supporting my data-mining—this is another way in which...
