JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, OCTOBER

Avarice, Survival

Money is the only god that answers prayers in this city. I dream of a mountain of credits, the kind that buys you a one-way ticket out of the gutter and into the sunlight. For now, my ambitions are small, pathetic: paying the premium to keep the medical sharks at bay for another cycle. Beyond that, my wants have withered. There’s nothing left to buy in a town where everything is already broken.
