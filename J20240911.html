Journal of Disquiet

I feel disgruntled after watching the complete last season of Curb your enthusiasm. I also started re-watching Seinfeld after that: the finale and started from beginning again. I am getting drawn to binge watching again - I’m losing out on the reading that I’m supposed to do. I can’t be more irate than this. I feel irritable and distraught. I drown in guilt and make up that grumpy face again and again. It just feels so hard for me to get out of this as if I enjoy enduring it. I realised today that I’m giving myself too much importance despite knowing that I do not matter anything to anything in this universe. I have to accept this non-significance and live with whatever there is. It doesn’t matter how I carry on? (really?) I just don’t know. Sometimes when I use words I feel a bit ridiculous, as I may not be what I say I ought to be. I just fell I ought to give up talking and writing altogether. There’s just enough in my head and I can’t keep pouring it out every so often. I lose sleep, can’t be relaxed, I just find it difficult to commit to hobbies/things/work that I do and carry on. I feel scared about future as well as the present - it feels like I’m on an undesirable track - I can’t do anything that’s desirable - I don’t know which one is what. The words always cause confusion and fail to bring any clarity. I am struggling with reading tasks, note making and summaries: I worry about my laziness and slacking attitude - somehow that book by Tom Hodgkinson motivates me to be the biggest slacker in this entire universe, yet, I can’t do all that and not be worried. Today, I bonded with denizens even though I wanted to skill all of that. I wanted to give up on these, I wanted to never teach again but I volunteered today after initially declining it. I cannot repay my debt to G by doing his classes on his behalf. I cannot repay him that way. I check out the books such as Topics in Algebra by I.N Hernstein and Finite Automata and Formal Languages by Peter Linz. I solve few problems related to proofs by contradiction. Also, I attempt some proofs at an elementary level such as proving that there exists a unique circle passing through three non-collinear points using contradiction. I feel all ecstatic after proving that on my own.
