JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, JUNE

INDULGENCE, OBSESSION

Yesterday, I did not sleep well as The Crimson Syndicate’s maiden victory caught me binging whatever they showed. I had a lavish day yesterday, an expensive treatment, a fancy dinner and late night game watching. I have taken up a room for tonight at the Neon Asylum and will be staying there. I have no plans to go back to the base.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
