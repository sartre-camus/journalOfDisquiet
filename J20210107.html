JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE PLEASURE DEN - THURSDAY, JANUARY

Desires, Solitude

A new cycle with too many goals to look at. Started off with an unnecessary incident at The Pleasure Den. I shall not be going there hereafter. The kind of treatment which is not worth any credit I pay. Lack of courtesy and due regard for the guest. I’m done. A self-respecting individual should never be visiting such a place. No matter what my carnal cravings are, I shall continue to find alternatives to satiate them. I have come to a realisation that these relaxing adventures of mine need to end and it’s time I move away from them for good. I shouldn’t allow my mind to be polluted with these cravings and undesirable desires. I’ve got an exam of the common tongue coming up this Sunday which I will give my best. I need to preserve my writings, journals and diaries at a safehouse and shouldn’t let them lose or get into someone else's hands. I am not happy with the aura around my current stay. Just banalities of existence and talks of pointless meaning and stupid reactions to the ever-changing reality. I need to make it on my own. I’m trying to finalise a safehouse where I can stay on my own and work out my expenses in my budget and continue to experience the way of life that I dream about. How long will I be able to sustain with the credits I have currently? Not for long I suppose, six months maximum.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
