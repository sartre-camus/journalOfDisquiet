Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

A new year with too many goals to look at. Started off with an unnecessary incident at the local spa. I shall not be going there here after. The kind of treatment which is not worth any penny I pay. Lack of courtesy and due regard for the customer. I’m done. A self respecting individual should never be visiting such a place. No matter what my carnal cravings are I shall continue to find alternatives to satiate them. I have come to a realisation that these relaxing adventures of mine need to end and it’s time I move away from them for good. I shouldn’t allow my mind to be polluted with these cravings and undesirable desires. I’ve got an English exam coming up this Sunday which I will give my best. I need to preserve my writings, journals and diaries at a safe place and shouldn’t let them lose or get into someone else hands. I am not happy with the aura around my current stay. Just banalities of existence and talks of pointless meaning and stupid reactions to the ever changing reality. I need to make it on my own. I’m trying to finalise a place where I can stay on my own and work out my expenses in my budget and continue to experience the way of life that I dream about. How long will I be able to sustain with the money I have currently? Not for long I suppose, six months maximum.
