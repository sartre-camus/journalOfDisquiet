Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Now that I’m left with very few cowries in my pocket, I’d have to play it carefully. The old desires have to be channelized through other means of ventilation. The costlier ones have to be chided away, to let what’s economical and sustainable. The ways of desires and delusions can always be swayed. Enough courage and mental strength can do that. I have to literally pray to myself to be more austere in my ways of living. this brings in a lot of baggage - this way of lavishness that I’m relying to seek pleasure. The pleasure of women through their companionship and sex. I have to understand that these come with a baggage that’s difficult to carry and takes away the precious time away from my life that could be better used for my personal freedoms. Freedom comes at the cost of convenience. The conveniences of lavishness, sex, pleasure have to be sacrificed for me to be free. Free from the necessary mundane chores of life. The reason freedom is important because it dictates the amount of joy I could experience. Not having to be held answerable to anybody - government, corporations, society, parents, peers, friends, coworkers and strangers. The most important thing in the current day and age, to be free is money. There is no second opinion about it. The more valuable I’m to the society, the more indispensable I become, and that allows me to be freer than most others. Free will is by default, every human has that but, to exercise and enjoy the freedom of living, one has to be valuable - money-wise, knowledge-wise, power, health and so on. My energies should focus along these directions rather than the quick to enjoy dopamine highs which are ephemeral and unsustainable. To prolong the effects of dopamine highs, one needs more freedom, which means more money and being invaluable to the society.
