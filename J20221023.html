Austerity, Ambition

The bankroll is thin, and the streets are hungry. I need to cut the fat, stop chasing the neon lights and the expensive company. In this city, pleasure is just a trap with a high price tag. I want the real prize: total freedom. And in this world, freedom is spelled with a dollar sign. I need to become indispensable, a man the city can't kill because it needs him too much. I'm focusing my energy on the long game now. No more quick fixes, no more easy thrills. I'm building a fortress of value, brick by brick, until I'm the one holding all the keys.
