The Wayland Shift - SATURDAY, MAR

technology, minimalism

I’ve stripped the Libreboot X200s down to its bones and built it back up with Guix and Sway-wm. Wayland is the new law in town, and it runs smoother than the old Mate syndicate. I’m sifting through the backups, wondering which ghosts are worth bringing back. Most of it is just digital hoard—I’d rather start fresh, installing only what’s essential. I’m tired of the clutter. Getting used to the new navigation feels like learning a new city’s back alleys. I still haven’t cut the SSH keys for the repository access; I’m not sure I’m ready to open that door yet.

Getting accustomed to the new layouts and navigations in sway-wm. Not sure how I intend to leverage this new window manager for my work. I still have generate ssh keys for git repository access. I’m wondering whether it’s needed to that yet!

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
