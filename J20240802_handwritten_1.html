JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FRIDAY, AUG

INTELLECTUALISM, APATHY

Yesterday, I ventured into The Codex Vault after an elaborate session at the bio-grooming bay, to acquire a few physical volumes and analog pigment applicators. I purchased 'The Constant Refinement' by S. H. Vane and 'The Capital of Sectors' by A. S. Forge after scanning the dusty shelves. I intended to procure texts related to neural-architecture as well but decided against it after sufficient data-mining.

I spend credits far too easily on physical media I never actually consume. There are dozens of volumes lying in my storage cabinet that remain unread even to this cycle. I make little effort to process them, yet I am eager to lose myself in the visual streams of the Global Feed. I am attempting to cultivate the habit of reading more physical texts in this age of the holographic haze.

I must reduce the cycles spent staring at my comms-slab, watching mindless projections and data-bursts off The Optic-Stream. Instead, I must read, learn, write, and read again.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
