JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER

Conflict, Nostalgia

Back at the Hive, a sprawling concrete maze where the suits play at being rebels. Last time I was here, it ended in a standoff over a refund they didn't want to give. I had to call in the big guns at the 'Bureau of Consumer Vengeance' just to get my own money back. Today was a blur of high-society events at the Grand Hilton, some talk about the new 'Machine Minds', and a stiff drink with an old ghost named Ajai. The city feels smaller every day.
