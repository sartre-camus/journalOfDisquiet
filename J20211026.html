Nightmares, Ambition

A nightmare claimed me last night—the Lady in White reaching through the glass. A consequence of flirting with horror before the lights went out. G told me I was screaming. I woke up chasing ghosts in the Knowledge Vault, a bizarre instinct I can't explain. I’m being hounded by emails from a 'Python' peddler, a sham that smells of desperation. My focus is on the Imperial Bank—a role as a software engineer. I need to forge a motivation letter that is honest, brief, and sharp as a blade.
