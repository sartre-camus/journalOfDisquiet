JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER

Fortitude, Endurance

I don’t need money from anybody for my survival. The very fact that I have to be indebted to the Shylocks of the concrete jungle or even get paid in exchange for work makes feel like a cog in the City's machine. I lose freedom when such a thing happens. The best thing to do here is to endure whatever hardships life throws at me in whichever form: health, happiness, misery, suffering, pain, loss, etc. I can endure anything including death. I have the fortitude and I shall use it all the time.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
