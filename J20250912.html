Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I don’t need money from anybody for my survival. The very fact that I have to borrow from somebody or even get paid in exchange for work makes feel like a slave. I lose freedom when such a thing happens. The best thing to do here is to endure whatever hardships life throws at me in whichever form: health, happiness, misery, suffering, pain, loss, etc. I can endure anything including death. I have the fortitude and I shall use it all the time.
