JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE CORPORATE RE-ENTRY - TUESDAY, AUGUST

AMBITION, LUST

I've cut ties with the tutor-agency. I'm putting my profile in The Honey-Trap, waiting for the corporate sharks to bite. I dream of being back in the belly of the beast—the deadlines, the agile ceremonies, the hollow laughter of the breakroom. It's a soul-crushing grind, but it's a grind I know. My own self-control is a frayed rope; I'm a man of many hungers—for gold, for flesh, for the lies I tell myself to sleep at night.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
