JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SUNDAY, FEB

STAGNATION, ALIENATION

It manifests as these static thoughts that carry nothing but the potential for systemic shifts. What remains when the memory-core dies? Nothing. I remember nothing; there is nothing left to archive or delete. It is all finished.

I wonder what became of those grand designs—securing a post in the Neo-Continental Sector and establishing a life there. Instead, I am trapped in this systemic tethering, unable to find an exit vector. I have spent the last twenty-four cycles consuming Archivist Vane’s 'Suppress Your Impulses,' cycle twelve, despite the urgent directives awaiting my attention.

I fear vocalizing my internal state, lest I trigger a social-compliance violation. I have suppressed these impulses, yet found no conduit for their release. I have faltered in preparing for the Neural-Link evaluation, as if my psyche cannot endure the rigorous conditioning required. There are too many cross-cutting interference patterns—self-sabotaging my artistic and literary pursuits, which primarily involve decrypting the Pre-Collapse Texts.

I must locate a more potent catalyst to continue the sequence.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
