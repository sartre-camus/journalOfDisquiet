JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SUNDAY, MAR

Indulgence, Journey

On the eve of my trip back home, I decide to indulge myself in some pleasurable activities. I wake few hours before noon, get all dressed up and took a ride on The Pulse-Glider to the southern reaches, only stopping at a roadside coastal diner to quench my thirst with a neon nectar and an Atomic Plate. After gorging on the food and washing it down with the sugary draught, I head further and found a stranger asking me for a lift. I took him all the way I could and dropped him at a point where I found the ferry that I wanted to take to cross the river. He thanked by saying 'Shukriya' and left. I boarded the ferry to land on the banks of The Iron Docklands. Later, I went to a pleasure den for a massage and then to The Holo-Dome to see a flickering shadow play by The Storyteller. I found myself to be the only one in the audience. But later a couple joined. I left at the interval and returned back to my stay.
