Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

On the eve of my trip back home, I decide to indulge myself in some pleasurable activities. I wake few hours before noon, get all dressed up and took a ride on Aprilla scooter to Nerul and Reis Magos, only stopping at a roadside Goan Restaurant to quench my thirst by an orange mocktail with a Goan Kingfish Thali. After gorgin on the food and washing it down with sugary mocktail, I head further towards Reis Magos and found a stranger asking me for a lift. I took him all the way I could and dropped him at a point where I found the ferry that I wanted to take to cross the river Mandovi. He thanked by saying ’Shukriya’ and left. I boarded the ferry to land on the banks of Panjim. Later, I went to Snip Spa for a Balinese massage and then to Inox theatre to see a Kannada movie called ’Hero’ directed by Rakshit Shetty. I found myself to be the only one in the audience. But later a couple joined. I left at the interval and returned back to my stay in Parra.
