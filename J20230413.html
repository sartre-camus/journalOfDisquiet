The Four Seasons Mirage

aspiration, escapism

My Emacs setup is hallucinating—asterisks turning into question marks like a fever dream. I spent some time at the Four Seasons today, a glimpse of the high life that feels a thousand miles away. I’ve got a long road ahead of me if I want to live like that. I could have stayed there forever, hidden in the luxury, far away from the room I call home. The desire to never go back is getting stronger every day.
