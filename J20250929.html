Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I suit up and come here to the Hilton to get myself some executive treatment. I don attires to make myself feel the part of a business executive as if this is all that mattered to me. I should clear the lavish, at least that’s what it seems like, cheque here at Double Up, Hilton and head back home. Not sure how many more days would I have to endure poverty - even though I dress up like a rich, privileged, business executive, I have only pennies in my pocket. Pennies that would barely get me through a day or so at this expensive place. Wish I had won a hand at the roulette or blackjack table, a big hand, enough to splurge for a year or so. I so desperately want to sell away the sovereign gold bond and get the money back. Somewhere, I feel there is little intention from me to do anything beyond that. A fat bank balance is all that matters to me right now - the one that would enable me to a comfortable, independent and reasonably better life.
