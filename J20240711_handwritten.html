JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, JUL - LOG I

LETHARGY, SUBMISSION

All those memories gushing in, they won't go away. All of them just torment me over and over, even when I least want it.

I still am reluctant to identify any problems that I have. I refuse to admit problems, thereby eliminating them at the source itself.

I have started sucking up to The Proctor's demands. I no longer enjoy the independence I had, the notion of freedom is dwindling, I have no free thought of my own, everything seems borrowed and put away.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
