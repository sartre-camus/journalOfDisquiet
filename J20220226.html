SECRETS, DECAY, DREAD

The display flickered, casting a sickly green glow on the rain-streaked window. Another block of encrypted static, coughed up by the Net-Grille, impenetrable to any but the chosen few. It pulsed with the promise of twenty-nine K-units, barely enough to keep the rats from your door, or perhaps a life's ransom, depending on which slick alley you called home. To crack its shell, to peel back the layers of digital night, you'd have to drop a whisper-burst into the Obscura.mesh, aimed at the spectral contact 'Oracle_Grim'. Don't expect easy truths; the only thing certain in this city is the metallic tang of decay and the endless, drizzling rain.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
