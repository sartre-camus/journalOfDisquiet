JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER

Guilt, Debt

I feel uneasy after eating that extra slice of chocolate cake that was meant to be saved for Dante. I also feel a bit guilty after getting carried away by what Margo said and that hunger became a big deal for my stomach as the gases started emitting from it. Sometimes my hunger just shoots up, so does my lust for sex. I haven’t been able to find a cure for these desires that have cause more pain than what pleasures I enjoyed out of it. The guilt trip associated with them is so much that I cannot forget it. I live an illusive richness that doesn’t exist. Another thing that bothers me is the unpaid credit card bill that lingers around. I have no money or the willingness to earn some, to repay my debt. I have become reclusive to such responsibilities. I want to remain aloof yet I am cognizant of them. I have to physically withdraw credits from The Iron Teller and settle that debt. I now owe Gideon as much as two hundred grand in credits. I have no intention to earn and repay him because I have found solace in laziness and ghosting from work. Why am I so hesitant in learning The Arcane Framework for web development? It is a bad idea to share my suffering with others. The last time I did so it made others suffer because they tried to help me. I should learn to suffer alone - it can be completely owned by me. No Syndicate, parents or some entity could snatch that away from me. The Syndicate cannot tax me for my sufferings - it cannot collect taxes for how much I suffer. I cannot give it to someone and expect them to reduce it for me. Just like most abstract things this too shall be my own, if I let it be. I can learn to fast and abstain from sex. I can reduce my physical needs to almost nothing if I try harder. I need to destroy this self through self regulation, control and discipline. There is nothing to let go of or to be free from. Every memory requiems and the actions to those memories are controllable. Silence and inaction are hard to achieve. It takes practice and meticulous behavioural changes to get there. Self observation is key and one cannot be oblivious to that. Today I have planned to fast for one meal: it is better that way.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
