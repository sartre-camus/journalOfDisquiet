JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, FEBRUARY

Ambition, Transition

The demons of the archives resurface in the form of Bureaucracy Roles and their 'secure' nature. For some reason, a role at The Central Eye for Data Surveillance as a Rank-C Data Scribe intrigued me. Mostly because of The Shrouded Basin location and the credits. However, the job profile has nothing to do with architecting code. There is no Digital Construct development or any such work but mostly academic administrative work. How could I ever think of taking up such a role? I cannot give up my ambition and craft of coding at any cost. That’s the skill I would like to hone and be proficient at. I should be self-aware of my biases and inclinations. Going further, I should commit to my work of software development and coaching. I need to understand my desires and handle them well. Being mindful of my behaviours and thoughts is essential to growth and well being of myself and my career. If I can take up a role in The Shrouded Basin, it might as well be a position in a software syndicate as a Senior Architect, rather than at an institute being a glorified clerk. I will be travelling to The Neon Coast for a moon cycle starting this Sunday. I have spent about 15.5K credits on accommodation and travel for now. I guess I would spend another 15K for food, travel and return. That’s about 30K for 30 days, roughly. I plan to explore the shores, go on long walks/runs and get some fresh air. The weather must be better than here and we’ll be having a pleasant time as opposed to The Emerald Metropolis. The time spent there brings about a change, hopefully, in my living and work. I wish myself a safe and joyful trip. I should try and bag a well-paying remote contract when I’m there. That’ll get me to be free and independent.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
