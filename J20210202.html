Journal of Disquiet

The demons of past resurface in the form of Government jobs and their ’secure’ nature. For some reason IUCAA job for Scientific and Technical Officer - C intrigued me. Mostly because of Pune location and the pay. However, the job profile has nothing to do with CS work. There is no code development or any such work but mostly academic administrative work. How could I ever think of taking up such a job? I cannot give up my ambition and craft of coding at any cost. That’s the skill I would like to hone and be proficient at. I should be self aware of my biases and inclinations. Going further, I should commit to my work of software development and coaching. I need to understand my desires and handle them well. Being mindful of my behaviours and thoughts is essential to growth and well being of myself and my career. If I can take up a job in Pune, it might as well be a job in a software company as a Senior developer, rather than at an institute being a glorified clerk. I will be travelling to Goa fro a month starting this Sunday. I have spent about 15.5K on accommodation and bus fare for now. I guess I would spend another 15K for food, travel and return fare. That’s about 30K for 30 days, roughly. I plan to explore the beaches, go on long walks/runs and get some fresh air. The weather must be better than here and we’ll be having a pleasant time as opposed to Bangalore. The time spent being their brings about a change, hopefully, in my living and work. I wish myself a safe and joyful trip. I should try and bag a well paying remote work when I’m there. That’ll get me to be free and independent.
