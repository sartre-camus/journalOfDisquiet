JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SYNDICATE CAFE - WEDNESDAY, APRIL

Gluttony, Decay

I hit the streets at dawn, stomach empty, heading for The Hive. Managed to hold out until mid-morning before folding at the Syndicate Cafe. One meager sandwich won't hold back the hunger for long. I’m already eyeing the upper levels for a real feast. I’ve always traded my dignity for a full plate; if the price is right, I’ll eat until I can’t move. My internal sensors are screaming—too much fuel, not enough engine work. My heart is a ticking bomb, and I’m just adding more powder. Now I’m just lazing in the shadows, waiting for the sun to drop so I can crawl back to my hole.
