JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SUNDAY, MAR

LETHARGY, EXISTENTIALISM

...and live forever—any attempt at trying to wipe the slate or solve the equation will only result in more stagnation than ever before.

I just would like to return to my Hab-Unit and enter stasis, but all those efforts invariably end up in losing stasis. There's no running away—everything and nothing cannot be it all. One lives through various stages and states; better to kill nothing than anything. Stasis, stasis, and stasis.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
