Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Spending my evening at WeWork Embassy One or rather at the Four Seasons hotel where I throng along, even when there’s no reason to be there. Some of the staff recognize me now due to my frequent visits there. Is this going to buy me any special privileges? Well I’m not sure though. It’s just another lame reason to be there. Why wouldn’t I keep it to myself here and WeWork? What is this obsession to go there and splurge my money that I have borrowed and not earned? I have just tried to be on my own for a long time now and without this job I can’t continue to be independent. I have just found myself in quandary or rather the needless, pointless, banal and futile series of events. For some reason, I find the world outside to be more engrossing than the world that I’m part of or have within - the one inside my mind. Why do I chase that’s something outside, looking at all that’s out there and obsess about them? Why care to even observe those rudimentary changes around me? The obsession for luxury, lavish and serene outings. Why don’t these coworkers fuck off for the day with their loser talk and pointless gossip?

How long is this escapade of mine to the Four Seasons hotel going to continue? Eventually the staff there would come to know that I do nothing there but louse around, sit there and buy nothing. They’d blacklist me and would throw me out soon. If I just go there and while away my time doing nothing, I’m probably going to be ostracized from that place soon enough. I’ve wasted enough time around that hotel, not just time but money - borrowed money, which could’ve been put to better use by now. What was the conviction, that I always have, to continue doing what I’m doing. What makes me think that I shall be able to live independently? What’s the belief that I’d always land on my feet, when it comes to money and job? Is there any surety at all that it’ll always be such that I’d have enough for myself, no matter what? This intuitive belief that everything is gonna be fine, no matter what debt I get into is a huge lie that I’m pursuing. It’s going to fall apart sooner than later and I’d have nothing to get back to, if I don’t act proactively and choose to make something of a job and earn enough soon. All my savings are already dwindling and with that gone there shall be nothing left but these journal entries and memories of the spa therapies. Just memories and nothing more.
