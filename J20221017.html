Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Woke up late today at about 11.00 pm and got ready to go out for the Fish Thali meal at Anjali Annapurna restaurant nearby. After having the sumptuous meal I head back home to get an afternoon nap and laid back in the bed. Later in the evening finished the class for Prince and had a Chicken Double burger from Cremeux. The patties were a bit salty so I fed it to the cat that lingers around my door everyday at dinner time. Fed it in that red plastic bowl by breaking the patties into little pieces. Now it’s lying out in the front of my door. I’m too scared to open up the door to get back that bowl as the cat may come in meowing all the way into the hall. I would rather prefer that to stay outside simply because it’s not clean - the saliva, mucus and faeces contains germs that might make me sick. Also, I’m little worried about the fur it sheds, I might be allergic to that. I would prefer to stay away and just watch it. Feed it left overs or those premium cat food when it comes to my door. I can’t pet it - not before it’s taken to a vet and treated for lice, ticks and fleas. The cat knows how to survive without me - it’s born predator and knows how to hunt to get food - unlike me. As a matter of gratitude for its cuteness I tend to feed it - purely out of curiousness and affection.
