JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

October 17, 2022

Loneliness, Sanctuary

The sun was long gone when I finally dragged myself out of the rack. Headed to 'The Gilded Scale' for a plate of something that used to swim. Spent the rest of the day in a haze, eventually feeding a salty bird-burger to the shadow-cat that haunts my doorstep. I broke the meat into pieces in a cracked red bowl, a small peace offering. Now the beast is guarding my exit. I’m not letting it in—this city is dirty enough without bringing more germs into my sanctuary. The cat is a born killer, a predator in a fur coat. I respect that. I’ll keep feeding it the scraps of my life, but I’m keeping the door locked. We’re both just trying to survive the night.
