JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE CHILD OF GRIEF

Grief, Death

I cried. Tears just started flowing down my cheeks. A flickering shadow play that I watched made me cry. Just sheer anguish and sadness. The Child of Grief, uttering the words in the last scene of the film - “I have to learn to be happy as my mother said”, made me rethink what happiness is really about. Is it a side-effect of an event or is it a fictitious thing that one has to learn to be. Happiness has no reality in itself. Something that one has to learn, in order to experience it. It could no longer be an occurrence of randomness then. There’s rainfall outside and here I am still in tears for all those whom I have seen vanish during my cycle. Death is such a tragedy, for someone we know closely. It is just so real than anything else that could ever be. Such stories tend to bring out our fragile emotions and deep insecurities. It just shows how emotional and sentimental we are.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
