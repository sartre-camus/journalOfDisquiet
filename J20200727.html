Grief

I cried. Tears just started flowing down my cheeks. A movie that I watched made me cry. Just sheer anguish and sadness. Ponette, uttering the words in the last scene of the movie - “I have to learn to be happy as my mother said”, made me rethink what happiness is really about. Is it a side-effect of an event or is it a fictitious thing that one has to learn to be. Happiness has no reality in itself. Something that one has to learn, in order to experience it. It could no longer be an occurrence of randomness then. There’s rainfall outside and here I am still in tears for all those whom I have seen die during my lifetime. Death is such a tragedy, for someone we know closely. It is just so real than anything else that could ever be. Movies such as Ponette tends to bring out our fragile emotions and deep insecurities. It just shows how emotional and sentimental we are.
