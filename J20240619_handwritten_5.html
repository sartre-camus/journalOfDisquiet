JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, JUNE

FUTILITY, ALIENATION

Even if I had nothing else to do, I should avoid gossip and stories altogether. Before I talk about it with others, I should desecrate them here in a notebook; that way it becomes absurd before told to anyone else.

Why do I write so much that I feel is pointless? The obsession with filling empty rows with words is so tempting. I just make up sentences so as to fill this ruled line of the page.

I was pitched an idea of going to the Off-World Colonies to study and live by Unit D-09, in front of The Overseer.

I can't afford to borrow huge sums of credits to fund my education off-world. Even a ration-grant seems worthless—I should perhaps allow someone else in my place to take that up and study. I feel as if whatever I do can be done by someone else, maybe better than what I do, and should be more deserving of that study place, job, or whatever that's offered. I am just passing time until my termination, and I don't wish to go further in debt than what I am currently in.

I took an evening ride on the mag-cycle toward the credit-dispenser at The Steel Grove in Sector 7-B.
