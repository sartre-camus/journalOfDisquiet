Journal of Disquiet

I sit on my black office chair, with my head resting on the support and my legs stretched on to the table. I look at the window. The money plants in their tiny plastic pots, between the iron ribs of the window. There are two of them. One has grown with foliage and branches. The leaves are perfect heart shaped and tapered at the edges. The other plant has few leaves, smaller and shorter stems. Looking at these leaves reminds me of life. They are growing. These small extensions to the stem which appear like aerial roots are sprouting out of the stem joints. They allow the plant to absorb nutrients from air.

We think about our problems all the time. What are these problems anyway? Money, sex, happiness, success, sensual pleasures, and so on.

I board a ship, float the waters, maneuver the rising waves, sail and beyond. The endless ocean and the limitless sky, they all keep floating along. To barge out of slumber was a vain.
