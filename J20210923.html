JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

The Money Plants - THURSDAY, SEP

Growth, Reflection

I sit in the Inquisitor's Seat, head back, boots on the desk. Through the iron ribs of the window, I watch the verdant parasites in their plastic cages. Two money plants, struggling for light. One is a riot of heart-shaped leaves, green hearts beating against the glass. The other is stunted, a few small leaves on a short stem. They remind me of us—sprouting aerial roots, desperate to suck nutrients from the very air when the soil fails.

We obsess over our failures. What are they but the usual suspects? Credits, desire, the hollow promise of happiness. I imagine boarding a freighter, sailing into the limitless black of the ocean, where the sky and the water are the same shade of gray. Waking from that dream is always a disappointment.
