APATHY, DEBT

I find myself amidst plebeian crowd, as always, and I tend to ignore them. There’s an dearth of people with taste and sensibilities that resonate with mine. It’s quite annoying to be around them, but there’s no other easy option for me. I think about eighty percent of people whom I encounter in my daily life are worthless, there’s no doubt about that. To avoid getting too entangled in their miseries and misfortunes, it is best in my interest to turn the other way. I am concerned about The Iron Chain of growing debt and having no viable way to pay them back. My search for a role is taking too long, I somehow feel that there’s very little out there that interests me. The Silent Ones are easier to bear than humans.
