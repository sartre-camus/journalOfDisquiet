Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I find myself amidst plebeian crowd, as always, and I tend to ignore them. There’s an dearth of people with taste and sensibilities that resonate with mine. It’s quite annoying to be around them, but there’s no other easy option for me. I am learning how to ignore people and what they have to say. I think about eighty percent of people whom I encounter in my daily life are a$$h0les, there’s no doubt that. To avoid getting too entangled in their miseries and misfortunes, it is best in my interest to turn the other way. I am concerned about my growing debt and having no viable way to pay them back. My search for job is taking too long, I somehow feel that there’s very little out there that interests me or cater to my sensibilities. Humans are the worst to put up with, cats and dogs are easier to bear.
