The Abrupt Ending - SUNDAY, SEP

Individuality, Purpose

Woke up at 3 AM, the past gnawing at my heels. An email from the Soul-Mender, Christy, arrived. She thinks we left things unfinished. She wants me to join her private circle. Strange how she reaches out just as I send my reports to the handlers. Do I need more of her brand of light? I need to embrace the cold logic of my own individuality. My vocation is the only thing that won't betray me.
