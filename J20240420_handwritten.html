JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE FOURTH CYCLE, 2024

EXISTENTIAL DREAD, ALIENATION

Why are there so many obstacles in everything that I wish to do—is it my creation or do they actually exist? Where does my pragmatism go when I encounter them? Does Elias Thorne think about being pragmatic at all while he writes those books or are they completely a universe apart from that topic? Why does it always hit me, so very often, that failure in whatever I undertake is imminent? I lie to myself, live in bad faith as The Existentialist says. I know my truth yet I fail to admit it to myself. I know what I am supposed to do, yet I live in apathy, denial, and indifference—why do I simply overthink? Where does my pragmatism dissipate? Is there a cure to this or is this the disease at all? Or are these words simply meaningless that keep mumbling and scribbling all day long?

The water here at The Collective Hub, Sector 7 tastes a bit sweeter than that at the hab-unit—perhaps it says that it's been a while since I showed up here. I wrote the other day in that room that I would never show up here again—all that commute, walk, and time spent here is an utter waste of time. Ultimately, a good timepass—walking up to here and scribbling my deepest fears and agony and going back all the way home. What a loser I should be to do this and keep it or expect to be doing it?

I am worried now if they would end up charging for those scans that I have made from the printer here at The Collective—I simply do not have any credits to pay for any overage. I am utterly broke and I do not wish to borrow from anybody. I shall not beg, borrow, or steal from anyone, including from my personal savings and investments. Perhaps I should issue a refund for the credits that The Syndicate pays for The Fourth Cycle—it simply seems awkward expecting money and siphoning it off. No matter what I argue or feel about my 'earnings', I should just let it go; probably it's a waste after all—fool's gold.

It feels like I'm drawing too much attention from people for petty things. I should forbid myself from composing any more transmissions to Kaelen or The Syndicate or anyone—what is the point of lamenting the same old shit, beating myself over things that ought to have been done and what not? The writings—these are all for myself. All those conversations, dialogues are with the fictitious or imaginary people that I spawn from reality and have no desire to talk to in reality or 'personally'. It is utterly absurd to do that in reality—I have no intention to engage myself in such chatter.

Why find other ways to kill time when I have myself—the books, writings, music, cinema, and games at my disposal? I have never been lonely, at all—these are very much part of me and my way of 'life'. These are always going to be around—people simply talk or rather make these sounds; they don't have anything to really say—that includes myself. I have nothing to say to anyone—all those messages are useless and serve no purpose.

I still don't understand my strange desire for credits—oh my goodness—why can't I let it go? Just let it die—there's nothing there—it just puts me into more work. Credits earned or spent—it always puts me to work whether I like it or not.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
