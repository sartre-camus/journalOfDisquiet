JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, FEBRUARY

Satiety, Solitude

O' how dearly I miss this journal entries, I should have written it a long time ago. I kept away in talks and arguments with others. Most of them needless and pointless. I ate the pungent salad I had made with the roots and nightshades I had cut along with the thin wafers I had scorched. I just cannot forget how to get on with it. I ate grain and spice too along with it, not to forget the white fermented cream. Now I'm full, I just sit with those tears that came while chopping the roots. Those tears are yet to go, as they stay and drool down by eyes, not so much but a tad bit. I can't get off this chair and turn off the other monitor that stands vertically as a monolith, as my belly's full with the spoils of the city.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
