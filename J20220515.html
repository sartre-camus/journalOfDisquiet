JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SUNDAY, MAY

Isolation, Mechanical Echoes

Back at The Hive at Manyata. The pull of the void is stronger than the pull of the family spire. I need to be free from their blessings, which feel more like gilded chains.

Sunday. The Dead Zone area is empty. Just me and the rhythmic clicking of the mechanical keys—a small, sharp pleasure in the silence. My iron-steed is stashed in the B1 vault for now. I'll move it deeper, to B3, when the shadows grow longer.

  • Secure a high-credit remote dispatch.
  • Patch the 'Cheese' surveillance bug on the EXWM interface.
  • The EXWM gateway is sluggish.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
