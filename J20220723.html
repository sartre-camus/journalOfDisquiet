JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE SMOKING GHOST - SATURDAY, JULY

DREAMS, MEMORY

She came to me in a haze of tobacco smoke, a face from a life I've almost forgotten. We used to share the same air in the corporate hive, two ghosts who barely spoke. In the dream, she had a message, a promise to meet later. But where? She's been a shadow for six years, and the digital trails I've followed are all dead ends. The internet is a liar, and reality is a cruel mistress. I'll have to be content with the girl in the dream; she's the only one who still remembers my name.
