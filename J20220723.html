Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Had a strange dream last night, saw an almost forgotten face in it. Remember her from long time ago, she used to used in a cubicle beside me and barely spoke. I too never spoke much with her except occasionally, felt that ’twas unimportant. In my last night’s dream she was smoking a cigarette, a very unusual happening and asked me catch up later. Barely, have her phone or address - how am I supposed to meet her? It’s been more than six years since I have seen her, where am I to find her? Even the internet is cruel, as the search for her name yields results that are spurious. I think I need to be happy about seeing her in my dreams, the reality (if that’s reality I’m living in) is not going to do me much.
