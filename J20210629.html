JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, JUNE

Motivation, Consumption

I just feel unmotivated. A strange laziness. A constant desire to do nothing but to rest. I keep pondering about all the happenings of the Archives, contemplate possibilities and chew on them. The ruminating thoughts keep my mind occupied. Even to do anything of importance (well I don’t know how to classify something as important, as it keeps changing and sometimes disappears) is a huge task. My past misadventures and actions are troubling me ever since they occurred. I always wish I had taken better decisions about the way I spent my hard earned credits. I always think why I chose not to think independently and worked towards my personal freedom, all these cycles. I wonder why I chose convenience and the grind all along. Whenever I had an opportunity for myself to gain freedom, I made poor choices which resulted in more suffering and pain and agony. I am already dozing off. I closed my eyes and went to sleep for a few minutes. The interaction via The Grid with my former Prime Architect over philosophy, religion, belief etc. continues. I sometimes have a strong rebuttal to what he has to say. I just share these reactions with him through my signals. I cannot find a reason for my Horizon or its existence. The lack of creativity is very bothersome. I just cannot find any original thoughts of mine or find enough content to write an essay. All the readings I started with are abandoned after a few a pages. I cannot find the motivations to go through the literature. I find it so hard to read something that’s not easy on my eyes. I somewhat fail to persevere through scrolls and finish reading them. Most often than not, I get swayed away by my own thoughts and emotions, I get lost with the happenings in the volumes and give up reading altogether. My indulgence in digital consumption of entertainment has peaked substantially. I spend hours viewing the Tube of Shadows, trying different searches and recommendations. I wish I spent more time reading, which would enable me to imagine stuff rather than accept what’s shown on a Display Panel or in a picture. The words should trigger my thoughts and create imagery in my mind.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
