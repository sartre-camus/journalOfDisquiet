Pulse, Ghost

The encrypted pulse is back, a digital heartbeat in the dark. Sartre and Camus are still whispering their mathematical riddles from the Proton Node. In a city where every word is recorded, these ciphers are the only way to remain a ghost. But every ghost has a price, and I’m still waiting for the bill to arrive.
