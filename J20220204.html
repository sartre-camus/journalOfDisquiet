JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

2022-02-04

Revival, Obsession

I assigned myself a fool's errand: to breathe life back into the silent partner, a hulking relic of a forgotten age. Its 'Giga-Byte' core, its vital code, had gone dark. To rouse it, I intended to pry open its shell and re-etch its core code, a delicate surgery with an outsider's tool. My chosen instrument: a cryptic script known as 'The Programmer,' whispered to be loyal only to the sprawling empire of Redmond. I’d fashioned a transient key, bearing the mark of Redmond's seventh decree, to slip past its defenses. But to make it work, I'd have to wipe clean my portable contraption, erasing the rebel's open-road manifesto it currently flew – the spirit of 'Ubuntu.' A dangerous game. To erase it meant losing everything, a void from which retrieval, even from my carefully curated shadows, was a torturous ordeal. I'd gather fragments, perhaps, but never the true reflection of what was. Surely, there was another path through this labyrinth.

Yet, the question lingered in the smoke-filled air: Why this desperate pursuit of the silent partner's resurrection, now? For the phantom M, perhaps, to ply his trade? Or the ghost G? Or was it for my own shadowed purposes? The truth, like a phantom in the fog, eluded me. One thing was certain: the forgotten vault, deep within the silent partner's gut, held secrets I hungered to uncover.
