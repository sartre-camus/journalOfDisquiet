JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, JUNE

NIHILISM, DECAY

I still don't know how to get back to what I did for a living while I was maintaining the Neural-Grid protocols. Now that dream of pursuing it again is taking backstage, more behind than ever. I simply should stop, give up and let myself fall through the abyss, as I have already done before.

To be or not to be—I choose not to be. There's no urge to become, arrive or do something; most times it is a sway away, a running away from whatever there exists. Towards nothingness. All there is seems to be nothing and nothing can come out of nothingness. It is just emptiness, void—a deep void, where nothing can come or go—like a black hole in the heart of the Sprawl.

Another day of enduring myself, amidst all the agony, apathy, lavishness and misery. I enjoy the synthetic rations, the brief intimacy of the flesh-markets, and the cozy comforts of the stasis-pod, but I still whine and worry about the decadent, decaying life of mine, dissolving into oblivion. Every day seems far more worse than what was before.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
