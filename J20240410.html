JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, APR

Desecration, Sleep

I'm here to desecrate again. It's just so much drama, I wish I was never summoned from the void. No matter what perks this world has to offer, I want none of it. I don't know how to put up with myself in any way at all, I have to put up a show all day long in front of the masses like one showpiece, clown, moron or whatever. I'm tired of everything of these. I wish I just etched and kept saying all these to myself. I have nothing to say to them. Today, I announced something like, 'I don't celebrate any festivals, that includes my day of summoning'. I can't run away from the fact that this day is coming and it's someday. People just want a reason to celebrate, I have celebrated for no reason at all - like a madman in my cell, danced around and what not. I had no good reason to celebrate at all - if someone saw from me outside they'd think I'm a madman. I have no reason to celebrate or to mourn. I don't care. Just like I don't care about anything that's out there or in me. Everything just seems irrelevant, yet I give some relevance to some things. I somehow want to be sick all the time, reluctant to go out, or to talk with someone or do something or whatever. I prefer to just lie around asleep. I wonder if being in a comatose state is perhaps the best thing. A long sleep and I don't wish to get out of it. A long long sleep in dreams and nightmares.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
