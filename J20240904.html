JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER

Study, Ambition

I still don’t know what to work on. I should stop asking myself these questions. I am finding some interest in solving mathematics problems from technical mechanics course that I had studied fourteen years ago. I feel like solving these questions on calculus, discrete mathematics, graph theory, probability and so on. The motivation is to learn these topics and then see if I can create something of my own. These are the study goals I have. It feels like after learning these things I must pursue a Mastery of the Higher Arts from a world renowned university abroad. I have been attempting this since past decade but haven’t gotten anywhere. I worry about finances as I don’t have credits to support such endeavors. My age has become a worry as I may not be able to fit into that young twenties student crowd. I just want to study by myself for the sake of studying and don’t wish anything more than that. This seems more palatable to me than all the adventures I dream of.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
