SILENCE, EXISTENCE

Slumber, deep and dark as a shallow grave, yet I keep drifting. I’ve got a long way to go before I find my escape. I need to squeeze more from the city's veins through my own grit. I have to play the part for now, sharpening my skills in the shadows. I should learn to listen to the whispers and only speak when the words are razor-sharp. Silence is the ultimate response in this town. It’s a tool that keeps the vultures at bay. Learning to keep your mouth shut is a power few possess. To look through people as if they were ghosts: that’s the real trick. It keeps the trouble from finding your door.

The only thing that feels empty is the idea of emptiness itself. The absurd notion of the void, all these words piled like cigarette butts in an ashtray—they need to mean something, or the whole game is rigged. The weight of meaning, the burden of the truth, the heavy cost of existing—it all takes shape in the dark.
