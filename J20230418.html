Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Slumber, deep as it seems, I keep drowsing away, I’ve got a long way to go to towards my freedom. I need to earn more through my efforts. I need to play along for the time being, focus on learning and improving myself. I should learn to listen and respond only the necessary ideas. Silence is always a good way to respond. Silence is a powerful tool that keeps most of the conflicts out of the picture. Learning to be quiet when needed is a powerful tool. To be able to ignore people is another powerful tool. Ignoring others as if they don’t exist in my world now: that’s a great thing to do. Relieves me of most troubles.
