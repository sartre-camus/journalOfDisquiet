JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SATURDAY, JUNE

DESPAIR, ALIENATION

...to surpass all those milestones I set for myself in self-critique.

I remember the containment units I have occupied during an afternoon in Sector G-9, when the cell was dark and the artificial sun glowed outside. I simply lay on the bunk under the ventilation unit dreaming about past muses and companions I think I had. I only disturb those memories to make myself feel human amidst the horrors of the sprawl—the lack of credit-earning labor, reconciling debt cycles and credit repayments, and so on. I simply exist with no hope of having a designated function, vocation, or any such thing which is almost inconceivable to anyone in the collective where I reside. I cannot just exist through this melancholy, nor endure it; the data-slabs I have accessed offer nothing much as I have not processed them with diligence or attempted to log a summary of what I extracted from them.
