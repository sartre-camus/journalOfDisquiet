JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, NOV

Hedonism, Anticipation

I’ve been lost in a week of neon lights and soft skin, trying to forget that my bankroll is nearly empty. The pleasures are real, but they’re just another smoke screen. I’m still waiting for 'The Syndicate' to send the credits they promised. Another month of living on the edge, waiting for the wire to hum.
