JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, JUL

FAILURE, GUILT

After a few days of simulated crime fervour, I resort to writing this entry as a way of acknowledging my ’sins’ of being drawn away from reality. Even writing these entries feels like work - unpaid labor for a silent audience. I spend most of my day watching inquisitions about The Biologist and others on the decentralized web. Yesterday, I spent half the day watching a movie on The Father of Ash and his biography. I feel I am not cautious about how I utilize my time. I am overdone with entertainment, unable to see anything beyond the screen. I have failed miserably in pursuing the boring things that add real value. This admission of failure is itself a daunting task; I would rather lie to myself. I’ve been lying about my productivity for months - no real work has been done, yet I choose to believe in my own efficiency.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
