Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

After few days of GTA V fervour and fever I resort to writing this entry as a way of acknowledging my ’sins’ of being distracted and drawn away from reality of life. I somehow feel that even writing these entries are getting to seem like work - ’paid’ work even though there’s no incentive. I spend most of my day watching interviews on Invidious about Richard Dawkins, atheism, and others. Yesterday I spent more than half the day watching a movie on Oppenheimer and his biography. Also, earlier I had seen this movie on Nike shoes called Air. I feel that I’m not cautious about how I utilize my time and on what activities. Somehow, I am overdone with entertainment that I cannot see anything beyond that. Remember? I had asked myself to pursue boring things and the ones that would add real value to my living? Well I have failed in that. I can’t keep up to the goals I set for myself. I fail miserably. Somehow, this admission of failure is in itself a daunting task - I cannot acknowledge these and would rather lie or mislead myself into believing something else that’s conducive to my mind. I’ve been lying about my work since past six months or so - there hasn’t been any real work done but yet I choose to make myself believe that I’m working and doing productive activities.
