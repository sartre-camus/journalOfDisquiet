SATURDAY, JUNE

STAGNATION, LETHARGY

The Internal Monitor — 'Has performing low-tier tasks made my cycle any more bearable?' 'Has the automation of the Assembly made my existence any easier?' Has anything effortless made me feel at ease at all?

Why have I retreated (retreated!) from the high-level anomalies and fixated on the 'ease' of processing, the 'ease' of existing, and so on? There have been no sacrifices logged, no hardships endured toward the Sector Quotas—what quality of existence do I expect to celebrate daily?
