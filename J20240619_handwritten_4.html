JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

JUNE 19, 2024

ISOLATION, COMPULSION

If it gets too late, will I be able to walk home? Along the murky street all by myself in a city which suffers from the greatest civic woes anywhere one could find. I somehow crave to stare at lines of code and feel comfortable—not that I intend to write any; that's best left to those who do it. There's nothing much to create after all. It's best that I stop communicating with Vance altogether.

It simply is unwise—I just should refrain from accepting a meeting invite or sending one—just stop communication, simply because there's nothing I have that's worth his time. If I really had any, I would publish it already—why beg them for feedback or credits? Screw them all—that's what I'd say—go solo—go elsewhere—do something else—write code or do whatever. Who has anything to say or do?

I know the mornings are difficult and so are the afternoons when I am all alone—the tendency to seek base gratification suddenly grows and I end up succumbing to digital distractions. I don't know why I really have to do that—it makes me feel good and calm for some time but later I resort to the same lows I had before. That's why I want to do something else during that time—like write or just read and relax. I don't know whether these impulses are good or bad, but if I could avoid them for that one time—it somehow boosts my self-esteem as if I have managed to overcome a craving of mine.

There are several such cravings which are quite useless if I look at them—food, validation, craving for relationship, talking to others—like today I called up Lyra from The Central Ledger so that I could dematerialize those Aether Bonds I have and sell them to get credits out of it—the credits that'll put me to work whether I earn it or spend it—using it to buy some product or service that I do not actually need. It just is boring you see. I mean the ordeals I have to simply go through—these temptations won't let one just be—the thing is if I succumb to those then I would develop a habit—a sick one actually. That only makes me crave for more—why is this such a thing?

I should just stop staring or checking out others here—there's no point—I don't want to do it—just makes me feel like a creep. I don't want to objectify—I should only make eye contact and not look elsewhere—this is what is needed—please stay away—stop demeaning them through mental objectification—don't need it. It's quite a touchy subject—best if I ignore them unless there's a dire need to establish communication, for example today when I had to ask Sora about scanning documents and their billing—that's okay but I would refrain from saying hi and just make that small talk—needless actually. If they want to talk then it's okay, but I should not initiate a conversation without a requirement—please do not create that requirement either.

The only thing that scares me a bit is that transmission from Vance, if it shows up in my terminal—discarding it might be a challenge. Not sure how to go there. I just need to put it in my head that such conversations are utterly pointless—whether I like it or not—please refrain, please refrain from talking, sending transmissions—listen to what's necessary like that protagonist from 'The Silent Specter' movie I saw yesterday—just listen and do things accordingly—there's no need to talk to anybody or say anything. Just write all of it here and do the ceremony—that's all there is to it—write, follow the ceremony and throw away the pages.

By tomorrow morning I could come here early as they would be serving rations, perhaps the first thing I do in the morning when I wake up—get a bath, carry that terminal in that bag tomorrow and have the rations—the only challenge is this that of going back home—now that's difficult in that hot sun and dusty air. Get those boots off the balcony—it might be dried up by now—use them for tomorrow's visit—perhaps come during the evening—that's more comfortable—any time else is just not possible—sun and then rain—disaster I must say. I chose the wrong cycle I guess—not really the best of weathers or the air quality. I guess I have to adapt to this atmosphere after all—believe that I can handle it—it's all fine.

So, don't come here tomorrow morning for those Nutri-Cakes, instead stay at home—they'll probably have a good meal ready that's even more tastier—enjoy that, along with the lunch and get a nap. When the suns begin to set, come here for a walk, have tea or coffee and just scribble whatever that's on your mind. That should be okay I guess.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
