JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, AUG

DEBT, ALIENATION

There's uncertainty that I can't find an antidote to. I fear more than what I used to before; I have no material possessions of my own but only debt to the Syndicate. The life that I spend seems more corrosive than ever before. The humongous decay surrounds everything in the Outer Rim. I have no existence in this decaying time as I fail to identify with it.

Today I desperately ask V for the digital credit ledger so that I could look up all the spendings I had made on their account. Now I'm left with nothing to repay them with. I should find labor in the lower sectors and repay them through earnings. I often get confused between debt and earnings. I have no earnings but a good debt—that's something to repay.

I had planned to solve some Logic-Gate decryption protocols today but I have failed in doing so; instead, I while away my time playing legacy holosimulations, watching sensory-stim feeds and indulging in self-gratification. Later in the evening I sleep, longer dreaming about...

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
