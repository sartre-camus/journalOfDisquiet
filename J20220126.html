JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, JAN

DREAMS, VOID

An early morning mechanical siren made me wake up and bruise my finger while opening the the steel vault. The the steel vault in which I had placed the mobile phone. It started ringing very early, as early as 7 am. I had forgotten to turn off the recurring schedule. It’s my left middle finger. The scars on it hurt a bit. Mr. Nobody is what I am. A nothing. A nobody.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
