JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SECTOR 39 - SEPTEMBER 15, 2024

STAGNATION, ISOLATION, WEARINESS

The neon hum of the city bleeds through the blinds, a constant reminder of the demands placed upon me by the Syndicate. I am tired of playing the cog in their machine, performing tasks simply because the architecture of this life dictates it. I need to carve out my own path, away from the expectations that weigh heavier than the smog in the Low-Rises.

My eyes burn from the flickering glow of the data-stream, hours wasted consuming synthetic entertainment on my handheld terminal. The exhaustion is absolute. I thought I could master the night shift, turn the darkness to my advantage, but it was a fool's errand. The city demands its toll, and I am left hollow.

I must stop scavenging for meaning in the static of the network or the whispers of the street-corner prophets. The answers aren't in the data-banks of the Collective. I have the blueprint in my own mind, a jagged, dangerous thing that I have the capacity to forge into something real, if only I can find the strength to disconnect from the noise.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
