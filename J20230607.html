Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Here at this fancy coworking space called WeWork Prestige Central, sitting amongst these people, I feel utterly fatigued after the commute. I should stop looking at these people while I type. Why do I feel like I am being stared at while writing this entry, as if my neighbour cares to read what I’m writing in here. This is just utterly lame. I wonder if I could get my car back from those Parkshed people without having to pay Rs.250, that would be such a huge relief. I’m not used to paying money for parking at place where I am already coughing up money. Will I be to last long here today? I wonder, I mean till 9.00 PM? I finally found a place here to sit in some privacy, at least a place where whatever I am doing on my screen is not visible to others. This nook also has a good background - the one ideal for a video call?
