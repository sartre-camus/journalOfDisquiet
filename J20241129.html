Journal of Disquiet

I used the serial light setup in the room and it looks bright as if festive. I celebrate nothing here though apart from a change in the lighting pattern - I was reminded my relative of mine who said not to sleep during the evenings and therefore I got up to put some lighting in and made my arms hurt reaching those far away nooks to suspend the lighting string. The keyboard back-light color matches the auburn or yellow light in the room. The blue light of the docking station and SATA drive along with the red waving lamp of the spike buster, glows.
