Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I tend to like the projection. The image of something. The thing in totality is unfathomable. Interfaces. That’s what I look at. Interaction with interfaces. Implementation is an intricate phenomenon. Involve in those interfaces. Belong to those places and farce. It’s hard to pretend to have work. Listening to the music around, jazz as it seems to be. The overweight women passing by looking for something. The girl with an infant in the cradle by the pantry. Sitting, eating, looking to the laptop, working perhaps, there she’s. The little one, the being. I should come out more frequently, otherwise I’d just end up looking at people. Just staring them. Every other women. Crazy as it may seem. Every individual. What the heck? Why isn’t the words and images on my screen involving me enough? I’m giving information to the Big Other through their cameras. I can’t find the old staff here. I’m staring to figure out and find familiar faces in the crowd. The familiar faces. Sometimes familiar body parts. The legs, thighs, buttocks, heads, hairstyles, and so on. Chicken salad lunch. Boosts some energy. Not enough but try to sit and write.
