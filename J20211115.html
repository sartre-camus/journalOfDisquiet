JOURNAL of DISQUIET

Interaction with Interfaces - MONDAY, NOVEMBER

Observation, Energy

I prefer the projection to the reality. The totality of things is unfathomable. I live in the interfaces—the digital veils. Pretending to work is a full-time job. I listen to the jazz in the background, watching the city pass by. The girl with the infant in the pantry, the office drones staring at their glowing screens. I should leave this place before I start staring too hard. Every individual is a puzzle I’m too tired to solve. I’m feeding the Panopticon through its cameras. A chicken salad lunch gives me just enough energy to sit and write a few more words before the apathy returns.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
