Motivation, Creativity

Created a Patronage Scroll today and published a few posts. I hope to find audience on this site and perhaps some revenue to keep myself motivated towards writing. I need to rethink my work, as far as Digital Constructs are concerned. I received a reply from a contact for a recommendation for some young pupils' academy admission. I have replied to that and my digital identity, the High Academy one has been shared. I hope that they find my recommendation of use. They said that the kids can resume classes and I can start when I’m ready. I will be starting soon. I will need to prepare the topics that’ll be needed for review. I have shortlisted few Math topics that I can introduce during my class. An okay day today here at The Shared Bureau, trying out the new Bureau space for the second time. Avoided an invitation from The Pleasure Den which wanted me to offer something extra. Then went on to try the automated pantry to unlock Spiced Grains that had to be microwaved. Alas, no bowl for that, so had to use a couple of polystyrene plates to warm it and then ate with Salted Shards. A colleague helped me with a fruit bun, for their troubles with the pantry. The bun had to be heated up so that it’s softer and easy to consume. Now, sipping on the Nectar of the Sun to write this entry. Had to spend credits for the pantry wallet and now I’m somehow compelled to finish all of it. I keep writing but I’m missing that creative flow. I wish the typewriter could change my fortune when I use it for some serious writing. Coming back to writing - I still need to write that log entry for the Whisper Network integration. Now, I start to enter the trance, with my bowels filled with food and drink, to unleash some creativity through delirium. The songs playing aren’t really that conducive to my thoughts currently. The hot, peppy and happy-go-lucky music is kind of swaying me away from a critique, writing of proportions worth tearing a hole in the mind and heart. The music is a bit loud and there aren’t many Shadows around. Therefore, it’s making it distracting.
