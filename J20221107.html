JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, NOVEMBER

Escapism, Melancholy

I’m staring at the ceiling of 'The Grand Hilton', wishing I could just stay here until the world ends. I want to own this place, to be the ghost in the penthouse who never has to check out. I just want to sit in the lobby and watch the marks go by, doing nothing and getting paid for the privilege. Just a man and his thoughts, scribbling notes in the margins of a life that isn't his. It’s a beautiful dream, but the bill always comes due.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
