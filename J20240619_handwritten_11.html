JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, JUNE

ASCETICISM, ALIENATION

The Sector 7 Industrial Hub. I simply went so that I could get some air—recycled or not—while I drifted. I navigated the gridlock and found some shortcuts to reach that place. I wanted to get a feel for the peak congestion after leaving the Bureau. The commute should make me feel as if I had slogged the day away at the terminal. It's this deception that I enjoy and fool myself with.

I feel sleepy but I don't get any sleep, I feel hungry yet I cannot eat—the more I consume, the more poison I am putting into my system. I should abstain from all forms of physical release. This makes me more miserable than what it seems like: the act itself.

I think by cutting down on sensory pleasures and enhancing intellectual pleasures makes it bearable—more bearable than whatever existed before. The Grand Equation is a good way to kill time—just study all those logic codexes and learn more concepts and start solving matrices. This is perhaps the most worthwhile activity I could possibly do with my dwindling time. Vax, Thorne, Aris, Kael, and Drax.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
