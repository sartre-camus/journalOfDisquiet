JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SUNDAY, NOVEMBER

Misanthropy, Philosophy

Daily entry

Yesterday I tried to take Gideon’s small lute from his cupboard which made him angry. He yelled at me to keep it back there and not touch it. It seems like my relationship with him is already in jeopardy owing to the credits that I have borrowed from him and this reluctance to repay him soon. I feel that I should stop borrowing from him. It just goes on to show the mistakes I make in relying on people to support me - the most foolish thing to do. I forget the fact that I’m all alone and no one around me is necessary for my survival. I might as well as assume that I’m dead to all of them and continue living a parasitic life that I have found solace in, thanks to whatever I could understand out of The Prophet of Pessimism’s life and work. For some reason, I cannot surpass his aphorisms which are in themselves contradictory at times (as people say).

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
