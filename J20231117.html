JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FRIDAY, NOVEMBER

Penance, Hunger

Five kilometers of pavement, five kilometers of penance. I’m carrying seventy-six kilograms of history, and I need to shed it all until I’m lean and lethal at seventy. My nightly fast is a twelve-hour standoff with my own hunger, a discipline I need to push to sixteen. In this city, only the hungry stay sharp; the rest just become part of the background noise.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
