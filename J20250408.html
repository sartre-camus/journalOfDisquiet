JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

GRAVEYARD WHISTLE

Cynicism, Regret

It’s a comedy of errors, really. I’m out here sweating over the scraps of silver while the world burns. I keep telling myself I’m the architect of my own fate, but I’m just a tenant in a condemned building. This grand internal monologue is just whistling in the graveyard. I thought the credits would give me a way out, but they just built a higher wall. Every time I taste the silver, I end up choking on the lead. I should have stayed in the rain.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
