JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, JULY

APATHY, INDEBTEDNESS

...hab-unit and argue with myself on various forbidden topics. It seems like I am talking to a ghost if a surveillance operative were to notice me doing that.

There is an often occurring temptation of mine to use that black-market credit-chip to buy luxury rations and binge on them. I somewhat assume that the chip is here for my endless consumption—I could go on for however much I'd like, pretending there is no bottom to the void at all.

I remember watching A. Vane's 'The Solstice Drift' holofilm, a decade ago. In that cycle, the protagonist's life transforms through the introduction of a rogue companion, forbidden travel, and adventure beyond the Sector walls.

I volunteered to tutor a Sector-7 initiate yesterday and continued to do it today as well. The reasoning behind it seems to be the mounting social credit deficit, and an overwhelming weight of sorts born from my own inaction and apathy.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
