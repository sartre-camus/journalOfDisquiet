JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, JUL

PROCRASTINATION, APATHY

I just switch on The Machine without knowing what to do, as if I have everything to figure out on the fly. I have learnt to procrastinate everything that involves effort and with no means for any form of gratification. The digital glow is the only thing that fills the room, a cold light for a cold purpose.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
