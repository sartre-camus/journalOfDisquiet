Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I sit here again in the same shades of the canopy that covered the sun, with shadows moving, as the breeze blows. I sit and write this entry on laid back bench with my diary on a wooden table painted white, as I collect my passing thoughts and scribble them here. I see gorgeous ladies all dressed casually in their shorts and loose pyjamas sitting nearby on the other benches with their laptops open and trying to get things done. At a distance, there’s another pretty lady dozing off on the sofa with a book on her face, she’s laying there to get a nap, perhaps on this lazy afternoon trying to relax without getting into the ever prevalent mundane work of theirs. Distant sneezes and coughs of the crowd here are heard, perhaps they had traveled longer or smoked stuff that is causing them to cough their lungs out. All those cigarette stubs lying in that ash tray shows that they were all in it and smoked their life out of themselves.
