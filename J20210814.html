JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SATURDAY, AUG

Waiting, Expectations

I’m waiting for things to happen. For events to take place and changes to happen. An indefinite wait. A meaningless longing. A wait which comprises of expectations. Expectations from Shadows. Despite thinking so much about it, I continue with a false hope of change from the external world. If I were to question myself and ask, “What changes have I made to improve my life?”, “What changes have I made with my lifestyle?”, “What changes I have made to my work that it’s more fruitful?”, “How have I done with my credits? Are they looking good or bleak?”

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
