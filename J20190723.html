JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE LIBRE TERMINAL - TUESDAY, JULY

Freedom, Privacy

Figured out systems that are truly free (libre). Question to self - “How do I become free?” Have my own work and not be instructed by The Syndicates, The Central Bureau or private guilds to do what I need to do. How does using a specialized transmitter to make calls from the terminal avoid snooping? No way! What advantages does it really offer over a pocket tracker? It would definitely prevent the device manufacturer from reading the data/scripts etc. sent to it. However, the signal provider still knows the contents of my frequency and conversation. Possible exercise that I can think of is to connect The Single-Board Terminal with the transmitter and access the web. Location info still would be disclosed and can be used for tracking. The best that I would achieve is disallow The Eastern Watcher from my data and maybe prevent foreign intrusion.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
