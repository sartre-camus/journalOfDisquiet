Daily entry

Figured out systems that are truly free (libre). Question to self - “How do I become free?” Have my own work and not be instructed by corporations, government or private to do what I need to do. How does using dongle to make phone calls from PC avoid snooping? No way! What advantages does it really offer over a cell phone? It would definitely prevent the phone manufacturer from reading the data/SMS etc. sent to it. However, the service provider still knows the contents of my message and conversation. Possible exercise that I can think of is to connect UDOO/Pi with the dongle and access internet. Location info still would be disclosed and can be used for tracking. The best that I would achieve is disallow Xaomi from my data and maybe prevent Chinese intrusion.
