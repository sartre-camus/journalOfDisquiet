JULY 17, 2024

Addiction, Hubris

I met 'The Contact' in 'The Steel Jungle'. He was blowing smoke and I was blowing hot air—rambling about my debts, my hubris, and the addictions that keep me on a short leash. He had the nerve to ask for 'The Banker's' credit line to pay his own way. I told him the signal was dead.

I'm drowning in a pool of my own lethargy, waiting for a job that'll never find me. I refuse to look back at what I've done—I'm always searching for something new to distract from the ever-growing boredom. I'm a man fed on self-help lies and expensive dreams.
