Daily entry

Yesterday evening I go out as A calls me for a missed call from me the day before. I go out to Manyata Embassy business park and roam around. He went for a smoke and I couldn’t bear it anymore and had to move aside. Kept rambling about various things, nothing much I could recollect or find noteworthy apart from few RGV’s quotes here and there such as ’no matter how many flops you make continue trying’ or something like that - Motivational quotes, positive opinions and so on. I couldn’t get away from my bleak, morose, fatalistic and pessimistic thoughts nonetheless. I kept bombarding him every now and then - spoke about my debt, my reluctance, hubris, addictions and few other things. I kept using hyperbole, exaggerations every now and then. I never wanted to sound relevant. I enjoyed being obscure, bizarre and flamboyant at times. He later asked me if I could pay his internet bills using the credit card that I had borrowed from G, for which I said I can only use it physically and not online as the OTP went to G’s phone and he doesn’t reply to my messages. I even had a quarrel about it some time ago regarding the same over OTPs, messages, and other stuff, I said. I say I would find a job, lookout for one soon, yet I feel the unbearable lightness of lethargy and apathy. I refuse to revise or study what I earlier worked on - I am always in search for something drastically new and different from what I did earlier and this has been a cause for my ever-growing boredom, fatigue and sorrow. There are only few things that I can do yet I believe that I could anything and everything - thanks to the self-help, motivational bullshit that I have fed myself up with over ages.
