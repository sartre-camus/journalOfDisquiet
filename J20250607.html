JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SATURDAY, JUN

SOLITUDE, STILLNESS

I make it to The Collective Hive today as I do not wish to skip this by staying at home. I would prefer to stay here however long I wish today and while away my time. I have no intention to go back to the base - the car parking seems free for today at the outset, not sure if they ask for credits. I just want to sit and do nothing here. Just sip on this Fog-Cutter brew and enjoy the silence.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
