JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, JUNE - TRANSMISSION TO THE VOID

DESPAIR, MEDIOCRITY

To Vaelen,

Perhaps when you are reading this transmission, you will still be unhappy, unfulfilled, bored, suffering with pain and turmoil through the enormous despair that cannot be purged. This despair will be with you forever—you’ll struggle to endure yourself amidst all the chaos of the Sprawl. You’ll struggle to fulfill your basic desires of food, shelter, clothing etc., let alone your luxurious ones. You’ll go into further debt to all those people with whom you live as a parasite.

Your idea of independence and freedom from cravings shall never be fulfilled. You’ll live in disharmony, anguish and perpetual mediocrity. Enjoy your mediocrity, it might just as well be the only thing you’d ever be capable of. Let go of these hobbies of reading data-logs, finishing them, summarizing and so on—that’ll never happen—you’ll always give up sooner than they. You’ll never earn credits again through any of your vocations as nobody cares for it anymore.

The best you could probably do is just sit here and inside that room and do nothing. Sit day in and day out breathing, drinking water and occasional food to keep yourself alive until you die. Consider you were a caged animal which is regularly fed, cleaned and kept alive until old age kills you. This is the sort of living that you should probably get used to.

Your desperation to write and post these letters shall not be fulfilled as you are broke—you don't have the credits to buy even the data-tokens to post these letters to your home. Are you even going to try to find yourself a high paying, sustainable work at all? Or are you going to continue to be humiliated every day from every nobody around you?

Time will eventually triumph against every plan you have or try to enact. This is the way it is. Just don't punish yourself too much, the world will do that on your behalf. It will not spare you. Oh, that's all there is for me to say at this point. You would go to every extent to keep on rambling and I cannot encourage you to do that. Instead, how about you keep your mouth shut, and just disappear into nothingness, an absolute yet perpetual abyss.

P.S. June 25, 2025

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
