JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FRIDAY, JULY

CONSUMPTION, DISQUIET

It is stagnant in this chamber, with the bio-parasites feeding on my essence for their daily ration. My physical form expands against the synth-fabric of this tunic—a grim testament to my lack of discipline. A parasitic hunger grows within me, a compulsion to acquire more, to trade more, to hoard more. The relics of my past existence no longer provide the necessary stimulation. The urge to procure new artifacts crawls through my mind like a virus: new synth-boots, a more powerful data-slate, fresh lower-garments. I am perpetually dissatisfied with my current inventory, and the craving for unissued gear dominates my thoughts. It is as if I am programmed to deplete my entire credit balance and face insolvency. The cycle of living from one ration-cycle to the next is eroding my mental stability.

Evading my duties at the Sector-Forge or lingering in this hollow space will not alter the trajectory of my decline. Other variables are in flux, demanding my immediate focus. I find myself paralyzed by a hyper-vigilance regarding my own speech, agonizing over every syllable, unsure if my words are sanctioned or even coherent.
