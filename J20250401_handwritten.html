JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE STAGNATION FUND - APRIL 01, 2025

DEBT, DECAY, ISOLATION

The credits haven't cleared. They never do when the rain is this heavy and the neon flicker is this erratic. I’m perched on the 8th Tier of the Spire, letting the siphon-flies feast on what’s left of my patience. They’ve evolved, or maybe the atmospheric filters are just failing again; either way, there’s no escaping the sting of the city’s smallest scavengers in this humid, metal-scented air.

I’m starting to think the payout from The Nexus Initiative is a ghost story, a digital carrot dangled by a syndicate that doesn't exist. It’s wiser to stop chasing shadows and settle for the scraps in the Stagnation Fund. If I pull those credits tomorrow, I can survive the next three cycles in this rain-slicked purgatory. I’ll have to forfeit the docking bay lease by the 24th. Better to walk the alleys than to cough up more coin to a landlord who doesn't care if I breathe or drown.

Then there’s the matter of the Logic Core. The Board wants a demonstration, an encrypted uplink to show progress that doesn't exist. I haven't even stabilized the root commands yet. What am I supposed to show them? Static? Despair? I’m holding the line until the previous invoice clears. They’re pushing for a rollout with a frantic urgency that smells like a trap, especially when they’re so unwilling to let the code find its own rhythm in the dark.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
