JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, MAY

Abstraction, Escapism

I need to pivot to the cold, hard logic of the abstract. Better to lose myself in the geometry of numbers than the messy business of being alive. The biological hustle is a losing game; it's all sweat and heartbreak.

I'll take the binary code and the equations instead. They don't bleed, they don't lie, and they sure as hell don't care about the rain. This is far better than understanding life.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
