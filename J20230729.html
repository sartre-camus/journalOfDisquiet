JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SATURDAY, JULY

DEBT, APATHY

I borrowed money to pay The Fixer yesterday. I also had to repay the others who hold my debts. Now I have interest compounding on my plate with no role to repay it. I am unable to absorb the gravity of this matter; the insane reluctance and apathy have led me to places where I don’t belong - debt, humiliation, codependency. I need to learn the front-end ciphers necessary for the work, otherwise, it is going to be a difficult scenario for my prospects. The Stoic refused to take money until I find a path; a small mercy in a cold world.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
