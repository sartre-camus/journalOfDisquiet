December 31, 2022

Solitude, Resolution

New Year's Eve, and I'm holed up in a place that's seen every version of me—the happy one, the broken one, and the one that's just tired. I’m staying away from the home-fires, carving out a life with my own sweat and credits. I’m not going back to the old ghosts. Solitude is my new partner, a cold but honest companion. Sometimes I see the happy couples and feel a twinge of the old envy, but then I remember the price of company. There’s a certain magic in doing nothing, in just existing in the space between heartbeats. This is my choice. This is my life. Let the clock strike twelve; I'm ready for the dark.
