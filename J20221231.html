Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Here I am enjoying my freedom away from my home through my earnings and work at a place where I have frequented many times and stayed. This place has lived in my life in times of pleasure, discontentment, disenchantment and more. I need to make it on my own with more money and freedom. Can’t go back to stay with parents anymore. This is the decision I’ve taken for myself and choose to abide by it no matter what difficulties I might face in my journey. Solitude is something that enchants me more than ever so before. I wish to have partner at times to enjoy the simple pleasures of life - feel this when I see couples enjoying themselves. It creates an envy in me - why can’t I have something like what they’re having? Is having a lady friend and partner such a hard thing? I mean someone whom I could share the simple things and activities with. I don’t know. The joy of being alone is unparalleled. The joy of doing nothing is also enchanting at times. Just sit and stare at things. There’s no need to interact yet. Just being able to enjoy this bliss is something that I yearn for.
