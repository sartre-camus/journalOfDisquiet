JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

2022-03-21

LUCIDITY, DECAY, STATIC

They talk about the 'pleasure of life' in the gilded uplinks of the High Spire, but down here in the gut of the Iron Labyrinth, it’s a different currency. It’s the jagged split-second before the synth-stim hits the bloodstream, or the way the neon bleed in a petrol puddle looks like spilled jewels if you squint through the smog. It’s a momentary truce with the crushing weight of the overcast sky, a brief lapse in the city's predatory hum.

I watched a rusted courier drone drop a crate of contraband into the slums, and for three heartbeats, the desperate screaming stopped. That’s the joy we’re afforded: a lull in the static. A taste of something that isn't recycled oxygen or the metallic tang of lukewarm nutrient-paste. In this sprawl, life doesn't offer pleasure; it just occasionally forgets to twist the knife.
