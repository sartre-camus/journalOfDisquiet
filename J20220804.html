Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

The incessant need to communicate with people is because of the feeling of loneliness. Being lonely is so despised that one always is desperate to talk, write letters, mails, messages, create artwork, and so on just to express oneself to someone. While it has never occurred to the person just to speak with themselves - exploring one’s own mind-palace, knowing more about oneself, engaging in hobbies and so on. Self talk seems to be frowned upon by many, usually considered lunacy.
