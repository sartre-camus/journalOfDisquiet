THE INNER MONOLOGUE - THURSDAY, AUG

SOLITUDE, MADNESS

The city's loneliness is a disease that makes people talk to shadows. They're so afraid of the silence that they'll scream into the void just to hear a human voice. They don't realize that the only person worth talking to is the one in the mirror. Exploring the mind-palace is a journey most are too scared to take. They call it madness, but I call it the only sane thing left.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
