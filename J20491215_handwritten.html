JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE GRID'S EMBRACE - DECEMBER 15, 2049

STAGNATION, DEBT, NOSTALGIA

I let the neural feeds wash over me again, the digital static now an inseparable graft onto my psyche. I spent the cycle drifting through the Aetheric Systems archives: a few segments of 'The Diminishing', some fragmented loops of 'The Kinship Satire', and that historical reconstruction of 'The Iron Conqueror'. I even endured the first half-hour of 'The Vile One' by the Silent Director before the flickering neon outside my window became more interesting.

The data-stream is a bottomless pit. I watched 'The Skeptic's' tirade against the old faiths and some low-res archives of 'The Jester' pulled from the Public Stream. I even found myself staring at feline frequency modulations until my vision blurred. The list of distractions is endless, but I lack the will to catalog the rot any further. My mind is too heavy with the city's smog to keep populating these logs. To hell with the record-keeping.

The hunger for credits is a constant, low-frequency hum in my gut. It feels like a betrayal, the urge to crawl back to the Syndicates and beg for a loan that would surely cost more than I have left to give. I swore I wouldn't beg, borrow, or steal from the shadows of this sector. I had intended to spend the morning chroma-etching, but I am haunted by a ghost of a memory—a sun-drenched day from the Frozen Cycle, long before the sky turned to lead. I felt the old, base desires for sustenance and skin. These are the temptations that erode my resolve. I can feel the slow dissolution of my self in the face of this eternal rain.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
