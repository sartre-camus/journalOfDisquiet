JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

2022-04-18

desire / distance

Was at The Grand Obsidian again, eating overpriced rice cakes and bitter curry. My hunger for indulgence exceeds my station. The spa session looms on Friday—a brief reprieve from the dirt. Saw the woman again, a divine beauty that feels more like a mirage. I’m a slave to the image, a prisoner of the distance between us. I enjoy the separation; it allows the fantasy to breathe. Proximity would only kill the dream.
