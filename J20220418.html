Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Had been to the Hilton again today, just to have a snack - ’Thatte rice cakes with chicken curry’. It was a bit too spicy - seemed like leftovers from yesterday. Anyhow, my craving for having overpriced food at fancy hotels continues. This is what I term as indulgence. Even though I don’t earn enough, I try to push the limits of my budget. Not to forget the spa session on Friday. Saw Sagarika today, can’t say that I am not attracted to her. Sometimes, she feels like a goddess. A divine beauty that sways me away. Maybe, I’m not being realistic here. But there’s something that keeps me in a high. The sexual energy can be sensed and it just keeps me on my toes. Why have I become a slave to this feminine beauty? There’s always something missing. The thirst for love. I can’t have it - the distance is large, unfathomable. I speak of stupidity - the irrational. I don’t want to fight, I don’t have the courage to chase her, to find her love, to persuade her or to make her fall. Guess, that’s how it always has been. Love at a distance. A separation - I enjoy this separation. It makes me feel the missing, see myself, drifting away. The joy is not in gaining, winning over or proximity of the subject, but, the distance. This gap makes it possible for fantasy to spawn. The fantasy of appearances and images.
