JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

JUNE 20, 2024

EXISTENTIALISM, RELAPSE

I haven't read K-Zan's original manuscript yet—it seems there are stark discrepancies between his vision and the script by Vane used in that old projection. I find myself hoping that re-watching these flickering ghosts might somehow mend my fractured psyche and help me endure whatever remains of this cycle.

I also sat through the projection 'The Dialogue of A-12' directed by The Overseer. It was another endless dialogue between two souls dissecting concepts of the ethereal, the unseen, the weight of matter, human potential, and the occult. It felt like a mirror to my own hollowed-out thoughts.

I had sworn an oath earlier this cycle to sever my connection to all neural-link terminals for the duration. Yet, I succumbed, returning to the portable deck to sift through encrypted packets, prying eyes on The Signal-Stream, and The Aether Syndicate's Ledger for the Free-Code Protocols.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
