JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FRIDAY, DECEMBER

Disillusionment, Solitude

Another cycle under the spell of the Ancient Epic of Conflict, cast upon the flicker-box. Is idleness truly a virtue, or just a slow descent? I chase the phantom of indolence, even in communion with the sacred scrolls. Better, I think, to let the shadow-plays recount the ancient tales. Why have the bound tomes lost their grip? Is it the stillness of their pages? The frozen pose of their players?

The old ways, they crumble to dust around me. My mind drifts, a derelict ship on a dark ocean, anchored by thought alone. The intricate dances of logic, the shaping of unseen forces—they recede, like a tide pulling back from a barren shore. I exist, a mere function: sustenance, slumber, release. A few motions in the confined space, yes, but they’re not the grand designs I once laid claim to. The very purpose of my honing, a whisper in the wind. I reach for a specter from the academies of youth, a man known as The Speculator, for a brief, aimless exchange. The relentless climb, the gilded cage, the cold lure of the ledger – such were the whispers exchanged. None of it stirs the dust within me. This hollow space, it permeates, a fog that refuses to lift. No trinket, no face, no fleeting drama can fill it. I must, it seems, shake hands with the solitude that dogs my steps.
