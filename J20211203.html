Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Another day of watching B.R Chopra’s Mahabharat. Is laziness a virtue? I try to be lazy all the time. Even with reading scriptures. I’d prefer to watch a TV series of the same instead. Why has reading books become a boring activity? Because there are no pictures in it? Or there is no movement of characters? My erstwhile habits are withering away. I spend more time wondering about things and stay there. Skills of programming, engineering etc. are fading away. I just eat, sleep and defecate. Well, I do a few home chores too, but they don’t match my skill investment. I’m slowly forgetting what I was supposed to hone. I call up Praveen, a college friend of mine for a casual chat. Career, marriage and money were the topic of discussion. I’m not interested in any of those. My emptiness is pervasive, it won’t go away. No matter how many things, people or events try to fill it. I need to make peace with my loneliness.
