Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Listening to some music on my laptop, and now trying to focus. It’s unbelievable that I tend to trust someone like Leah and her Libreboot project so easily. I never realised that the website could be a scam and never cared to check the reviews of Retrofreedom.com (earlier Minifree and Gluglug.org.uk) on other websites such as TrustPilot. After reading what other buyers said about their experience, I have come to realise that I have been scammed. The modus operandi of the infamous Libreboot selling website is this that they put up their shop for selling refurbished laptops. These laptops are bought by buyers through either wire transfer or cryptocurrency such as Bitcoin. After the payment is confirmed by Leah or (someone who gets the money from Retrofreedom/Minfiree/Gluglug) nothing happens. The laptop is never shipped to the buyers address. Some buyers have waited for more than a year and have got nothing. Not even a refund. This is outright fraud. Leah’s company continues to operate through scamming people. As far as my order for the T400, placed on August 10 is concerned: I’m pretty sure that I have lost my money. ’Burnt it’ as Leah had once used the term in one of her emails few years ago, when I was trying to get information about the laptops that were sold by her. I seem to have trusted someone because of what they had to show for themselves on the website. I believed without doubting what they had to say. Not even once did I doubt their intentions or their reputation. This was my mistake. I feel sad about this. I know I shouldn’t be hard on myself for making this error as it would make my situation even more terrible. The only thing that I could do is to try do something else and ignore this incident. It’ll be difficult but I shall try.
