JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

2021-08-23

Scammed, Trust

Listening to some music on my terminal, and now trying to focus. It’s unbelievable that I tend to trust someone like The Tech Rogue and her Freedom Project so easily. I never realised that the site could be a scam and never cared to check the reviews of The Legacy Merchant on other networks. After reading what other buyers said about their experience, I have come to realise that I have been scammed. The modus operandi of the infamous terminal selling site is this: they put up their shop for selling refurbished units. These units are bought by buyers through either wire transfer or Shadow Currency. After the payment is confirmed by the Rogue or her associates, nothing happens. The terminal is never shipped to the buyer's address. Some buyers have waited for more than a cycle and have got nothing. Not even a refund. This is outright fraud. The Rogue’s outfit continues to operate through scamming Shadows. As far as my requisition, placed cycles ago, is concerned: I’m pretty sure that I have lost my credits. 'Burnt it' as she had once used the term in one of her signals cycles ago, when I was trying to get information about the units that were sold by her. I seem to have trusted someone because of what they had to show for themselves on the network. I believed without doubting what they had to say. Not even once did I doubt their intentions or their reputation. This was my mistake. I feel sad about this. I know I shouldn’t be hard on myself for making this error as it would make my situation even more terrible. The only thing that I could do is to try to do something else and ignore this incident. It’ll be difficult but I shall try.
