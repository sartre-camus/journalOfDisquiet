JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, AUGUST

Dreams, Envy

Last cycle I had been to The Collective Hive. I was there to meet The Community Liaison after a long time and say 'Hi'. Just to not make it entirely about that meeting, I also showed interest in a dedicated desk there. Just so that she doesn't feel that I was there only to meet her. She is responsible for community management there. Now, over the past few days I have been thinking about whether I should move in there or not. I am a bit worried about whether I would be able to achieve the goals that I have for myself by working from that shared space. I had a dream early morning today, I was asleep and The Ghost of the Past was there in it. She had come to meet me in my safehouse and I was sitting in my chair in front of terminal setup, showing it off. I had a game, The Plumber's Maze, running on one of the screens, which I turned off and asked her, “What do you do for fun?” for which I couldn't hear any reply. There wasn't much of talk that I could remember. Later she gave me a hug and a peck on my cheek. I could feel her body when she hugged me. After that, she picked up her device. I asked for her signal ID and she seemed a little perplexed. She moved her eyes away from me, and frowned. I said it's fine. I don't need it. Then she left my room and went away. It's a strange dream. I haven't met her in reality for more than fifteen years. I am, however, aware of her, from what I know about her from The Grid. She won't remember me for sure. I probably am still affected by the last meeting that I had with her. Where she said, “All the best” to me for my future and left. I felt snubbed and belittled. For all the help I did cycles ago was probably forgotten. In the current time, I feel envious of her success in her chosen field. I wonder why I have not been so successful in mine. Why am I comparing myself with her and her achievements? Why haven't I worked towards the goals that I have set for myself?

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
