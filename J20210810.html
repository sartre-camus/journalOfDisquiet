Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Last week I had been to WeWork Manyata co-working space. I was there to meet Sagarika after a long time and say ’Hi’. Just to not make it entirely about that meeting, I also showed interest in a dedicated desk there. Just so that Sagarika doesn’t feel that I was there only to meet her. She works for WeWork and is responsible for community management there. Now, over the past few days I have been thinking about whether I should move in there or not. I am bit worried about whether I would be able to achieve the goals that I have for myself by working from that co-working space. I had a dream early morning today, I was asleep and Ananya was there in it. She had come to meet me in my room and I was sitting in my chair in front of computer setup, showing it off. I had a game, Mario, running on one of the screens, which I turned off and asked Ananya, “What do you do for fun?” for which I couldn’t hear any reply. There wasn’t much of talk that I could remember of. Later she gave me a hug and a peck on my cheek. I could feel her body when she hugged me. After that, she picked up her phone. I asked for her number and she seemed a little perplexed. She moved her eyes away from me, and frowned. I said it’s fine. I don’t need her number. Then, she left my room and went away. It’s a strange dream. I haven’t met her in reality for more than fifteen years. I am however, aware of her, for what I know about her from the internet. She won’t remember me for sure. I probably am still affected by the last meeting that I had with her. Where she said, “All the best” to me for my future and left. I felt snubbed and belittled. For all the help I did few years ago was probably forgotten. In the current time, I feel envious of her success in her chosen field. I wonder why have I not been so successful in mine. Why am I comparing myself with her and her achievements? Why haven’t I worked towards the goals that I have set for myself?
