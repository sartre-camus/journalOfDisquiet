Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Checked out of the suite, the cost for the four day stay has been about ten thousand. Well was all of it worth it? The spend, the stay, the work, the sleep and the food? Well, what would have been otherwise useful is if I had a steady inflow of cash to keep myself afloat. Running into debts is not a good idea, I’m still far off from the goal. I will have to get a good score in the upcoming language test and ensure my progress towards emigration to the Americas. An uninspiring day it is. My imagination to make a new work is wavering and fading away. The acumen of my thoughts to form a prose or an essay is stagnating and coming to sluggish pace. Not an eventful outing as it may turn out. The pills from yesterday might keep me drowsy. I feel that drowsiness taking over my eyes and other senses. My eyes are barely open, as they try to shutdown quite too often. I was anticipating a flow in my writing. But it’s too drowsy and too boring. Our body is made of chemicals and the reactions of which cause all the changes. An armchair finally! I wish I get one of these in my room. An old armchair that lets me sit down stretching my back into a relaxed mode and my thoughts start to flow. I start to observe them and write them down. An activity that would involve me to let myself free and allow all the thoughts to flow through, without any bias or contradiction, just write them all down. Bringing myself to attention, as I had dozed away for a few moments. The cold waves of AC hits my outer ears and makes me feel the chill. The music all EDM, I am slowly getting bored and start wishing that it all stops.
