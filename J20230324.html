The Mock-Up Shakedown - FRIDAY, MARCH

career, doubt

Tomorrow, I’m up against a mock-shakedown with the GKS crew. C++, UML, Design Patterns—the high-society language of the coding world. I’m supposed to be prepared, but my heart isn’t in the books. I’ll go in, answer what I can, and let the chips fall. It’s a pity I can’t find the motivation to study their patterns. My mind doesn’t work in objects; it works in commands, sharp and imperative. I’m still not convinced the world needs all that abstract architecture. Sometimes, a straight line is the best way through a labyrinth.
