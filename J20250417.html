JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE CONFESSIONAL - THURSDAY, APRIL

Overwhelm, Penance

The weight of the city is pressing down on me. I find the company of other souls increasingly difficult to stomach. I sent the final payment to The Confessor this morning—one last session to purge the demons before the weekend. The safe is nearly empty, the bills are piling up like snow, and the hunger... it's a living thing inside me now, right alongside the lust. I feel like a vessel under too much pressure; one day the hull will snap and everything I’ve buried will come screaming out. Even this 'Quiet Zone' is a lie, filled with the constant drone of voices that have nothing to say.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
