JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, JUNE

Submission, The Wait

At the Manyata Hive, the assignment is finished. I've sent the encrypted dispatch to Erik. I couldn't find the ghost in his code, the bug he described, so I sent proof of my own working machine. Now, I wait. If someone else finds the ghost before me, my chance is gone.

I spent the afternoon crafting the response and uploading the captures. Then, a feast of Hyderabadi spice at Biriyani Zone. I'm drowning in drowsiness. I watched a flickering reel of 'Family Guy' and wandered the campus. The shadows are long.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
