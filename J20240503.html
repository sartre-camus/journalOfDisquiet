Memory, Tragedy

I stumbled upon some old memories out of the blue, wish I hadn't. It just reminds me of few horrors I had been through. Maybe some pleasant experiences here and there but they're just some that I'd like to forget. I am bored by optimism. My narratives won't go away, they just keep changing here and there with no sight of improvement or fading away. I am compelled to live a social life purely out of my nature, demeanour, conditioning, upbringing, whatever... I get bored of endurance many times, I see no glory in it. I want my life to be easier than ever before, I hate suffering in whatever way it comes. Yet, there's no way out. Should I call it tragedy or fate or any of those morose words, I'm not sure. What's aesthetics, what's novelty, why are they worth chasing? I think I am trying hard to be a loser, and I'm sure I will fail at it too.
