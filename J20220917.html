JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SATURDAY, SEPTEMBER

Ambition, Preparation

It’s been an age since I dared to put pen to paper. I’ve been hunting for a new gig for months, and now the big one is finally on the horizon—an interrogation with 'The Jungle'. I’ve been memorizing every back-alley shortcut and code-breaker trick in the book. If I pull this off, I’m headed for the northern territories—Toronto or Vancouver, places where the rain washes away the past. They say there are five layers of hell to cross before they let you into their inner circle. I just need to stay sharp. One slip, and it’s back to the gutters.
