JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, APR

Sustainability, Time

Is there anything sustainable at all, I wonder, everything has a time period until which they seem sustainable. I guess they always talk in terms of cycles of eons, maybe billions or higher. Whatever be it, everything seems to wane away. I have no imagination or even a vague idea of how big these things are.

If I were to wonder based on the number of cycles I am still going to live or remain 'dead' (as I have already claimed myself to be). Does it even matter to contemplate what am I going to do in these years, how will I spend it and so on. I am living in the past and projecting that into the present and future. Is there a present at all I wonder.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
