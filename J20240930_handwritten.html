JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, SEPTEMBER

NIHILISM, ISOLATION

I regret delving into the forbidden codices of Elias Vane. The existential decay he describes is a weight I am ill-equipped to carry. I recall warnings on the Omni-Mart data-slates, whispers that his philosophy was 'not for the weak-hearted'—a warning I should have heeded.

A few cycles ago, I resolved to focus solely on the directives at my disposal. I constantly remind myself that my labor serves a higher purpose, one that precludes the luxury of distraction. I cannot afford to waste mental cycles on the chatter of the hive-mind, their hollow opinions, or the suffocating weight of their collective gaze.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
