The Clock-Watcher’s Manifesto - WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY

productivity, resistance

I pinned a check sheet to the wall—a grid to track the hours I trade for coin. It’s staring at me, but I’m not ready to start the clock. I’d rather let the time slip through my fingers, wasted on nothing but the sweet, heavy pull of laziness. The grid remains empty, a testament to my disinterest in the rat race.
