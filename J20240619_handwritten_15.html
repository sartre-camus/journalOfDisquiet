JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE LEDGER OF THE DREGS - SECTOR 4 - WEDNESDAY, JUNE

NIHILISM, COMMODITY, APATHY

I believe that everything in this neon-choked sprawl can be quantified to a credit-value. There is nothing abstract or beyond the Syndicate's cold valuation. All I can do is sit in the shadows and decide on the worth of the dregs as they shuffle through the acid rain.

Ad-hoc existence. The illusion of work. It’s a form of exposure therapy, I suppose—sitting amidst the masses in the Low-Rise Atrium, surrounded by the hollow chatter of the un-chipped. I laze until my optics flicker and the words wane into static. I watch the colors of the synth-silks, the traditional district-garb of the drifters passing through the lobby when I first arrived.

The wastage of time—the slow passage until the final shutdown is all that remains. We kill it in various ways, despising the renditions of pointless attitudes towards the unnecessary industrial waste of enormous proportions that the city vomits out. It is the waking of needless lies and the hum of machines that never sleep.

I am merely stating my observations. No conclusions, no inferences, no judgments. All I have is what my senses feed me through the grime and the flickering light. There is nothing beyond the flicker of the signs or the hum of the cooling fans. Only silence. Only apathy.
