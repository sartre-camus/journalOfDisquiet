TRANSMISSION FROM THE ABYSS - FRIDAY, JUN

PRIVACY, TRANCE

The faces at The Smoke-Filled Hub blend into a gray blur as the days bleed together. I'm obsessed with wiping my digital footprint clean, following the teachings of The Ghost of the Wire. I need my handheld terminals running on ghost-code, free from the prying eyes of The Syndicate. Today, a strange calm has settled over me. I feel myself slipping into a trance, a slow descent into the neon abyss where thoughts are few and the static is soothing.
