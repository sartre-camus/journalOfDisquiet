MONDAY, JUNE

Waiting, Memory

I'm back in the same smoke-filled room where 'The Associate' once stood. The fire drill was just a noisy distraction, and the chess game on the floor felt like moving bodies instead of pieces.

Now the room is quiet, and I'm just a man waiting for a move that'll never come. The board is empty, and the clock is ticking against the silence.
