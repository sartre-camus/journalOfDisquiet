I visit Hilton F5 restaurant after a long time (as told by staff here). Yesterday, I had pleasurable time with Mahalakshmi offering a full body massage using olive oil and later a chocolate body scrub. It cost me quite a bit as the prices seem to have surged a bit. I later went on to have a Caesar’s salad with bacon and anchovies at the restaurant. It almost feels like I have to stay out and enjoy life as much as I could, even if it meant borrowing money from G or anybody. To repay that money I would have to figure out a way. Most probably, by finding work.

I just don’t want to lose the feel of the massage yet, it’s as if I want to prolong the effect. I want to enjoy more than what’s needed. It’s as if I would like to go back and sleep on that massage table and let my body be devoured by the masseuse.

I somehow like this idea of not only splurging money but also being taken advantage of. I want to suffer and let myself be devoured.
