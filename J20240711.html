JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, JULY

Luxury, Masochism

I stepped into 'The Grand Terminal' for a taste of the high life I can't afford. 'The Healer' spent an hour trying to knead the tension out of a soul that's already frayed. It's 'The Banker's' plastic I'm burning, and I'm enjoying the heat of the fire.

I want to be dismantled, piece by piece, on a massage table. Let the body be devoured by the hands of a stranger. I like the idea of not only splurging what I don't have, but being taken advantage of in the process. I want to suffer and let it all go.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
