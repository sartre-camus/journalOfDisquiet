Obsession with Emancipation - THURSDAY, OCTOBER

Freedom, Individualism

I don’t want to go there. I’ve developed a neurosis where every act must be converted into credits. But some work is intangible—labors of the soul that don't pay the rent. I am a self-centered man, choosing my own gains over the collective. Emancipation is my obsession—antinatalism, the bachelor's life, the single path. Why am I so desperate to be free?
