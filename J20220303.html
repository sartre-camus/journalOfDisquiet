JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, MARCH

DESPAIR, GRIND, SURVIVAL

The greasy dawn brought another low murmur of desperation, echoing off the cracked ferrocrete with D and G. The grating litany of the grind, the hustle, the phantom promise of scrip—it’s all the same tune in this city that never truly sleeps, only twitches. A ritual of quiet desperation, this endless chase for coin just to keep the creeping chill from settling deeper into the bone, to silence the gnawing void. My spirit, like my worn-out soles, is threadbare from this soul-sick pilgrimage across the phosphorescent sprawl, my last remaining spark dragged across the endless, oil-slicked concrete, forever searching for a sliver of existence in the shadows.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
