Mystery, Pulse

The encrypted pulse arrived at midnight, a digital scar on my terminal. Sartre and Camus are still out there, weaving their web of ciphers in the dark. Every message is a challenge, a test of will against the encroaching static. If you want to know what’s buried in the math, you have to play the game on their terms.
