JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE IRON VAULT - WEDNESDAY, APR

Solvency, Debt

The Iron Vault finally released the blood money from the old world stash. It’s enough to keep the shadows at bay at The Hive for another month. I decided not to lean on The Broker for the latest bill; there’s no sense in tightening that noose just yet. I still find myself a spectator in the theatre of my own impulses, watching the lead actor miss every cue.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
