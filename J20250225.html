Journal of Disquiet

Yesterday or the day before I had the strangest of dreams in which I dispose off my father in the garbage and ask the pourakarmika to take care of it. I could visualize my father being tossed into the solid waste management truck and then being recycled by crushing his bones and body together into pieces. The pourakarmika didn’t need much taking to. He understood what needs to be done and with a nod he carried out all of it without much hesitation and offered me a free service. He took my father in the trash, put into the giant solid waste truck and then recycled him by crushing his body starting from his legs and I could see his face with tears while this happened. I had strange sense of contentment after seeing him getting disposed off. After I woke up, I could vividly remember this dream. I had never dreamt of anything like this before as far as I can remember.
