JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE HUNGER OF THE VOID - APRIL 8, 2025

APATHY, DECAY, SURVIVAL

After choking down a bowl of synthetic street-sludge in the Lower Docks, the morning fog has settled into my bones. I feel heavy, a dull ache behind the eyes that makes me want to let the neon-soaked hours bleed into one another until the sun—if it still exists behind the smog—finally sets.

The drive to service the Syndicate's machines has evaporated. I am a hollow shell, devoid of vision or movement. It seems only the primal gnaw of hunger can force me into the hunt; everything else is just a slow descent into the long sleep.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
