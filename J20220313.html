JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SUNDAY, MARCH

DISQUIET, FUTILITY, DRUDGERY

The damp chill in the ferrocrete chamber only deepened as I wasted another cycle, funneling scraps from the Grid-Spire Echoes towards the Ledger-Keeper. A pointless endeavor. I owe him nothing for the occasional data-shard or a whispered word of 'guidance' in this echoing labyrinth. Nothing. Utterly without purpose.

Later, I wrestled the 'Freeboot' unit from its auxiliary perch, the metallic tang of stale air biting at my throat. Dragged it to the recessed maw of the main console. It sits there now, a silent sentinel, nestled within the cold embrace of the drawer. A temporary fix, perhaps. But the damned optic tether, too short for comfort, means the rig can't fully escape its dark recess. I'm forced to hunch, like a broken thing, just to coax glyphs onto the phosphor screen.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
