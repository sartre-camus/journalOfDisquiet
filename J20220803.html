THE PHILOSOPHER'S SHACKLES - WEDNESDAY, AUGUST

PHILOSOPHY, LABOR

The old Greek philosopher was right: working for the sake of gold is just a polite word for slavery. Am I just another man in chains, selling my soul to the highest bidder? I'm reading the old scrolls on labor and toil, trying to find a way to be free in a city that charges for every breath.
