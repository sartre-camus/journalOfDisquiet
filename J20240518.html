Daily entry

After the draining past couple of days, I return back to write about it. I have settled the debt with G, at least for now. I still owe money to Hemanth though. He had asked me to build enough savings before I return his money. Not sure when that’s going to happen or I even what that to happen. I reconnect with my past on Thursday, go back to the same old ways of living, knowing that I wasn’t happy to do them either. Suddenly, I was filled with ego and high self-esteem, as if I owned things and I commanded respect, honor and privilege. I still haven’t learnt enough to endure myself, I haven’t endured my constant need to learn and philosophize and write. I am still insincere to my beliefs of austerity, simplicity and modesty. I am still a slave to my senses and old habits - nothing seems to have changed there. I need to learn to endure more of myself, my thoughts, writings, creations, agonies, sadness, boredom, laziness, apathy and entitlement. I think I should write using a pen and a paper in a book - that seems rather convenient as it consumes no electricity. I assume that writing on a paper using a pen has a lesser carbon footprint than writing using a word processor and a computer.
