GILDED COMFORT - SATURDAY, APRIL

Idleness, Complacency

Comfort is a dangerous drug. When the heat is off, I start to unravel. I’ve forgotten the next move, or maybe I’m just hoping the world will stop turning if I stay still long enough. Doing nothing is the hardest job of all, yet I’m an expert at whiling away the hours in this gilded cage.
