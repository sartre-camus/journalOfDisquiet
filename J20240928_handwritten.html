JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SATURDAY, SEPTEMBER

MAINTENANCE, OBSESSION

Managed to disassemble the neural-link terminal, purging the nanite sediment from its core before reassembling the chassis. I spent hours verifying the integrity of the sub-routines, ensuring every circuit fired in perfect sequence. The grey grime of the lower sectors seems to find its way into every crevice.

It appears I am becoming increasingly tethered to the sensory feeds. I find myself lost in the archives of ancient kinetic sports on the Aether-Net Uplink, unable to pull away from the flickering ghosts of a world that no longer exists.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
