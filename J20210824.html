Journal of Disquiet

One of the ways I keep myself motivated in the work that I intend to do is by buying new things. I buy things that helps me in doing that work. They can be called tools. These tools bring about changes in the way I work. For example - for writing I had bought a typewriter, a tool exclusive of writing. I bought display monitors for my computer to write programs that can be easily edited, read and copied from on window to another. I purchased a mechanical keyboard to provide a tactile feedback when I type an article or program on the computer. I have tried to introduce new tools in my work to keep myself immersed and motivated. New tools and a sense of newness and brings small changes that are tangible. Even the effort of spending money on these tools or discovering them and later using them is also something that makes me use them more and at some time improves my work contribution too.
