JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MARCH 20, 2025

LETHARGY, CONSUMPTION

To do nothing—the hardest of all to do. While most of the time is simply spent staring, gazing at things and not doing anything—there’s no need to go anywhere, talk to anybody, touch that obsidian slate or punch keys into that terminal grid, move the haptic cursor around, click on things. All I think of happens to be sustenance and carnal desire—why can’t I think of anything else—or nothing perhaps? My body constantly needs to be fed something or the other all the time.

I am at The Collective Hive today without having breakfast or anything at the hab-unit. Now, I need to figure out what to eat, drink, etc.

I have started the day with Grade-D starch filaments.

A lunch from The Iron Pot Rationer—had a reconstituted protein bowl and after this, I would head back to the hab-unit as I have spent enough now already.

Now I commute to The Redwood Citadel; hope they don’t ask me to cough up another two hundred fifty shards, which I would gladly throw up as I believe I’m rich.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
