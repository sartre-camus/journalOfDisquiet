JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, APRIL

Cynicism, Neighbors

Made salty eggs and fed it to myself, I gave some to The Ward, but he spat it out. I ate it all anyway even his left overs. Every morning it is the same old saga - why was I born, why did they bring me anyway, I keep wondering. Mondays are hard on me, just because they're Mondays. I wonder if the problem-solution way is what I should follow, will it be what it should be? I have a problem and there should be a solution to it, but should I find it, will it be useful, will it be the solution I'm actually looking for, was there a problem in the first place, the questions go on... I have made myself utterly useless by pestering myself with questions I don't want answers for but keep ruminating over them all over and over. The cynicism and ego of mine doesn't seem to go away, it just eats me from within. I'm farther away from being honest or humble, I just immerse myself in neighboring stories and play parts I don't wish to. I should write about these stories that interest me more than my own career, life, well-being and so on. These stories that I can't undo or wipe out of my memories. The excitement of the day starts with quarrels, loud noises, bedlam, domestic violence at neighbor's home, and so on. I wonder what kind of a locality am I living in. My mother is a monster and father is the devil, I am a mongrel, a despicable charlatan, a parasite and a moron.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
