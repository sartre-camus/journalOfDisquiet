JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER

Wanderlust, Determination

I’m waiting at the gate in the Port of Palms, watching the black birds circle over the coast. The four-day blur is over, and the only thing left is the flight back to the Steel City. I’ve got a window seat to watch the world shrink beneath me. The trip was just another smoke screen, a way to forget I’m out of work and out of luck. But the dream is still there, flickering like a neon sign in the rain: a remote ops job that pays enough to keep me in a high-rise in the North. Toronto, Vancouver—those are the only places left for a man with my particular set of skills. I’m heading back to the grind, back to the code, and this time, I’m playing for keeps. No half measures.
