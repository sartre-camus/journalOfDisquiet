Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Sitting at the Goa airport gates for boarding the flight to Bangalore. The four day trips arrives to end except for the flight journey over the Goan coast. Have selected a window seat this time to catch a good glimpse of the coast during the afternoon. As the trip comes to an end, and the urge to splurge continues, I try to find solace in the craft that I own - programming. Getting back home is the important thing followed by the preparation for the coding assignment. What was the point of the trip though? Well it is just another distraction like many others - a way to escape the immediate reality - job situation and refocus. No matter what I say, or convince myself of - it just is the same thing recurring every time - remote job that pays well enough to live a solo life in a city that is advanced in its thought, lifestyle and technology. Toronto and Vancouver seem to satisfy that requirement of mine - I want to get there and start the programming gig all over again. This is the trip back to modernity and win back all those things that were left abandoned. A fight that’s completely worth it and needs all the energy I’ve got. No half measures this time.
