JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, OCTOBER

Pretense, Waste

The truth is I do not have any work to do. Yet I am all dressed up and acting the act. No matter how much ever time I spend on all these it might just not help in any way. I was planning to take up a lease in the Hive's collective again - not sure how it would impact in any way the things that I do. It almost seems like a waste of everything. I am back to my feasts for the condemned, yet again.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
