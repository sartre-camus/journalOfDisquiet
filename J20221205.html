JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, DEC

Alienation, Nihilism

I’m sitting in the corner of a smoke-filled room, watching the marks crawl by. They’re nothing to me, just shadows in suits. I wish I had the strength to stop caring what they think, to stop looking for meaning in a city that’s hollowed out. I’m here, I’m breathing, but the world feels like a movie I’ve seen too many times. The dame behind the counter doesn’t even see me. She’s got the 'Monday Blues'—a lethal case of apathy. I’m not even going to ask for a drink; I’ll just let the jazz from the jukebox wash over me. I’m a ghost in my own life, and that’s just fine. Let the world spin; I’m staying right here.

People around just come and go for their businesses - tending to all that’s around. I’d prefer to stay alone by myself. There’s no need to pay attention to the denizen - just a waste of my headspace. What’s the point of staring at the next good looking beauty and then feel guilty about it? Would rather sit here and continue typing the boring thoughts of mine.

Just leave them be… Just leave them be… No need to run, no need to find those, just leave them be.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
