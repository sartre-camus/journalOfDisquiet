Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

For some reason, I get inhibited by myself by looking at people who matter nothing more than faeces. Wish I new myself better and put up with things in a much more conducive manner. Treated myself better, not because someone is looking at me and trying to figure out what’s in and what’s not. All that innocence and needless, pointless search for something beyond and under, when there’s nothing worth pursuing. A lacklustre life with less than pedestrian things. I am already present, present in this moment - breathing, thriving, thinking, talking and listening. There’s no future or past, what there is this longing pleasure for things that are around me and beyond me. Why is there this incessant need to please someone? It’s just unnecessary, the people around me couldn’t care less about me and so do I about them. The waitress across doesn’t even care to come and ask what I would like to have. Such is the apathy of service at this place - not unless they knew that I drink or maybe it’s the case of the Mondays! She’s super pissed at things and can’t manage something here or there - do you think the coffee that she’d serve would be any good? for the money I’m going to cough up? Not at all. It’s a waste of my effort even to approach her and ask for a cappuccino. Simply sit here enjoy the good lounge music playing - like it! The music is good!

People around just come and go for their businesses - tending to all that’s around. I’d prefer to stay alone by myself. There’s no need to pay attention to the denizen - just a waste of my headspace. What’s the point of staring at the next good looking beauty and then feel guilty about it? Would rather sit here and continue typing the boring thoughts of mine.

Just leave them be… Just leave them be… No need to run, no need to find those, just leave them be.
