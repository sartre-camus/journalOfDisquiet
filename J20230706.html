JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, JUL

CHOICE, TECHNOLOGY

I canceled the meeting with The Client for the system installation. It’s a pointless thing to do - imposing my software choices on him and getting him to use what is ’better’. I decided not to do that; it’s best I keep my preferences to myself rather than spreading them to people who don’t care. I shall not use The Gilded Ledger too often; there is nothing useful coming through it. Best to stop checking it altogether. I am starting today. No matter what, I won’t see my messages in that deceitful, ad-filled service from the giant.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
