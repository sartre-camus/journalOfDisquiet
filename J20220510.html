JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, MAY

Insomnia, Digital Decay

I began descending into the flickering shadow-play of 'Lucia' on my pocket terminal. A relic from a decade ago, yet it felt as fresh as a new wound. My jaw aches, my scalp burns, and sleep is a ghost I can't catch.

The late afternoon nap offered a temporary truce, but the void remains. I contemplated drifting towards The Collective, to breathe life into the iron-slab running Ghost-code. A few whispered commands, a fix for the IceDove dispatch, and the illusion of purpose.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
