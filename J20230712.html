JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, JULY

DEPRESSION, REFLECTION

I resisted my urge to go to The Steel Forest today, as I know I’ll have to spend credits I don’t have. I stayed back in this little room and worked from here. I spent some time learning about Emil Cioran and his works. I would like to understand how he managed to live in The City of Echoes without a conventional role. I also applied for a couple of jobs today, in the hope that I will get them. Yesterday’s chat with The Landlady made it uncomfortable for me to live here. The struggles I had to go through trying to put up with the shadows in my mind, in order to prepare for inquisitions, have been difficult. The constant rejections have demotivated me, yet I continue to try my luck in this cold world.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
