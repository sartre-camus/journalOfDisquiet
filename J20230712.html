Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I resisted my urge to go to Manyata Embassy Tech park today, as I know that I’ll have to spend money that I don’t have. I somehow convinced myself to stay back in this little room of mine and work from here. I can’t even afford to pay for the fuel to go there now without any money with me. I spent some time to learn about Emil Cioran and his works. I would like to understand how he managed to live in Paris without a conventional job. I also applied for a couple of jobs today, in the hope that I will get them. Yesterday’s chat with P made it rather uncomfortable for me to live here like the way I have - it seems that I have to move out of here soon. Not sure about how long will it take to find a reasonably well-paying job so that I can get out of here and live independently somewhere else. The struggles that I had to go through trying to put up with depression, anxiety and loss of interest, in order to apply for these jobs, sit through there interviews: prepare, appear and participate - it’s all been difficult on me. The constant rejections has demotivated me many a time, yet I continue to try my luck.
