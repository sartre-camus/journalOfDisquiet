JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FRIDAY, SEP

Burnout, Consumption

Another cycle spent untangling the digital webs of The Project and the encrypted whispers of The Comm-Node. I’m drowning in code, trying to bury these ghosts once and for all. I broke the fast with some greasy street-vendor offerings—a salt-crusted pastry and half a fried triangle of doubt—washed down with a brew as dark as my thoughts. I wonder how much of this filth I can ingest before my own machinery starts to fail.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
