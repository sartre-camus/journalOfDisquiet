JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SATURDAY, OCTOBER

EXISTENTIAL DREAD, RESOURCE DEPLETION

At the end, I shall never be able to return to the Biosphere what I take from it. There's no way to restore materials taken away from the Green—the Toxicity Index continues to mount with every bit of Neon-Current that I consume, the Recycled Hydrate that I drink—the resources that I use. It's this enormous baggage of the natural world on me that disallows me from doing anything.

I only wish if I could just sit in silence for the rest of my bodily life and let myself drift towards the end. No matter what I do, I'll always have a burden on the Megastructure around me. Sometimes I just wonder if 'The Rite of Cessation' would be a reasonable thing to do. I should gradually give up synthetic rations and lead myself towards the end through starvation.

Starvation could lead to me having lesser dependency over what's around. I should give up consumption as it only causes more harm than any good. The hunger will not go away; I don't have the will power strong enough to give up the rations and simply get by. I am driven by habit.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
