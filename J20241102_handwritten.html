JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SATURDAY, NOVEMBER

ANXIETY, INDEBTEDNESS

The idea of debt being inescapable torments me. Even if I worked in exchange for benefits such as Credit-Chits, conveniences, etc., I still would remain in debt to the Overseer who pays for my expenses. I am skeptical whether there is anything of value that I could ever give to anyone for the things they give me.

I feel this huge sense of inadequacy. How am I ever going to repay anybody? The debts are unpayable. I will never be able to repay anyone—no matter how hard I try. I will owe it to the Biosphere-Grid.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
