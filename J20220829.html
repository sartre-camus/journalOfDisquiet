JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, AUGUST

Anxiety, Escapism

The air in the room was thick with a nervous energy I couldn't shake. I had an interrogation scheduled with the Syndicate heads at dusk, and I’d already sent out word through the underground channels for support. It felt like I was leaning too hard on the few allies I had left, especially since my latest operations had barely made a dent in the city's thick skull.

The city never sleeps, but I needed to. Daydreaming has become my only escape, a dangerous habit in this line of work. I just wanted to lose myself in the fog, or better yet, surrender to the cold embrace of the dream-world where the shadows don't bite back.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
