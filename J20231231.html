JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SUNDAY, DECEMBER

Hunger, Void

The Eve of the New Era and I fast after the first light. I skip the family gathering at the Gilded Cage, and stay in the shadows of my room. I'm hungry now but the cupboard is bare. Earlier I was watching 'The Brothers of the Void' (1969) on the ancient archives, wearing a blood-maroon robe. I feel empty and hungry, a hollow man in a hollow city. I try to prolong my hunger - does this shell need so much fuel at all? I had at least three cakes of the East in the morning and a few yellow fruits later, shouldn't it be enough to keep me crawling? Not to forget the bitter brew I had after dawn: green leaves with liquid gold. Somehow without fuel in the engine I feel there's no purpose to the grind altogether. I feel hunger was always my deepest shadow and I love to feed it whenever the gods allow.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
