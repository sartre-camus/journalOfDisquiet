Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

The New year’s eve and I fast after breakfast. I skip the family lunch outside, and stay indoors sleeping. I’m hungry now but I don’t know what to eat. Earlier I was watching Brothers’ Karamazov (1969) movie on archive.org, wearing a maroon colored robe. I feel empty and hungry, not knowing what to eat. I try to prolong my hunger - do I need so much food for my body at all? I had at least three dosas in the morning and a few bananas later, shouldn’t it be enough to keep me up and running. Not to forget the beverage I had after breakfast: green tea with honey. Somehow without food in stomach I feel there’s no purpose to living altogether. I feel food was always my deep desire and I love to have more of it whenever possible.
