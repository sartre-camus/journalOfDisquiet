Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I heave, I sigh, I breathe and then, I write this entry. Spent a little while to recollect my thoughts as there were too many or too little of them. I postponed the OpenCraft interview to the next week as I wasn’t ready for it. I was scared to give the interview. Or perhaps, I didn’t care for it anyway. The fear of failure in interviews and a caustic feedback for a bad performance in an interview makes me give up the interview process altogether. My self critique is too strong and powerful. It destroys everything that tried to bud out of mind. Any creative idea, even if it is not original is thwarted and nipped in its bud. Performing an activity without the fear of failure or the expectation of reward is almost impossible. If reward was the driving force behind my actions then, I would have tried to get it by doing what’s needed. However, my apathy towards life makes it difficult for me to enjoy the process of doing creative work. The strife to build new software or write a new article lacks inspiration. These motivational speakers and best-selling authors of motivational books with their inspirational parables and talks feel so lame. Shiv Khera for example, in his book “You can win”, talks of self-motivation, even to this day I never found one, even doubt that really exists. When I don’t know what this self is or what is its nature, how can I motivate it or be motivated? It’s just unbelievable that people paint these stories of fantasy in the name of self-help and reality. I guess it’s an utter waste of someone’s time to think about all these. I should look at what is that I want and try to get it. If I don’t want anything then, I shouldn’t try.
