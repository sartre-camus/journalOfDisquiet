JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, AUG

Failure, Apathy

I heave, I sigh, I breathe and then, I write this entry. Spent a little while to recollect my thoughts as there were too many or too little of them. I postponed the trial with The Craft Guild to the next cycle as I wasn’t ready for it. I was scared to give the trial. Or perhaps, I didn’t care for it anyway. The fear of failure in trials and a caustic feedback for a bad performance makes me give up the process altogether. My self-critique is too strong and powerful. It destroys everything that tried to bud out of the mind. Any creative idea, even if it is not original, is thwarted and nipped in its bud. Performing an activity without the fear of failure or the expectation of reward is almost impossible. If reward was the driving force behind my actions then, I would have tried to get it by doing what’s needed. However, my apathy towards life makes it difficult for me to enjoy the process of doing creative work. The strife to build new Digital Constructs or write a new article lacks inspiration. These Success Prophets and best-selling authors of motivational scrolls with their inspirational parables and talks feel so lame. One such Prophet, in his volume “You can win”, talks of self-motivation; even to this day I never found one, even doubt that it really exists. When I don’t know what this self is or what is its nature, how can I motivate it or be motivated? It’s just unbelievable that Shadows paint these stories of fantasy in the name of self-help and reality. I guess it’s an utter waste of time to think about all these. I should look at what it is that I want and try to get it. If I don’t want anything then, I shouldn’t try.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
