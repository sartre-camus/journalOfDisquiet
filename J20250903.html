Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I still don’t know what sort of jobs should I apply for. It feels like I shouldn’t apply for any. It is better off to simply be by myself and endure the boredom. The desperation to earn and spend money should wane out. Freedom is disguised as money whereas they are two different aspects. One need not have money to be free or independent. These ideas are manufactured by me and they keep crawling in my head every now and then. There’s no real need to be whatever that I think of. Maybe, it is better off just to be by myself doing nothing - it’s hard but probably desirable.
