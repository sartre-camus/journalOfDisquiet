JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

OCTOBER 31, 2024

EXISTENTIALISM, OBLIGATION

An embodiment of ideas—that is what I am. I wonder if ideas exist outside of biological shells at all. The High-Caste say 'great minds discuss ideas,' but aren't those ideas already a mere representation of the minds themselves?

It is a tragedy when I start witnessing this deprecation of the meanings of the words I encounter. It feels like a systemic degradation of the memory of those words and their original intents.

Today I encountered the notion that the only meaning I could possibly extract from my existence is that of The Debt (Protocol-R). My progenitors brought me into this world in arrears, raised me, maintained my functions, and kept me within their sphere—all of this is a debt in whatever form it manifests: credits, gratitude, protocol, affection, mercy. All that remains for me is an ever-growing debt. I cannot settle the full account, but at least I could attempt a partial payment. It is in this repayment that I have faltered periodically. I am currently in a state of credit-arrears to my kin-unit V.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
