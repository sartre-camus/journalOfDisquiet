Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Went to the new Spa around my home to get an Indonesian massage therapy to relieve my anxiety surrounding the job prospects and later decided to shop a little, eat some noodles I made for myself and get a good sleep in the evening, an evening with rain and cold winds. Slept well and felt relaxed. The cost of this relaxation was a hefty one, something that I believe I would be able to earn back. Today, I still feel like lazing out. Don’t want to go anywhere or do anything. Just want to eat, sleep and get myself entertained through movies, TV shows and gaming.

Spoke with Rachit from Hilton yesterday regarding the refund, still not done. Sent an email today to remind them. I feel really drowsy after the lunch and the pleasant A/C breeze blowing in here. I feel equanimous, relaxed and pleasant right now. Just lazed out and lethargic. Guess, it is the side effect of food and drug I take. Drug here is Montair FX. Feel so drowsy and not anxious. Feels like laying down and relaxing.

Had the car pushed away into a local garage nearby with M’s help. Not sure whether the real issue pertaining to the fuel hose would be fixed properly within a reasonable budget. The mechanic seemed eager to get the car fixed. Feel that it might take a longer time for the car to be returned. I came here to WeWork Manyata and cannot collect it by evening. Suspect that the car is going to stay there overnight. My attachment to that car is not fading away. Still going strong and want to make maximum use of it. Why can’t I for once forget that I don’t own it. Just because I spend money on fuel and service for that car doesn’t make me the owner of that vehicle. Simply feel that I should forget about it and be free from it.

Just want to stay here and do nothing. While away my time and sleep and live. Last thing I want to do is to talk to people. Just want to be alone, even in a large crowd of people. Despite have a simulant in the form of Sulemani chai, I can’t find enough energy to carry out work that I wish to. Things seem to fall out of place, finances go haywire, dreams and ambitions astray, misery and despondence bequeath, love and sex float away, what remains is the falling me, failing me, dying me.
