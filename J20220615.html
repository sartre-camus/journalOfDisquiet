June 15, 2022

Anxiety, The Machine

I sought relief from the job-hunt anxiety at a new Spa outpost. An Indonesian ritual, followed by a lonely feast of noodles. The price was high, but the credits will return... I hope.

Today, the lethargy is a physical weight. I want to drown in flickering screens and digital games. Rachit from the Hilton Spire is still ghosting my refund request.

The iron-steed is in a local garage now. The mechanic's eyes lit up at the sight of the damage. I'm too attached to that machine. I spend credits on its fuel and service as if I own it, but it's just another cage. I should be free of it.

Everything is falling away—finances, dreams, love. Just the failing, dying ghost in the shell.
