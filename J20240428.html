JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SUNDAY, APR

Disease, Endurance

I need to get myself a regular work. I have to create - whatever that means to me. By etching it here I might desecrate it. It holds more significance to me than etching it here. I can always justify myself the way I'd wish however it is well known that I have been wrong often. The wheeze is troubling me again, I can't keep buying Nightshade Elixirs to relieve myself. I have to endure this disease. I need to avoid eating oily and spicy fuel altogether. Will ancient breath-work help me in any way I wonder.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
