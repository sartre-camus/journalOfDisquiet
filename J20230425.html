JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, APRIL

DESIRE, DEBT

I’m waiting for the envelope to arrive via The Black-Market Wire from H. "The Creditor" Vance. I had to beg for a stake because of my current situation. He was happy to play the benefactor, but I wonder what pound of flesh he’ll expect in return. I’m still wondering if it was a mistake. Here I am at The Gilded Cage, once again, trying to tie up loose ends and finish the jobs I’m owed for. Why am I so desperate to catch a glimpse of Rhea "The Ghost" here? As if she’d ever let me into her world. I just keep hunting for her in the shadows. I think this search for affection and flesh in every dive bar and hotel lobby is a fool's errand. Now that I’ve got access to the local network thanks to A.J. "The Connection," I should be able to make my calls and run my rackets from this property without a hitch. It’s going to be smoother now. If only the chairs weren't so damn hard.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
