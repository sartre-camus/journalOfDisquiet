JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, MARCH

Multitasking, Culture

The habit of multitasking won’t go away. I encumber myself with doing many things despite the foreseeable advantages of doing one thing at a time. The lack of brevity harms me. I destroy the language by using so many words that are just superflous. I have adopted the Imperial Tongue however failed in borrowing the culture that comes with it.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
