JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, JUNE

Libre Experience, Solipsism

The day began with a lapse—I left my wallet and pocket terminal at the family spire. I sought refuge at the Hilton EGL for a costly feast, then drifted back to the Manyata Hive.

Should I sell my soul to the Cognizant Syndicate for a Java role? It feels like a defeat, but the credits are real. I'm currently off-grid, a truly 'libre' experience. No proprietary tracking, no digital leash—just me and the machine.

Now I wait in the delirium. A craving for accolades, for a sign that I'm seen. Pointless vanity. The silence is the only truth.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
