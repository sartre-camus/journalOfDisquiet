JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SUNDAY, OCT

Survival, Solitude

Holed up in the 'Whispering Palms' safehouse, Room 203, out in the Parra district. I burnt through my last favors with 'The Associate' and the 'Executive Express' to get here. Now the well is dry. I need a new pipeline of credits. I put in a call to Tahir for some offshore sponsorship—some greenbacks would go a long way in this tropical purgatory. I also sent my file to a contact named Muriel at 'The Creative Syndicate' in Panjim. A steady check would take the heat off. The room is small, but it's got a desk where I can work my shadow-ops. I've got my tech manuals—the SVR4 internals—and enough coffee to see through the night. The real work starts tomorrow. Eight hours of digging through the 'Code Crypts' and applying for open-source bounties. I’m my own cook, my own cleaner, and my own boss. As long as the bankroll holds, I’m free.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
