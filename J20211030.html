JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

The Art of Being - SATURDAY, OCTOBER

Emptiness, Art

The birds melody is indifferent to the city's rot. They are just as they are. When I have nothing to do, I am a hollow vessel. Emptiness is my true state. I try to rediscover the meaning of science, engineering, and art. Art is a broad shadow, covering everything from the stroke of a brush to the logic of a machine. The Infinite Archive says art is a creative expression of technical proficiency and emotional power. A conceptual idea made flesh.
