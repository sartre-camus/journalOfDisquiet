Journal of Disquiet

The chirping of the birds in the trees. The melody. There is no difference in the way things are. They’re just the way they are. I feel empty when I have nothing to do. Emptiness of being. I try to discover, perhaps rediscover the meanings of words such as science, engineering and art. There may be water tight definitions to them. However, I still find it difficult to discern them. Art is an encompassing term. A wider term for almost anything? Computer programming is also considered an art. Engineering systems is an art. Art of science. Art of this and that. What is art anyway? The Wikipedia definition of art may help. Let me look it up -

“Art is a wide range of human activities (or the products thereof) that involve creative imagination and an aim to express technical proficiency, beauty, emotional power, or conceptual ideas”
