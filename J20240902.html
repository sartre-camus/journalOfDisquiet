JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, SEPTEMBER

Absurdity, Struggle

Separation of concerns is sought after, despite it being inseparable. Is it like The Sisyphus Curse? that everyday I set myself a goal and at the end I realize that I’ve failed in it and try from beginning all over again, the next day? Is this the absurdity I have to live with? I guess so.
