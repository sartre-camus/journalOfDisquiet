JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE LETHOS DRIFT - TUESDAY, NOV

LETHARGY, SEDATION, DECAY

The cycles in Sector 7 have lost their rhythm. Time flies like a mag-lev train through the fog, yet I remain stationary, anchored by a heavy weight of apathy and the ghosts of the Old City. The shame of what I left behind in the Low-Rises clings to me like the soot on the windows.

This endless fog in my skull is the work of the 'Lethos-C' capsule I procured from a Syndicate runner. It has smoothed the jagged edges of my nerves, plunging me into a state of synthetic tranquility. The hum of the city's ventilation is the only thing I hear now.

My thoughts are no longer rapid-fire bursts; they are slow, sluggish, like oil leaking into the harbor. I feel the pull of the void, the desire to sleep through another rotation of the neon sun, doing nothing but existing in the shadows.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
