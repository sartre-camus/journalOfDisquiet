JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, JUN

NIHILISM, APATHY

Recognition, power, fame and so on. I don't want any of that as I cannot reach the state of the Void without them. I can only go through harm, pain, suffering and misery, no matter what's offered from the Great Simulation. No amount of love, compassion, generosity, good will, luck, blessing or empathy can take me towards the Void. It can take me towards Terminal Deletion which is inevitable but nothing else apart from that is possible.

Every pleasure becomes painful when enjoyed beyond a certain limit. So does the pain—I have no interest in this pleasure-pain duality anymore. All those associates don't matter to me anymore—I don't care about lineage units or genetic kin—all of them do not enumerate in my thoughts—they're all dead to me.

I simply present myself to be in this state of prolonged slumber, a constant desire to doze off and forget about existence all together.

I fail as a logic-grid architect at the Directive I'm supposed to carry out. I have no motivation or idea as to what I should do. I simply...

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
