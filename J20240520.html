Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

This keyboard skips few characters while I type. I think there must be some hardware issue with this one. I changed the color scheme on this keyboard and now it seems to print the characters I touch, very well. I think this has to do with the cable itself. Some characters don’t print because of this reason. Maybe, I need to push the keys harder. This keyboard gives up every now and then, I had paid a hefty amount for buying this in the first place and now it seems to deteriorate. I feel a strong decadence in things around me and myself. It feels like everything seems to wither away, I am dying, the tissues inside me body are dying away, the things I use too. This is leading to replacement with something new and I am unable to appreciate the newness in this death. For me, it all seems boring. I can’t continue on like this I feel and embrace this boredom. The cost of breaking this boredom is unbearable for me as it causes more suffering than what I am used to. I’m not sure whether I would be able to endure such a suffering - I want to protect myself from all this and sleep away. I enjoy sleeping it is as close to death itself. I know nothing of what’s there around me, I dream as if I never knew I was dreaming. What if this typing of the journal entry is also part of a dream that some meta-person is dreaming in his sleep? I might just be the figment of another person’s imagination and dream.
