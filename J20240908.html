JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

September 8, 2024

Insignificance, Clutter

Yesterday I spoke too much. I should have kept quiet. Trying to get things off chest is not a wise thing to do with people around me. It could prove to be detrimental to me. I should avoid being candid with these folks: Pauly, Margo, Dante, Gideon etc. What is this anger that I am living with? Why should I be angry towards ’people’ for whatever they do? Why don’t I understand that these things are ephemeral? Why do I not understand that one day I’ll be left with nothing to endure? Why do I give myself so much importance as if I’m some celestial body? There’s nothing in me that is of any significance. I should stop treating myself with significance. There’s no reason to self identify, market or sell myself. I exist just like those rocks, stones, mountains and water. Like any other thing I too exist. There’s no need to attribute so many qualities to myself or demand for anything from anybody. All my desires, anger, disappointments, mistakes, regrets, achievements mean nothing and are as insignificant as anything else that I could possibly think of. All that credits and what not: they’re all nothing, they simply exist just like I do. All ideas are simply a nothing that manifests in various forms to behave, show, masquerade, portray as something. There is nothing to my fears or anxieties, whatever that exists is simply a way to protect this body from harm. There’s nothing at all just there to do, to be, to show or whatever. I simply wrote for the sake of scribbling, none of my experiences are of any significance, none of it exists, none of it thrives, none of it remains forever - it’ll all perish just like myself. My memory itself is a deception and the memory is meant for me to survive and nothing more - it only manifests as these thoughts that carry nothing but with potentials to create changes. What is left when memory dies? Nothing. I remember nothing, there’s nothing to remember or forget: it’s all finished!

I wonder what happened to those grand plans of me taking up work in The Old World Metropolis and living there. I am stuck here in this codependency that I cannot make ways to escape it. I spend the last twenty four hours watching The Grumpy Clown's Chronicles twelfth season despite knowing that there are more important things to do. I am scared of speaking my mind as I would offend people. I have bottled up these things and somehow not found a way to channelize it. I have struggled in preparing for that tech interview as if I cannot endure the preparation necessary for it. These are enough cross-cutting concerns such as sabotaging my artistic, literary pursuits which primarily involve reading the classics. I need to find better motivation to carry out these. My head is cluttered and becomes a mess because of overthinking. I cannot obsess over people and what they’d say to me when I encounter them. I should stop asking philosophical questions: they provide no answer but instead lead me to an abyss of disillusionment. I should do simple tasks, that’s a good way to make progress from this stagnation/slump that I’m going through, with respect to work. I need to get some sunlight, at least in the morning - I cannot live without it. Maybe I should go out for a walk every now and then. I still don’t know whether these ’healthy’ exercise habits would make me healthy in any way? or possibly sicken me further. There’s no sun anyway now, due to the cloudy and windy weather - I need to endure these things.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
