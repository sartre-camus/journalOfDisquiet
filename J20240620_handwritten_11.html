LETHARGY, APATHY

A stupid idea. I'm plagued by lethargy, stupidity, and apathy disguised in various virtues and traits that prevent me from achieving anything at all. The pessimism, Elias Thorne's works—The Hollow Doctrine to be more precise—prevents me from doing anything worthwhile. The first question I ask myself is, 'Should I do it?' and the answer to it would more often than not be a 'no'. I don't want to do anything except enjoy basic rations and carnal pleasures. Entertainment can never be rejected. I enjoy or feed on it more habitually than ever before. The extravagant lust for these things has become a primary cause for my misery.

So much noise pollution around Sector 4—what a tragic phenomenon. I can't ignore but endure the noises of a deep-core excavation drill, the cacophony of the masses, all those decibels piercing my ear constantly despite covering it with acoustic dampeners that ache.
