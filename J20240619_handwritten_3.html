JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FRAGMENT: THE LEAKING MIND - WEDNESDAY, JUN

ANXIETY, ESCAPISM

...all the dancing and singing debris of this Sector—a completely forgettable affair, as insignificant as the void itself.

When my mind is flooded with static, the only respite is the leak. If I am writing, I cannot think too much; the noise drains onto the parchment. I can sacrifice these pages, along with every drop of synthetic ink in this stylus. I could write for eternity about the ghost-stories that surface during my existence. I could write about the absurd fever-dreams that haunt me—a chaotic collage of memories and expectations manifesting in the dark.

I am left with these echoes, writing all day as an escape from the mandatory labor I am bound to: logic-weaving for the sake of credits. Earning currency merely to pay off the life-debt to my Progenitors and the Guild. I am feeling a deep anxiety today for neglecting the priority work: mastering the Prism-Logic and Neural-Flux protocols. Most importantly...

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
