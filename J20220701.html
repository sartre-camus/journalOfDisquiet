JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

RETREAT TO THE DEN

REBELLION, ISOLATION

The new operation begins in the shadows of my own apartment. I'm through with the corporate charade. Yesterday's goodbye at The Glass Hive felt as hollow as a spent shell casing. Now, I'm going underground, back to the pure logic of the machine. I'll strip my handheld terminal of the Syndicate's tracking-scripts and replace them with ghost-code. I'm becoming a ghost in the machine, a digital hermit. No more outsiders, no more prying eyes. Just me, my den, and the code.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
