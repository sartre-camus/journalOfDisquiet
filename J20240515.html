JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, MAY

Futility, Argument

I argue with The Innocent today, quite needless actually explaining her about futility of birth, the cruelty of birth, the trouble with being summoned and so on. I wish I had refrained from such a discussion with an imbecile, yet somehow I believe that I have the capability to make them see my point. This is what my life has boiled down to: trying to explain and convince people about what I think. What an utter waste of time. I somehow still have this conversation.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
