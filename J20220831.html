JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, AUGUST

Exhaustion, Routine

I didn't hit the sack until the clocks were mocking me at 3 AM. By 9, a heavy thud against the door jolted me from a shallow grave of sleep. I couldn't find the strength to face whatever ghost was knocking. It turned out to be the 'Cleaner', just looking to sweep the sins of the previous night from my floor.
