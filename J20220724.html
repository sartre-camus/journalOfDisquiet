JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE CANDLELIT VIGIL - SUNDAY, JULY

ANXIETY, MEMORY

Three candles on the nightstand, flickering like the last hopes of a condemned man. If the grid goes dark, this is all I'll have left. It reminds me of a weekend in the shadows of my youth—two days of darkness before a crucial test. I spent the time drifting, avoiding the inevitable, only to call in sick when the reckoning arrived. These tests, these deadlines set by others—The Matriarch, The Patriarch, the faceless teachers—they're all just bars on a cage. They want me to run at their pace, but my heart only knows the rhythm of the city's slow decay.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
