Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Three candles lit in my room: a fat one, a little one and a tiny one. They lie on the small nightstand. If there were a power outage for a long time then, all that remain is fire and candle; to give the light when there’s none. Remember an old incident that happened to me when I was studying in high school. Couldn’t study for a test on the Monday when there was a power outage during the weekend before. Back then, I used to wake up early in the morning around 4.00 AM to study. However, the weekend before the test was a tough one, as there was no power for two days in a row. The only time I had to study was during the day which I spent doing other stuff - household chores, playing outside, or just having a heavy breakfast and then going to sleep. I might have skipped the test as I couldn’t prepare enough, and with the fear of failure looming around, chose to stay at home, calling sick. All these exams, tests, interviews, demos, deadlines, and so on make me anxious and scared. Always hated them as I would never be able to do something on my own pace. Someone sets a target - do this by such and such time or else there would be consequences. The stress to do enough and sometimes ’well enough’ to satisfy my own expectations. These expectations are dervied from someone else’s - mother, father, teacher, neighbour, cousin, younger brother - the list is endless.
