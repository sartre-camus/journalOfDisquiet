JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, DECEMBER

Boredom, Distance

Another night at 'The Grand Hilton', another expensive meal I can't afford at 'The Neo'. The scene is getting old. There’s a singer on stage, her voice like velvet and broken glass. I don’t know her name, and I’m not going to ask. In this city, knowing too much is a dangerous thing. I’ll just listen to her song and keep my secrets to myself.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
