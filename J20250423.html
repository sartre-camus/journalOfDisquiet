JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE WEAVING - WEDNESDAY, APRIL

Purpose, Survival

My brain is a traitor, whispering of hunger when I have enough stored energy to outlast a siege. I could fast for a day and a night and still be standing, yet the habit of consumption is a leash I can't slip. Why does the mind crave the very things that slow the body? I was debating the 'Why' of the job today. It’s not just about the credits, or so I tell myself. The weaving of code is a survival skill. Each line I write is a brick in a wall that keeps the darkness out. The Syndicate might change, the tools might rust, but the craft is mine. It’s the only thing that makes me more than just a ghost in the machine.
