JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE CARBON STAIN - WEDNESDAY, JULY

OBSESSION, DEBT, ISOLATION

These empty, ruled fiber-pulp sheets mock me, demanding I refine my analog strokes as if the physical act of staining them with carbon could somehow validate the void. I have spilled enough ink without purpose; at best, my records are absurd, banal, and utterly meaningless. Yet, the addiction has taken hold. I crave the scratch of the stylus and the scent of the chemical stain, a desperate hope that by documenting the decay, I might feel something other than the constant hum of the machines.

I sit here in a synthetic-leather coat, damp with the recycled humidity of the Sector F5 Transit Hub, trying to conjure a world of ideas from the smog. It is a strange curse—to be burdened with a flicker of optimism while harboring a deep affection for the works of The Void Architects and their nihilistic scriptures. I feel a phantom wealth despite the crushing weight of my credit debt, an entitlement to desires I have no means to satiate. The neon flicker outside the window promises everything and provides nothing.

I am lost in the static, unsure which combination of neural-link protocols or ghost-code arrays will secure the next scrap of scrip. I have not even endured enough of this crushing boredom and isolation to find the bottom. I simply drift, a ghost in a machine that stopped caring a long time ago.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
