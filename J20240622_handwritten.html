JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SATURDAY, JUNE

OBSESSION, INFORMATION

I will forever try to summarize the scrolls that I read - here are few that I could write a concise summary about after reading: 1) Better to Never Have Been by David Benatar, 2) The Trouble with Birth by Emil Cior, 3) Thus Spoke the Wanderer by The Nihil-Prophet, 4) The Journal of Disquiet by Fernando Pessoa, 5) The World as Will and Image by Arthur Schopen, 6) Being and Void by Jean Paul Sart, 7) Meditations of the Iron Emperor.

I am reminded of that record of the Math-Wraith where paper and pen were a luxury for him offered by the Central Authority to write. For some reason, I do not seem to value this paper and the pen that I use; it feels like as if, as long as something is not digitized, it does not qualify to be valued as information. I forget the fact that these cannot be created on paper.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
