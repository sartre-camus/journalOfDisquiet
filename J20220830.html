JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, AUG

Isolation, Paranoia

The wires are buzzing again, and I can't stand the sound. Every ring feels like a gunshot in a quiet alley. I’d rather kill the power, cut the lines, and let the world go dark so I can finally hear myself think.

Yesterday’s meet with the fixer named 'The Vault' was a wash. It felt more like I was the one doing the digging while he sat there, tight-lipped and empty. I expected a real test of my steel, but it was just small talk in a smoke-filled room. My gut tells me that lead is dead. I tried to peer behind the curtain of the Vault, but it was just more shadows. Now I'm looking at the 'Board of Lost Souls' for work, but the thought of those vultures calling me makes my skin crawl. I’d rather go rogue, finish my own shadow-work, and release it into the void on my own terms.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
