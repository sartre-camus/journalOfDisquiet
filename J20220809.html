THE DIGITAL NARCOTIC - TUESDAY, AUG

ESCAPISM, ANXIETY

Lost another day in the digital underworld of New Bordeaux. Chasing virtual cars and collecting glossy ghosts on the gaming terminal. It's a cheap hit of dopamine in a life that's running low on real thrills. But the game can't hide the truth: I'm three years into a dry spell, and I still don't know how to catch the next big tech-whale. The pressure is a knot in my stomach that no game can untie.
