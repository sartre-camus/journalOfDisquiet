Daily entry

Feel a strong urge to finish off those Mafia III DLC missions. After having spent an entire day yesterday playing it on Playstation 4, I still seem to not get enough out of it. Collecting all those Playboy magazines, cars, posters and others, I feel the instant gratification of dopamine induced highs. The artwork seems so appealing and the graphics too, even though there are many glitches in the gameplay.

For some reason, I haven’t been able to figure this out for the last three years - “What should I be doing to get the next big tech job?” I feel alarmed by the expectations I had set for myself and knowing that I haven’t been able to achieve many. Feel nauseating when I think about it.
