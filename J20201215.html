JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE VAULT AND THE BUREAU - TUESDAY, DECEMBER

Routine, Materialism

Purchased this new diary from The Archive Merchant a week ago. Had it delivered to my safehouse. Received it today through a local courier service, along with the Chess game set. The chess game that has wooden borders and cardboard chequered draught board. the pieces are plastic with a ceramic finish. They are shiny pieces with a distinct sound when they fall on the floor or when hit against a wooden or metallic wall. I wish the pieces held their ground (the board) without falling off so easily, with barely a vibration, they tend to fall. The bottom finish of the pieces could have been cushioned with some velvet or sponge to have that stability and deft movement while playing. My door lies open to the winter breezes that are cold and sickening as I write my first entry in this diary. The match between The Northern Lions and The Southern Wolves is playing live on the screen, but here I am duly scribbling in my diary. I have made few saving arrangements with The Vault and The Dead Letter Bureau recently. I managed to pay my installment for The Sovereign Fund and also created a managed account that is better for holding few hundred thousand credits with an interest rate better than standard accounts. The Bureau savings account was created with an intention to save credits, mostly physical currency deposit that can avoid the taxmen. As I lay on my bed covered in thick fibre bed sheet, I write this note to myself. It’s cozy in here and comforting. I could hear the birds chirping at distance and hawkers crying out loud to sell their wares. The temperatures are low as we near the end of a boring, tepid and mundane cycle which is better to be forgotten than to remember much about. I stare at these interviews and work prospects ahead to earn more. I need to set my work in order to ensure consistent pay checks that settles my bills. My kin rightly said that I am on a hiatus now. My way of relaxing and doing nothing for a brief but substantial time period.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
