Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Purchased this new diary from Sapna Online a week ago. Had it delivered to my home. Received it today through a local courier service, along with the Chess game set. The chess game that has wooden borders and cardboard chequered draught board. the pieces are plastic with a ceramic finish. They are shiny pieces with a distinct sound when they fall on the floor or when hit against a wooden or metallic wall. I wish the pieces held their ground (the board) without falling off so easily, with barely a vibration, they tend to fall. The bottom finish of the pieces could have been cushioned with some velvet or sponge to have that stability and deft movement while playing. My door lies open to the winter breezes that are cold and sickening as I write my first entry in this diary. The cricket match between India and Australia is playing live on the TV, but here I am duly scribbling in my diary. I have made few saving arrangements with my bank and the Post Office recently. I maaged to pay my installment for the SBI PPF and also created an MOD account that is better for holding few hundred thousand rupees with an interest rate better than savings account. The Postal savings account was created with an intention to save money, mostly cash deposit that can avoid taxes. As I lay on my bed covered in thick fibre bed sheet, I write this note to myself. It’s cozy in here and comforting. I could hear the birds chirping at distance and hawkers crying out loud to sell their products. The temperatures are low as we near the end of a boring, tepid and mundane year which is better to be forgotten than to remember much about. I stare at these interviews and work prospects ahead to earn more. I need to set my work in order to ensure consistent pay checks that settles my bills. My cousin rightly said that I am on a hiatus now. My way of relaxing and doing nothing for a brief but substantial time period.
