JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

The Savior Search - WEDNESDAY, OCT

Faith, Solitude

Life goes on, a slow leak that never ends. Watched 'Life of Pi'—a story of a castaway. Maybe there's a Savior for my suffering after all. I just haven't looked in the right gutters yet. Had a dialogue with a ghost in the machine today. He asked if I remember who I used to be. I told him I’m not my best self right now. He said he was doing just fine.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
