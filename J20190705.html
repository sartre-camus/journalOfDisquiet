JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE CROSSROADS - FRIDAY, JULY

Ambition, Relocation

What is it that The City of Temples has to offer compared to my current residence?
a) A quaint and peaceful life in a place of meditation.
b) A better livelihood in terms of freedom and lifestyle.
c) Another chance at love.

What can possibly go wrong in reaching where we want?
a) Hostile work environment and deadlines.
b) Disapproval from the denizens.
c) Financial crisis due to various reasons.
d) Kith and kin can reach me overnight and stay along.
e) Myself, commitment, food, and the climate.
f) Unsure whether work in The City of Temples can meet my salary expectations.

Again, the feeling of settling for something less can creep up. We thought of relocating to The Sprawl of the North or The Waterway District, but now we find ourselves in The City of Temples. Is that a compromise?

What is the argument behind working here versus The Sprawl of the North?
a) The weather is much more hostile there.
b) The cost of living is a concern.
c) The freedom I seek here is mostly to satiate my libido and nothing less.
d) The gestation period for adapting to an unknown place is longer.
e) More suitable for a trip once in a while and not for staying long.
f) Most importantly, it allows me intangible freedoms that are fictional, currently. This is a step closer to independence.

We started this journey on 15 June. Today is 7 July. Almost 22 days have passed. The progress made so far:
a) Hardware setup is ready for most parts.
b) Released the first candidate of the connector for The Infinite Chatroom.

Thoughts

Initial thought was to contribute to The Global Ledger, but the setup was paused. The intention is to ensure we don't do things for free—at least the things we're good at.

Parallel Thoughts

a) Pursuing studies again in The Desert of Scholars or the distant halls of Europe.
b) A gig at The Iron Fortress for a permanent solution.

As a software engineer, I should know how the machine works. It shouldn't be that I work like a mechanic who tweaks and tries things at random—"monkey coding" solutions. These are deterministic and verifiable solutions. No more brute force.
