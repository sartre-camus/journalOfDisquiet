Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I find myself in reluctant position when it comes to doing things that require mental activity. Thinking and focus are few things that I’m missing the most. The tendency to avoid brain work and do brawn work is disturbing development. My conditioning is worrisome unless tried to act upon. The conditioning to remain unproductive, over analysing is making it difficult. The over reliance on food as a reward mechanism to my mind and body is proving to be detrimental to my overall well being. The constant worry of freedom and living takes over when left to be all by myself. Why am I not working on developing new programs? What is the reason behind my indifferent attitude towards work? Why is ti so hard to get over my habits, control them and make my mind follow the necessary steps for making myself content and happy? The drowsiness of my brain to always sleep and forget about things that perhaps matter is a negative development.
