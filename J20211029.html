Sleepy Desires - FRIDAY, OCTOBER

Sloth, Philosophy

The body’s desires are heavy with sleep. The rain is a constant drizzle, a gray curtain that tells me to stay low. I’ve started reading the Prophet of Pessimism, Arthur Schopenhauer. The World as Will and Representation—a fitting companion for a rainy day in this city.
