August 25, 2024

Logic, Failure

It’s hard now to create a daily plan, allocate hours and try to work on tasks. These tasks are seemingly difficult to commit to. I go through intense thoughts about almost whatever I could think of. They go deeper and deeper with eventual quietness. I can’t endure these thoughts as it is difficult to put up with them. It’s as if I am some god and have to make decisions and/or have opinions on every worldly matter. I studied algorithm analysis yesterday and tried to solve problems from Skiena’s Grimoire of Logic. I poured myself with looping problems and used summation to arrive at a complexity function as well as the return value. I don’t understand summation identities very well, find it difficult to solve many of them. I looked through the books I have on summation but couldn’t find many examples, exercises or explanation regarding that topic. Now, I need to look through my ebook collection to study this topic before I get back to algorithms. How easily I forget mathematical preliminaries with no practice, I wish I remembered them as if I remember a movie actors’ name, plot or lyrics of a song. A dull day as usual wherein I try to solve mathematics questions of elementary nature during the mornings. I can’t get enough out of my brain to solve them - it’s as if I never understood those concepts very well. If I had understood them, then, I should be able to recollect and reproduce them. It’s as if my entirety of learning has faded away, I fail to recollect them when I try to reconcile. I am drawn towards sensual pleasures and want to enjoy them guilt-free but it’s impossible. I feel low and dejected as I find it a bit challenging to study mathematics and computer science - the two subjects or disciplines that I want to devote my energies and time towards.
