Homework

I was thinking through the points that you mentioned in the counselling session. ’How does it make me feel when I am rejected by a woman?’, ’Even if she says ’No’ why does it affect me?’. Perhaps, the reason I see it as is: I regard myself with pride and high self esteem, even to an extent of narcissism. Moreover, I haven’t figured out the dating game yet, thoroughly. There is little or no learning from my past flings. Every situation seems new and challenging. I can’t change myself to impress a woman. I am what I am. That’s why this activity of finding a sexual and/or emotional companion is taking longer than expected. I also blame it on the conservative and religious society we live in with high standards of morality, expectations, beliefs, rules, rituals and so on. This has conditioned everyone to live according to a ’socially acceptable’ standard which I fail to fulfil. This is one of the reasons why I wanted to leave India for good and settle down in Western Europe (where individual freedom and choice is acknowledged as supreme). My attempts to settle in Western European countries such as Germany, Netherlands, Sweden, Denmark etc. has failed in the past few years. Had interviewed for many jobs there for my profession but never had the grit to pursue further and land the offer. I blame this particularly on my unwillingness to leave the comforts of my home where I have been mollycoddled by my parents (especially mother) to an extent that everything seems so comfortable. This hesitance to make the necessary sacrifices to achieve what I dream of: is the primary reason for my state of despair. The idea that ’life could be far better elsewhere than here’, never convinces me enough to pursue my dream of settling down in the West. I am plagued by this notion that ’everything I need is around me and is enough to keep me satisfied’. This belief has deceived me many times and fuelled laziness and procrastination in realizing my dream.
