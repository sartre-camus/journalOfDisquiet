The feeling to run away, escapism as they say, makes get away from patterns. The proven pattern - breaking those patterns to discover something beyond it. To transcend the obvious. To search for things beyond what there is.

My problem is I don’t know what my problem is. Does that mean I’m dissatisfied with everything? There are two possibilities: first one is I’m satisfied with everything and the second is I’m dissatisfied with everything. Let’s keep these two possiblities for simplicity. The first one definitely doesn’t seem to be the case, which leaves me with the latter - dissatisfied with everything. The way to solve this is to focus on things that I can change - myself, my thoughts, my consciousness, my actions and so on.
