JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, MAY

Escapism, Internal Audit

The urge to run, to slip through the cracks of the established patterns, is a constant hum. To transcend the obvious. To seek the unseen.

My problem is a lack of definition. Am I satisfied with the rot, or dissatisfied with the cure? Dissatisfaction seems the more honest path. The only solution is to rewire the machine from within—consciousness, actions, the ghost in the shell.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
