JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SATURDAY, FEBRUARY

Nature, Company

I go on a tour of unseen places with a host from The Neon Coast to show me around. I spend a good amount of credits to pay for this trip and boarded her vehicle for the ride. The host takes me to an art gallery with a cafe for the breakfast and coffee. Later, we go to The Sunken Atoll and the backwaters to get some scenic views of nature. With my music from the device being played on the vehicle's audio system via the Sub-Sonic Link, I sit in the air-conditioned interiors with the host. She was a Shadow in her late twenties or maybe early thirties, dressed in shore-wear and a dress on top of it. I could see that she was all set for some beach visit and sightseeing. She kept singing along with the music that played on my device and was always engaged in some conversation or the other. She took me to an art gallery of a local storyteller and bought for herself few things. Mostly, things that could be gifted to someone else.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
