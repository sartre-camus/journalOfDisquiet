Journal of Disquiet

Post-dated entry

I go on a tour of unseen places with a host from Goa to show me around. I spend a good amount of money to pay for this trip and boarded her car for the ride. The host takes me to an art gallery with cafe for the breakfast and coffee. Later, we go to the Divar island and the backwaters to get some scenic views of nature. With my music from the phone being played on the car’s audio system via bluetooth, I sit in the air-conditioned interiors with the host. She was a women in her late twenties or maybe early thirties, dressed in beach wear and a dress on top of it. I could see that she was all set for some beach visit and sightseeing. She kept singing along with the music that played on my phone and was always engaged in some conversation or the other. She took me to an art gallery of a local cartoonist and bought for herself few things. Mostly, things that could be gifted to some one else.
