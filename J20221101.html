Daily entry

Another day in this old place where I have spent many years, I choose to remain nonchalant about what’s happening around me. I ignore the visitors who try to invade my thoughts and manipulate my thinking. So, I chose to stay away from them both physically and emotionally. I don’t want to give my remote control to some other person. Best would be not have one at all.
