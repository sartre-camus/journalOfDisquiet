JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, NOVEMBER

Stoicism, Autonomy

Another day in this decaying sanctuary. I’ve been here too long, watching the shadows stretch across the floor. I’ve become an expert at being invisible, even when the room is full of people. I ignore the ghosts and the grifters who try to get inside my head, trying to find the remote control to my soul. I’m not giving it up. In this city, if you don’t hold the reins, someone else will ride you into the ground. I’d rather have no reins at all.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
