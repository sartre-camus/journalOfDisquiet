JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, JUNE

STAGNATION, OBSOLESCENCE

I spent a few hours watching 'The Sovereign of Sector 7' episodes as if it were going to be worth my time based on a random reference from Subject D. I should've just dropped that idea after watching that quote on 'the purpose of the Academy' and should've moved on.

I wonder where all those important works that I had to carry out went. Are they still there or have I become redundant now? Remember the advising I gave myself the other day regarding priorities, labor, and a truckload of important things that were worth spending time on—where did it all go? Did it materialize into those phosphor-tabs at least? Or have they just vaporized the moment they showed up without a trace?

Maybe the latter. I hesitate to break the work into tasks that have to be done, put a deadline to it, and start working—it's as if, if I do that, I would stop from doing whatever that task was on creation. I give up, dear journal.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
