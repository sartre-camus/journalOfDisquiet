JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, APRIL

NIHILISM, ISOLATION

The Plague Years. I cannot continue to bear the noise—I have no way to escape the industrial excavation or the constant hum of the Sector. I simply sit and endure. I endure my own existence day in and day out until the final silence.

I despise the labor of the machine, work that yields credits, or anything of the sort. I feel a desperate urge to remain shielded, consumed by my own shadow, preserved in stasis. I have no desire to expose myself to the hardships of the outer world.

I question the programmed morality, the ethics of the collective, the sacred texts—all that is inscribed and broadcasted. I have mastered the art of negating every construct thrown my way—in the style of the Sage of Negation!

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
