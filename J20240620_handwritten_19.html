JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, JUNE

DESPAIR, MATERIALISM

The sleep beckons as I write slowly. The words need to go still. Yesterday's carnal release was good after all—I was unable to sleep later. Somehow, scrip as a concept constantly bothers me. On one hand, it enables me to enjoy luxuries, and on another, it makes me toil for things I cannot easily do. Why do you bother me so much?

I go ahead with some of the mundane choices that find me elusive—to see the work fall apart monetarily, to see the funding dry up makes me sad and hurt. It makes me feel desperate, wanting, and restless. I try to relax, yet I find myself amidst work. It has been a long journey to break into; somehow the idea that I will succeed is highly unlikely.

I wait again. I think those Aether Credit agents are deliberately going to make this transfer longer—push it to another three or four cycles. I don't trust what they say, not even one bit.

Why do I feel like another parasite? I feel like splurging, spending all the scrip I've got and going broke. Maybe I should write to Vaelin and explain whatever has been done so far—give him a demo or something like that.

Let me go and get the Void Protocol integrated and see if it works—try to make that work. I can do that after all. I want no one.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
