JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

September 1, 2022

Rejection, Existentialism

Word came back from 'The Vault'—the answer was a cold 'no'. I should have walked away first, left a note for the handler, Esada, and kept my pride. But hope is a dirty habit, and I let it get the better of me. Now I’m back at the starting line, staring down the same rain-slicked streets I’ve been walking for three years. How many times can a man hit the same wall before he breaks? I start to wonder if I’m even cut out for this shadow-game. Is this life of code and gears really what I want, or am I just another cog grinding itself to dust?

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
