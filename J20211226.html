JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SUNDAY, DECEMBER

OBSESSION, FRUSTRATION, MEMORY

That illicit flicker-show, 'Flesh & Frame Uncut,' on the old Static-Net back in the turn of the Cycle, '03-era... that was my poison, see? When the city's hum first truly resonated in my young blood, I caught them on the flicker-screens – ghosts of bare skin, barely shrouded, dancing like digital wraiths. They're still out there, echoes adrift in the vast Aether-Web, but locked tight behind iron-clad data-gates. A slow, gnawing obsession, this hunt for a keycard, a cipher to a phantom past. Every alley of the net, every back-channel probe, just another dead-end street in the rain.

The Grand Archive of Alexandros, a whispered name in the dark corners, supposedly holds them. But its portals only open for the cloistered scholars of the Spire, the privileged few who speak the language of sanctioned data. I've battered against its digital walls, but the iron hand of the new protocols, the ever-tightening noose of the data-lords, keeps them just out of reach. And the tariff? A gouge in the soul: a hundred and fifty glimmer-shards for a single ghost of an image. Always just beyond the grasp, like every truth in this godforsaken city.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
