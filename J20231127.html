Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Why am I obsessed with nostalgia? Is it the reason why I come back to this place over and over? Or is it just to see Sagarika? I’m not sure though. Perhaps, it was to see that girl whose name I don’t remember. The one who paid for my last meal. It’s just absurd the way things are - the people whom I search for and the idea of where I belong. It’s just the idea, that I actually am nowhere. I see myself somewhere but it’s just false.

Why am I always looking for someone in that crowd, in which no one recognizes me? What is this infatuation with trying to belong somewhere? Always this urge to fit in the picture, no matter where it is. The absurdity of finding.
