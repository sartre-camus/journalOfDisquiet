JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, NOVEMBER

Nostalgia, Absurdity

Nostalgia is a dangerous drug in a city built on ruins. I keep coming back to the same rain-slicked streets, looking for the ghost of Sagarika or that nameless girl who once bought my survival. It’s an exercise in absurdity. I’m searching for a sense of belonging in a crowd that’s already forgotten my face. I see myself everywhere, yet I am nowhere. Just another shadow trying to fit into a picture that’s already been burned.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
