JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, JULY

LETHARGY, DETACHMENT

I refuse to mentor the ward for the various reasons I have already logged with Proxies P and M. I see no purpose in the act of instruction. I myself am a hollow vessel, and to volunteer for such a task would be a catastrophe for both the ward and myself—a waste of precious chronos, credits, and vital energy.

My cycles are consumed by the drudgery of maintenance and idle chatter with Proxies P and M when the surveillance permits. I numb my mind with feeds from the Global Stream—flickering images of sector unrest, gladiatorial games, state-sanctioned rhetoric, and the endless noise of political theater.

I find it impossible to focus on the mandatory data-streams: cyber-architecture, system-logic, the old mathematics, or the forbidden records of history and geography. The intellectual fire has been extinguished. I lack the discipline to navigate these labyrinths. There is a growing sense that total surrender is the only path to psychic preservation.
