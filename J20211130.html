JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

2021-11-30

VOID

Nothing ever started, nothing ever ended. Things have been, things shall be. There’s a little sunshine today. Few clouds cleared and I could feel the sun. Then there were clouds again: the sun’s playing hide and seek with me. I have questions: big ones. I feel comfortable living with them. There’s no intention to find answers to those questions, but just let them tag along.

Ideal weather Sunrises at around 8.00 and brightens up by 9.00. It stays the way till evening 6.00. There’s cool breeze blowing by. The sun shines nonetheless. The clouds come and go. But don’t stay the way they should. It’s sunny and cool.

I wish I were an introvert who loved solitude. Seems that I crave for human company often. Be it through sexual gratification, romantic affairs, friendship, relationships and so on.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
