JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, NOV

Longing, Uncertainty

Nothing ever started, nothing ever ended. Things have been, things shall be. A sliver of light pierced the murk today. The shroud parted, briefly kissing my skin with warmth. But the grey conspirators returned, snatching the glow. The great orb, a trickster, playing its game with my weary eyes.

Queries fester within me, vast as the city's underbelly. Yet, I find an uneasy truce in their company. No intention to chase their fleeting shadows, merely to let them linger, unwanted companions on this long ride.

The elements conspire. The dawn, a slow reveal, bleeding color into the sky from the eighth bell to the ninth. Holding its breath until the sixth chime of dusk. A chill whisper threads through the street's forgotten corners. The light, an unyielding eye, pierces through nonetheless. The grey phantoms drift, restless, refusing to hold their appointed posts. A paradox of light and shadow, both chilling and stark.

Sometimes, I dream of the quiet life, a recluse untouched by the world's grasping fingers. But the truth is, the need for another soul burns, a constant itch beneath the skin. Whether it's the momentary oblivion of flesh, the tangled web of a shared heart, the fragile bond of a confidant, or the desperate grasp for a connection, any port in the storm of loneliness.
