Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I check-in to an AirBnb near my home to get myself some seclusion from the home and to its denizens. I feel free when I’m away from my family and neighbours. It just feels so much better to read and write at my own place with my own company. I use my AMEX card to pay (for obvious reasons) but don’t find the payment accepted yet. I try to run a program, debug it and help myself with other things instead. I should be more conscious of what I eat. My food intake is the biggest concern for my health. My strong appetite and craving for food needs to be curtailed otherwise, it might make me an addict and also make me sick. I need to avoid spicy and oily food under all circumstances. Need to stick to healthier options and avoid stress eating. Food is something that one must have to satiate hunger and nourishment requirements. It shouldn’t act as a way to satiate carnal desires and give dopamine highs (as induced by sex, drug or alcohol). It should be handled in limited amounts and at right intervals. How do I turn a deaf ear to the unnecessary sounds and people’s noises around me? I shouldn’t heed to any such nonsense because they’re not worth my time. I need to learn to ignore and focus on my work/priorities at hand. Running away from it may be a solution, but won’t be a long term one. How best I ignore is going determine how best am I going to thrive joyfully.
