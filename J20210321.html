JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SUNDAY, MAR

Seclusion, Discipline

I check-in to The Temporary Haven near my safehouse to get myself some seclusion from the home and its denizens. I feel free when I’m away from my family and neighbours. It just feels so much better to read and write at my own place with my own company. I use my Golden Token to pay (for obvious reasons) but don’t find the transaction accepted yet. I try to run a program, debug it and help myself with other things instead. I should be more conscious of what I consume. My intake is the biggest concern for my health. My strong appetite and craving for sustenance needs to be curtailed otherwise, it might make me an addict and also make me sick. I need to avoid spicy and oily food under all circumstances. Need to stick to healthier options and avoid stress eating. Consumption is something that one must have to satiate hunger and nourishment requirements. It shouldn’t act as a way to satiate carnal desires and give Chemical Highs. It should be handled in limited amounts and at right intervals. How do I turn a deaf ear to the unnecessary sounds and the noise of Shadows around me? I shouldn’t heed to any such nonsense because they’re not worth my time. I need to learn to ignore and focus on my work/priorities at hand. Running away from it may be a solution, but won’t be a long term one. How best I ignore is going to determine how best I thrive joyfully.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
