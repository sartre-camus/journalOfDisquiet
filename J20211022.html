Journal of Disquiet

Epiphany

I’m not in a hurry. Not in a hurry to live or to die. Not in a hurry to follow a career, passion or love. Not in a hurry to marry and settle down. Not in a hurry to buy pleasurable things. Not in a hurry to sacrifice for others. Not in a hurry to pick up a fight. Not in a hurry to be angry or resented. Not in a hurry to be happy and ecstatic. Not in a hurry to earn money. Not in a hurry to spend it either. Not in a hurry to follow an ideology and believe in a philosophy. Not in a hurry to discover for myself. Not in a hurry to learn and know. Not in a hurry to complete this passage!
