JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

No Hurry

Patience, Epiphany

I am in no hurry. Not to live, nor to meet the Final Curtain. I feel no rush for a career, no burning passion, no desperate need for love. Settling down is a trap for the impatient. I don’t crave pleasurable trinkets, nor do I seek the glory of sacrifice. Money—those ghost credits—can come and go. I’m in no hurry to believe in a philosophy or to discover the secrets of my own wreckage. I’m not even in a hurry to finish this sentence.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
