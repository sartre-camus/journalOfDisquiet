JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE SMOKE AND THE CRAFT

Creativity, Self-Improvement

I travel some dozen miles to reach this place in the heart of The Smoke to safely park my vehicle and get inspiration to build something creative. A long drive through the dusty, pulverized roads that fail to the test of time and weather. Destroyed almost to a level of no recovery unless built again from bottom up. “Perpetually under construction”, is what would be the status of the city’s roads. I just try to use my energy in overcoming stuff that seem obviously boring and pedantic. Now, that is a waste of my precious time. I plan to write and make something for myself. Writing takes courage, dedication and the commitment to make it through the ups and downs, shallows and pinnacles of experiences. A craft that requires effort and care to hone. Originality of thought is crucial and so is the content for writing. What goes into is not just facts and fun but a more polished, chiseled and fine piece of art. As I write, there is always that sense of recluse, a detachment from this being, from the physicality and the obvious realities of existence. I try to fabricate and imagine fantasies for myself. I should stop chasing Shadows and try working on improving myself. All there is to this life lies within me and how I act, not really much to do with the approval or denial of the truth by others. The worry of seeing this diary run out of pages troubles me. As I pen down every word, I see that the diary is inching closer to exhaustion. Power over life is a deep desire that I have. The free will to make choices and live them. Sometimes, I write for the sake of it. Not a wise idea. I need to be careful. Tomorrow, I plan to visit The Dead Letter Bureau to get my tokens and passbook updated with terminal banking. Honing a skill such as architecting code takes an effort worth investing in. I plan to read more about the digital tongues and their usages. I should use my time in learning new skills which includes studying programming languages and writing apps.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
