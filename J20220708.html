JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE LONER'S ANTHEM

SOLITUDE, POVERTY

The night was a silent standoff. The Enforcer tried to breach my sanctum with the promise of a meal, but I know the price: more domestic drudgery. My credits are running dry, so I'm back to hunting for bounties in the digital wasteland. I told The Headhunter to stop calling; their games aren't worth the effort. Now I'm waiting for a word from The Tech Giant, hoping a former contact can pull some strings. I'm wearing my maroon shroud today, feeling the weight of the city. I watched an old flick about a man who lives in the shadows—'The One Who Walks Alone'. The refrain haunted me: 'Alone to be happy... happy to be alone'. It's a bitter truth, but it's mine.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
