Daily entry

After the silent night yesterday, when P tried to break into my room just to get me to eat dinner, which I abandoned, as I felt dejected, bored, and something that I better not do. Felt as if P was play-acting just get work out of me - household chores done when I’m around. It’s just something I would keep away from unless there is a personal need of mine that I need to take care of. As my finances linger in shallow waters, I sit and try to find work, so that I can get compensated. Such a rudimentary requirement but yet essential for survival. Wrote back to Michelle regarding Toptal test yesterday, told her I won’t take the online coding challenge as it was not worth my time or effort. A detailed mail was sent and no reply from Michelle yet - think that’s the end of it. Now, I wait for other responses for the job’s I’ve applied for - especially from Microsoft after Hemanth referred me to a few jobs there. Feeling low and tired this morning, after a heavy breakfast and a warm shower, I feel sleepy, in the maroon robe that I’m wearing, feel like laying down for a nap. Isn’t there anything exciting to do? Why can’t boredom be enough for myself? Last night I watched a Kannada movie from 2002 called Ekangi - which literally means Loner or someone who lives alone. A song lyric from that movie goes like this - ’Alone to be happy O’… Be happy to be alone’. Appealed to me, aloneness is enough, no need to communicate unnecessarily. Silence is magic when used wisely.
