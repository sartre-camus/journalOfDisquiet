JOURNAL of DISQUIET

Illusion of Ownership - FRIDAY, NOV

Identity, Lethargy

I live in an illusion of ownership. The place, the clothes, the food—they aren't mine. They are just things borrowed from the external world. I dressed up today to pretend I had a goal. But I’m just hungry and sleepy. Finding a new job is a mountain I’m too tired to climb. Lethargy is a sweet siren, luring me into the deep sleep of the bored.

Watched a movie about emancipation and the rot of discrimination. It moved me, but also made me feel the threat of what humans can do to each other. I want to remain aloof. I want to explore violence through the eyes of the Hegelian Joker, Zizek. What is identity anyway? Just a framework of self-identification within a biological hierarchy. I refrain from thinking too much; the abyss is right there. I’ll just lay down and sleep it away.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
