Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I live in an illusion of ownership. What I feel is mine it isn’t mine. The place where I stay, the things that I use, the clothes that I wear, the food that I eat and others. All that is there is something else. The external as I might say. I got dressed up today so that I could get some work done. I feel hungry and sleepy. I close my eyes gently and look at the screen. To find a new job is a challenge for me. My unwillingness to learn the relevant and go through the process of interview is a bothersome development. Somehow, staying idle fascinates me. It lures me into lethargy and boredom eventually. Close your eyes and go to sleep. Doctor Sleep. Watched another movie late this afternoon about emancipation, discrimination and social issues. I feel moved and threatened when I look at what a human is capable of. I just want to remain aloof of the world around me. Do something else, and then something else and so on. Unrelated events and just go away with time. What is violence? I want to explore this topic. Planning to view Zizek’s video on this. There’s so much to know about. Not all may be necessary but knowing what’s important to me is unknown. What is identity? The identity of self within the social constructs, biological hierarchies and any other framework which exists. Frameworks of self identification. I refrain from thinking too much as it may end up in an abyss. Lay down and sleep away.
