JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

NEON DRIFT - WEDNESDAY, MAY

ISOLATION, APATHY, DECAY

The rain is relentless tonight, drumming against the rusted vents of Sector 4. I find myself nursing a synthetic stimulant, watching the shadows shift. I’ve decided to break the silence, to approach one of the lost souls drifting through the neon haze. No more hiding in the static. I’ll speak to the first stranger I find, just to prove I still possess a voice in this hollowed-out sprawl.

The air tastes of ozone and burnt wiring. I remember when this district was a bustling hub of commerce, a frantic market of flesh and scrap. Now, it’s just a graveyard of ambition. The Collective has scrubbed the history clean, leaving only the hum of surveillance drones and the flicker of dying advertisements.

I tell myself it matters, this urge to connect, but the cynicism is a heavy coat I can’t shed. There is no point, really. The words are just noise against the siren’s wail. I’ll play the part, act the role of the social animal, and keep my head down. Don't be the moralist, don't be the sociopath—just another ghost in the machine, playing along until the power fails.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
