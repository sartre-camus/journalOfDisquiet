THE SILENCE OF THE SHADOWS

SILENCE, KNOWLEDGE

Knowledge is a burden I've carried for too long. Perhaps I've seen too much, heard too much from the whispers in the dark alleys. All I crave now is the silence—the kind that only exists in the eye of a storm or in the depths of a discarded file cabinet. A silence that drowns out the world and the ghosts within me.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
