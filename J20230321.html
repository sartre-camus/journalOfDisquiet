The Backend Rejection - TUESDAY, MARCH

career, integrity

The ZopSmart syndicate is knocking on my door again. They want me in their HSR office, drudging away on Golang for their e-commerce racket. They keep sending the wires, but I’m not biting. I’ve seen their kind before—the micro-management, the mundane grind, the toxic air of a workplace that eats its own. I know the payout would clear my debts, but my soul isn't for sale this time. I’ve spent enough time in the trenches of toxic shops to know that the mental scars aren't worth the credits. I’d rather stay a ghost, living on my own terms, even if it means counting my pennies. There are better paths than becoming another cog in their machine.
