Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Should I interview for the role of Senior Backend Developer (Golang) for ZopSmart Technology? They seem to be sending me these emails and want me to work from their office in HSR. Not sure whether going there and working for their company does any good to me. Even for the sake of interview, I don’t wish to give one. It’s simply a long way away and I’m not really motivated to work for an E-Commerce company based in India. It just feels so mundane - the job, their offerings, the micro-management and so on - is it really worthy of my time and energies? I know they may pay me adequately, but is it something I really want to spend doing for the next three years or so? Am I so desperate for finding a job? I know I could pay off that credit card debt over time - that’s not the reason why I should be taking up this job. Definitely not! Even interviewing with their likes is a waste of time according to me. Why spend all the energy preaching about my work and then expect them to believe it and make me an offer? My past experience has convinced me that working in toxic workplaces has had a drastic impact on my physical and mental well-being. I don’t want to be in such a workplace again, drudging away all my intellectual faculties, working through the hurdles. I feel I have got better alternatives, one of them being continuing the way I’ve been so far.
