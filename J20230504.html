Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

The unscrambled writing of diaries through journal entries. Unlike those handwritten pages, this seems a lot more readable and palatable to anybody’s senses. Isn’t it much more clearer here? The fonts and legibility of typed characters? The wait continues for responses from people regarding the various applications I had applied to at their companies and organizations for work. How am I self employed when I am depending on income from others? What is that there is that I have to do anyway? The open source project I started working on has reached a dead end because I don’t know how it can be used for raising money. The patches and an eventual release may not do it. I just hate the idea that even after doing all these things, I would have to beg for donations from people to support my work. I had seen this middle aged white woman on the first of May, who wore a kurta and a salwar just like a women from the subcontinent. I can’t remember her name though but I was told that she was a civil engineer. I’m not sure whether that’s her vocation or is it something else. I can see those gray hair on her forehead pulled backwards to expose it and then she sports the same clothes she wore the other day or is it a different pattern on her dress? Looks different though I guess she has different dresses of the same color with different patterns that she wears every other day. What pulled me towards her was the stoic demeanour and the quiet. She spoke only sparingly when it demanded her to. Otherwise, she bore that dead silence and a rather fatigued look on her forehead. As if she’s tired from work.

The obsession of just doing nothing. All that effort, is that even what I’d want to do? Whereas, I choose to do nothing, I would want others to do things for me. Just that’s what it is all about. I fail to be productive, I’ve become utterly unemployable. No one can ever get work done from me. I will never be able to do anything that benefits anybody. My whole point of existence is futile and impact-less. I just live with no purpose or goal. Withering away, dying every second I breathe and every second I die, I realise that it’s all going to end - the agony, the pain, the despair, the failure and the despondence.
