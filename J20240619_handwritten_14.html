JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, JUNE

EXISTENTIALISM, DETACHMENT

I am letting someone else own my work and everything that I happen to produce. It's being sold and harvested by some rich and powerful person from Sector 7.

The possibility of being something else than what I'm now builds hopes in myself to go on for another day. The magic of something happening that could make my life free from suffering is unknown. Why so much emphasis on suffering? As if it were all there was and all that should be worked upon. In this vast ocean of emptiness, a vacuum of nothingness, what is this urge to fill it with anything? Let there be nothingness, there is no need for anything that has to be filled with - pleasures, pains, memories and so on.

Time is a deception, reality is a myth, senses are ambiguous. The walls we built have limitations, what is there to whine about? When there is nothing that demands anything? Today, I shall exist, like I have existed before in time, there's no purpose to this and whatever dances I saw are simply an ephemeral rendition of nothing.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
