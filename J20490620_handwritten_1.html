JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

SUNDAY, JUN

EXISTENTIAL DREAD, APATHY

There is simply enough contagion, malice, agony, mourning, and sorrow in the Outer Sectors that would crush me under the weight of fatigue and force me to grovel.

I am simply forced to endure the existential stagnation that arises from being, a void more agonizing than any physical wound. No chemical suppressant or digital distraction has shielded me from it. Every pursuit eventually dissolves into apathy—some consume me quickly before I abandon them. Even the study of cyber-logic and algorithmic geometry becomes a tedious grind after a few cycles.

The previous stylus ran dry of obsidian fluid, so I switch to the cobalt reservoir now. An excess of self-perception, self-pity, and the like. There is always a justification for these aches in one form or another—I merely drift, attempting to ignore them into non-existence.

I find it difficult to grasp the nihilistic theorems Vaelen posits in his codex. There are numerous archives I have attempted to decrypt and comprehend, only to fail. The treatises of Sartos, Nitzke, Shopen, Coran, and the other Old World thinkers remain frustratingly elusive.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
