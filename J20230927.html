JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER

Cynicism, Digital Decay

The Platute job was a bust—a low-rent scam designed to bleed students dry and leave the talent holding an empty bag. Now, I’m adrift in the city, another ghost with a keyboard and no paycheck. The Open-Node plugin in the Go dialect is finished, but it’s just another piece of digital refuse destined to rot in the back alleys of the GitHub sprawl. No one listens to the signal anymore. I’m just shouting into a void that only echoes with my own cynicism.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
