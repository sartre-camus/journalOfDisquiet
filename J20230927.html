Daily entry

After a small ’stint’ to get paid through Platute, I decide to chuck it all together. What a waste of time to work through a scam app to scam students and universities in education and then get paid nothing at the end. I have become utterly jobless after finishing the Go plugin for OpenProject. I still can complete the Wiki and documentation for the plugin, but who cares? Or even write few more tests that won’t change much? If there’s no feedback from users or interest from community, why care to build it at all? I know that I myself am not going to use it, anyways. The best thing for me to do is just leave it as it is. If there’s any interest from community then, I might look into it, otherwise, it’s a dead software that no one wants to use. GitHub is a place to host such garbage and let it stink and rot eventually.
