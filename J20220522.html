JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

May 22, 2022

Defiance, Distant Horizons

Who is the architect of my misery today? The Elders—P, K, M—and their ilk. They want me bound in their marriage rituals, a mirror of their own mundane lives. I long for a distant sector, a foreign land from which the path back is overgrown and forgotten. I've let them hold the remote to my life for too long. I can remain a stone, unmoving and cold, or I can be a ghost, vanishing into the unknown. My cage is built from my own comfort.
