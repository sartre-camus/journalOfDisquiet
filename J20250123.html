JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THURSDAY, JAN

Lust, Hunger

There’s been so many things that have happened in the last week or so. I can’t keep track of all of them through journals. Most of things that happened has to do with money, sex and food. I wish I had anything else to talk about. I have made myself a servant of the pleasures the world has to offer. It just feels I like all the self-motivation needed to do something - most of it is driven by lust and hunger. There’s nothing beyond it - it seems.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
