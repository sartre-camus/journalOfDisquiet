JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE PRICE OF INDULGENCE - THURSDAY, JUNE

REGRET, LUXURY

Finally sat across from The Gilded Rose, after a month of waiting in the rain. I had to let go of the blood-money I'd been chasing—a refund that meant nothing in the grand scheme. It was all vanity, a hollow urge to live like the high-rollers in the glass towers. I've sworn off The Ivory Spire from now on. No more five-star cages, no matter which corner of this cursed globe they're in.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
