PARANOIA, ANGER

I’m looking over my shoulder just to write this. The air in here is thick with prying eyes. I feel like they’re ready to pounce the moment I slip up. The will to keep moving is thinning out. I left my wheels with the valet at The Grand Continental, and the thought of those grease monkeys touching my car makes my skin crawl. I should have just buried it in the basement myself. And that cocksucker at the gate—blocking my way, making me walk the long way around like a common beggar. Not sure what game he’s playing, but I’m tired of being the pawn.
