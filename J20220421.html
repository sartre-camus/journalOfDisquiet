  • Lack of team diversity (most of them are Indian)
  • Cyber security is not yet my core interest: it has to do with privacy and anonymity. Here the work is more focused on protecting interests of big tech companies, government organizations - basically all the plutocrats and people in power

Should the money obtained from these ’plutocratic’ organisations be used for funding non-profit foundations? I doubt that. There has to be a bottom-up / ground-up work that needs to be done

When in doubt about something: Don’t Do. The known failures are better than an unknown success. Accept failure and misery; rather than seek an unknown success.
