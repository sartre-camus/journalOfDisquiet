JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY

Return, Focus

I decide to go back to The Emerald Metropolis. After all the stints of finding work and stay in this God-forsaken place called The Neon Coast, I choose to make heads towards my safehouse back in The Emerald Metropolis. There are cons of that place in terms of Shadows' attitudes and behaviours. However, I choose to save some credits and effort in doing unnecessary stuff. What my kin said about my stay for a moon cycle and not more than it seems to be coming true. However, they don’t realise what I have up my sleeve. I need to work towards that plan of mine. Sometime or later I shall get there. After this much-needed recluse from mundane, pedestrian banalities of my safehouse, I find myself a tad bit rejoiced and peaceful, before I head back to The Emerald Metropolis. I might have a role waiting back there at home, but I need to focus on my dream and work towards it. It’s highly important and I never forget that and move on.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
