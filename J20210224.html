Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

I decide to go back to Bangalore. After all the stints of finding work and stay in this God forsaken place called Goa, I choose to make heads towards my home back in Bangalore. There are cons of that place in terms of peoples’ attitudes and behaviours. However, I choose to save some money and effort in doing unnecessary stuff. What my cousin said about my stay for a month and not more than it seems to be coming true. however, they don’t realise what I have up my sleeve. I need to work towards that plan of mine. Some time or later I shall get there. After this much needed recluse from mundane, pedestrian banalities of my home, I find myself a tad bit rejoiced and peaceful, before I head back to Bangalore. I might have a job waiting back there at home, but I need to focus on my dream and work towards it. It’s highly important and I never forget that and move on.
