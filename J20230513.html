Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Another day with mediocrity. That’s how it always pans out to be. What’s the point of believing that there’s going to be something different or any change at all. I’m here at F5 after my brief time at adjacent WeWork. I travelled late today afternoon to come here and figure out about my work situation, which is still under a lot of trouble. I try to get back to my earning ways through some form of regular income. However, it’s taking more than expected. The wait is too long. The OpenRefine team hasn’t responded back to my job application yet. I’m left with no choice but to move on, apply for other roles elsewhere and join them. I had an email from a Singapore based recruitment firm for the position of software engineer, I’m still looking out to connect with the client and get my work started. Money’s always good to have, considering my lifestyle and my ways of spending lavishly. I am not sure how to curb my spending behaviour however, I’m worried about crossing this unknown limit of mine - what if I end up in a debt cycle, that becomes vicious and I’m unable to get out of it? This is a wild concern that I’m pestered by every now and then. How much is it all going to be worth? Am I going to earn it back, all of it? That’s the question I pose myself.
