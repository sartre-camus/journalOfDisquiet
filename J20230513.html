J20230513 - SATURDAY, MAY

MEDIOCRITY, ANXIETY

Another day drowning in mediocrity. That’s the only way the cards fall in this town. Why did I ever believe the game would change? I’m here at The Fifth Floor after a brief stint at the adjacent Collective Hive hub. I drifted in late this afternoon, trying to untangle the mess of my current situation. It’s still a disaster. I’m trying to find my way back to a steady score, a regular payout. But it’s taking longer than the runners promised. The wait is a slow torture. The crew at The Grinder still hasn’t sent word on my application. I’m left with no choice but to keep moving, knocking on other doors. I got a message from The Lion City Syndicate about a specialist role; I’m still waiting for the connection to be made so I can get to work. Money is the only language this city speaks, especially with my taste for the finer things. I don't know how to stop the bleeding, and I’m terrified of crossing that line where the debt becomes a noose I can't slip. It’s a shadow that follows me every step of the way. Is any of this worth the price? Will I ever earn back what I’ve lost? That’s the question that keeps me awake when the neon flickers out.
