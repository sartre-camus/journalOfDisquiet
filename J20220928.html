JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

September 28, 2022

Freedom, Exile

After a night of heavy spirits and low stakes, I secured a 25K loan from 'The Associate' to clear my ledger. The plan is simple: skip town, head for the 'Emerald Coast', and set up a remote station. I need the sea air, the distance, and a room where the only voice I hear is my own. This isn't just a trip; it's an extraction. I'm cutting ties with the ghosts I've lived with for thirty-two years. It’s time to see if I can survive on grit and code alone. I want a life that’s mine, one where I'm the only one answering for my sins. I've been chasing this ghost of independence for years, but my ideals always ran faster than my legs. Maybe this time, I’ll finally catch up.
