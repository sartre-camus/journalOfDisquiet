Daily entry

After a spirited evening yesterday, I borrowed 25K from A for paying off few debts I have. Well, basically to travel to Goa and work remotely from there. Motivation being good weather, decent food and independent apartment to work/live from. A solo trip for the longer duration I had planned so many times but failed is going to be accomplished now. Also, it is a way of getting away from the undesirable elements here where I am and the people whom I have lived with for thirty two years. It’s better for me to travel, live, explore and survive in the world on my own through my strength and will. That creates a meaning for me - freedom respecting living - being responsible for everything I have and do. A self righteous individual and independent being is what I aspire to be - the dream that I saw many years ago, worked on it, however, couldn’t achieve it to the ideals I set for myself. My ideals outgrew the pragmatic ways of life and business, which became a hindrance for my happiness.
