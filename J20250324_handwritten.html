JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, MAR

EXISTENTIALISM, POVERTY

The credit drain of existing outside the designated Hab-Zone is becoming unsustainable. I simply lack the clearance levels to cover the overhead. The core conflict remains: the friction between the executable and the impossible.

The illusion of agency—the supposed freedom to act at any given moment—feels like a systemic failure. I am cursed with the burden of unscripted choice.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
