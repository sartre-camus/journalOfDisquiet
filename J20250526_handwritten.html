JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, MAY

NIHILISM, LETHARGY

I come in leisure to this sector, hoping to produce something creative, as if my mind will churn out words and patterns that weren't tried before. This is how hopeful I am about writing—this ability of producing sentences that shall seem coherent and convey sense yet with a touch of abstractness laid in the midst of everything. It is convenient for me to write down a journal entry every now and then; now that it's being handwritten and later digitized, it feels special. The contents of what I write about shall always be vague; it is a reflection of my state of mind. There is no need for me to remain committed to what I ought to produce—it will be more than what I'd possibly imagine. With the abundance of paper, ink, and time, I shall not inhibit myself in putting down words on paper. I still wonder if time is still the luxury I have; it seems like it will never be enough.

As far as today is concerned, it was just another day of me trying to look busy but trying to kill time in disguise, as if waiting for some external event to take place that might benefit me. The coffee that I got before writing this down has turned cold and it tastes bad. I will have to warm it up again—the chilly temperature makes it cool down quicker, barely lasting a minute.

I got my Legacy Sim-Station back today along with some books I wanted to read. I just kept watching feeds on the Ether-Net the whole afternoon after having the lunch here at The Collective Hub. The only good reason to show up here happens to be the lunch that is affordable and decent enough to fill my belly. When I get back to Aether Corp Habitats, I shall have dinner—the big tasty dinner. I feel blessed to be able to enjoy such good things despite the pain and misery that otherwise surround. It is good to have a place to stay, food to eat, clothes to wear and relax doing nothing whenever possible. All of this costs credits; luckily I had enough to spend it now. It would be difficult to continue if I run out of it—I only wish my progenitors would take me back in if I do not have credits.

I have anchored myself in a certain mindset regarding work and show no intention to change—it is as if nothing matters anymore. Everything shall perish and wane away anyway—why try? is always the question that beckons me. Why not let it all fall down and break apart and scatter and rot?

My body still hurts after so many days of fever—it feels so painful at times especially the lower back, shoulder, joints etc. It compels me to a physical therapy that I cannot afford. I have base desires that keep coming up every now and then; I do not wish to lose any more credits or time on those. I still am unable to recollect that big spend that is due—maybe it will hit me one way or another sometime or later.

Why do I not have this 'Sacred Zeal' or devotion towards the logic-grid architecture work that I do? What will it take to make me do something worthwhile there? Why do I not treat myself with love and compassion that could encourage me to pursue this craft further? Why do I not motivate myself, encourage myself and console myself enough to keep this craft ongoing? I believe that I can be more compassionate towards myself and be kind with the failures I endure. I can be kind towards myself in recognizing these inabilities, inadequacies, redundancies, laziness and so on.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
