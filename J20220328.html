JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, MAR

waiting / stillness

Sitting at the Dedicated terminal in The Collective. I sit and wait for the rain to start or the world to end. Most days, I wonder what gravity holds me in this state of decay. As I try to focus my mind on the void, a transmission comes from Dante. Should I acknowledge? Better to remain silent. The silence is my only ally.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
