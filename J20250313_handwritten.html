JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MARCH 13, 2025

LETHARGY, NIHILISM

I am still in the stasis, unable to commit myself to systemic labor as I remain anchored in this heavy drowsiness. There is nothing to be said or shared—it feels as if there is only a vast nothingness which has manifested itself in various forms, always masquerading itself amidst the machinery of existence.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
