JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE STATIC BETWEEN FREQUENCIES - WEDNESDAY, APRIL

ISOLATION, STAGNATION, DISQUIET

Another cycle lost to the static. I drift through the rain-slicked alleys of Sector 7, unsure where to find the spark required to fulfill my quotas. The math of survival doesn't add up anymore; the credits don't stretch, and the creative pulse is flatlining. There is only the constant hum of the city’s distractions, a digital siren song that leads nowhere and leaves me stranded in the void.

Focus is a luxury I can no longer afford. It feels like a jagged piece of shrapnel lodged in the mind—a wound I refuse to let heal because the pain is the only thing that feels real. There is a grim satisfaction in recording these transmissions, a commentary on the slow rot of the soul. I should simply cease to strive.

Interfacing with the Drones has become an exercise in futility. I lose myself in the internal feedback loops, over and over, until the world outside the window is nothing but a blur of flickering neon and distant sirens.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
