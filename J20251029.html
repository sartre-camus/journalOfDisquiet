JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, OCT

Mundane, Silence

The Grey Monolith of existence tries to kill me again. I find some way or the other to discover fresh conspiracies and ask someone to solve for me. I wish I could just keep quiet and move on. There’s no problem at all - why this desire to keep asking for trouble every now and then. There’s little desire to solve anything that’s to be. In fact I should probably just consume the rations of the dispossessed and keep quiet.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
