JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FRIDAY, JUNE

DISQUIET, FRUSTRATION

This ledger was acquired for 300 credits—a small price for a sanctuary of noise. I had foolishly believed I would use it to map the architectures of the old world, the logic of the machine, the geometry of the void. But at the very outset, it feels like another broken promise. I’ll desecrate these pages with the same mediocre static that haunts my mind, a collection of useless thoughts that refuse to stay buried. I’ll make this 'Daily Ledger' an extension of the long, weary journals I’ve kept, another plague of trash to defile the emptiness. I have no desire to focus, no will to build. I only want my mind—whatever’s left of it—to sway and fizzle out in the dark. This concludes the much-anticipated preface for this book, which has been begging for the ink of a ghost. Soon, it will be filled with the illegible marks of a man who has nothing left to say.
