Journal of Disquiet

Daily entry

Just how absurd is my life going to be? I’m just wandering here aimlessly, in this tech park. Sitting in this hotel lobby and trying to be creative with my writing. This is perhaps how boring life could get - without a penny in my pocket, only left with tools that can be used for work and with an attitude of lethargy and indifference, where am I headed? Is this a good scenario for me? Most of the work that I have been doing, or at least that I call as ’work’ is utterly pointless without any value being generated for me. There’s no income coming by helping somebody set up VPN on a Linux system for free. They would hardly care to reimburse me for my time and effort. This is working with a hope that something will come in my way - such a misdirected attempt.
