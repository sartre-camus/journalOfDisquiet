JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, JULY

APATHY, POVERTY

Just how absurd is my life going to be? I’m just wandering aimlessly in this Steel Forest. Sitting in The Gilded Cage lobby and trying to be creative with my writing. This is perhaps how boring life could get - without a coin in my pocket, only left with tools that can be used for work and with an attitude of lethargy. Most of the work I call ’work’ is utterly pointless, generating no value. There’s no income coming from helping someone set up a Ghost Tunnel on a system for free. They would hardly care to reimburse my time. This is working with a hope that something will come my way - a misdirected attempt in a cold city.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
