JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, JUN

Consumption, Disgust

Codependency—the fancy word for needing to be needed. I'm just another consumer in the great machine, eating up movies and digital lies to fill the hole. There's been no attempt to create, just a long saga of swallowing what I'm fed.

I'm paying for the privilege of being distracted, and I hate every cent of it. I'm a man buying his own chains and calling it a lifestyle. This simple consumption is a trap I walked into with my eyes wide open.
