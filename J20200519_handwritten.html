JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

TUESDAY, MAY

AVARICE, ADDICTION

I desire to have an infinite amount of credits in my central ledger, irrespective of whether it is a source of pain or pleasure. I just feel overwhelmed today due to the lack of scrip in my account—my expenditures are so vast that I am unable to generate enough on one hand, and on the other, I cannot secure a loan. No one would lend to me anyway, and moreover, I have no intention of repayment. I call myself a parasite; I live like one and loathe other parasites, viewing them only as competition.

I have spent a significant portion of my credits on carnal distractions in the last cycle. The reason for this has been a never-ending compulsion to satisfy base urges. I must acknowledge that it is indeed an addiction, and I fail to control it with any regularity. Somehow, I feel exhausted now. The Monolith-LC tabs are kicking in, and they make me drowsy.

Today, I surrendered my garments to the cleansing sector. I am not sure how many credits they will charge me. I sent them there because I do not want to return to Unit P and expect her to process them in the machine and hang them. Another bit of domestic drivel I choose not to be part of—just cast it aside and wave it away. I am sure they can exist without me, and I shall be able to continue existing as long as I have credits here.

I hate being too rigid in my thinking; it bothers me sometimes. Perhaps I should return to the sleep-pod for a few more hours as the sedative takes hold, and then try to return to retrieve my belongings. Cycle-Start mornings are unforgettable, even when I try hard to purge them from memory altogether.

I shall scribble some more as I fail to perform the labor I thought I would do here. The reason is unclear, but for some reason, I believe it is better to simply doze off and sleep.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
