Daily entry

Here at the Goa international airport again after not even month. Catching an early morning flight back to Bangalore. Traveling by AirAsia this time - hope my luggage gets safely transported back to BLR. I’m getting the sleep now.. had to say goodbye to the feline friend which got agitated today and tried swinging its paws against my leg. Thankfully, I had trousers on, sometimes the pyjama provided enough protection. Only wish the nails haven’t pierced through the fabric and scraped my leg - can’t find any visible marks though - guess, I’m okay. Felines have no sense of gratitude whatsoever. They are whimsical and live by their own rules - guess they play around with humans and attack us when pissed off. I should have left it to sleep for some more time - but I couldn’t. I had to come over to the airport to board this AirAsia flight back home. The uncertainity of finding a Goa Miles cab from Parra to Dabolim airport at 5 AM made me come here early at 3 AM. Done with all the stuff - baggage check-in, security screening and the only part left is the boarding. My eyes are sleepy - the lounge is not open for me to go and sit there - now I have sit on these chairs that are no different from the ones that are found at a bus station back in Bangalore. The airport amenities at Goa are sub-standard compared to BIAL. Wish I could just sit at the lounge and wait - reminds of the Above Ground Level and the other domestic lounge at BLR airport. Anyways, that’s that now. I’ll have to go home for breakfast today - as I had told. Anything out of that schedule needs money to be spent and I can’t afford to do that with my current condition. Guess, I’ll just wait it out. One of the primary reasons for me to choose to get back to BLR home is to leverage the rig to finish up the pending tasks for the Mattermost integration which recently got sponsored by OPF on GitHub. I will have to ensure that I deliver on what Neils has asked me for. Also, the donations that are promised by OPF shall be used for the purposes as mentioned in the GitHub sponsors page - it shall not be used for personal purposes such as food, sex, entertainment, etc.
