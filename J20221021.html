JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

FRIDAY, OCT

Betrayal, Discipline

Back at the 'Emerald Port' terminal, waiting for a bird to take me back to the 'Steel City'. My feline informant turned on me this morning—swung a paw and tried to draw blood. No gratitude in this town, not even from the strays. I had to ditch the safehouse at 3 AM just to make sure I could catch a ride before the city woke up. Now I’m sitting on a plastic chair that feels like a park bench, waiting for the green light. I’m heading back to the 'Rig' to finish a job for 'The Open Foundation'. Neils is counting on me, and I’ve got a code of honor about these things. The credits I’m getting are for the mission, not for the high-life. I’m going back to the grind, back to the basics.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
