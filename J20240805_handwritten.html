JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, AUG

PARANOIA, CODEPENDENCY

An unwarranted visit to the Sector 7 Infirmary, compelled by a request from V. A wretched place to exist; the unseen pathogens drifting through these sterile halls are a constant irritation. Despite the facility’s veneer of modern efficiency and high-tech amenities, a cold dread settles in my chest. I should have arrived equipped with a respirator, but my supply is depleted—the last of my filters were surrendered to K.

Now I sit amidst the clinical paraphernalia in a corner kiosk, attempting to force some productivity—work, of all things! Picture me here, hunched over a terminal in a house of sickness, rewriting logic-streams while nursing a glass of synthetic citrus. I pay for the drink only to alleviate the guilt of occupying space without contributing credits.

How do I endure my own nature? This compulsive need to be needed—codependency, as the psych-evaluators call it. I understand so little of it. I have become reckless, squandering my existence on trivialities. I retreat from the abrasive edges of reality far too easily, dwelling instead on the path of least resistance. It is this hollow ease of living that I sustain myself upon. The question still strikes me with a heavy force...
