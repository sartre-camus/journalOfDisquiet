JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE WAYSTATION - SATURDAY, JUN

NIHILISM, STAGNATION, VOID

This is not the philosophy of The Cynic of Sector 9, for whom existence was a containment cell where he wove scrap wire to kill the hours. To be a true pessimist, one must view this cycle as a grand tragedy—an overblown, uncomfortable stance for anyone breathing this smog. It is true that we have no metric for the value of the data we churn out for The Collective. We produce it merely to let the clock run down, but we do so not like the convict weaving wire to hide from his termination date, but like a street urchin soldering circuit boards just to watch the sparks fly and nothing more.

For me, this city is just a waystation where I must wait until the automated hearse from the Void arrives to collect my remains. I have no idea where that transport leads because I know nothing of the world beyond the smog. I could view this hub as a prison, given I’m tethered to this sector; I could see it as a social club, given the ghosts I encounter in the neon-lit halls. Yet, unlike the others, I am neither restless nor eager for company.

I leave those who lock themselves in their hab-units, staring at the flickering ceiling fans, unable to find sleep; I leave those who chatter incessantly in the rain-slicked corridors, trying to drown out the hum of the machines with their own hollow voices.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
