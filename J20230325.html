JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

The Ledger of Lessons - SATURDAY, MARCH

introspection, purpose

The point of this ink isn’t to hoard memories that should have stayed buried. It’s to map the failures, to find the lessons hidden in the wreckage. If I’m going to improve, to make it through this city in one piece, I need to understand the hurdles. Journaling is the only way to see the obstacles for what they are, so I can make the right move when the shadows start closing in.
