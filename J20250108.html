JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

WEDNESDAY, JAN

Analysis, Idleness

I arrive at the Mind Sanitarium for CBT from the Grey Analyst. The idea of being ’cured’ from some psychological condition of mine that keeps me obsessively compulsed, pessimistic, troubled, indolent, and few more things that I cannot think of. A vacuum of idleness seems intolerable to me. I just cannot be idle, the challenges of being so are quite immense.
