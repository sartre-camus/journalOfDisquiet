JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

THE ANALOG SEVERANCE - SECTOR 7 - FRIDAY, NOV

ISOLATION, PARANOIA, DECAY

The ink is thick, black as the oil slicks in the gutters of the Low-Rises. I have enough paper to last until the neon finally burns out. I refuse to let my thoughts be digitized, uploaded, or processed by the monolithic servers of The Collective. Every stroke of this pen is a quiet rebellion against the constant hum of the machines. It is more than just cost-effective; it is the only way to keep the ghosts in the wires from harvesting my mind.

I have powered down the data-slates, the portable terminals, and the neural-simulators. They are nothing but tracking beacons for the Overseers, feeding them a constant stream of my location and my desires. The flickering screens are dead now, and they are of no use to me. I better stop using all of them once and for all. I shall refrain from the broadcast-shroud as well—that glowing idiot box is a nuisance designed to rot the spirit with its manufactured noise.

I am saying goodbye to every Grid-linked device. No more streaming propaganda, no more synthesized music from the cloud. This is my total severance from the digital web for the cycles to come. I will avoid watching anything at all. I shall also stop swiping those biometric chits and credit-chips. I refuse to handle their currency in any form, even the physical scrip that smells of the Iron Docks.

Venturing toward the Manufactory, even on foot, is a waste of what little life I have left. I don't wish to fall sick from the industrial fallout, the smog-choke, and the grey rot that hangs over the concrete wastes. Whatever is left out there in the rain, it can have the city. I am staying in the shadows.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
