JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

DECEMBER 20, CYCLE 24

STAGNATION, CONSUMPTION

The past week is a blur of archived transmissions I can barely recollect: Cargo-Hold (Cycle 24), Rituals on the Mound (Cycle 23), Sky-Seizure (Cycle 24), the Sector-814 Air-Theft, The Revenant (Cycle 13 or thereabouts), Diminishing (Aether Stream, Cycle 24), and several other seizure-related simulations I can no longer name.

I lose track of the fact that Cycle 24 is nearing its conclusion and Cycle 25 is looming. For an entire rotation, I scribbled '24', and now it will be '25'. It matters little; the chronometers just keep ticking forward into the void.

Perhaps a separate log is required for the endless stream of diversions I consume. My cognitive faculties have withered, reduced to the mere intake of synthetic entertainment. It disturbs me—the realization that I have nothing else to discuss, nothing else to record.

Beyond that, I find myself watching the Orb-Strike Endurance Trials and renewed my Neural-Net Uplink just to revisit Wasteland Survival IV and Capitalist Hegemony. I have since purged them all. The engagement is hollow; I have grown weary of the loops.

Occasionally, they provide a flicker of interest, but I lose hours—entire cycles—submerged in these simulations. I have petitioned the Bureau of Neural Records to cease their data-bursts, and I have done the same. There is a surplus of noise, yet a profound absence of substance.

BACK TO THE VOID. OR SEEK RANDOM.
