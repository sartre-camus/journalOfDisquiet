JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

MONDAY, APRIL

paranoia / anxiety

Anxiety claws at my throat after losing the access key to The Collective. Is this the city’s way of balancing the books? I need to cage the anger before it consumes me. The credits are low, the inflow has dried up. I’m not sure how long I can stay ahead of the collectors.
