JOURNAL OF DISQUIET

Search for Meaning - FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER

Philosophy, Duty

A warm bath and semi-formals—the uniform of a man pretending to have somewhere to be. I thought about the Monolith Corp, but decided to stay in the shadows of my room. The wire says my application is 'submitted,' but the silence is deafening. I spent the afternoon as a kitchen hand, peeling garlic and wiping the grease from the plates. It’s a sweaty, thankless work, made worse by the starch of my shirt.

I’m reading the Survivor Sage, Victor Frankl. I need a weapon against the Architects of Despair, Sartre and Camus. His words are simple, like a blunt knife, but they cut deep. I could finish it tonight. The thought of 'codependency' haunts my chores. Helping them feels less like love and more like a debt I can never fully pay. An ethical shackle I choose to wear.
