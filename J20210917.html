Daily entry

Today, I got a warm bath in the morning and got dressed up in semi-formals. I had plans of going to WeWork but later decided to stay back at home. I received an update regarding the SSE job application at Dell. Not an interview call but it says ’submitted’. I ordered half kilo mutton (goat) for preparing curry. I helped P with few things around the kitchen. Peeled garlic and cleaned the table. Later, I wiped all the washed utensils and placed them in the drawers of the kitchen cupboards. I feel sweaty and tired after doing all these because of the full-sleeves shirt that I’m wearing. P is preparing for masala for the curry and has started cooking the mutton. I hope P and D like the mutton, as I had given instructions to the butcher to get tender and less-bony mutton pieces for the curry. While doing the chores, I had the concept of codependency crop up every now and then. The only thought which mattered was - “Since I’m coexisting with people around me, I have do something and offer help with their chores”. Felt more like a ethical responsibility than a duty. Yesterday, I started reading the book written by Victor Frankl - “Man’s search for meaning”. Currently, I am trying to find a counter to Sartre’s existentialism and Camus’ absurdity of life. Seems like an easy read, as the language used is quite simple. I could breeze through it given few more hours or a day.
