Regret, Failure

I'm the king of the U-turn. I set a course, then steer straight into the rocks and wonder why I'm sinking. I live with regrets every day of my life, regardless of the pleasures I've tasted.

It's the same old reel, playing on loop in a deserted theater. No matter where I go, it's this repeat of defying what I wanted and hating what I got.
